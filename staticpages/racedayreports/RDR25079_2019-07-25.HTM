RACE NO.1 –  THE BEYOND EXPECTATION PLATE (DIV.II) : (About) 1000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

THEA’S PET (App.A.S.Peter) swayed its head as the start was given.

 

At the start, FABIO (App. Niranjan S. Parmar, dr.8) jumped inwards and went across THEA’S PET (App. A. S. Peter, dr.7) who was marginally slow to begin. Thereafter, FABIO further shifted in thereby inconveniencing ZEEMO (App. M. Ayyar, dr.6). App. NIranjan S. Parmar has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 200 metres, LOVELY FAIRY (Amyn Merchant) shifted out thereby inconveniencing ZEEMO (App. M. Ayyar). Jockey Amyn Merchant has been cautioned.

 

Passing 100 metres, BOHEMIAN (App. D. R. Shubham) shifted out under urgings and carried LOVELY FAIRY (Amyn Merchant) and ZEEMO (App. M. Ayyar) outwards. App. D. R. Shubham has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Apprentice A. S. Peter (THEA’S PET) reported that the Handler was holding the horse while the start was given, due to which he lost ground. The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into this incident and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, App. Raghuveer Singh (ROMANTIC EYES) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

After the race, PADMAKOSA was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

RACE NO.6 –  THE D K ASHISH TROPHY : (About) 1000 Metres – 9 Runners

 

DANCE SMARTLY (D.A.Naik) was reluctant to be stalled. After being stalled, GOLDEN ERA (Santosh G.) played up and hooked one of his fore leg in the adjoining stall and TURMERIC TOWER (P.Trevor) started leaning in the stall and momentarily tried to duck the front gate. Both the horses   were taken out and examined by the Club’s VO who certified them fit to race. Trainers C.Rajendra and Adhirajsingh Jodha have been asked to give their respective charge DANCE SMARTLY and GOLDEN ERA more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

An objection was lodged by App.A.S.Peter, the rider of CIPHER (Placed Second) against TURMERIC TOWER (P.Trevor, placed First). The reason being, TURMERIC POWER shifted in at the 200 metres and bumped his mount and thereafter kept him pocketed whereby his filly lost her stride and he could not ride her out. Thereafter, he pulled the filly out and she covered lot of ground from the outside and the interference had cost  him the race.

 

After considering the submissions made by App.A.S.Peter who was accompanied by Trainer Asad Siddqui as also Jockey P.Trevor, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule the objection.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that SACHERTORTE had right eye injury (sore).

RE: APPEAL FROM JOCKEY P.TREVOR AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB CONFIRMING THE DECISION OF THE STIPENDIARY STEWARDS SUSPENDING HIS JOCKEKY’S LICENCE FOR 2 RACE DAYS FOR RIDING AN INCOMPETENT RACE ON “STRONG STORM” IN RACE NO.157 – THE NOBLE OPINION PLATE RUN ON 21ST FEBRUARY, 2019 – WAS GRANTED A STAY UNTIL HIS APPEAL IS HEARD AND DISPOSED OF:

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 24th July, 2019 considered Appeal dated 16th April, 2019 from Jockey P.Trevor in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted including Jockey P.Trevor’s grounds of appeal and also heard the submissions made by him and viewed the video tape recording of the race in question as also the video recording of Race No.138. The Board of Appeal deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds to the same, decided to reject the appeal and forfeit the deposit. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands.  In view of the stay granted to Jockey P.Trevor, his punishment would now come into force on 3rd and 4th August, 2019 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race)

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “LABURNUM” (A.SANDESH, DR.7) IN RACE NO.248 – THE GOODBYE PLATE (DIV.I) (1400 METRES) RUN ON 14TH APRIL,  2019 WHICH THE FILLY WON AS COMPARED TO HER THREE PREVIOUS STARTS DURING THE MUMBAI MEETING 2018/19 WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Sanjay Kolse in the above connection. He stated that prior to Race No.248, the filly raced thrice during the Mumbai Meeting 2018/19. In her first run in Race No.90 over 1400 metres, the filly raced in a terms race for horses 4-years-old in strong company and she finished unplaced. In her next run in Race No.138 over 1600 metres in Class-IV (for horses rated 20 to 46) she was ridden by Jockey A.Sandesh and she finished a decent sixth, about 5 ½ lengths from the winner. Thereafter, in her third run, the filly ran in Race No.171 over 1600 metres in Class-V (for horses rated 4 to 30) where she was ridden by Jockey R.Ajinkya and in that race the filly received interference in the straight despite which she finished fifth. He then raced the filly after a gap of 38 days in Race No.248 over 1400 metres and again declared Jockey A.Sandesh and she won the race. Also, there was a change of equipment this time i.e. he discontinued the use of Blinkers and used a Sheep Skin Cheek Piece, which probably helped her.

 

Having heard the submissions made and considering that the filly had met with interference in her previous start in Race No.171, there was a change of equipment and she raced after a gap of 38 days, no action was initiated against Trainer Sanjay Kolse except warning him to be more careful in the future.
