ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.
PUNE MEETING 2023, SIXTH DAY, SATURDAY, 16TH, SEPTEMBER, 2023.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 4.4 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POSITION FROM 2000 METRES TO 1400 METRES, 2 METRES  WIDE AT 1200 METRES, 4 METRES WIDE AT 1000 METRES, 5 METRES WIDE AT 600 METRES, THEREAFTER 6 METRES WIDE UP TO THE WINNING POST. RAILS WERE PLACED 5 METRES WIDE IN THE CHUTE.

 

RACE NO.1(73) – THE STAR OF GIBRALTAR PLATE: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS -  10

 

ANOUSHKA (S. Saqlain, dr.2) was reluctant to stall. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

MYRCELLA (T. S. Jodha, dr.8) was restless and was not allowing the rider to settle in the stall. Trainer Shazaan Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

COME BACK PLEASE (S. G. Prasad, dr.3) jumped out slowly losing about two lengths at the start.

 

ESFIR (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.7) jumped out slowly losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BETWEEN FRIENDS (App. Haridas Gore, dr.6) jumped inwards and had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 150 metres, SILVER STEPS (H. Zeeshan) leaned inwards onto DAGGER’S STRIKE (M. S. Deora) who had to be steadied. Jockey H. Zeeshan has been cautioned.

 

After the race, MYRCELLA was observed to have a gum injury.

 

After the race, DAGGER’S STRIKE was found to have an injury on left hind hock.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ESFIR was found to have sore right eye.

 

RACE NO.2(74) – THE CAMBRIDGE PLATE: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  13

 

At the start, MILLI (A. K. Aniket, dr.4) jumped outwards onto LIAM (S. Mosin, dr.5).

 

Passing the 800 metres, MILLI (A. K. Aniket) leaned inwards and brushed the railings for no apparent reason.

 

Approaching the 100 metres, LIAM (S. Mosin) leaned inwards due to which MILLI (A. K. Aniket) shied in and went across MOJO (Amyn Merchant) who had to be steadied.

 

After the race, PHENOM was trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, MILLI was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(75) – THE EXCELLENT TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  7

 

After the race, Jockey S. Saqlain (THE PROTECTOR) reported that passing the 200 metres he was held up for room momentarily causing him to steady his mount.

 

RACE NO.4(76) – PARIMATCH PUNE CITY GOLD CUP: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES –RUNNERS- 6

 

Approaching the 300 metres, it was observed that Jockey P. Trevor (FOREST FLAME) lost his left rein momentarily. On questioning, Jockey P. Trevor stated that his whip got entangled in the rein due to which his left rein slipped.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that PRIDE’S ANGEL was found to have sore right eye.

 

RACE NO.5(77) – THE FOURTH ESTATE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS -  12

 

It was observed that RISING POWER (A. K. Aniket) & AXIS (K. Nazil) were observed to have run detached from the field. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to ensure that his charges put up a reasonable performance in the future.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor (CAPUCINE) reported that his mount was inclined outwards throughout the race due to which he could not ride out his mount to full advantage. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CAPUCINE was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on left hind pastern. Further, at the behest of the Stipendiary Stewards, endoscopy was performed on CAPUCINE at the Equine Hospital and no abnormality was detected.

 

RACE NO.6(78) – THE JACK FROST PLATE: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 13

 

LIT (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.3) took a fly jump at the start.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe (LIT) reported loss of whip at about 150 metres. This being his first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2023, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, STAR LAP was observed to have lost its right fore shoe.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that STAR LAP was found to have sore right eye.
