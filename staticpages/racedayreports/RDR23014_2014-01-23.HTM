RACE NO.2(158) –THE APRIL BLOSSOM PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres –Runners - 7  

 

Two objections were lodged in this race. The first objection was lodged by Jockey P.Trevor, rider of STRATEGY (Placed Fourth) against DREAMS R MINE (Kavraj Singh, Placed Second) and BOURBON STREET (S.Mosin, placed Third). The reason being, at about 350 metres, DREAMS R MINE (Kavraj Singh) came in sharply and bumped him out of the race whereby he lost his balance totally, and simultaneously BOURBON STREET (S.Mosin) shifted out and squeezed him whereby he was unbalanced and this cost him a certain second place.

 

The second objection was lodged by Mr.Sanjay Kolse, Trainer  of BOURBON STREET (S.Mosin, Placed Third) against DREAMS R MINE (Kavraj Singh, Placed Second).  The reason being, between the 400 and 300 metres, DREAMS R MINE (Kavraj Singh) came on to BOURBON STREET whereby Jockey S.Mosin was unbalanced for a few strides and could not ride out the horse or else he could have finished closer to the winner.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned as also Trainer Sanjay Kolse in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection lodged by Mr. Sanjay Kolse, the Trainer of BOURBON STREET (S.Mosin, Placed Third) against DREAMS R MINE(Kavraj Singh, Placed Second). The Objection lodged by Jockey P.Trevor, the rider of STRATEGY (Placed Fourth) against DREAMS R MINE (Kavraj Singh, Placed Second) and BOURBON STREET (S.Mosin, Placed Third) was upheld and the order of placing revised as under :-

MAGIC STAR            -        FIRST;                    DREAMS R MINE        -        THIRD;

STRATEGY               -        SECOND;                 BOURBON STREET    -        FOURTH

 

The Stewards after the last race interviewed Jockey S.Mosin for careless riding on his mount BOURBON STREET and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 9th February, 2014 to 13th February, 2014 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

The Stewards also interviewed Jockey Kavraj Singh for careless riding on his mount DREAMS R MINE. However, in view of the Club’s Sr.V.O. reporting that the horse was observed after the race to have broken a blood vessel, the Stewards decided to give Jockey Kavraj Singh the benefit of the doubt and he was warned to be more careful in the future.

After the race, DREAMS R MINE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

RACE NO.4(160) – THE AVANTAGE PLATE :  (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 6

 

HIDDEN GOODNESS (P.Trevor) was reluctant to reach the starting point. Trainer S.S.Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the horse.

 

DOMINGO (Dashrath Singh) jumped out throwing its head.

 

Trainer Nina M. Lalvani and Jockey Dashrath Singh were questioned regarding the running and riding of DOMINGO. The instructions to the Jockey were to be in 2nd / 3rd position, and if not, he may go to the front. It was put to the Jockey that he has fought with his mount for nearly 500 metres from the start and had placed his mount at a disadvantage. The Trainer opined that the Jockey had ridden an ill-judged race in the early part. The matter was reported to the Stewards.

The Stewards after the last race interviewed Trainer Nina M.Lalvani and Jockey Dashrath Singh and having considered the submissions made and viewing the video recording of the race, decided to give Jockey Dashrath Singh the benefit of the doubt. However, he was warned to follow the instructions in the future.

 

Jockey S.Sunil (NEW WORLD), when questioned, in respect of his travelling wide while negotiating the bend and in the straight, stated that he was following DOMINGO (Dashrath Singh), the only threat to him and due to DOMINGO racing a bit erratic ahead of him, he could not get a clear passage and he was forced to travel wide. An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of NEW WORLD and a report will be submitted shortly. 

 

After the race, DOMINGO was found to be sound.

RACE NO.7(163) –  THE ANMOL PREET PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners – 17

 

At the time of weighing out, Jockey Prabhu Naidu (CURIOSITY) informed that he would carry an overweight instead of him claiming an allowance of 3.5 kgs over the published weight of 54.5 kgs. Trainer Dallas Todywalla was not agreeable for the Jockey to carry an overweight and requested for a change of Jockey, which was acceded to and Jockey Prabhu Naidu was replaced by Jockey S.P.Ranjane.

 

CARTEL (C.S.Jodha) was reluctant to reach the starting point. Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

UCHESHRAWA (S.G.Prasad) lost about 5 lengths at the start. Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, ADMINISTRATE (R Woodworth, dr. 7) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to HYDE PARK (Dashrath Singh, dr. 8) who went across KAIZER (Dharmendar, dr.9), who was slow, and then went out on to PRESENCE (N.S.Parmar, dr. 10).

 

Near 1400 metres, SHINING PATH (P.Trevor) shifted out and carried HYDE PARK (Dashrath Singh) outwards who was tightened on to PRESENCE (N.S.Parmar) and steadied, losing position. Jockey P.Trevor has been cautioned.

 

When questioned, Jockey S.Sunil (RUBENS) stated that SHINING PATH (P.Trevor) had shifted-in and was racing in front of him due to which, he could not continue to ride out his mount and to avoid clipping the heels of SHINING PATH (P Trevor), he had eased up his mount just before the winning post. Whilst accepting the explanation, Jockey S Sunil has been cautioned.

 

After the race, LAURUS PRIDE, CURIOSITY, ADMINISTRATE and THE PACE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(164) – THE  CAPTAIN G.HALL TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 17

After the race, KATNISS was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

RE : CONFIRMATORY REPORT IN RESPECT OF THE COMPULSORY SAMPLE OF URINE TAKEN FROM  dk b f “BULLSEYE” ON 6TH DECEMBER,  2013 AS THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED FROM THE STUD FARM:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 18th January, 2014 interviewed Trainer P.Shroff regarding the Confirmatory Analysis Report dated 17th January, 2014 from LCH, France, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge BULLSEYE on 6th December, 2013 since the filly had arrived to his charge  from Nanoli Stud Farm,  which read as under :-

 

“…..It is hereby confirmed that the sample, which has been analysed as set out above, contains BOLDENONE”

 

A copy of the above report was handed over to Tr.P.Shroff and he was asked to continue his own enquiries and to lead evidence, if any, to prove mitigating circumstances, within 15 days and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.Veterinary Officer of the Club, as and when he would be required to.

 

Further, as per the Club’s Notice dated 23rd November, 2013, the Stewards of the Club have decided not to permit BULLSEYE to race for a period of six months i.e. up to and inclusive of 5th June, 2014.
