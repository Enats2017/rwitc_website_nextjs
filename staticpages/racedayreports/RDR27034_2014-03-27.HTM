RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “SCARAMOUCHE” IN RACE NO.280 RUN ON 20TH MARCH, 2014 WHICH THE GELDING WON AS COMPARED TO HIS PREVIOUS START IN RACE NO.243 RUN ON 6TH MARCH, 2014 WHERE HE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait in the above connection. It was put to him that SCARAMOUCHE had shown marked improvement in a gap of two weeks and that there was a common horse ASAHI who had finished ahead of SCARAMOUCHE in Race No.243 whereas SCARAMOUCHE has beaten ASAHI by 8 lengths with the same weight difference.

 

Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait explained that today the horse was running over 1400 metres as compared to 1600 metres in the previous race and he had instructed Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar to take a good jump, settle the horse and to do his best. He added that he had raced the horse in a short span considering that this was an age group race and there were not many age group races programmed in the Prospectus and the next one is in mid April, 2014.  He further stated that in today’s race ASAHI was settled behind which may have not suited that horse. He said that there was no change in the work pattern except that due to the hot weather he had swan the horse which may have helped the horse. He said that the horse was at longer odds.

 

After viewing the video recording of both the races, the explanation tendered by Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait was noted.

RE :  ENQUIRY  INTO  THE  POSITIVE  REPORT OF THE COMPULSORY SAMPLE OF URINE TAKEN FROM  dk b f “BULLSEYE” ON 6TH DECEMBER,  2013 AS THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED FROM THE STUD FARM:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Monday, 24th February, 2014, Wednesday, 12th March, 2014 and Friday, 14th March, 2014 considered the above matter.

 

         The Stewards deliberated the matter at length. The Stewards after having thoroughly enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and the submissions made by Trainer P.Shroff, decided by majority that Trainer P.Shroff was in breach of the Medication Rules governing the finding of BOLDENONE (an anabolic steroid) in the urine sample of BULLSEYE and held him vicariously responsible for the following reasons :-

 

1)    BULLSEYE had left Trainer P.Shroff’s yard in mid April, 2013 for summering at Nanoli Stud as per the Left Charge form submitted by him. Though BULLSEYE went for sampling directly from Nanoli Stud on 6th December 2013, it is a fact that the filly was under the care of Trainer P Shroff till 15th August 2013 and thereafter was not subjected to any test of her urine by the RWITC Ltd. As an anabolic steroid could remain in the system of a race horse for upto 180 days, it cannot be conclusively established as to when and where the BOLDENONE was administered to the filly.

 

2)    It has been stated that anabolics are not typically given to fillies and hence the finding of BOLDENONE in BULLSEYE (a filly) was unlikely. However, it is gathered that BULLSEYE had a sesamoid/lameness problem and Veterinary experts have stated that anabolics are also given to help “stress and strain” in all race horses. Further, BULLSEYE moved between Nanoli Stud and Trainer P.Shroff’s yard twice between April, 2013 and August, 2013.

 

3)    Though BULLSEYE went for sampling directly from Nanoli Stud in December, 2013, all the charges of “conspiracy and sabotage” remains a conjecture. It has been established in the enquiry that there is a close relationship between Trainer P.Shroff and the proprietor of Nanoli Stud and his horses often shuttle between Nanoli Stud and his yard.  Also, it was noted that Nanoli Stud had employed a farrier who was warned off by the RWITC and he was the prime suspect of sabotage, as alleged by Mr. Shroff.

 

4)    It is gathered that Trainer P.Shroff accompanied Mr.K.N.Dhunjibhoy to a meeting held in New Delhi with Mrs Maneka Gandhi to appoint an independent committee to enquire with this infringement, even before the Stewards had begun their enquiry. At the same meeting Trainer P.Shroff and Mr.Dhunjibhoy participated in a discussion suggesting possible names of the Enquiry Officers. Arising out of this meeting and an independent letter by Mr.Dhunjibhoy to the Chairman of the Animal Welfare Board of India, an attempt was made to delay the enquiry of the Stewards, which not only established the relationship between Trainer P.Shroff and Mr.K.N.Dhunjibhoy but was also seen as a pre mature attempt to bear influence on the Stewards.

 

5)    Trainer P.Shroff has stated that a private sample collected at the stud farm was sent by Nanoli Stud in November, 2013 which was clean and this strengthened his suspicion of sabotage, though the Rules of Racing do not take cognizance of the result of such private samples as there is no chain of custody under the control of the RWITC. Ltd.

 

6)    Trainer P.Shroff has been unable to explain the relatively low level of BOLDENONE in the urine sample taken on 6th December, 2013 which would be unlikely if the horse was sabotaged. The subsequent private sample showed a much higher level, as alleged by him, was not taken by the RWITC, Ltd. and hence cannot be relied upon.

 

7)    Some of the comments made by Trainer P.Shroff in his complaint dated 23rd January, 2014 as the possible reasons for the alleged sabotage are entirely conjecture and remain unsubstantiated.

 

8)    From the experience of past positives for the same drug of horses coming from Nanoli Stud, and in his own interest, Trainer P.Shroff ought to have had BULLSEYE tested by the Club’s Vet before she left Nanoli Stud and ensured her security before her sampling in Mumbai.

 

9)    The Stewards noted that as per the earlier decision of the Stewards which has been notified in the Club’s Racing Calendar, should the urine sample of any horse, whenever tested, be reported positive for the presence of any anabolic steroids the Stewards would deal with such cases at their absolute discretion in the most stringent manner. The Stewards also noted that even as per the Calendar Notification dated 24th September, 2008 concerning “Sampling of Racing Stock at the Stud Farms” states “in case of a positive, the same penalties will be applicable to the Trainer as are published in the Racing Calendar for the breach of norms for Random Sampling of horses in Training” and hence the responsibility for the horse which had specifically gone for summering remained with Trainer P. Shroff. The Stewards further noted that at various meetings of the Turf Authorities of India starting from November, 11, 2013 and recommended unanimously by the Chairmen of the Turf Authorities of India and by the Veterinary Commission of India, and not objected to by the RWITC Stewards at any stage, a two year ban on a trainer is applicable on the finding of an anabolic steroid in the sample of any horse in training. Notwithstanding all these facts, taking an overall view and taking into view the submissions made by Trainer P.Shroff, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided as under:-

 

i)             Instead of imposing stringent punishment as per the Calendar Notification dated 17th April, 2011, it was decided to suspend Mr.P. Shroff’s Trainer’s license w.e.f. 29th March, 2014 till the end of Mumbai Meeting 2013/14. Thereafter, if he applies for a Trainer’s license for the Season 2014/15, it may be granted to him, however, his license shall stand suspended up to and inclusive of 31st December, 2014;

 

ii)           To immediately test all the horses under the charge of Trainer P.Shroff for the presence of anabolic steroids and sex hormones;

 

iii)         After completion of his punishment when Trainer P.Shroff starts training again, all his runners will be compulsorily sampled every alternate month for a period of two years;

 

iv)          After completion of his punishment on 31st December, 2014, Trainer P.Shroff will not be allowed to take charge of any race horses from any Stud Farm except 2-year-olds, other than the horses leaving and returning from another racing centre. This restriction will be applicable for the next three years after Trainer P.Shroff resumes training of horses under the Rules of this Club.

 

         Trainer P.Shroff has appealed to the Board of Appeal against the above decision of the Stewards of the Club and the Board of Appeal has granted him a stay until his appeal is heard and disposed of.
