RACE NO.1(10) - THE AGNI PLATE (DIV.II):  (About) 1000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, ADMIRAL GRAY (Shrikant Kamble, dr.8) shifted-in and went across EXACTLY (M A Roushan, dr.7) and at the same time, ANNABETH (C Umesh, dr.6) shifted out, due to which both, ADMIRAL GRAY and ANNABETH, made contact with each other, which resulted in Jockey Shrikant Kamble (ADMIRAL GRAY) losing his balance and being dislodged. Since the incident was accidental no action has been initiated.

 

Soon after the start, KATNISS (App.S Joseph) shifted-in on to WITHOUT FEAR (App.Akshay Gaikwad), who in turn shifted-in and made contact with RISING ANGEL (Altaf Sayyed). App S Joseph has been cautioned. 

 

After the race, WITHOUT FEAR was observed to have a cut injury at the left fore bulb of heel. EMBER LADY and ICE ICE BABY were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(11) - THE RUBEN STAR TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – 11 Runners

 

At the start, YOU’RE THE BOSS (App Akshay Gaikwad) jumped awkwardly due to which the rider brushed the Gates and lost his right stirrup which he regained shortly.

 

Jockey S Mosin (HALLE) reported that his mount was uncomfortable during the race. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the mare and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

Near 300 metres, QUEST FOR LOVE (Hanumant Singh) was hampered for a run as RUNNING SUN (App K N Bhati) shifted-in. App. K N Bhati has been asked to be more careful.

 

After the race, WINGS OF GLORY, YOU’RE THE BOSS and RUBENS were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(12) - THE AGNI PLATE (DIV.I):  (About) 1000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

TO THE MOON (I.Pasha) lost about 3 lengths at the start.

 

An objection was lodged by App.D.S.Daman, rider of CEASARINA (Placed Second) against SUNSHINE HEART (App.Santosh, Placed First). The reason being, SUNSHINE HEART shifted in at about 50 metres and took his ground whereby he had to check his mount and come out and inspite of that his filly was moving fast and gaining ground on the winner. If he had not received the check, he would have won the race.

 

After considering the submissions made by both the Apprentice Jockeys/Trainers of the horses concerned in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection.. The Stewards directed the Stipendiary Stewards to caution App.Santosh.

 

Passing 600 metres, EARTH QUAKE (S P Ranjane) shifted-in and tightened CEASARINA (App.D S Daman), who in turn caused interference to UNICO CORAZON (Vikram Walkar). Shortly afterwards, LUCKY CHARM (J E Pereira) shifted-in and went on to EARTH QUAKE, who in turn went on to CEASARINA who went across UNICO CORAZON. An enquiry has been initiated into the incident and a report will be submitted later.

 

Approaching 200 metres, LUCKY CHARM (J E Pereira) shifted out and travelled wide losing ground. An enquiry has been initiated and a report will be submitted later.

After the race, CUT DIAMOND, LUCKY CHARM, CEASARINA, TO THE MOON and SNOW KING were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(13) - THE STAR WISH PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 12 Runners

 

On a request from Trainer Adil Dajee, MCHUMBA was examined at 0800 hours on 25/07/2014 and was found to be sore by the right hind leg as such the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit to race by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

MAHIMA (Neeraj Rawal) lost about 3 lengths at the start.

 

Trainer H J Antia has been fined Rs 1,000/- for declaring Jockey C S Jodha on his two of his horses i.e. ICE RINK and STAR BUILDER in the same race.

The Sr. Veterinary Officer reported that DOUBLE OR QUITS (Towfeeq R Shaikh) splayed one of its hind plates in the saddle Enclosure, as such the other hind plate was removed and the mare ran with Aluminium, hind bare.

 

Jockey P Trevor (BRAVE FIGHTER) reported that he found his mount uncomfortable in the straight. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

ICE RINK (C Umesh) travelled wide near 1000 metres and continued to do so for the remaining part of the race. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that ICE RINK was examined at the stables in the morning on 26/07/2014 and was found to be sound.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (MAHIMA) reported that his mount was inclined to lug-in during the race. Trainer Ivor Fernandes has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

App D S Daman (QUEEN) reported that he lost his left stirrup at the start as his leg brushed the gate which he regained near 900 metres.

After the race,  MIGHTY SPLENDOUR and MAHIMA were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(14) - THE AMOROUS KNIGHT TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres - 9 Runners

 

UKULELE (Neeraj Rawal) and ATHENS (Dashrath Singh)  refused to be stalled despite all efforts, as such, both the horses were withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Orders.  INNOCENT AFFAIR (C.S.Jodha) whipped around after the start thereby dislodging the rider. Trainers Sanjay Kolse, Dallas Todywalla have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. 

 

GOLDEN MILLENIUM (M A Roushan) ran unsettled during the race and near 900 metres scrapped the race.

After the race, GOLDEN MILLENIUM was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(15). THE SECRET ROMEO PLATE : (About) 1000 Metres – 12 Runners

 

After being stalled, BRYNHILL (N.S.Parmar) reared up and hooked one of its fore leg in the adjoining stall. The gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. who certified him fit to race. 

 

At the start, SAINTS N SINNERS (V Chandrakant) jumped out awkwardly and then was steadied as SO TRUE (App Akshay Gaikwad) shifted out and took the running of the former. App Akshay Gaikwad has been cautioned.

 

Jockey C S Jodha (INTESAR) has been fined Rs 2,000/- for improper use of the whip wherein he has used the whip in a fore-hand position on the neck of his mount.

 

After the race, DEEP DIVER, IN THE LIMELIGHT, SO TRUE, INVINCIBLE and INTESAR were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(16) - THE RUBEN STAR TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – 10 Runners

 

UCHESHRAWA (App.S.Joseph) planted and took no part in the race. Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

MERCHANTOFVENICE (P Trevor) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer B Prakash has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Approaching 200 metres, DHANYAVAAD (Sandeep Jadhav) shifted out and took the running of MERCHANTOFVENICE (P Trevor) who was checked. Jockey Sandeep Jadhav has been cautioned.

 

FIRE ENGINE (R Shelar) was tightened for room in the straight while racing between ACHIEVE (N S Parmar) and ZENOFIC (D K Ashish) and thereby did not get a clear run.

 

After the race, SOUND OF RIVER, ZENOFIC and MERCHANTOFVENICE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(17) - THE ASCOT LASS PLATE (DIV.II):(About) 1200 Metres -11 Runners

 

On a request from Trainer Altaf Hussain, MUMBAI KING was examined at 0845 hours on 23/07/2014 and was found to have an injury on the right shoulder, right stifle and quarter, subsequent to a fall. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting with him to race.

 

SCALANAYA (Nirmal Jodha) jumped out slow and thereafter abruptly stopped galloping and took no part in the race.  Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race

 

At the start, WIND AND STARS (C S Jodha) lost its footing and began awkwardly.

 

Jockey J Chinoy (MAJESTERIAN) reported that his mount ran erratic and unsettled during the race. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the colt and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (SEVEN SEAS) reported that his mount leaned-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

RACE NO.9(  18) - THE ASCOT LASS PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 12 Runners

 

ALEJOR (Kishore Kadam) was restless in the stall. GRAND DUKE (N.S.Parmar) lost about5 lengths at the start. Trainers Altamaash A. Ahmed and M.K.Jadhav have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Near 300 metres, GREAT EXPECTATION (P Trevor) shifted out to make his move where there was insufficient room and jostled with EL WONDER (TS Jodha) who had BRAZIL (Nirmal Jodha) on his outside, whereby Jockey TS Jodha lost his balance. Jockey P Trevor has been fined Rs.5000/-.
