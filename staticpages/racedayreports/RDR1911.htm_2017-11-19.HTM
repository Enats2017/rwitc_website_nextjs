RE : APPEAL FROM JOCKEY P.S.CHOUHAN IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE FOR EIGHT RACE DAYS  - W..E.F. 28/10/2017 TO 29/10/2017 AND FROM 19/11/2017 TO 07/12/2017 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR UNSATISFACTORY RIDING– ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “JUNE” IN RACE NO.89 – THE RADICAL FORCE PLATE RUN ON 3rd SEPTEMBER, 2017 :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 7th November, 2017 considered Appeal dated 11th October, 2017 from Jockey P. S. Chouhan in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal having considered all documents and papers that were submitted including Jockey P. S. Chouhan’s Grounds of Appeal, and having heard the submissions made by him and his Spokesman, Mr. Javed Tapia and having viewed the video recording of the race in question as also Race No.185, deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds felt that it was injudicious riding on his part and not unsatisfactory riding. The Board therefore decided to partially allow the appeal by modifying the period of his punishment from 8 Race Days to 4 Race Days as also a fine of Rs.10,000/-. In view of the stay granted to Jockey P. S. Chouhan on his punishment, his Jockey’s license now stands suspended w.e.f. 19th November, 2017 to 30th November, 2017 (both days inclusive). The deposit was ordered to be refunded.

RE : APPEAL FROM APPRENTICE VISHAL N.BUNDE  IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS APPRENTICE JOCKEY’S LICENSE W..E.F. 15/10/2017 TILL THE END OF THE MUMBAI MEETING 2017/18 FOR GROSS MISCONDUCT ON THE PREMISES OF THE RACECOURSE – ENQUIRY INTO THE FIGHT THAT TOOK PLACE IN THE JOCKEYS ROOM ON 28th AUGUST, 2017 BETWEEN HIM AND TRAINEE APPRENTICE PRAKASH AKHADE :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 7th November, 2017 considered Appeal dated 18th October, 2017 from Apprentice Vishal N. Bunde in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal having considered all documents and papers that were submitted, including Apprentice Vishal N. Bunde’s Grounds of Appeal, and having heard the submissions made by  him and his Spokesman, Mr. Neeraj Rawal, deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds, decided to reject his appeal and forfeit the deposit. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands.

 

Further, the Board of Appeal was perturbed to have such an incident taking place on the premises of the Racecourse and felt that the punishment meted out by the Stewards of the Club appeared to be inadequate considering, the gravity of the offence. The Board therefore decided to issue a show-cause to Apprentice Vishal N. Bunde asking him to show cause as to why his punishment should not be enhanced.

 

Accordingly, Apprentice Vishal N. Bunde was asked to show cause in writing latest by 1.00 p.m. on 22nd November, 2017 as to why his punishment should not be enhanced as stated above and the matter will be placed  before the Board of Appeal at its meeting to be held 1 ½ hours before the first race on 24th November, 2017.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “GENTILESSE” IN RACE NO.131 – THE DANCING GLANCES PLATE RUN ON 23RD SEPTEMBER, 2017:

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Sanjay Kolse and Jockey Neeraj Rawal in the above connection. Jockey Neeraj Rawal stated that he was instructed to keep the filly covered and upon turning for home, to be in a striking position. To a specific question, Jockey Rawal stated that he was not instructed to sit in any particular position. Trainer Kolse responded stating that he had asked the Jockey to sit well up preferably 2nd/3rd, sit under cover, and since she is a big striding filly, to give her plenty of galloping room.

 

When asked about his observations of the race, Trainer Kolse stated that he chose Jockey Neeraj Rawal as he had ridden the filly earlier. He felt that since the filly has good initial speed, the Jockey should have made an effort to sit further up in the field. Jockey Neeraj Rawal explained that at the start, he was interfered by the horse drawn on his inside, thereafter, the said horse veered in creating room for him on the inside and instead of using the filly in the early part, he chose to shift in and sit under cover. However, the horses that went to the front soon slowed down the pace and that did not suit the filly.

 

Taking into account the submissions made and after having viewed the race from all available angles and keeping in mind that Trainer Sanjay Kolse had expressed his dissatisfaction with the riding efforts of Jockey Neeraj Rawal, the matter was placed before the Stewards for consideration.  

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 19th November, 2017 interviewed Trainer Sanjay Kolse and Jockey Neeraj Rawal in the above connection and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of the race, decided that no action was called for in the matter.
