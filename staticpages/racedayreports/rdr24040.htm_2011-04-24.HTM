RACE DAY REPORTS

RACE NO 1. (356) THE  ALCHEMY PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres. Runners – 5

 

STARFALL (Suraj Narredu) lost about 3 lengths at the start.

Near 200 metres, ASHWA PUSKAR (Dashrath Singh) drifted outwards as the Jockey used his whip once. Jockey Dashrath Singh has been cautioned.

RACE NO 4. (359) THE TRAINERS TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres.  Runners – 9

RACE NO 7. (362) THE  RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres.  Runners - 16

After the race, OCEAN OF FAITH was observed to have upper gum injury. CIRCUS RING, ATLANTIC STAR & TAG ALONG TOMMY were found to be sound.

ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “STUD OF GOLD” IN RACE NO.349 – THE GOLD DUST PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 23RD APRIL, 2011 WHICH THE GELDING WON AS COMPARED TO ITS SIX PREVIOUS RUNS DURING THE CURRENT MUMBAI MEETING WHERE IT WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Sunday, 24th April, 2011 considered the Stipendiary Stewards note in the above connection. They interviewed Trainer H.J.Antia and having heard the submissions made by him and having viewed the video recording of Race No.349 as also the previous runs of STUD OF GOLD during the current Mumbai Meeting, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer H.J.Antia for the reversal of form shown by his horse STUD OF GOLD in Race No.349. In that,  the Stewards noted that prior to Race No.349, STUD OF GOLD ran six times during the current Mumbai Meeting 2010/1, in all the races the horse was ridden by mediocre Jockeys and was  available at longer odds and finished unplaced. Further, there were two common horses in Race No.349 and the previous Race No.312 viz. BEMISAAL and RAJDOOT who had placed 3rd and 4th respectively in Race No.312 and his horse STUD OF GOLD had finished more than 10 lengths behind RAJDOOT and in Race No.349 STUD OF GOLD finished more than 5 lengths of both the common horses, thus showing marked improvement over its previous run. Also, the Stewards noted that there was a change in the running pattern of the horse i.e. in all the previous runs, STUD OF GOLD was settled behind, contrary to his style of running whereas in Race No.349, Jockey S.Zervan settled the horse nicely in 3rd/4th position and the horse won the race. Therefore, the Stewards decided :-

a)   that should Mr.H.J.Antia apply for a Trainer’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Trainer’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f.  21st July, 2011 to 20th August, 2011;

b)   to fine him a sum of Rs.25.000/-

 

ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “CONZTANZA” IN RACE NO.312 – THE WINNING HEART PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 4TH APRIL, 2011 WHICH THE FILLY WON AS COMPARED TO HER SIX PREVIOUS RUNS DURING THE CURRENT MUMBAI MEETING WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 23rd April, 2011 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. Having heard the submissions made by Trainer Mansoor Shah and having viewed the video recording of Race No.312 as also the previous runs of the horse during the current Mumbai Meeting, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer Mansoor Shah for the reversal of form shown by his horse CONZTANZA in Race No.312. In that, the Stewards noted that prior to Race No.312, CONZSTANZA ran six times during the current Mumbai Meeting 2010/11, in all the races available at 15/1 and above and was unplaced, in the last two starts, having finished more than 15 lengths behind the winner and subsequently the horse was ridden by Jockey S.Zervan in Race No.312 and won well fancied. Therefore, the Stewards decided :-

 

a)   that should Trainer Mansoor Shah apply for a Trainer’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Trainer’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f.  21st July, 2011 to 29th July 11;

b)   to fine him a sum of Rs.25.000/-

RE : JOCKEY D.S.RATHORE REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR INDISCIPLINE, IN THAT HE WAS IN BREACH OF THE APPRENTICE JOCKEYS CONTRACT WITH THE CLUB :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 24th April, 2011 interviewed Jockey D.S.Rathore and Trainer Dallas Todywalla, in the above connection.

 

Having heard the submissions made by Jockey D.S.Rathore as also by Trainer Dallas Todywalla and having deliberated the matter, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey D.S.Rathore in the subject case and held him guilty of indiscipline i.e. for breach of Apprentice Jockeys contract with the Club wherein it is mandatory to seek prior permission from the Stewards in case he wished to ride at any other centre which he failed to do and rode in races at D.R.C. without permission. However, keeping in mind that Jockey D.S.Rathore admitted his guilt and apologized for the same, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f.  21st July, 2011 to 20th August, 2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race) for indiscipline.
