RE : POSITIVE FINDINGS OF  “O-DESMETHYLVENLAFAXINE” IN THE URINE SAMPLE OF ch f “MAISHA” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.17 – THE MAHARAJA OF MORVI TROPHY RUN ON SUNDAY, 23RD NOVEMBER, 2014 AND  ch f “ARRIVEDERCI” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.79 – THE N.M.IRANI TROPHY (DIV.II) RUN ON THURSDAY, 25TH DECEMBER, 2014 :

 The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 22nd April, 2015 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry and having heard Trainer P.Shroff, adjourned the hearing.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 1st June, 2015 interviewed Trainer P.Shroff who was accompanied by his Spokesman, Mr.Pervez Andhyarunina. The Stewards considered all documents including the opinions of the Expert Panel and Dr.John Phillip McCaffrey and applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and all submissions made by Tr.P.Shroff/his Spokesman as also his Witness, Dr.S.K.Kulkarni. The Stewards having deliberated the matter, unanimously held the view that Tr.P.Shroff had no mitigating circumstances to offer and held him vicariously responsible for positive finding of “O-Desmethylvenlafaxine” in his two horses viz. MAISHA (Race No.17) and ARRIVEDERCI (Race No.79).

 

Further,        the Stewards also held him guilty of not maintaining his log book properly.

 

Taking an overall view and after having heard him/his spokesman  and having considered all submissions, papers, proceedings, documents in the captioned matter and after having deliberated on the matter at length, the Stewards decided :-

a)  For the positive finding of O-Desmethylvenlafaxine in the urine sample of MAISHA, this being his first offence, to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 60 days and to impose  on him a fine of Rs.15,000/-.

b)  For the positive finding of O-Desmethylvenlafaxine in the urine sample of ARRIVEDERCI, it was considered as his third offence (the positive case of RAMESSES for CAPSAICIN in Race no 77 run on 21st December 2014 being his second offence), and it was decided to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 180 days and to impose on him a fine of Rs.15,000/- i.e. a total of 240 days for MAISHA and ARRIVEDERCI.

 

However, considering his clean record in the last ten years, the Stewards decided that out of the total period of 240 days of suspension of his license, a period of 120 days would be a suspended sentence over the next three years i.e. if he is found guilty of breach of the Medication Rules, within the next three years, commencing from the date on which he would resume training of his horses after the expiry of the suspension of his Trainer’s License, then this suspended sentence of 120 days would immediately come into force and effect and which aforesaid punishment would be in addition to any other punishment which the Stewards may impose on him for any subsequent act of his as regards the aforesaid.

Tr.P.Shroff was told that the 120 days suspension of his Trainer’s license would be w.e.f. 23rd July, 2015 to 25th October, 2015 and w.e.f. 14th November, 2015 to 8th December 2015.

 

Further, the Stewards decided to fine him a sum of Rs.15,000/- for not maintaining his log book properly.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF  ch g “RAMESSES” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.77 – THE GULAMHUSEIN ESSAJI TROPPHY RUN ON SUNDAY, 21ST DECEMBER, 2014 – POSITIVE FOR “CAPSAICIN”::

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 22nd April, 2015 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. The Stewards considered all the submissions made by Trainer P.Shroff. Trainer P.Shroff admitted in the enquiry that RAMESSES is a wind sucker and he used to apply chilli powder paste to the gates to prevent the horse from grabbing the bar. The Stewards after having enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and also having permitted him to make submissions in the subject matter and having heard him, found that he had no mitigating circumstances to offer in the subject case and as such, held him guilty of breach of the Medication Rules.

 

The Stewards noted that other than RAMESSES, there are two more positive cases i.e. MAISHA and ARRIVEDERCI which are still pending. Since RAMESSES is the second case in the sequence of positive cases, the Stewards decided to await the outcome of the enquiry into the positive case of the first case which is pending to be dealt with i.e. MAISHA and based on the outcome of the case, the Stewards would determine whether the case of RAMESSES would be first offence or second offence and based on which the Stewards would give their decision in the matter.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 1st June, 2015 having further deliberated on the matter found that Tr.P.Shroff had no mitigating circumstances to offer in the subject case and as such, held him vicariously responsible for the positive finding of CAPSAICIN in the urine sample of RAMESSES in race No.77.  The Stewards treated this as his second offence, MAISHA (Race No.17) being the first offence.

 

The Stewards noted that Mr.P.Shroff’s Trainer’s license already stands suspended up to 8th December, 2015 in the enquiry into the positive finding of “O-Desmethylvenlafaxine” in the urine of his two horses, MAISHA and ARRIVEDERCI.

 

Therefore, for positive finding of CAPSAICIN in the urine sample RAMESSES in Race No.77, the Stewards decided :

 

a.    to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 30 days i.e. w.e.f. 9th December, 2015 to 7th January, 2016 (both days inclusive);

b.    to fine him a sum of Rs.10,000/-.

RE :  POSITIVE REPORT OF THE RANDOM SAMPLE OF URINE TAKEN FROM 4-YEAR-OLD b g “DAZZLE N DAZE”  ON 22ND JUNE, 2015 - IN CHARGE OF TRAINER MAGANSINGH P.JODHA :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 23rd July,  2015 interviewed Tr.Magansingh P.Jodha and Tr.Adhirajsingh Jodha, (who had signed the sampling documents in respect of the above horse on your behalf), regarding the positive report dated 15th July, 2015   from Racing Laboratory, The Hong Kong Jockey Club, in respect of the random sample of urine taken from his charge 4-year-old b g “DAZZLE N DAZE” on 22ND June, 2015,  which  read  as under :-

 

 “THE ANALYSIS HAS SHOWN THE PRESENCE OF ”TESTOSTERONE”. THE CONCENTRATION OF FREE AND CONJUGATED TESTOSTERONE WAS DETERMINED TO BE 0.040 MICROGRAM PER MILLILITRE. THIS EXCEEDS THE INTERNATIONAL THRESHOLD FOR TESTOSTERONE IN GELDINGS, WHICH IS 0.020 MICROGRAM FREE AND CONJUGATED TESTOSTERONE PER MILLILITRE IN URINE. THE MEASUREMENT UNCERTAINTY AT 0.020 MICROGRAM PER MILLILITRE WAS ESTIMATED TO BE 0.0044 MICROGRAM PER MILLILITRE (AT 99% 1 –TAILED CONFIDENCE LEVEL)”  

 

At this meeting, Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha was handed over a copy of the above report and was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, within five days of opening the envelope that identifies the sample, i.e. latest by 28th July, 2015, whether he wished to send the split sample to LGC, New Market, UK, or L C H, France, for Confirmatory analysis and also the name of the witnessing analyst, if any, failing which the Stewards shall treat the report of the primary laboratory as conclusive.

 

Tr.Magansingh P.Jodha was asked to conduct his own enquiries and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.V.O. of the Club, as and when he would be required to.
