RACE NO.2 (2) – THE ELEGANCE PLATE - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

It was observed that TOOFAAN (App. S. Siddharth) was brought to the paddock sporting the wrong saddle. Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi was immediately asked to re-saddle his charge after duly weighing-out. The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey S. G. Prasad, TALKING POINT was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified him fit to race.

 

At the start, MAJOR SAAB (Amyn Merchant, dr.8) jumped inwards at the same time TALKING POINT (S. G. Prasad, dr.6) jumped outwards thereby tightening PURPLE MARTINI (Nirmal Jodha, dr.7) thereafter LEO THE LION (A. Prakash, dr.9) leaned inwards thereby bumping MAJOR SAAB who had to be steadied. Jockey A. Prakash has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 1000 metres, GOLDEN RULE (Vishal N. Bunde) leaned in onto WE STILL BELIEVE (App. Haridas Gore) who had to be steadied due to which SAMSON (C. Umesh) racing on its haunches brushed the rails and had to be checked. Jockey Vishal N. Bunde has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 700 metres, BOHEMIAN RHAPSODY (App. Aditya Waydande) leaned inwards onto WE STILL BELIEVE (App. Haridas Gore) who in turn  shifted in thereby tightening and  inconveniencing  SAMSON (C. Umesh) racing along the rails. After questioning and not being satisfied, App Aditya Waydande has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, TALKING POINT was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, TOOFAAN was trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that LEO THE LION had a right eye injury (sore) with ulcer.

RACE NO.9 (9) – THE COLUMBIA PLATE: - NULL AND VOID

 

 After the Race, SCARAMOUCHE (App. Aditya Waydande) was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and  was found to have broken a tooth in its lower jaw and was observed have an injury on the left nostril.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ARIES had a right eye injury (sore).

 

Immediately after the race, the Starters reported that there was a reasonable delay between the flag being lowered and the gates having opened due to which horses had rushed the front gates as a result they were unbalanced thus impacting the start.

 

In view of this incident the Starters had immediately raised the red flag and sounded the siren thereby denoting it a false start.

 

In view of the above report a Stewards enquiry was initiated into the incident, the Stewards having viewed the video of the start from all angles and considering the evidence given by the Jockeys, Starters and the Stipendiary Stewards, unanimously declared the race “Null And Void”

 

Further, the Stewards directed the Stipendiary Stewards to hold a detailed enquiry into the start and place their findings before them.
