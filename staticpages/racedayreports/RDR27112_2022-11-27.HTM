RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METERS ( MILE CHUTE ) LEADING TO 2 METERS AWAY AT 1200 METERS, 3 METERS AWAY AT 1000 METERS AND THEREAFTER 4 METERS AWAY UP TO THE WINNING POST.

 

FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WERE PLACED FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1200 METRES LEADING TO 3 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1(8) – THE CAPTAIN G. HALL TROPHY  (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  12

 

At the start, HOUSE OF LORDS (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.3) jumped outwards and went across ADONIS (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.4) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

At the start, BETTER HALF (Akshay Gaikwad, dr.7) jumped inwards and made contact with DRAGONLORD (App. Kirtish Bhagat, dr.6).

 

Soon after the start, LADY DI (R. Ajinkya) shifted in thereby inconveniencing DRAGONLORD (App. Kirtish Bhagat). Jockey R. Ajinkya has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 400 meters, GOLDIVA (P. Trevor) shifted out under urging and carried BETTER HALF (Akshay Gaikwad) outwards. Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe (ADONIS) that his mount reared up as the start was effected.

 

After the race, App. Kirtish Bhagat (DRAGONLORD) reported that his mount hung-in the straight. Tr. P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FLASHING FAMOUS (S G. Prasad) was observed to have lost its footings and stumbled.

 

After the race, MOON BELLE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, DRAGONLORD was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (9) THE D. W. REID PLATE  (ABOUT) 1000 METRES. – RUNNERS -  13

 

At the request of Trainer S. K. Sunderji, ALMAS was examined in the stable area at 6.15 p.m. on 26/11/2022 and was found to be lame by right hind, the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

PHANTA (S. J. Sunil, dr. 10) took a fly jump thereby losing about 3 lengths at the start.

 

Soon after the start, DILBAR (App. A. S. Peter, dr.8) shifted in at the same time DON’T BE SHY (S. G. Prasad, dr.5) leaned outwards thereby tightening and inconveniencing IMPERMANANCE (App. P. Vinod, dr.6) and ARBITRAGE (K. Nazil, dr.7)  drawn between them.

 

Passing 900 meters, DILBAR (App. A. S. Peter) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening and inconveniencing HELA (Bhawani Singh) causing the latter to be checked. After questioning App. A. S. Peter and not being satisfied with the explanation, he has been fine Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 700 metres, HABIBI (Neeraj Rawal) leaned inwards thereby inconveniencing DON’T BE SHY (S. G. Prasad) who had to be steadied. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

App. A. S. Peter (DILBAR) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2022/23, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey S. J. Sunil (PHANTA) reported that his mount leaned-in in the stall and was not allowing him to settle. Tr. M. Narredu has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HELA was trotted and found to be sound and RISING BRAVE had gum injury.

 

RACE NO.3 (10) THE G. A. TROPHY  (ABOUT) 1400 METRES. RUNNERS -  14

 

At the start, MIDSUMMER STAR (R. Ajinkya, dr.7) jumped inwards and made contact with JACK BAUER (C. Umesh, dr.6).

Passing 1200 meters, TYRONE BLACK (J. Chinoy) leaned inwards onto LORD FENICIA (Suraj Narredu) who in turn shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing MAJORELLA BLUE (S. Zervan) causing the latter to be checked. Jockey J. Chinoy he has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 500 meters, JUBILANT JOURNEY (Vishal N. Bunde) racing on the haunches of LORD FENICIA (Suraj Narredu) was observed to have clipped the former and stumbled.

 

Passing the 300 metres, OUTLANDER (P. S. Chouhan) shifted out under urgings thereby tightening and forcing MAJORELLA BLUE (S. Zervan) onto FLASH FORCE (R. Shelar). Jockey P. S. Chouhan has been cautioned.

 

After the race, TYRONE BLACK and SWEET EMOTION were trotted and found to be sound.

RACE NO.6  (13)  THE BEJAN BHARUCHA PLATE (ABOUT) 1600 METRES. RUNNERS -  15

RE: RACE NO.134: THE FOURTH ESTATE CUP – RUN ON 6TH NOVEMBER 2022.

 

On a report from the Stipendiary Stewards that in race no.134 The Fourth Estate Cup, run on 6th November 2022, Jockey K. Nazil (ARABIAN PHOENIX, dr.3) had shifted out soon after the start thereby tightening MULTISTARRER (S. Saqlain, dr.4) on his outside causing the latter to clip and stumble which resulted in the rider being dislodged. On being questioned by the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 27th November 2022, Jockey K. Nazil was apologetic for the incident and attributed the interference to the bend that came immediately after the start also explained that he had carried the whip in his left hand and had done his best to avoid the accident.

 

Race no.134 was viewed and it was pointed out to Jockey K. Nazil that he had made no effort to correct his mount and/or keep a straight course, which resulted in the accident due to which the rider had fallen.

 

Having heard the submissions and after watching the video of the race in question the Stewards of the Club held Jockey K. Nazil guilty of dangerous riding for the above incident.

 

In view of the above, Jockey K. Nazil was informed that his Jockeys licence has been suspended for 4 race days i.e. on 18th & 25th December 2022, 6th & 15th January 2023, for dangerous riding.
