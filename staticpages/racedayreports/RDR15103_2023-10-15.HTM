ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.
PUNE MEETING 2023, TENTH DAY, SUNDAY, 15TH, OCTOBER, 2023.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 4.3 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POSITION. RAILS WERE PLACED 5 METRES WIDE IN THE CHUTE.

 

RACE NO.1(113) – THE VINAYAK TROPHY: (ABOUT) 2400 METRES – RUNNERS -  6

 

NO REPORT

 

RACE NO.2(114) – THE J. M. SHAH & C. M. SHAH GOLD CUP (THE PUNE POLICE COMMISSIONER'S TROPHY - DIVISION II): (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  9

 

Soon after the start, FIERY RED (M. S. Deora) was inclined outwards and in the process bumped and forced SOVEREIGN GREY (Akshay Kumar) out on more than one occasion. After the race, Jockey M.S Deora reported that his mount was inclined outwards throughout the race. After the race, the filly was trotted and found to be sound. Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, NEW DIMENSION was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SOVEREIGN GREY, FIERY RED, RISTRETTO & SKY HAWK were trotted and found to be sound. Further, SOVEREIGN GREY was found to have wolf tooth on his left side. As such, Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.3(115) – THE PUNE POLICE COMMISSIONER'S TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  9

 

Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been fined Rs.1,000/- for accepting with his charge TREASURE GOLD in  this race, whilst the gelding was under Starter's ban. The gelding was struck out at the acceptance stage.

 

Soon after the start, DREAM SELLER (R. Ajinkya) veered outwards despite the riders efforts thereby carrying BELLAGIO (A. S. Peter) outwards who had to be checked. In view of the same and a similar behavior of the gelding in his last start, Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ATHENIAN was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(116) – THE NOSHIR & DOLLY DHUNJIBHOY SPRINT MILLION  (GRADE 3): (ABOUT) 1200 METRES –RUNNERS- 5

 

NO REPORT

 

RACE NO.5(117) – MAYOR BABURAO SANAS MEMORIAL PUNE DERBY (GRADE 1) powered by MR. S. R. SANAS: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS -  8

 

Jockey Yash Narredu (ENABLER) was observed to have stood up and raised his hand as a victory sign before passing the winning post. This being his 1st offence during the current Pune Meeting 2023, he has been fined a sum of Rs.5,000/-.

 

After the race, TRANSCEND was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore, the filly has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, KINGS LOVE was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

After the race, JAMARI was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(118) – THE JIMMY UMRIGAR TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 11

 

There was a dual declaration in respect of Jockey T. S. Jodha by Trainer Behram Cama on RICOCHET and UZI.  After questioning all concerned, Trainer Behram Cama was permitted to declare Jockey H. Zeeshan on his charge UZI.  In this connection, Trainer Behram Cama has been fined Rs.1,000/- for his lapse.

 

BRAHMOS (Dashrath Singh, dr.10) jumped out awkwardly losing about a length at the start.

 

After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (DECACORN) reported that his mount was hanging-in in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ZUCCARO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(119) – THE F. A. & K. A. IRANI TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

Soon after the start FORTUNATE SON (N. S. Parmar, dr.5) stumbled.

 

Approaching the 500 metres FLYING SCOTSMAN (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing WINDERMERE (A. S. Peter) who had to be checked. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

After the race, WINDERMERE was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.
