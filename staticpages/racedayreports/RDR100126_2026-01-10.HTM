After the race, Jockey David Allan (FORTUNATE SON) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight despite his efforts, and the same was noted.

 

Jockey Akshay Kumar (BREAK POINT) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2025/26, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

RACE NO.3(61) THE PLEASURE HUNT TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES  - RUNNERS -  8

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey R. Ajinkya rider of FONTANA placed Second by the Judge against App. Ramswarup rider of ESPERANZA placed First by the Judge stating that “in the last 50 metres  the winner shifted out, because of which I had to check my horse, had to change my course, this cost me a certain race”.

 

After considering the submissions made by  Jockey R. Ajinkya and Trainer Shazaan Shah, Master of App. Ramswarup, in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the Video Recording of the Race from multiple angles and acting on their own observations of the Race, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing as under:

 

FONTANA                      FIRST                         SOCIAL BUTTERFLY      THIRD

ESPERANZA                  SECOND                   BIG BAY                            FOURTH

 

In view of above, the Stewards of the Club held App. Ramswarup guilty of Careless riding on his mount ESPERANZA.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey Yash Narredu rider of BIG BAY placed 4th by the judge against Jockey A. Sandesh rider of SOCIAL BUTTERFLY placed 3rd by the judge stating that “ in the last 200 metres my horse was in full momentum catching up with the leader, when SOCIAL BUTTERFLY started drifting out and in the last 100 metres completely took my ground where I had to completely stop riding, this not only cost me placing but a certain Race”.

 

After considering the submissions made by both the Jockeys, in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the Video Recording of the Race from multiple angles and acting on their own observations of the Race decided to overrule the objection, placings remain unchanged as declared by the Judge.

RE: POSITIVE REPORT IN THE POST RACE URINE SAMPLE OF b  f “NEBULA” (WINNER)  IN RACE NO. 25 THE PRINCE  KARIM AGA KHAN IV MEMORIAL TROPHY ON 7TH DECEMBER 2025:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday,10th January 2026 interviewed Trainer Behram Cama, in connection with the Positive Report dated  7th January 2026 issued by Quantilab, Mauritius, pertaining to the post-race urine sample of his charge b f NEBULA (Winner) in Race No. 25 The Prince Karim Aga Khan IV Memorial Trophy  on 7th December 2025, which states as follows:-

 

“The analysis of the above sample has shown the presence of Lamotrigine”

 

At the said meeting, Trainer Behram Cama was provided with a copy of the aforementioned report as per the Rules. He was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, whether he wishes to send the "B" sample for Confirmatory Analysis within 48 hours of opening the envelope identifying the sample, i.e., no later than 12th January 2026, and also indicate his choice of laboratory for the Confirmatory Analysis of the split sample of his charge NEBULA. The available laboratories for such analysis are: The Hong Kong Jockey Club Laboratory, Hong Kong; LGC, Newmarket U.K.; Laboratoire Des Courses Hippiques (L.C.H.), France; Laboratory of Racing Chemistry (LRC), Japan. Failure to comply within the stipulated period shall result in the Stewards treating the report of the Primary Laboratory as final and conclusive.

 

Additionally, he will be required to ensure the presence of the Witnessing Analyst, if he so desires, at the laboratory conducting the Confirmatory Analysis for the urine within 30 days of the nomination. In the event that he is unable to arrange for the presence of the Witnessing Analyst within the said period, notwithstanding the nomination, the Confirmatory Analysis shall proceed in the absence of the Witnessing Analyst.

 

Further, in accordance with the Medication Rules presently in force, he is hereby informed that 50% of his runners shall be subjected to follow-up samples.
