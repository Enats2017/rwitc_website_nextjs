RACE NO.1 (141) – THE PROLETARIAN PLATE: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 14

 

At the request of Trainer Narendra Lagad, LIGHTNING BLAZE was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. at 09:00 a.m. on 3rd November 2024, at the stables and was found to be sore by right fore with injury on right fore knee, as such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was permitted to be withdrawn on Veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BETWEEN FRIENDS (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.12) jumped inwards and made contact with ADONIS (Santosh G., dr.11). Jockey Vishal N. Bunde has been cautioned.

 

At the start, Jockey S. A. Amit (RECIPROCITY) shifted in and cut across the field before passing the red marker. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey S. A. Amit has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, RECIPROCITY (S. A. Amit) shifted out thereby inconveniencing OPERATION FINALE (H. M. Akshay) who had to be steadied. On questioning, Jockey S. A. Amit stated that his mount was hanging out despite his efforts, his statement was kept on record.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Vinod (CASCADE) reported that his mount was hanging out despite his efforts from the 600 metres. Trainer Nosher Cama has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, App. Bharat Singh (SHE’S A TEASER) reported that his mount was hanging out throughout the race despite his efforts. Trainer S. Waheed has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, TALKING POINT was observed to have an injury on right fore fetlock and right hind bulb of heel. RECIPROCITY was observed to have lost its right hind shoe.

 

RACE NO.2 (142) – THE B. PRAKASH TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

ATTAINED (Vishal N Bunde, dr.10) planted in the stall, taking no practical part in the race. Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy has been asked to give her charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

RICOCHET (T. S. Jodha dr.1) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Behram Cama has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, CACHE (Vivek G., dr.2) stumbled thereafter made contact with RICOCHET (T. S. Jodha, dr.1) and leaned outwards thereby inconveniencing ART COLLECTOR (K. Nazil, dr.3) who had to be steadied.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey P. Trevor, the rider of SINGER SARGENT (Placed 2nd) by the Judge against Antony Raj S., the rider of OPUS DEI (Placed 1st) by the Judge. The reason stated was, coming into the straight SINGER SARGENT was moving very comfortably and between the 300 & 250 metres, OPUS DEI started shifting out and took SINGER SARGENT out all through and specially in the last 100/150 metres, OPUS DEI nearly clipped him as he cut across him badly. Due to which P. Trevor had to break his strides completely and stop riding, which cost him a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race from various angles and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing as under:-

 

SINGER SARGENT              -           FIRST;            TREASURE GOLD    -           THIRD

OPUS DEI                              -           SECOND;       BELIEVE                    -           FOURTH

 

In view of above, Jockey Antony Raj S. was reported to the Stewards of the Club for careless riding after the last race. The Stewards of the Club at their meeting, held Jockey Antony Raj S. guilty of careless riding and keeping in mind his past record, decided to suspend his Jockeys licence for two race days i.e. on the first two race days of the Mumbai Meeting 2024/25 (permitted to ride work/mock race), dates of which will be intimated in due course.

 

After the race, GHIRARDELLI was observed to have lost its right fore shoe.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that CACHE had a left eye injury (sore) with ulcer. ART COLLECTOR had both eye injuries (sore) with ulcer.

RE: APP. S. SABA REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR PASSING RACING INFORMATION TO OTHERS :

 

In this connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 2nd November 2024, having considered all papers, proceedings before them and after deliberation, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. Jockey S. Saba and held him guilty of misconduct under Rule-101(iii) of the Rules of Racing, wherein he had exchanged information and facilitated tips on the whatsapp message to an Ex-Jockey Mr. Hanumant Singh and had also confirmed the message received from Mr. Hanumant Singh i.e. “you call up Mr. Srikanth n tell him this n ask him play for you  also” referring to his earlier message wherein he had tipped “PRINCE IGOR” in the first race. On being asked by the Stewards in this regard, he had no plausible explanation to offer and he made a feeble attempt to mislead the Stewards in this regard.

 

Therefore, the Stewards of the Club taking an overall view and keeping in mind all the circumstances of the enquiry, have decided to suspend his App. Jockey’s Licence  w.e.f. 17th November 2024 upto the end of the Mumbai Meeting 2024/25.

 

Please note that during the period of his punishment :-

 

a)         He is not permitted to ride work/mock race;

b)         He is not permitted to attend racing, on course or off course.

c)         All other disabilities applicable to a person suspended/disqualified shall apply to him.
