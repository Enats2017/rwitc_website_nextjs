ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

MUMBAI MEETING 2021/22, EIGHTH DAY, SUNDAY, 6TH FEBRUARY, 2022.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 3.9 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT.

 

On a request from Trainer Vishal Gaikwad, permission was granted to Trainer Vinesh to saddle his charge VICTORIOUS SERMON in the 4th race, REDIFINED in the 8th race at his own (Trainer Vishal Gaikwad’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO.1(80) – THE PLAY.RWITC.COM MILLION: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  7

 

After the race, RED CARNATION was observed to have broken a blood vessel second time and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.2(81) – THE BREEDERS' PRODUCE STAKES (GRADE 3): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  6

 

At the start, LAST WISH (Antony Raj S., dr.2) jumped outwards onto O HANSINI (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.3).

 

Passing 150 metres, SUPERNATURAL (P. S. Chouhan) was observed to have  drifted out. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.3(82) – THE SIR HOMI MEHTA MILLION: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  6

 

After the race, CHAT was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(83) – THE RUSI PATEL TROPHY (GRADE 3): (About) 1600 Metres –Runners- 8

 

Passing the 300 metres, VICTORIOUS SERMON (S. Zervan) leaned inwards thereby tightening and inconveniencing PETRONIA (P. Trevor). Jockey S. Zervan has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 100 metres, FLYING VISIT (Antony Raj S.) shifted in onto VICTORIOUS SERMON (S. Zervan). After questioning Jockey Antony Raj S. has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

RACE NO.5(84) – THE ZAVARAY S. POONAWALLA ECLIPSE STAKES OF INDIA (GRADE 2): (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  6

 

After the race, ALLURING SILVER was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(85) – THE B. K. RATTONSEY MEMORIAL MAIDEN MULTI-MILLION: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 6

 

No Report

 

RACE NO.7(86) – THE KINGFISHER ULTRA INDIAN DERBY (GRADE 1): (About) 2400 Metres – Runners -  11

 

MIRACLE was examined at 4.15 p.m. on 7/2/22 in the Equine Hospital was found to be sore by right fore. The filly has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.8(87) – THE GATEWAY OF INDIA MILLION: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  8

 

Approaching the 200 metres, Jockey N. S. Parmar (PURE) was observed to have lost his reins on examination it was found that the reins had got unbuckled. Jockey N. S. Parmar has been asked to be more careful with his riding gear in the future.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Zervan reported that his mount REDIFINED lugged-out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Vishal Gaikwad had been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, REDIFINED was trotted and found to be sound.
