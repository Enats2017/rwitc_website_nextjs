RACE NO 1 (42) - THE NELSON RIVER PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres.  Runners -  10

 

PURPLE MOON (App.Akshay) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, PURPLE MOON (App.D.Akshay) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside and went on to LAKE ERIE (R.Shelar) who in turn went on to ASHWA GAURAV (Suraj Narredu), who itself had shifted in at that stage.

 

RED TABASCO (Ladjadj Stephane) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, ASHWA GAURAV was found to be sound. SECRET MAGIC had a bruise at the right nostril.

 

RACE NO 2 (43) - THE GAMBLE FOR LOVE TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres.  Runners - 5

 

As the start was given, DESERT RULE (A.Sandesh) reared up, dislodging its rider and jumped out riderless. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (SCHNELL) reported that his mount lugged in throughout the race. Trainer Amjad Khan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

An Objection was lodged by Jockey S Zervan, rider of ELIZABETH, placed second by the Judge, against Jockey Y.S.Srinath, rider of BEAUTIFUL STRANGER, placed first. The reason being that coming into the straight BEAUTIFUL STRANGER lugged in front of him and pressed his mount as soon as he passed him, due to which he could not push his mount immediately and had to wait for a stride or two and pull his mount out to make his run.

 

The Stewards having heard the evidence of the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their observation of the incident, decided to overrule the Objection.

 

After the race, SCHNELL & DESERT RULE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 3 (44) - THE MAYOR’S TROPHY: (About) 1800 Metres. Runners - 8

 

An Objection was lodged by Jockey C .Alford, rider of KATHELYNN, placed second by the Judge, against Jockey C.Rajendra, rider of MARAUDER, placed first. The reason being, at about 200 metres, MARAUDER came on to him due to which he had to stop riding and check his mount and then pulled out his mount and his horse came back with a run and he lost a certain race.

 

The Stewards having heard the evidence of the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their observation of the incident, decided to overrule the Objection.

 

In the above connection, Jockey C.Rajendra was asked to meet the Stewards for careless riding. Consideration of the matter was deferred to next week.

 

Jockey C.Rajendra (MARAUDER) has been fined Rs.1000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

 

Jockey C Rajendra (MARAUDER) has been cautioned for easing up his mount before passing the Post.

 

After the race, SAM BAHADUR was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The colt has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SWAGGER was found to be sound, having a bruise at the left hind pastern.

 

RACE NO 4 (45) – THE KHUSHRO K.JALNAWALA TURF CLUB TROPHY (GR.3): (About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 10

 

Near 200 metres, DEAR HENRY (App.P.Trevor) shifted in on to MIGHTY CRUSADER (Shrikant Kamble) who in turn shifted in and tightened STAR TRAINER (J.Chinoy), who was checked and caused interference to ONASSIS (Kishore Kadam).  Since Jockey Shrikant Kamble had made no effort to correct his mount, he has been fined Rs.2000/- for failing to exercise proper control over his mount and allowing his mount to shift inwards. Further, App.P.Trevor has also been fined Rs.2000/- for allowing his mount to shift in under the use of the whip which contributed to the inward movement of MIGHTY CRUSADER.

 

RACE NO 5 (46) – THEJALNAWALA TAYLOR & SHROFF ORDER OF THE KNIGHTS TROPHY:   (About) 1400 Metres.   Runners – 13

 

At the request of Trainer Altaf Hussain, TZIGANE was examined on 1120 hours on 06/08/2011 and found to be sore by the left fore leg. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and hence withdrawn from the race on veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

ROYAL PORTHCAWL (D.K.Ashish) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

HOUDINI (J.Chinoy) lugged out in the straight. Trainer Faisal A.Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, FIERY FORTUNE was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HOUDINI was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 6 (47) – THE HAVOVI KHUSRO JALNAWALA MEMORIAL JUVENILE MILLION: About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 21

 

Soon after the start, SUPER HERO (Mansoor Khan) shifted in and made contact with BRAVE VISION (App.P.Trevor) twice, who in turn hampered STORMING HEART. Jockey Mansoor Khan has been fined Rs.3000/-.

 

Passing 600 metres, HIGHWAY EXPRESS (C.Alford) commenced to drop back and was also tightened between ASHWA PRAJOT (App.A.Sandesh) and  BRAVE  VISION (App.P.Trevor) and in doing so, caused a chain reaction, in which HIGHWAY EXPRESS went on to VELOCITA (Dashrath Singh) who went on to GARARO (S.Sunil) who went on to ASHWA BABHRI (Neeraj Rawal).

Near 300 metres, MOUNTAIN PRINCE (Suraj Narredu) shifted out to progress in the race and in doing so, caused HIGHWAY EXPRESS (C.Alford) to shift out and jostle with FAHRENHEIT (Sunny Chinoy) who was tightened on to GARARO (S.Sunil). Jockey Suraj Narredu fined Rs.3000/- for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

Jockey Y.S.Srinath (SUGAR LOAF) has been cautioned for shifting ground inwards approaching 200 metres and carrying STUNTACULAR (Akash Agarwal) inwards.

 

Jockey D.K.Ashish (KING JULIEN) reported the loss of whip near 400 metres.

 

After the race, SUPER HERO was found to be sound, having a wolf tooth on the left side. Trainer Faisal A.Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

After the race, ASHWA PRABIR was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind coronet. FAHRENHEIT, KING JULIEN, STORMING HEART were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 7 (48) – THE AURORA AUREALIS PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres.   Runners – 7

 

After the race, SCENIC SILVER was found to be sound, having a cut injury at the right hind fetlock and tendon.

 

RACE NO 8 (49) - THE NELSON RIVER PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres.   Runners – 11

 

ROSE BOWL (Hanumant Singh) was restless after being stalled. Trainer M.Narredu has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice before next accepting to race.

 

After being stalled, SAN RAFAEL (C.Rajendra) swayed its head to the right and the Jockey asked the Handler to correct it. As the Handler was trying to correct the horse’s head, the start was effected and the Handler was unable to remove the Blindfold in the first attempt. The Blindfold was removed on the second attempt, resulting in the horse losing about 4 lengths at the start.

 

Immediately after the race, Mr.Suresh G.Advani, part owner of SAN RAFAEL, gave a written complaint that the Blindfold on his horse was removed late as a result his horse had lost considerable ground at the start.

 

A Stewards Enquiry was held into the start of the race. After hearing the Stipendiary Stewards and the Starter and viewing the video replay of the start of the race and relying on their own observation, the Stewards held the view that SAN RAFAEL was denied a fair start in this race and therefore, decided to declare SAN RAFAEL a Non Starter and as per the relevant Rules, all bets on this horse were ordered to be refunded.

 

The Stewards after the last race, further interviewed the Starter, the Handler who was deputed to hold the horse, SAN RAFAEL, as well as Jockey C.Rajendra and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of the start of the race, the explanation tendered by them was noted.

 

After the race, SPRAUNCY was found to be sound.
