RACE NO 1. (310) THE  WINNING HEART PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 9

 

At the start, THE SAINT (Ishwar Pardeshi) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside.

 

Jockey Harish (EVANGILIST) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight and he found it difficult to ride out his mount. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly. 

 

On questioning, Jockey P.S.Chouhan (OCEAN TREASURE) stated that he was instructed to be in 1st or 2nd position but his mount was uninterested in the race and was not responding to his urgings. The Club’s Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the the gelding and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running of this race and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

ALCHEMIST stopped galloping near 800 metres and did not finish the race. The gelding was examined by the Club’s V.O. and found to be sore from left fore leg, having suffered incurable injury at left fore fetlock and complete rupture of suspensory ligament. The horse was euthanized after consulting the Trainer who had no objection. 

 

After the race, THE SAINT was observed to have broken a blood vessel. Being a second time bleeder, the gelding is not to race for the stipulated period of 8 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, EVANGILIST was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 2. (311) THE W.BUCKLEY PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres.  Runners – 6

 

MEGA BUCKS (S.Sunil) planted and lost over a distance at the start. Since the gelding was slowly out in its previous run also, Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

ARZAAN (N.S.Parmar) and URUJ (T.S.Jodha) lost about 3 lengths each at the start.

 

After the race, MEGA BUCKS was found to be sound.

 

 

RACE NO 3. (312) THE  WINNING HEART PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 9

 

SHARP RUNNER (C.S.Jodha) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

The Nose Band on CONZTANZA (S.Zervan) flapped during the race,  as such, Trainer Mansoor Shah has been advised to properly fit the equipment that he uses on his charge.

 

Trainer Mansoor Shah was questioned regarding the comparative performance of CONZTANZA in this race as compared to her previous runs during the Mumbai Meeting. The Trainer explained that this was the horse’s maiden win. In the previous race over a mile, the horse had run a forward race. He has been trying her in all distances. Today, the set was poor and the horse had won by a shortest of margin. He added that in his opinion there was only one horse to beat i.e. STAR VISION. Further enquiry would be held into the matter and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, CONSTANZA was found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “TOMORROWS DREAMS” IN RACE NO.256 – THE RONALDO PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 13/03/2011 WHICH HE WON AS COMPARED TO ITS EARLIER RUN IN RACE NO.175 RUN ON 5TH FEBRUARY, 2011 WHERE HE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer H.J.Antia in the above connection. Trainer Antia explained that in Race No.256 it was the fifth run of the horse. In the first run i.e. Race No.80 the horse ran unplaced. However, in the 2nd run i.e. Race No.91 over 1400 metres, the horse finished 3rd and was just ¾ lengths from the winner. Thereafter the horse ran in Race No.151 over 1600 metres and finished 1 ½ lengths from the winner despite not so good ride by the Jockey. Subsequently, the horse ran in Race No.175 on the Derby Weekend over 1600 metres and the company was tough and the horse finished 10th. He said that in the race in question i.e. Race No.256 which was over 1200 metres, the field was poor as compared to last time and he had selected this race for that reason even though he was aware that he would be shortening the trip by 400 metres. He said that there were two common horses BEMISAAL & EXCELLTASTIC who he beat in both the races and in his opinion the horse has not improved all that much. He further added that the work pattern of the horse was same.

 

The explanation tendered by Trainer H.J.Antia was noted.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING OF HORSES UNDER THE CHARGE OF TRAINER H.J.ANTIA DURING THE CURRENT MUMBAI MEETING 2010/11 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Monday, 4th April, 2011 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. Trainer H.J.Antia was questioned regarding the running pattern of his horses i.e. declaring ordinary Jockeys on his horses, using bandages and the horses being available at longer odds  and running nowhere and the same horses winning next time out, well fancied with a better rider and without bandages. Trainer H.J.Antia explained that most of his horses have soundness problem and he has to gradually nurse them along into peak condition and normally good Jockeys do not always accept such rides and therefore he puts up his stable riders and the moment the horse peaks in condition and shows some spark in a race they accept the ride and since he does not hide anything and it is the racing public who open his horses as favourites and he obliges them by winning as public favourite and many of his favourites have obliged irrespective of the bandages. As regarding the use of bandages, he explained that he uses ordinary crepe bandages with sponge as a support to the horse and he removes them when he feels that the horse has come right and in his opinion the bandages do not  have any effect on the performance of the horse. He cited a few cases where his horses had performed better with bandages than what they had performed subsequently without bandages.

 

The Stewards having considered all papers and proceedings before them and having heard the explanation given by Trainer H.J.Antia  and having deliberated the matter at length, held the view that there was no clinching evidence to charge Trainer H.J.Antia of any malpractice, as such they decided to give him the benefit of the doubt in the present case. However, Trainer H.J.Antia was warned to be more careful with regard to the running pattern of his horses in the future and not to use the race track as a training facility.
