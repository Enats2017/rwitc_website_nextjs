RACE NO.4(56) – THE  PUNE SUB-AREA TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 9 Runners

 

Soon after the start, WINDSOR QUEEN (App.Yash Narredu, dr.5) shifted-in on to SAMAY BALWAN (B.Nikhil, dr.4), who shifted-in on to NATIONAL PRIDE (App.D.S.Daman, dr.3), who in turn hampered the running of ARUSHA (D.K.Ashish, dr.2). Further, WINDSOR QUEEN (App.Yash Narredu) raced keen during the race and near 700 metres the mare shifted out on a few occasions whereby SOMBRERO (S.Mosin) who was following, clipped the hind heels of  WINDSOR QUEEN, stumbled and dropped out considerably out of contention and in doing so, NATIONAL PRIDE (App.D. S. Daman) who was following SOMBRERO, received interference. The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards after the last race interviewed App. Yash Narredu who was accompanied by his Master, Trainer M.Narredu. The Stewards also heard Trainer Faisal A.Abbas and having viewed the video recording of the race, were not entirely satisfied with the explanation tendered by App.Yash Narredu. The Stewards noted that WINDSOR QUEEN was very keen and App.Yash Narredu in trying to hold his position, the horse kept an erratic course and

 

negotiating the final bend shifted out due to which SOMBRERO (S.Mosin) who was racing behind on his outside, clipped WINDSOR QUEEN and stumbled almost dislodging the rider. However, keeping in mind that App.Yash Narredu was specifically instructed not to hit the front and the horse was very keen, the Stewards decided to give him the benefit of a grave doubt for careless riding and warned him to be more careful in the future.

 

Jockey Sandeep Jadhav (GLORIA) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount and he has been severely cautioned for the future.

 

Trainer H.J.Antia has been fined Rs.1,000/- for taking away his charge STARRY OCEAN (Placed Fourth) from the paddock, before the ALL CLEAR was given.

 

After the race, WINDSOR QUEEN was found to be sound, but having a cut injury at the left hock.  ARABIAN LORD, SOMBRERO and ARUSHA were found to be sound.

RE:: CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS REPORT IN RESPECT OF gr f  “MAHAAKAUSHAL” (NOW NAMED “GREY TYPHOON”) WHO WAS COMPULSORILY SAMPLED ON 9TH JULY, 2014 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED FROM TRAINER ARTI DOCTOR, R.C.T.C. TO TRAINER SUBHAG SINGH :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 15th August, 2014 interviewed Trainer Subhag Singh regarding the Confirmatory Analysis Report dated 14th 2014 from LCH, France, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge MAHAAKAUSHAL on 9th July, 2014 since the filly had arrived to his charge  from Trainer Arti Doctor, R.C.T.C.,  which read as under :-

Further,  as per the Clause of the Medication Rules, MAHAAKAUSHAL has been banned from Racing for a period of six months i.e. up to and inclusive of 15th February, 2015.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RIDING OF JOCKEY A.SANDESH ON “FLASHING CHIEFTAIN” IN RACE NO.31 – THE AUGUST HANDICAP RUN ON SUNDAY, 3RD AUGUST, 2014 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Friday, 15th August, 2014 interviewed Trainer Karthik G. and Jockey A.Sandesh in the above connection and having heard them and viewed the video recording of Race No.31, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey A.Sandesh and held him guilty of incompetent riding on his mount FLASHING CHIEFTAIN. The Stewards noted that in the final 200 metres when he pulled the horse to make his move, he was nearly two lengths behind the leader, WESTPHALIA, and he had  the chance to use the whip at that stage, which he failed to do and this may have cost him the race since he lost the race by a Head. The Trainer also confirmed that he did not tell Jockey A.Sandesh not to use the whip and that a couple of cracks could have made a difference to the result of the race. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 30th August, 2014 for incompetent riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).
