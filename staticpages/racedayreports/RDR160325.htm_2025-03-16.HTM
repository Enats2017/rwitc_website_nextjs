RACE NO.1 (143) – THE STAR SHINE TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

MANOR HOUSE (P. Trevor dr.2) charged the front gate while all the horses were loaded the gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified him fit to race. In view of the behavior of the gelding, Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

ROCKET (T. S. Jodha, dr.8 ) lost about a length at the start.

 

At the start, CINDERELLA’S DREAM (App. Bharat Singh, dr.4) jumped inwards and made contact with FLEETWOOD (P. Vinod, dr.3). App. Bharat Singh has been cautioned. 

 

Soon after the start, SHIRSA (S. G. Prasad, dr.7) shifted in and made contact with GALLOPING GLORY (App. Sonu Chouhan, dr.6). Jockey S. G. Prasad has been cautioned.

 

FLEETWOOD (P. Vinod) pulled up lame before passing the winning post. After the race, the filly was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. Subsequently, the filly was sent to the Equine Hospital for further diagnosis and after an x-ray it was found that the filly had multiple fractures of carpal bones in the right fore knee. As such, with the consent of Trainer Shazaan Shah the filly was euthanized on humanitarian grounds.

 

ROCKET (T. S. Jodha) was observed to have trailed the field. Trainer Behram Cama has been asked to ensure that his charge puts up a reasonable performance in the future.

 

After the race, MANOR HOUSE was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its lower lip.

 

After the race, ROCKET was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (144) – THE J. P. VAZIFDAR TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

On arrival at the starting gate, RAMBLER (P. Vinod) was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified the gelding fit to race.

 

Passing the 500 meters, Jockey Bhawani Singh (SPLENDIDO) shifted out thereby taking the ground of CELESTIAL (Vivke G.) who had to be steadied. After questioning and not being satisfied Jockey Bhawani Singh has been suspended for one race day i.e. on 5th April 2025 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

In the final stages of the race, RAMBLER (P. Viond) shifted out under urgings thereby bumping FLAMING LAMBORGINI (Mustakim Alam, dr. 2) .After questioning and not being satisfied Jockey P. Vinod has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race Jockey Vivek G (CELESTIAL) reported that his mount hung in- in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

It was observed that Jockey P. Vinod (RAMBLER) failed to wear his racing goggles in time and wore the same passing the 1200 metres. As such, he has been fined Rs.500/-.

 

After the race, FLAMING LAMBORGINI was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. The rig has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, RAMBLER was trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, ARDAKAN & CELESTIAL were observed to have gum injuries.

 

RACE NO.3 (145) – THE J. RUSTOMJI SOPARIVALA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 12

 

SOCIAL BUTTERFLY (A. Sandesh, dr.5) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

VEGATHA (Imran Chisty, dr.12) lost about two lengths at the start

 

At the start, AZURE (Antony Raj S., dr.8) veered out despite the riders efforts and had to be steadied.  After the Race Jockey Antony Raj S. reported that in an effort to settle his mount the bit shifted onto one side. In view of the filly’s behavior, Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race   

 

At the start, KEY WEST (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.4) jumped inwards onto LEGACY (Yash Narredu, dr.3) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, DREAMER (N. S. Parmar) shifted in and went across FOXY (T. S. Jodha) who had to be steadied thereafter DREAMER further shifted in thereby starting a chain reaction due to which HEAVEN’S RHYTHM (S. J. Sunil) and MAJESTICUS (App. Mustakim Alam) were inconvenienced and NYANZA (A. Prakash) and BISHOP (Vivek G.) were tightened for room and had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey N. S. Parmar has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Approaching the 700 metres, MAJESTICUS (App. Mustakim Alam) shifted out thereby bumping HEAVEN’S RHYTHM (S. J. Sunil). After questioning and not being satisfied, App. Mustakim Alam has been fined Rs.3,000/-.  

 

Approaching the 200 metres, BISHOP (Vivek G.) shifted in and made contact with MAJESTICUS (App. Mustakim Alam). Jockey Vivek G. has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe (KEY WEST) reported that his mount was running in green throughout the race.

 

After the race, Jockey Imran Chisty (VEGATHA) reported that his mount was running green.

 

After the race, MAJESTICUS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, FOXY was observed to have an injury on its right hind fetlock and lost its right hind shoe.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that BISHOP had left eye injury (sore) with ulcer.

RE: APPEAL BY JOCKEY AKSHAY KUMAR AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STIPENDIARY STEWARDS FOR DEPRIVING HIM THE USE OF WHIP IN RACES WITH IMMEDIATE EFFECT TILL THE END OF THE MUMBAI MEETING 2024/25 FOR USING THE WHIP IN EXCESS OF THE PRESCRIBED LIMIT ON HIS MOUNT “CIRCLE OF DREAMS” IN RACE NO.127 RUN ON 23RD FEBRUARY 2025: FOURTH OFFENCE DURING THE CURRENT MUMBAI MEETING 2024/25.

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 25th February 2025, considered appeal dated 25th February 2025 from Jockey Akshay Kumar, the Stewards having heard the submissions made by him, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race in question and having deliberated in the matter at length. The Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to reject his appeal and held him guilty of using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount “CIRCLE OF DREAMS” in the above race.

 

This being his fourth offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, of using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit and in view of stay granted on his punishment, it shall be noted that now he is deprived the use of whip in races w.e.f. 20th March 2025 till the end of the Mumbai Meeting 2024/25 i.e. upto and including 6th April 2025 thereby covering a period of 5 race days and the balance 5 race days shall come into effect from the 1st race day of Pune Meeting 2025 covering the period of 5 race days.
