RACE NO.1(166) – THE ELOISE PLATE : (About) 1400 Metres – 8 Runners

 

Jockey P.Trevor (ACE JET) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.  

Approaching 1000 metres, ACE JET (P.Trevor) was steadied due to which CAESARS WIFE (R.Vaibhav) who was following, was steadied and in the process lost position.

 

After the race, ACE JET, FLAMING HEART, COURTESY CALL and CAESARS WIFE (examined on 23/09/2013) were found to be sound.  FLAMING HEART had lost its left hind plate.

 

RACE NO.2(167) – THE PROTEUS PLATE: (About) 1800 Metres – 9 Runners

 

MERCK (Ishwar Pardeshi) jumped out awkwardly and thereafter stopped abruptly after a few strides and took no part in the race.  Trainer Jayantilal K.Gor has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

While straightening for home, NEON LIGHT (Kavraj Singh) hung outwards and hampered the running of ROMANTIC BEAUTY (N.S.Parmar). Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, ROMANTIC BEAUTY, NEON LIGHT, CORNELIAN, MERCK, SPEEDING and ALWAYS THERE were found to be sound. NEON LIGHT had a cut injury at the right hind coronet.

 

RACE NO.3(168) – THE  MYSORE RACE CLUB TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – 4 Runners

                                      

MONZA (Dashrath Singh) was inclined to lug out in the straight. Trainer Adhirajsingh has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

RACE NO.4(169) – THE FLAMING ACE PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres – 7 Runners

 

After the race, TARA was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(170)  – THE RAO SAHEB KEDARI GOLD TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – 9 Runners

 

At the start, CAPITANO (Shrikant kamble) shifted in and hampered the running of DREAMS R MINE (P.Trevor) who was tightened and lost position. Jockey Shrikant Kamble has been cautioned.

 

Soon after the start, EMPEROR OF FRANCE (C.S.Jodha) leaned outwards on to INCENTIO (S.Sameer) who went  on to DREAM R MINE (P.Trevor).

   

Near 800 metres, ACE ANGELFIRE (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in due to which BOURBON STREET (Malcolm Kharadi) shifted inwards and inconvenienced ALBUURZ (Kavraj Singh). Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

In the concluding stages of the race, ACE ANGELFIRE (Neeraj Rawal) was denied a clear run.

 

After the race, ALBUURZ, ASTROMIA, CAPITANO and ACE ANGELFIRE (examined on 23/09/2013) were found to be sound. BOURBON STREET had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.6(171) – THE  VILLOO C.POONAWALLA GOLD TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – 8 Runners

 

After being stalled, SANTA MARIA (S.Zervan) reared up and hooked one of its fore legs in the adjoining stall. The filly was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. who certified her fit to race. Trainer P.Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

At the start, STAR OF PEACE (P.Trevor, dr.6) shifted in and went across MAUNA KEA (A.Sandesh, dr.5) and then jostled with SULTAN OF SWING (J.Pradeep, dr.4), who in turn made contact with SANTA MARIA (S.Zervan, dr.3). Jockey P.Trevor has been fined Rs.3000/-

 

Jockey R.Vaibhav (RAJSAMMAN) reported that his mount shied away and was unbalanced in the concluding stages of the race.

 

Jockey Nirmal Jodha (ADVANCE TO CONTACT) reported that his mount lugged-in the straight. Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey S.Zervan (SANTA MARIA) reported the loss of whip near 150 metres from the Winning Post, the reason being that his whip hit the rails. 

 

STAR OF PEACE (P.Trevor) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer S.S.Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, MAUNA KEA was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind pastern. STAR OF PEACE and ADVANCE TO CONTACT (examined on 23/09/2013) were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(172) – THE  S.A.POONAWALLA MULTI-MILLION (GR.2): (About) 1600 Metres – 7 Runners

 

At the start, ISADORO (P.Trevor) veered to the outside, losing ground. Trainer S.S.Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the colt.

 

PROVIDENCE (C.S.Jodha) leaned outwards soon on entering the straight and caused FALCON (Suraj Narredu) to shift outwards, who in turn caused TRANCE (Dashrath Singh) to travel wider.   

 

Jockey D.K.Ashish (BOMBARDIER) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being the third offence with regard to the use of the Whip, the matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards interviewed Jockey D.K.Ashish and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 12/10/2013 to 13/10/2013 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race)

-          3 -

Further,  Jockey D.K.Ashish has been fined Rs.3,000/- for using his whip in an Improper manner, wherein he had used the whip on the shoulder of his mount in a forehand position. 

 

Jockey Suraj Narredu (FALCON) has been fined Rs.3,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Further, he has been fined Rs.5,000/- for allowing his mount to shift in under the use of the whip and made contact with BOMBARDIER (D.K.Ashish)  in the final stages of the race.

 

After the race, DYNAMO was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind pastern.

 

PROVIDENCE (C.S.Jodha) was placed fifth and INDIAN TOUCH (B.Sreekanth) was placed sixth by the Judge, in this race.

 

RACE NO.8(173) – THE  RAMBUS PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 11 Runners

 

After being stalled, STRIKING SHADOW (Akshay) leaned in the stall and was not allowing the rider to settle. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

SEEKING MAGIC (Shrikant Kamble) took a fly jump and WINDSOR (V.Chandrakant) was slow in the strides, losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Jockey A.J.Nilesh, the Declared Rider for VALOROZKY, did not report to the Scales in time, therefore he was substituted by Jockey J.Pradeep and an inquiry has been initiated in the matter.  

 

At the start, MARENGO (Dashrath Singh, dr.5) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to PUNE WARRIORS (D.S.Rathore, dr.6), who in turn made contact with STAR GAZING (Vikram Jodha, dr.7).

 

Near 900 metres, PUNE WARRIORS (D.S.Rathore) shifted out and forced VALOROZKY (J.Pradeep) to shift outwards, who in turn caused STRIKING SHADOW (Akshay) to travel wider. Jockey D.S.Rathore has been severely cautioned.

 

Jockey J.Pradeep (VALOROZKY) has been fined Rs.5,000/- for failing to persevere with his mount throughout the race.

 

After the race, MARENGO was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, PUNE WARRIORS, VALOROZKY and STAR GAZING were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.9(174) – THE  RAMBUS PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 10 Runners

 

After being stalled, SMALL TREASURE (S.S.Baria) reared up awkwardly and flipped over and got stuck on to the rear gates.  The gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. who certified him unfit to race, as such, SMALL TREASURE was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order. STAR PRINCE (App.S.Mosin) was fractious and refused to be stalled, as such, the gelding was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order. Trainers Mansor Shah and H.J.Antia have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Trainer Asad Siddique has been fined Rs.3,000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge CALYPSO QUEEN.

 

At the start, CALYPSO QUEEN (A.Ashhad Asbar, dr.6) jumped awkwardly and then shifted in and inconvenienced MATEUS ROSE (P.Trevor, dr.5).

 

Soon after the start, EROTIC FLAME (Akshay) shifted in and tightened the field on its inside whereby MY FREEDOM (A.Sandesh) and CREAM OF THE CROP (R.Vaibhav) were tightened and NEGREDO (App Sandeep Jadhav) who was following, was also hampered and as the filly was steadied, she shifted out on to CALYPSO QUEEN (A.Ashhad Asbar). Jockey Akshay was reported to the Stewards for Careless Riding.

 

The Stewards  interviewed Jockey Akshay in the above connection and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 12th October, 2013 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

Jockey C.S.Jodha (WINDSOR QUEEN) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

 

After the race, MATEUS ROSE was observed to have an abrasion at the right hind fetlock. CREAM OF THE CROP and MONTE CARLO were found to be sound.
