RACE NO.1(81) –  THE DELHI RACE CLUB CUP: (About) 1000 Metres – 4 Runners

 

Jockey P. Trevor (RIQUEWIHR) has been fined Rs.2,000/- for coming in before the red marker

 

Soon after the start, DESTINED FOR GLORY (G. Amit), HIGHLAND EMPRESS (P. S. Chouhan) and KILKARRY BRIDGE (C. S. Jodha) were observed to have jostled due to which DESTINED FOR GLORY dropped back.

 

After the race, DESTINED FOR GLORY was observed to have a cut injury at the right fore fetlock. KILKARRY BRIDGE and HIGHLAND EMPRESS were found to be sound in the trot.

 

RACE NO.2(82) – THE ISHPINGO PLATE (DIV.II) : (About) 1200 Metres – 11 Runners

 

KISS FROM A ROSE (Akshay) was restless in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. ABBEYROAD (T.S.Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 5 lengths at the start and COMMANDPERFORMANCE (Neeraj Rawal) planted in the stall and took no part in the race. Trainers S.Waheed, Amjad Khan and Dallas Todywalla have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. 

 

RACE NO.3(83) – THE DINKOO N.CHENOY TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – 6 Runners

 

At the request of Jockey S Zervan, the saddle on his mount CLYMENE was adjusted at the starting gates.

 

WILD FIRE (Dashrath Singh) was restless in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

RACE NO.4(84) –  THE ATMOSPHERE PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 12 Runners

 

GENTILLESSE (S.A.Amit) jumped out slow losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, GREY FLANNEL (J. Chinoy, dr. 7) jumped outwards onto THE CIVILIAN (N. S. Parmar, dr. 8) who had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 400 metres, GOLD FIELD (D R Shubham) got inconvenienced between CHARGING TIGRESS (D S Daman) and TRIPLE THREAT ( P S Chouhan), then dropped back and made contact with LADY SERGEANT (M Ayyar).

RACE NO.5(85) –  THE RAMBUS PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 13 Runners

GREEK LEGEND (T.S.Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, ANGEL COIN (S. G. Prasad) shifted in and made contact with NAVIGATOR (Dashrath Singh) who was tightened for room and had to be steadied. After questioning Jockey S. G. Prasad has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 800 metres, OUTSTANDING (C. S. Jodha) shifted out thereby tightening ZEEMO (App. M. Ayyar) who had to be checked and further carried NAVIGATOR (Dashrath Singh) outwards. Jockey C. S. Jodha has been fined Rs.1,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh (NAVIGATOR) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

After the race, NAVIGATOR was trotted and found to be sound.

After the race, ARC OF PASSION was observed to have a cut injury on the inside of the right hock.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “HIGHLAND EMPRESS”  IN RACE NO.29 – THE RUBEN STAR PLATE (DIV.II) - RUN ON 5TH AUGUST, 2018 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Rehanullah Khan and Jockey S.A.Amit in the above connection. Trainer Rehanullah Khan said that he had instructed the Jockey to take a good jump and sit in 3rd/4th position and coming into the straight to do his best.  Jockey S.A.Amit confirmed the instructions. When asked to explain his riding, Jockey S.A.Amit said that the filly took a fly jump and thereafter she was keen and since no one was going to the front at that stage he found himself alongside STORMY ATLANTIC (S.Zervan). He said that he was trying to settle the filly but the riders behind kept shouting and the filly was too keen hence he let her roll.

 

The video recording of Race No.29 was viewed and on being asked whether he had niggled/pushed the filly after the start, Jockey S.A.Amit stated that he had only given the filly loose rein in order to maintain his position but the filly became very keen. When asked why he had not settled the filly, he reiterated that he did try to settle the filly but she was very keen.

 

Trainer Rehanullah Khan felt that there was no mala-fide intent in the Jockey’s riding, however, he should have made more efforts to follow the instructions.

 

Having considered the submissions made and having viewed the video tape recording of the race, Jockey S.A.Amit has been severely cautioned that in future he should make all efforts to follow his instructions.
