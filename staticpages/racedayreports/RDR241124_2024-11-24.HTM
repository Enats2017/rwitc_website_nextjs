RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1200 METRES (MILE CHUTE) LEADING TO 2 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES AND THEREAFTER 4 METRES AWAY UP TO THE WINNING POST. FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WERE PLACED FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1200 METRES LEADING TO 2 METRES AT 1000 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1 (1) – THE FEDORA PLATE: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

Tr. Imtiaz A.Sait informed that his charge AWAY SHE GOES was under medication and had requested to withdraw the mare from the race due to late realization of medication. As such, he was permitted to withdraw his charge after the declaration stage and is fined Rs.15,000/-. Further, in accordance with Calendar Notification the sample has been collected for analysis.

 

MARIELLA (P. Vinod, dr.3) threw its head up at the start.

 

At the start, ANOUSHKA (Amyn Merchant, dr.4) jumped outwards at the same time SORRENTO SECRET (A. Prakash, dr.6) jumped inwards thereby bumping and inconveniencing ATOMIC ANGEL (App. Bharat Singh, dr.5).

 

Approaching the 400 metres, PRINCE IGOR (App. N. Bhosale) shifted in under urgings thereby brushing the haunches of ATOMIC ANGEL (App. Bharat Singh) who was unbalanced thereafter passing the 300 metres PRINCE IGOR shifted out and carried MARLBORO MAN (App. Haridas Gore) outwards. App. N. Bhosale has been cautioned.

 

In the final stages of the race, Jockey A. Prakash (SORRENTO SECRET) was observed to have dropped his hands before passing the winning post thereby losing a certain third place. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey A. Prakash has been suspended for two race days i.e. on 15th and 22nd December 2024 (Permitted to ride work / mock race).

 

It was observed that the saddle used on ATOMIC ANGEL (App. Bharat Singh) shifted forward during the race. This being the first instance of faulty saddling during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, PRINCE IGOR was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, DAGGER’S STRIKE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (2) – THE K. J. K IRANI PLATE: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 5

 

At the start, BUBBLY BOY (Yash Narredu, dr.2) jumped outwards at the same time FAST PACE (Suraj Narredu, dr.3) leaned inwards and they made contact.

 

Passing the 1400 metres, BUBBLY BOY (Yash Narredu) shifted out and made contact with DREAM ALLIANCE (A. Prakash) who had to be steadied. Jockey Yash Narredu has been cautioned.

 

After the race, CHRISTOFLE, FAST PACE & DREAM ALLIANCE were trotted and found to be sound. BUBBLY BOY was observed to have lost its right fore shoe.

 

RACE NO.3 (3) – THE DR. S. R. CAPTAIN SALVER: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

Passing the 400 metres, MUTANT (App. Haridas Gore) shifted out despite the riders efforts thereby carrying DASH (Yash Narredu) outwards. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey A. Prakash (PYRITE) reported loss of whip passing the 250 metres. This being the first instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, Jockey R. Ajinkya (FONTANA) reported that his mount hung-in throughout the race. Trainer P. S. Chouhan has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Yash Narredu (DASH) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, DASH, JADE & FONTANA were trotted and found to be sound. PYRITE was observed to have an injury on right elbow.

 

RACE NO.4 (4) – THE P. B. AVASIA PLATE: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

ODYSSEUS (Suraj Narredu, dr.2) jumped outwards onto ATATURK (C. Umesh, dr.3).

 

Soon after the start, ENDURANCE (A. Prakash) leaned inwards and went across AMADEO (Yash Narredu) who had to be steadied. Jockey A. Prakash has been cautioned.

 

After the race, ALACRITY was observed to have a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.5 (5) – THE WELCOME TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 12

 

At the start, GYPSY SOUL (Santosh G., dr.6) shifted in and went across REMY RED (T. S. Jodha) who clipped the former and stumble. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Santosh G. has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

At the start, LION KING (Amyn Merchant, dr.2) jumped out awkwardly thereby unbalancing its rider.

 

From the 400 metres, ELOQUENT (App. N. Bhosale) drifted out and carried THE GENERAL (Bhawani Singh) outwards. App. N. Bhosale has been cautioned.

 

After the race, App. Haridas Gore (CREATIVE GIRL) reported that his mount was running green in the straight. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey Bhawani Singh, the rider of THE GENERAL (Placed 3rd) by the Judge against Jockey C. Umesh., the rider of VINCERO (Placed 2nd) by the Judge. The reason stated was, In the last 200 metres, VINCERO drifted out alarmingly right across his horse due to which he had to completely check his horse which broke his stride after which he had covered ground and finished only ¾ of a length behind him. Further, Jockey C. Umesh had his whip on his right and did not take any corrective measures until it was too late.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race and acting on their own observation of the race, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing as under:-

 

DIVINE STAR            -    FIRST;                    VINCERO                      -    THIRD;

 

THE GENERAL          -   SECOND;              MOONLIGHT KISS     -   FOURTH.

 

After the last race, the Stewards having heard the submissions made by Jockey C. Umesh noted that despite his mount shifting out he had persisted with the whip on the right and had not taken corrective measures, hence, held him guilty of careless riding and decided to suspend his Jockeys licence for 2 race days i.e. on 15th & 22nd December 2024 (permitted to ride work/mock race). Further, Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6 (6) – THE ROYAL CALCUTTA TURF CLUB TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

At the start, MAGILETO (Suraj Narredu, dr.6) jumped outwards onto BABY BAZOOKA (C. S. Jodha, dr.7) who in turn leaned outwards onto DEXA (N. S. Parmar, dr.8).

 

RACE NO.7 (7) – THE M. D. PETIT PLATE: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

Soon after the start, LIGHTNING BLAZE (K. G. Likith Appu) leaned outwards onto MIRAE (App. N. Bhosale).

 

Passing the red marker, FIDATO (S. J. Sunil) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening the field on his inside due to which MIRAE (App. N. Bhosale), KHALEESI (Suraj Narredu) & GOLDEN RULE (App. S. Siddharth) were tightened and had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered, the Stipendiary Stewards held Jockey S. J. Sunil guilty of careless riding and decided to suspend his Jockeys licence for two race days i.e. on 15th & 22nd December 2024 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, ELIZABETH REGINA was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, KHALEESI, MIRAE & LIGHTNING BLAZE were trotted and found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE: APP. MUSTAKIM ALAM REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR CARELESS RIDING ON HIS  MOUNT “BAKLAVA” (DRAWN 7) IN RACE NO.49 – THE F. D. WADIA TROPHY (GRADE 3) (DIST. 1400 MTRS.) RUN ON SATURDAY, 17TH AUGUST 2024:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, App. Mustakim Alam was called before the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 24th November 2024. On being questioned, App. Mustakim Alam stated that considering that DASH (Yash Narredu) was racing on his outside and since there was no gap, he tapped his mount and shifted in and added that the Jockey on his inside did not shout out to him otherwise he would have maintained a straight course and he apologized for his mistake.

 

In this connection, the Stewards of the Club having considered all the submissions made by him and having viewed the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and also taking into account his past record held him guilty of careless riding, decided by majority to suspend his Jockeys licence for three race days as well as to invoke the suspended sentence of three race days accorded to him by the Board of Appeal. As such, his Jockeys licence shall stand suspended w.e.f. 15th December 2024 to 9th January 2025 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Subsequently, vide his letter dated 25th November 2024, he stated that he does not wish to appeal against the decision of the Stewards and requested to start his suspension with immediate effect. In view of his request the revised dates of his suspension shall be w.e.f. 8th December 2024 to 5th January 2025 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: APPEAL BY MR. N. B. KULDEEP AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY'S LICENCE FOR A PERIOD OF 3 MONTHS W.E.F 27TH OCTOBER 2024 UPTO 26TH JANUARY 2025 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR BEING GUILTY OF MISCONDUCT UNDER RULE (101) OF THE RULES OF RACING - PASSING RACING INFORMATION ON THE JOCKEY'S WHATSAPP GROUP FOR THE RACES HELD AT PUNE ON 28TH JULY 2024:

 

In connection with the above subject matter, the Board of Appeal at their meeting held on Thursday, 7th November 2024, considered Appeal dated 21st October 2024 from Mr. N. B. Kuldeep, considered all documents and papers that were submitted including his grounds of appeal and heard the submissions made by him and his spokesman, Mr. P.S. Chouhan. Whilst making submissions, Mr. P.S. Chouhan had withdrawn point 4 of the grounds of appeal which stated "The inquiry was initiated based on a misunderstanding without allowing him a fair opportunity to explain the context of his message and this is against the principle of natural justice as he did not get any opportunity to explain his case. Proper procedural fairness was not uphold as he was not given the chance to clarify that the message was simply his personal opinion and not an endorsement of betting on those horses”. Mr. Chouhan had also apologized to the Board for making this statement. This matter was duly recorded in the Minutes of meeting.

 

The Board of Appeal having deliberated on the matter at length, decided to reduce the period of suspension of his Jockey's licence. Since he was granted a stay on his punishment till his appeal is heard and disposed off, his jockey's licence now stands suspended from 8th November 2024 till 31st December 2024 (both days Inclusive). Further, he is permitted to ride work/mock race.
