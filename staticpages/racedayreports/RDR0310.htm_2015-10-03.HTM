RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “STARRY MOMENT” (DRAWN. 9) IN RACE NO.152 - THE ATLANTUS PLATE (DIV.II), RUN ON SATURDAY, 26th SEPTEMBER 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed and App.Bhawani Singh in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed  stated that he had instructed the Apprentice to try and go to the front if possible, if not, then to sit wherever the filly is comfortable, come into the straight and to do his best. App.Bhawani Singh confirmed the instructions. Trainer Altamaash Ahmed further stated that the filly has won only one race in her career and she  has been a big disappointment. To a specific question, he stated that he takes no objection to the riding of App.Bhawani Singh. He added that after the race, the Apprentice reported that he had received interference. To a further query, he stated that the filly had drifted out in the straight in her previous run and subsequently he had her mouth examined and was found to be normal.

 

App.Bhawani Singh stated that after the interference, the filly was keen and in an effort to settle her, she threw her head up. Trainer Altamaash Ahmed stated that the filly had behaved in a similar manner in the past.

 

Having heard the submissions made and  keeping in mind that the Trainer was satisfied with the riding and that he has a low opinion about the filly, no action was initiated in the matter. However, but both were informed that the enquiry was being kept open for the next couple of runs.

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “STRIKING STORY” IN RACE NO.154 - THE NOBLE PRINCE PLATE RUN ON SATURDAY 26th SEPTEMBER 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer B.Prakash and Jockey H.G.Rathod in the above connection.  The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer B.Prakash stated that he had instructed the Jockey to take a good jump and sit in 4th/5th position, come into the straight and to do his best. The Jockey confirmed the instructions. Trainer B.Prakash opined that STRIKING STORY is a pure Class V horse and has been running on board,  however, every time he gives up in the last furlong, hence this time around he wanted to change the tactics and hence had asked the Jockey to brush up and sit 4th/5th and to do his best.

 

After hearing the submissions made and viewing the video of the race in question as also the previous runs of the horse, the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted for future reference. However Jockey H.G.Rathod has been cautioned to be more vigorous in his riding efforts in the future.

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ALIEN” IN RACE NO.158-THE DANCING GLANCES TROPHY (DIV.I), RUN ON SATURDAY, 26th SEPTEMBER 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi and Jockey S.G.Prasad in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi stated that he had instructed the Jockey to sit in 4th/5th position and if the gelding took a fly jump then to push him and sit close to the field. Further, he had specifically told the Jockey to sit close to the rails, come into the straight and to do his best. Jockey S.G.Prasad confirmed the instructions. To a specific question, the Jockey stated that in an effort to sit close to the rails he had dropped back, and in the straight the horse drifted out.

 

On viewing the various T.V. angles, the Trainer agreed that the Jockey could have been more vigorous and could have made better effort to follow the instructions.

 

After hearing the submissions made by the Trainer and Jockey, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.G.Prasad, particularly with regard to his riding efforts in the straight as also for failing to follow his Trainer’s instructions. However, keeping in mind that the horse was at longer odds, the Stipendiary Stewards took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for16th October 2015 for incompetent riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “SPEAR OF TRIUM” IN RACE NO.165 - THE PUNTO ABARTH TROPHY (DIV-I), RUN ON SUNDAY, 27TH SEPTEMBER, 2015 VIS-À-VIS ITS LAST RUN ON 5TH SEPTEMBER 2015.

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Narendra Lagad in the above connection. He  stated that the 22 days bench mark was a good time for a horse to improve. He added that there was only one common horse, ABBEY, who was giving his horse 4.5 kg in the previous race and beat him by 3 ¼ lengths, whereas in the race in question the weight difference was 5.5 Kg. Further, the run of ABBEY was under enquiry. He pointed out that BLUE FLAME who ran 2nd  to his horse this time, had finished 7th in his previous start and PASSION PRINCE who ran third, may have beaten his horse if it had not hung in. Also, there was a slight change of instruction from the previous run i.e. in the previous run there were many fast horses (MULTINATIONAL, ABIGAIL and ABBEY) where he had told the Jockey to sit 4th/5th and to do his best,  however, in the race in question, his horse was drawn-1 and seeing that there were no fast horses in the race, he had instructed the Jockey to hit the front. He further added that in the previous run i.e. Race No.105, the weight difference between SPEAR OF TRIUM and MULTINATIONAL was 7 kg. Thereafter, MULTINATIONAL has run 3rd with a good set of horse so he felt that his horse had a good chance in this race.   

After going through the Work and Medical Log Books, viewing both the races and considering that the horse was at long odds both the times and there was almost no betting on the horse in Race No.165 when he won, no action has been initiated against Trainer Narendra Lagad beyond cautioning him to be more careful in the future..

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SAMBA WARRIOR”  IN RACE NO.169-THE DAMODARAN D PILLAI PLATE, RUN ON SUNDAY 27th SEPTEMBER 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait stated that Jockey P.Trevor had ridden the gelding before, as such he had told the Jockey that the gelding takes time to come into stride, so pick him up from the four Furlongs and thereafter ride his own race and to do his best. To a specific question, he stated that his first reaction was that this is not Jockey P.Trevor’s riding and even his Owners were not happy with the ride and asked him to complain against the Jockey. Further, on seeing the replay, he noticed that the Jockey looked to his right and dropped back at 1200 metres. When he questioned Jockey P.Trevor after the race, he said that he had received interference from HONEST PLEASURE (D.K.Ashish) who shifted out at that stage and he had lost position. Thereafter, he had found himself on the outside and in the straight his horse did not get a clear run and still finished a good fifth. Trainer Imtiaz Sait added that when he got back to the stables he had examined the gelding and found that it had injury marks on the right suspensory ligament and on the right hind pastern which was confirmed by the Sr.V.O. of the Club.

On viewing the various T.V. angles it was evident that Jockey D.K.Ashish (HONEST PLEASURE) in an effort to make a forward position, had tightened SAMBA WARRIOR (P.Trevor) and thereafter, STAR OF BABYLON (T.S.Jodha) had come in, further tightening SAMBA WARRIOR. Considering the interference and the injuries to the gelding, the enquiry has been closed and the explanation tendered was noted for future reference.

RE : POSITIVE FINDING OF “CATHINONE” IN THE URINE SAMPLE OF “SOUL CENTRIC (WINNER) IN RACE NO.86 – THE RAPHAELA PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 29TH AUGUST, 2015 – IN CHARGE OF TRAINER S.S.SHAH

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 3rd October,  2015 interviewed Tr.S.S.Shah regarding the positive report dated 29th September, 2015   from Racing Analytical Services Ltd, Australia, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge SOUL CENTRIC (Winner) in Race No.86 – The Raphaela Plate (Div.II) run on 29th August, 2015, which  read  as under:-

“THE URINE SAMPLE WAS SHOWN TO CONTAIN CATHINONE”

 

At this meeting, Trainer S.S.Shah was handed over a copy of the above report and was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, within five days of opening the envelope that identifies the sample, i.e. latest by 8th October, 2015, whether he wished to send the split sample to Hong Kong Jockey Club, Laboratory, LGC, New Market, UK, LCH, France or Quanti LAB, Mauritius, , for Confirmatory analysis and also the name of the witnessing analyst, if any, failing which the Stewards shall treat the report of the primary laboratory as conclusive.

 

Tr.S.S.Shah was asked to conduct his own enquiries and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.V.O. of the Club, as and when he would be required to.
