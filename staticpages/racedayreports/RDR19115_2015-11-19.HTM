RACE NO.1 – THE WELCOME PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 8 

 

WAR PRINCESS (App.Parbat Singh) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Himmat Singh has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

At the start, ZAFIRAH (Dashrath Singh, dr.3) was observed to have shifted out on to GYAHANA (App.Vishal, dr.4) and at the same point, WAR PRINCESS (App.Parbat Singh, dr.7) shifted in on to TERRIFIC (App.Jaykumar, dr.6) who in turn went on to MOROCCAN (App.R. Ajinkya, dr.5) thereby bumping and inconveniencing the latter.

 

An objection was lodged by App.R.Ajinkya,  rider of MOROCCAN (Placed Second) against GYAHANA (App.Vishal, Placed First). The reason being,  GYAHANA came on to him before the Red Marker and bumped his horse who got unbalanced and unsettled and lost position. Thereafter, he brushed his horse and used him in the early part because of which he lost the race by a neck.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule the objection.

 

After the race, App. Bhawani Singh (HERE I AM) reported that his mount was hanging in. Trainer Subhag Singh has been advised to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Tr.D.J.Surti was questioned regarding the performance of his charge GYAHANA in this race as  compared to her previous runs during the Pune Meeting 2015.  The Trainer stated that in the last two runs in Pune i.e. Race No.193 and Race No.225 he was of the opinion that the filly had a fair chance and he put up Jockey Neeraj Rawal who had requested for the ride, however the filly performed poorly since possibly she did not enjoy the Track conditions. He also pointed out that the filly probably likes the Mumbai track since she had run a good race last Mumbai Season. In the race in question the filly was carrying bottom weight and in addition he claimed 5 kg. allowance, which may have worked in her favour. The Stipendiary Stewards were not entirely satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Trainer, however, since the filly was at longer odds and there was negligible betting, no action was initiated against the Trainer beyond cautioning him to be more careful in the future.  

 

The Stipendiary Stewards have initiated an enquiry into the running and riding of MOROCCAN and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that HERE I AM was examined at the stables in the morning on 20/11/2015 and found to be sore by the left fore leg. Also, the gelding had an abrasion at the left hind fetlock. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, GYAHANA was observed to have lost its left hind plate. MOROCCAN and WAR PRINCESS were found to be sound.

SUBJECT :  ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE FINDINGS OF “O-DESMETHYL-VENLAFAXINE” IN THE URINE SAMPLE OF 1) ch f “MAISHA” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.17 – THE MAHARAJA OF MORVI TROPHY RUN ON 23RD NOVEMBER, 2014 & 2) ch f “ARRIVEDERCI” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.79 – THE N.M.IRANI TROPHY (DIV.II) RUN ON 25TH DECEMBER, 2014 – MATTER REMANDED BACK TO THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB BY THE BOARD OF APPEAL – DE-NOVO ENQUIRY:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 17th October, 2015 and thereafter on 29th October, 2015 considered the above matter.

 

At the outset, in Tr.P.Shroff’s presence, the Stewards congratulated the Sr.Stipendiary Steward and his team for holding a detailed and fair enquiry into his positive findings. Tr.P.Shroff was informed that the Stewards considered all submissions, papers, proceedings, documents and various scientific opinions before them. The Stewards heard him/his Witness Dr.Hasneyn Mirza as also two members from the Expert Panel viz. Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan. They also gave him the opportunity to cross question Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan. Having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and all submissions made, the Stewards did not accept his mitigating circumstances and held him vicariously responsible for the positive finding of O-Desmethylvenlaxine in the urine sample of MAISHA and ARRIVEDERCI. Further, the Stewards also held him guilty of not maintaining his log book properly.

 

However, that being said, taking an overall view and having considered all the submissions made and keeping in mind that the two Members of the Expert Panel viz. Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan had said that there is a possibility of environmental contamination in this case, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to club the two positives of MAISHA and ARRIVEDERCI as one offence and to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 60 days and a fine of Rs.15,000/- for vicarious liability as per the norms for Class-2 drugs since Rule-XII of the “Medication Control For Horses in Training and Racing”  states “The Trainer is always responsible for the protection and security of the horses in his care. Trainers are warned that they will be held responsible if an examination of a horse results in the finding of any prohibited substances.”

 

Tr.P.Shroff was informed that the suspension of 30 days and fine of Rs.10,000/- imposed on him by the Stewards of the Club for the positive finding of CAPSAICIN in the urine sample of RAMESSES in Race No 77 run on 21st December 2014 and for which he had not appealed, would stand.

 

Therefore, his Trainer’s license stands suspended for a total period of 90 days (60 days for MAISHA & ARRIVEDERCI + 30 days for RAMESSES) i.e. w.e.f. 19th November, 2015 to 16th February, 2016 (both days inclusive).

 

Further, the Stewards decided to fine him a sum of Rs.50,000/- for not maintaining his log book properly.

SUBJECT: ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE FINDINGS OF “O-DESMETHYLVENLAFAXINE” IN THE URINE SAMPLE OF (A) ch g MOHEGUN SUN” (UNPLACED) IN RACE NO. 30 – 30TH NOVEMBER 2014; (B) ch m “BURGUNDY ROSE” (PLACED SECOND) IN RACE NO. 66 – 18TH DECEMBER 2014 (C) ch f “ARGYLE PINK” (WINNER) IN RACE NO. 88 – 28TH DECEMBER 2014; (D) b f “ULRIKA” (SPECIAL SAMPLE) IN RACE NO. 148 – 22ND JANUARY 2015; (E) gr f “GREY MIST” (WINNER) IN RACE NO. 172 – 1ST FEBRUARY 2015 (F) b g “AME” (UNPLACED) IN RACE NO. 190 – 12TH FEBRUARY 2015 - MATTER REFERRED BACK TO THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB BY THE BOARD OF APPEAL- DE-NOVO ENQUIRY :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 17th October, 2015 and thereafter on 29th October, 2015 considered the above matter.

 

The Stewards considered all submissions, papers, proceedings, documents and various scientific opinions before them. The Stewards heard Tr.M.K.Jadhav/his witness Mr.Hasmukh Chawda as also two members from the Expert Panel viz. Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan. They also gave Tr.M.K.Jadhav the opportunity to cross question Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan. Having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and all submissions made, the Stewards did not accept his mitigating circumstances and held him vicariously responsible for the positive finding of O-Desmethylvenlaxine in the urine sample of his six horses. Further, the Stewards held him grossly negligent in running his horse AME on 12th February, 2015 after having knowledge of the probable cause.

 

Nevertheless, taking an overall view and taking into account his excellent track record of 44 years with only one infringement and keeping in mind that the two Members of the Expert Panel viz. Prof Tatu and Dr.Karthikeyan had said that there was a possibility of contamination in this case also, even though they had opined differently on the 1st

of April, 2015, the Stewards took a lenient view and without precedent, decided to give him some benefit of doubt and  to club his six positives cases as one offence and to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 60 days and a fine of Rs.15,000/- for vicarious liability as per the norms for Class-2 drugs since Rule-XII of the “Medication Control For Horses in Training and Racing”  states “The Trainer is always responsible for the protection and security of the horses in his care. Trainers are warned that they will be held responsible if an examination of a horse results in the finding of any prohibited substances.”

 

Tr.M.K.Jadhav was told that the suspension of 30 days and fine of Rs.10,000/- imposed on him by the Stewards of the Club for the positive finding of FLURBIPROFEN in the urine sample of ch m “BURGUNDY ROSE” (Unplaced) in Race No.158 run on 24th January, 2015, and for which he had not appealed, would stand.

 

Therefore, his Trainer’s license stands suspended for a total period of 90 days (60 days for the six cases of O-Desmethylvenlafaxine + 30 days for BURGUNDY ROSE (Flurbiprofen) i.e. w.e.f. 19th November, 2015 to 16th February, 2016 (both days inclusive).

 

Further, the Stewards decided to fine him a sum of Rs.30,000/- for being grossly negligent in running his horse AME on 12th February, 2015 after having knowledge of the probable cause.

RE : POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF  b g “ASTAPI”, b g “ALAINDAIR” AND ch g “AN ACQUIRED TASTE” WHO WERE COMPULSORILY SAMPLED ON 16TH JULY, 2015 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE HORSES HAD ARRIVED TO TR.ALTAMAASH A.AHMED FROM A STUD FARM :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 2nd November, 2015 and thereafter on 16th November, 2015 considered the above matter.

 

The Stewards considered all submissions, papers, proceedings, documents and various scientific opinions before them. The Stewards heard Tr.Altamaash A.Ahmed/his Spokesman Dr.Nevill R. Devlaliwalla. Having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and all submissions made, the Stewards did not accept his arguments and in the absence of any mitigating circumstances, held him responsible for the positive finding of 16β-hydroxy-stanozolol in the urine sample of his three horses.

 

Taking an overall view and keeping in mind that 16β-hydroxy-stanozolol is an Anabolic Steroid, which is banned by the Government of India, the Stewards of the Club decided to withdraw his Trainer’s license and to warn him off w.e.f. 15th December, 2015 to 30th April, 2017 from the Race Courses at Mumbai and Pune and the Enclosures and other places where the powers of the Stewards are in force.

 

Tr.Altamaash A.Ahmed was informed that all the disabilities under the Rules of Racing for Warned Off /Excluded/Disqualified Person, shall be applicable to him.

 

Further, since he was a Warned off person, the permission granted to him to own and race horses under the Rules of this Club, stands revoked.
