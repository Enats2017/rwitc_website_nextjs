RACE NO.5(202) - THE R J KOLAH TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres - Runners - 8

 

At the start, THE AWAKENING (Yash Narredu, dr.8) jumped out awkwardly losing about 2 lengths.

 

Passing the 600 metres, CONSTANT VARIABLE (Santosh G.) was inclined outwards due to which ARCTIC BAY (S. A. Amit) was inconvenienced and had to be steadied as a result of which THE AWAKENING (Yash Narredu) racing on its haunches was held up for room.

 

After the race, Jockey C S Jodha (COUNT OF SAVOY) report that his mount was running green.

 

After the race, COUNT OF SAVOY was trotted and found to be sound but had an abrasion at the left gaskin & hock.

 

RACE NO.6(203) - THE J RUSTOMJI SOPARIVALA TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres - Runners - 12

 

At the request of Trainer M. Narredu, ANASTASIA was examined at 1100 hours on 04/04/2021 and found to be sore by the right hind leg, as such the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race. 

 

At the request of Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait, IMPERMANENCE was examined at 1130 hours on 04/04/2021 and found to be sore by the right fore leg, as such the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After being stalled, NISHK (R. Ajinkya, dr.7) reared up dislodging its rider.

 

At the start, MARVELLOUS (P. S. Chouhan, dr.10) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths.

 

At the start, DIVINE SOUL (M. Ayyar, dr.8)  jumped inwards and FETE ACCOMPLI (N. Nadeem, dr.5) jumped outwards thereby tightening NISHK (R. Ajinkya, dr.7) drawn between them

 

Soon after the start, CHAMPERS ON ICE (S. A. Amit) shifted out and went across MARVELLOUS (P. S. Chouhan) who was slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, CHAMPERS ON ICE (S. A. Amit) shifted in thereby tightening DEMETRIUS (T. S. Jodha) along the rails causing the later to be checked and dropped back. Jockey S. A. Amit has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, LOADED DICE (J. Chinoy) leaned inwards thereby bumping WHISTER BLOWER (Prashant P. Dhebe) who had to be steadied.

 

Approaching 350 metres, FETE ACCOMPLI (N. Nadeem) shifted in and went across MOONSHOT (A. Sandesh) causing the later to be checked. Jockey N Nadem has been cautioned.

 

After the race, FETE ACCOMPLI was found to be sore by the left hind leg. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7(204) - THE FLAREON PLATE (DIV I): (About) 1200 Metres - Runners - 11

 

On the arrival at the start, at the request of Jockey S J Sunil his mount HILAD was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified him fit to race.

 

At the start, REGAL PRINCE (App. P. S. Kaviraj, dr.11) was observed to have lost its footing and stumbled.

 

Passing the 900 metres, due to a tightly packed field HISTORIC (H. Zeeshan) had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, PERFECT PERFECTO (D. R. Shubham) leaned inwards onto VICTORIA PEAK (A. Sandesh) causing the later to be checked and dropped back due to which STREEK (S. Zervan) racing on its haunches had to be steadied. Jockey D. R. Shubham has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 700 metres, REGAL PRINCE (App. P. S. Kaviraj) was observed to have stumbled on its own and had to be steadied. After the race, App P S Kaviraj reported that his mount was not galloping freely.

 

Passing the 200 metres, SPICE (App. M. S. Deora) shifted in thereby inconveniencing COUNT THE WINS (Raghuveer Singh). App M S Deora has been cautioned.

 

After the race, VICTORIA PEAK was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HILAD, HISTORIC and REGAL PRINCE were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(205) - THE R R KOMANDUR PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres - Runners - 13

 

At the request of Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait, SENIORITY was examined at 1015 hours on 04/04/2021 and found to be sore by the right fore leg, as such the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After being stalled, AME (T. S. Jodha, dr.12) played up in the stall. As such the gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified him fit to race. Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures.

Soon after the start, JUDY BLUE EYES (S. J. Sunil) shifted out at the same time JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe) shifted in thereby tightening COLLEGIUM (Satish Nayak) racing between them.

Passing the 450 metres, JUDY BLUE EYES (S. J. Sunil) shifted out at the same time ARC SHINE (Amyn Merchant) leaned inwards thereby tightening JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe) causing the later to be checked and change course. Jockey S. J. Sunil has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh (DIVINE GLORY) reported that his mount hung out throughout the race. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures.

After the race, CELLINI and DIVINE GLORY were trotted and found to be sound.
