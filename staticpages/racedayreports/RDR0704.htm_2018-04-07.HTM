 RACE DAY REPORTS

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT

 

RACE NO.1(265) –THE M.D.PETIT PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners - 5 

 

At the request of Trainer C. Rajendra, SILVER EDGE was examined at 1100 hours on 07/04/2018 and was found to have a capped elbow on the right fore leg. As such, the horse was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The horse has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor reported that he found his mount FINEST MOMENT uncomfortable in the straight.

 

After the race, Jockey A. Sandesh reported that his mount YELLOWSTONE missed a stride in the last 50 metres and had to be steadied.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of YELLOWSTONE (A. Sandesh) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that FINEST MOMENT (P. Trevor) came back in distress after the race. The gelding recovered after cold water hosing and first aid treatment and was sent to the stables.

 

After the race, YELLOWSTONE and FINEST MOMENT were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(266) – THE GIRL FROM IPANEMA PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners – 7

 

Passing the 200 metres, GRAND ACCORD (Bhawani Singh) was observed to have hung out despite the rider’s effort. Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha reported that his mount SAMARJEET was inclined to run out throughout the race. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, GLORIOSUS, SAMARJEET, MASSIMO and GRAND ACCORD were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(267) –THE BAHRAIN TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 9 

 

At the start RIQUEWIHR (P. Trevor, dr. 6) jumped inwards and went across IMPALA (Dashrath Singh, dr. 5) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching 800 metres, GODSWORD (J. Chinoy) shifted in thereby tightening EXCELLENT (A. Sandesh) who had to be checked and drop back. Jockey J. Chinoy has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Zervan reported that about 100 metres from the start the bit used on his mount ARROWFIELD was displaced to the right. As such he could not ride his mount to complete advantage. Trainer P. Shroff has been fined Rs. 2,000/- for the lapse.  

 

After the race, Jockey N. Tauseef Ahmed reported that his mount DEAN’S GOLD ran green.

 

Passing the 300 metres, AUGURY (Vishal N. Bunde) was observed to have maintained an erratic course.

 

Passing 200 metres, RIQUEWIHR (P. Trevor) shifted out. At the same time Jockey A. Sandesh was observed to have used the whip on EXCELLENT due to which it shied further outwards.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of EXCELLENT (A. Sandesh) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, RIQUEWIHR and AUGURY were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(268) –THE MADRAS RACE CLUB TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres–Runners - 4

 

At the request of Trainer Nazak Chenoy, BRYNHILL was examined at 2145 hours on 06/04/2018 and was found to have fever. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting with her to race.

 

At the start, ABBEY (P. Trevor, dr. 3) jumped outwards and at the same time, TIMELESS (C. S. Jodha, dr. 4) jumped inwards and they made contact due to which TIMELESS shifted out and made contact with GOVERNOR GENERAL (App. K. Nazil, dr. 5).

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor reported that his mount ABBEY was inclined to run out from the start. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey C. S. Jodha, the rider of TIMELESS (Placed Second) against GOVERNOR GENERAL (App. K. Nazil, Placed First) and ABBEY (P. Trevor, Placed Fourth). The reason being, on jump out ABBEY (P. Trevor, dr.3) came out and GOVERNOR GENERAL (App. K. Nazil, dr.5) came in instead of travelling straight in the first 100 metres as per the Rules of Racing, due to which, his mount TIMELESS was badly squeezed and lost its rhythm and position and he had to cover lot of ground in the straight and this cost him a certain race, the verdict being half a length.

 

After considering the submissions made by Jockeys C. S. Jodha and P. Trevor as also App. K. Nazil who was accompanied by Trainer M. K. Jadhav, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection.

 

After the race, ABBEY and TIMELESS were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(269) –THE  STYLECRACKER STAKES: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 7

 

At the request of Jockey P. Trevor, the bridle on his mount QUEENS BEST was adjusted on arrival at the start.

 

Passing the 200 metres, NEMBEE GREEK (G. Amit) was observed to have drifted out under the use of whip. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered, Jockey G. Amit has been cautioned for failing to change his whip hand despite his horse drifting out.  

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that at the request of Trainer Mansoor Shah, ALSACE was examined at the stables in the morning on 08/04/2018 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, QUEENS BEST and SPIRIT OF OATH were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(270) –THE  AHU PLATE  (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 7

As such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

After the race, ABRAXAS, STORM STAR, ARVAN and ROYAL BLOOD were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(271) –THE  AHU PLATE  (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 8

 

At the start, POINT THE STARS (Kuldeep Singh, dr.4) was observed to have reared up and jumped outwards on to DANCE OF FIRE (Hindu Singh, dr.5) who in turn shifted out thereby inconveniencing RICH N RARE (Raghuveer Singh, dr.6) who was slow to begin.

After the race, SCION, DANCE OF FIRE and RICH N RARE were trotted and found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “LORD COMMANDER” IN RACE NO.230 – THE QUEST FOR CASH PLATE – RUN ON THURSDAY, 15th MARCH, 2018 – TRAINER BEZAN CHENOY AND JOCKEY P. S. CHOUHAN :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 3rd April, 2018 interviewed Tainer Bezan Chenoy and his Spokesperson, Mrs. Bakhtawar B. Chenoy as also Jockey P. S. Chouhan, in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club having heard Jockey P. S. Chouhan extensively as also Trainer Bezan Chenoy and his Spokesperson, all statements/documents made/presented by the veterinary officers and the stipendiary stewards and viewed the video recording of the race in question, as also the horse’s two previous runs during the current Mumbai Meeting 2017/18 i.e. Race Nos.104 and 177. In addition, the Stewards also viewed the video recording of Race No.196 to compare Jockey P. S. Chouhan’s riding style on ADMO and having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards did not accept the explanation tendered by Jockey P. S. Chouhan and held him liable for not permitting his mount LORD COMMANDER to run on its merits in Race No.230. In that,:-

 

a)       He did not follow the Trainer’s instructions;

b)       His riding efforts in the straight on a favourite were found wanting as compared to his known riding ability, as one of the senior most jockey’s in India. 

c)       There was a drift in odds (both in win and place) on LORD COMMANDER, while the winner, FINE TUNE, owned by the same part-owner was backed down.

 

Taking an over-all view, the Stewards held him liable of not permitting his mount LORD COMMANDER to run on its merits in Race No.230. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 21st April, 2018 till the end of the Mumbai Meeting 2017/18 and not to grant him a Jockey’s license, if he applied for one, till 20th April, 2019.

RE : REQUEST FROM JOCKEY NEERAJ RAWAL TO RECONSIDER THE CHARGE OF UNSATISFACTORY RIDING - ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “TEMERITY” IN RACE NO.165 – THE DELTIN CASINOS ECLIPSE STAKES OF INDIA (GR.2) – RUN ON 10th FEBRUARY, 2018 –– THE STEWARDS HAD GRANTED A STAY ON THE ABOVE PUNISHMENT:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 3rd April, 2018 interviewed Jockey Neeraj Rawal and his Spokesman, Mr. Altamaash A. Ahmed, in the above connection and having considered all papers and proceedings before them and having heard Jockey Neeraj Rawal/his Spokesman and having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards regretted their inability to accede to his request.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal was informed that in view of the stay granted to him, the suspension of his Jockey’s license will now be w.e.f. 4th April, 2018 till the end of the Mumbai Meeting 2017/18.
