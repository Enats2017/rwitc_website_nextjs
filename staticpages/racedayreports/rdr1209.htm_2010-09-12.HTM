RACE NO.1 (141) - THE ENAKSI  PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 9 

 

Near 1200 metres, PHAROAH (Vikram Jodha) shifted in as a result SUZY WONG (AM Tograllu) was tightened and checked and dropped back and in turn hampered the running of MISCHEVIOUS Z (Dashrath Singh). Jockey Vikram Jodha has been fined Rs.2000/- or failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

POUND FOOLISH (App P Trevor) hung in during the race and near 600 metres shifted in and hampered the running of ITSPEARLOFRACING (S Sunil). Trainer MK Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, POUND FOOLISH & SUZY WONG were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO .2 (142) THE  NATIONALISTIC PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres. Runners - 7 

 

LIVING THE DREAM (B.Prakash) jumped out and veered to the outside. TARA (C.S.Jodha) was slow in the strides, losing about a length at the start.

 

Jockey C Rajendra (SUNLIGHT) has been reprimanded for shifting ground under the use of the whip in the final 200 metres.

 

After the race, SUNLIGHT was observed to have a streak of blood from the left nostril as such the filly was moved to the Sampling Yard and a communication was sent from the Sampling Yard to that effect and an announcement was made on the Public Address System that the filly had broken a blood vessel. On further examination in the Sampling Yard, it was revealed that the streak of blood was a result of a wound in the nostril which had freshened up and not due to the filly having broken a blood vessel. As such, the filly has not been subjected to any sanction as notified in the Racing Calendar for Bleeders. Also, SUNLIGHT was observed to have an abrasion on the right hind fetlock.

 

After the race, APACHE INDIAN was observed to be sound, having an abrasion on the left hind pastern. BORN SPECIAL had an abrasion on the right hind fetlock and TARA had a cut injury on the right fore bulb of heel.

RAJDOOT (S Zervan) hung out while negotiating the bend and in the early part of the straight. Trainer SS Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

RE: APPEAL AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF THE SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 12TH SEPTEMBER 2010 TO 16TH OCTOBER 2010 FOR FOUL RIDING ON HIS MOUNT “SEA TREASURE” IN RACE NO 46 RUN ON 7TH AUGUST 2010 – JOCKEY JE PEREIRA.

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 12th September 2010 and having considered all documents and papers that were submitted and having heard the submissions made by him and having deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds decided to reject his Appeal and forfeit the deposit. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands. In view of the stay granted to him on his punishment till Sunday, 12th September 2010, his punishment would now be w.e.f 13th September 2010 to 17th October 2010 (both days inclusive) (not permitted to ride work / mock race).

 

However, the Board of Appeal felt that the punishment meted out by the Stewards of the Club for foul riding in his case appeared to be lenient considering the gravity of the offence.

 

The Board therefore decided to issue a show-cause to him asking the Jockey to show-cause as to why punishment i.e., suspension of his jockey’s licence imposed on him by the Stewards of the Club should not be enhanced.

 

The Chairman of the Board of Appeal explained to him the abovementioned decision of the Board of Appeal in detail and informed him that he could take his own time to prepare his reply and appear before the Board of Appeal during the next week and accordingly, a meeting of the Board of Appeal could be fixed to hear him on the show-cause. However, he had indicated that he would like to reply to the show cause notice immediately and with the permission of the Chairman and consent of the Members present, he had made his submission in response to the show cause at the abovementioned meeting which was considered by the Board of Appeal.

 

Since his reply to the show-cause notice was not satisfactory, the Board decided to enhance the period of suspension of his jockey’s licence for a further period of 3 months i.e. w.e.f 18th October 2010 up to 17th January 2011 (both days inclusive) (not permitted to ride work / mock race). However, the incremental punishment of three months was decided to be kept pending as a suspended sentence for the next 3 years commencing from 18th October 2010 and if he was found guilty of a similar offence of foul riding during the aforesaid period of 3 years, then, in such event, the abovementioned suspended sentence of 3 months will be invoked and added on to any other punishment that may be imposed on him by the Stewards of the Club for that offence.

 

 

Pune: September 13, 2010.
