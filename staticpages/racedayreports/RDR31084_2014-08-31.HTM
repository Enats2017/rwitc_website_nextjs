RACE DAY REPORTS

FALSE RAILS WERE PLACED 8 METRES WIDE FROM 1100 METRES LEADING TO 900 METRES, 9 METRES WIDE AT 800 METRES, 10 METRES WIDE AT 600 METRES AND THEREAFTER 12 METRES WIDE TILL THE WINNING POST.  FALSE RAILS WERE ALSO PLACED FROM 1200 METRES CHUTE, WIDTH ABOUT 3 METRES.

 

THE STARTING GATES FOR THE1000 METRES RACES WERE POSITIONED AT APPROXIMATELY 1037 METRES AND FOR THE 1800 METRES RACE AT 1810 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1(97) – THE  GLASNOST PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 7 Runners

 

Approaching 200 metres, ANNABETH (S.G.Prasad) shifted-in and tightened CUT DIAMOND (P.Trevor). Jockey S.G.Prasad has been fined Rs.1,000/-. 

 

Trainer Altaf Hussain and Jockey P.Trevor were questioned following poor performance of CUT DIAMOND. The Jockey stated that his mount did not travel well from 400 metres onward and did not respond to his urgings. He further stated that, at his instance the Blinkers and Tongue Strap were used this time since the filly ran scared in her previous run. To another question, the Jockey stated that his instructions were since the Blinkers were used this time and she could be keen, so to ride her from the 2nd position. Trainer Altaf Hussain, when questioned  regarding the poor performance of CUT DIAMOND, he stated that both the runs of the filly at Pune have been disappointing. The Trainer cited possible two reasons for the poor performance of the filly, firstly, the Pune Monsoon Track may not have suited her and secondly, the distances of 1000 metres & 1200 metres in her two starts during the current Pune Meeting may be short for her. He added that he would try her over a longer distance after a gap. The explanation was noted.

 

After the race, CUT DIAMOND was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(98) – THE GRUEZI  PLATE (DIV.I) : (About) 1000 Metres – 11 Runners

 

Soon after the start, SUNSHINE HEART (App.Santosh G) shifted in and went on to GATES OF HEAVEN (D.A.Naik).

 

Approaching 800 metres, WINDS OF CHANGE (S.P.Ranjane) shifted ground inwards, due to which HIGHWAY EXPRESS (C.S.Jodha) was forced to shift out and hampered the running of SUNSHINE HEART (App.Santosh G) who lost position and in turn hampered HERZELIYAH (Sandeep Jadhav) who was following SUNSHINE HEART. Jockey S.P.Ranjane has been cautioned.

 

Jockey C.S.Jodha (HIGHWAY EXPRESS) reported that his mount did not respond to his urgings. The Sr.VO was requested to examine the mare and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

App. Yash Narredu (ZOE) reported that his mount lugged-in during the race. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

In the final stages of the race, WINDS OF CHANGE (S.P.Ranjane) was held up for a run when steadied in anticipation, while racing on the inside of GRAND MARCH (Nirmal Jodha) who shifted ground as MR MORRISON (R.K.Mahesh) lugged-in. Trainer M.Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (PASSION PRINCE) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that HERZELIYAH was examined at the stables on 01/09/2014 and found to be sore by the right fore leg. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, PASSION PRINCE was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(99) – THE  AEROBICA PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 9 Runners

 

At the start, SOUL CENTRIC (P.Trevor, dr.5) jumped awkwardly inwards on to THERE SHE GOES (I.Pasha, dr.4) who had also jumped awkwardly and went on to MARQUET (Malcolm Kharadi, dr.3).

 

Soon after the start, OCEAN DREAM (Dashrath Singh) who was drawn on the extreme outside, commenced to shift-in and carried GOLDEN LILLY (Neeraj Rawal) inwards, who tightened CENTAURUS (S.S.Baria), who in turn went on to SOUL CENTRIC (P.Trevor), and in the process, SOUL CENTRIC lost position. Jockey Dashrath Singh has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

Near 1000 metres, THERE SHE GOES (I.Pasha) was steadied and ridden in check as the filly raced keen and continued to race unsettled till passing 800 metres.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that after the race GOLDEN LILLY showed symptoms of distress in the paddock. First Aid treatment was given and the filly was sent to the Equine Hospital in the Horse Ambulance for further treatment.  Also, the filly was observed to have cut injuries at the left hind gaskin and a bruise on the forehead.

 

After the race, OCEAN DREAM was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.4(100) – THE  GENERAL RAJENDRASINHJI MILLION: (About) 1800 Metres – 3 Runners

 

Trainer Karthik G. and Jockey A.Sandesh were questioned regarding the running riding FLASHING CHIEFTAIN as the Jockey was observed to have made his move late in the straight and lost the race by a narrow margin. The instructions were to sit behind since this was a 3-horse race. The Trainer added that the Jockey had ridden according to his instructions and he was satisfied with the Jockey’s riding. The Jockey confirmed the Instructions and stated that he followed QUASAR throughout the race since that was the danger horse and he kept following QUASAR as it improved in the straight and then he made his challenge.

 

Not being satisfied with the explanation of the Jockey, the matter was reported to the Stewards as the Jockey was observed to have waited for too long in the straight to make his move. Consideration of the matter was deferred to the next meeting of the Stewards.

 

RACE NO.5(101) – THE DR.S.R.CAPTAIN TROPHY:  (About) 1400 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, REMEMBER ME (C.S.Jodha) jumped out awkwardly and then was inconvenienced.

 

Jockey S.Zervan (CARUSO) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Janardhan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, Jockey A.Sandesh (ICE ZONE) reported that the whip of Jockey S.Zervan (CARUSO) hit his arm near 400 metres. Since the incident was accidental, no action has been initiated in the matter.

After the race, CARUSO was found to be sound, but having cut injury at both the hind fetlocks and left hind bulb of heel. ICE ZONE was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(102) – THE B.K.LAGAD MEMORIAL GOLD TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – 14 Runners

 

MINE FOREVER  (S.Zervan) was reluctant to be stalled. NEPTUNE’S OCEAN  (App.K.N.Bhati) jumped out throwing its head thereby losing about 6 lengths at the start. Trainers P.Shroff and Mansoor Shah has been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, JOSEPHINE (Vinod Shinde, dr.7) drifted outwards due to which DIVINE SOLITAIRE (T.S.Jodha, dr.8) also shifted out and was awkwardly placed close to the heels of SOUND OF RIVER (I.Pasha, dr.9) and was steadied.

 

Passing 400 metres, RECKLESS (App.R.Ajinkya) shifted-in and made contact with ZEDA (A.Sandesh), who shifted in and in turn made contact with BLUE BAY (C.S.Jodha), who became unbalanced and shifted out and jostled with RECKLESS who was unbalanced and lost position and in turn hampered ICE SKATES (Akash Agarwal) who was following.

 

Approaching 200 metres, BLUE BAY (C.S.Jodha) shifted out and tightened JOSEPHINE (Vinod Shinde) on to RECKLESS (App.R.Ajinkya). Jockey C.S.Jodha has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Trainer P.Shroff when questioned with regard to the poor performance of MINE FOREVER. He stated that the filly was very excited in the Saddling Enclosure as well as on her way to the Starting Gates. He had instructed the Jockey to settle her as horses going to the front have not been performing on this day. After the start, the filly found herself in front and there was nothing much that the Jockey could do. Further, the track may not have suited the filly and therefore she may have performed poorly. The explanation was noted.

 

After the race, RECKLESS, MOUNTAIN SONG and MINE FOREVER were found to be sound. JOSEPHINE had lost its left hind plate.

RACE NO.8(104) – THE SONALIKA PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 15 Runners

Approaching 600 metres, SATURDAY AFFAIR (Sandeep Jadhav) clipped the heels of the front runners and stumbled.

 

Approaching 200 metres, BROWN DYNAMITE (Vikram Jodha) was steadied momentarily while racing between DURER (Akshay) and DYNA (App.Yash Narredu).

After the race, AFFIRMATION was observed to have an abrasion at both hind bulb of heel. SINDBAAD and  VENTURA were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.9(105) – THE AEROBICA PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 9 Runners

 

FEEL LIKE DANCING (S.Sunil) whipped around after the start and took no part in the race. Trainer Faisal A.Abbas has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Near 1000 metres, ACKER BILK (Ishwar Pardeshi) was eased off while racing between ETERNAL ROYALTY (P.Trevor) and BLEND OF ELEGANCE (D.K.Ashish).

 

Near 300 metres, EGYPTIANA (P. Kamlesh) was steadied in anticipation of TROPPA BELLA (A.Sandesh) shifting-in at that stage.

 

Jockey A.Sandesh (TROPPA  BELLA) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

APACHE (S.Zervan) hung-out in the straight and finished the race along the outer rails. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures and show the gelding to the satisfaction of Stipendiary Stewards in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey D.K.Ashish (BLEND OF ELEGANCE) reported the loss of whip near 150 metres.

 

BLEND OF ELEGANCE (D.K.Ashish) lugged-out in the straight. Trainer S.K.Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, SWEET AS HONEY was observed to have a cut injury at the left fore cannon. ACKER BILK and APACHE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.10(106) – THE GRUEZI PLATE (DIV.II): About) 1000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, NEW STAR (App.Akshay Gaikwad) jumped out awkwardly and tried to drift outwards. OCEAN STAR (Akash Agarwal) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Trainer Rehanulah Khan and Jockey V.Chandrakant were questioned regarding the running and riding of VIVA CAPRI. The Instructions were to be in 3rd/4th/5th position and to ride out in the straight whilst tapping with the whip. The Jockey was observed to have lost his position in the middle stages of the race with no effort to improve his position and did not tap the horse whilst riding out in the straight as the mare observed to be running on. The Trainer admitted that the Jockey should have tried to improve his position but also opined that the mare could not have finished any better. The Trainer added that the mare is not a sound horse and has her issues and was operated on both her knees during the just concluded Mumbai Meeting.

 

Whilst noting the explanation, Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to ensure that his horses run to the best of their ability and Jockey V.Chandrakant has been severely cautioned for failing to ride out his mount.       

 

After the race, NEW STAR was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding  has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, OCEAN STAR was found to be sound, but having a cut injury at the right hind cannon. MUMBAI KING, BETHLEHEM, WAVES OF MAGIC and VIVA CAPRI were found to be sound.

RE : APPEAL FROM MR.S.SHAIKH AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB – ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF ch m “MATRIOSHKA” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.359 – THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY (DIV.II) RUN ON 26TH APRIL, 2014 – POSITIVE FOR “16B-HYDROXYSTANOZOLOL, A METABOLITE OF STANOZOLOL” :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 29th August, 2014 considered the Appeal preferred by Mr.S.Shaikh. The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted, including his Grounds of Appeal and having considered the submissions made by him and his Spokesman, Mr.Usman Rangeela, deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds, decided to reject his appeal and forfeit his deposit. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands.

RE : APPEAL FROM TRAINER MANSOOR SHAH AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB TO SUSPEND HIS TRAINER’S LICENSE W.E.F. 20TH AUGUST, 2014 TILL THE END OF MUMBAI MEETING 2014/15 AND THEREAFTER NOT TO GRANT HIM A TRAINER’S LICENSE IF HE APPLIED FOR THE SAME, TILL 19TH AUGUST, 2016 FOR BEING GUILTY OF DISHONEST, CORRUPT, FRAUDULENT, IMPROPER PRACTICE & DISHONOURABLE ACTION IN CONNECTION WITH RACING – ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “TUSCANIA” IN RACE NO.237 – THE WASHINGTON IRVINE PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 6TH MARCH, 2014 :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 29th August, 2014 considered the Appeal preferred by Tr.Mansoor Shah.

The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted, including his Grounds of Appeal and considered the submissions made by him and his spokesman, Mr. Pervez Andhyarujina. During the hearing  Mr.Pervez Andhyarujina, produced a certificate given by Dr H.Mirza and also stated that he would like to bring Dr.Mirza before the Board as his witness on another day since Dr Mirza could not make it convenient to appear before the Board on this day. Since there was fresh evidence forthcoming, the Board decided to remand the case back to the Stewards of the Club. In the meantime, the punishment meted out to Trainer Mansoor Shah by the Stewards of the Club was stayed until the matter is considered by the Stewards of the Club and his deposit was ordered to be refunded. 

--------------------------

 

RE : APPEAL RECEIVED FROM JOCKEY A.SANDESH AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENCE FOR 30TH AUGUST, 2014 FOR INCOMPETENT RIDING ON HIS MOUNT “FLASHING CHIEFTAIN” IN RACE NO.31 – THE AUGUST HANDICAP RUN ON 3RD AUGUST, 2014 :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 29th August, 2014 considered the Appeal preferred by Jockey A.Sandesh. The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted, including his Grounds of Appeal and considered the submissions made by him and his Spokesman, Mr.M.K.Jadhav. The Board of Appeal was convinced that there was a technical error on the part of the Stewards to reopen the case without fresh evidence forthcoming and therefore the decision taken by the Stewards is illegal and void. The Board of Appeal having deliberated on the matter and having applied their minds decided not to go into the merit of the case and to allow the appeal and return the deposit.
