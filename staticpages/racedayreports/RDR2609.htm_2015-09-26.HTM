RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RIDING OF JOCKEY SANDEEP JADHAV ON HIS MOUNT “SPEED BABY” IN RACE NO.268 – THE ROMAN ROSE PLATE RUN ON THURSDAY, 19TH MARCH, 2015  AND HIS ASSOCIATION WITH A WARNED OFF PERSON – JOCKEY SANDEEP JADHAV WAS SUSPENDED PENDING ENQUIRY :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 12th September, 2015 interviewed Jockey Sandeep Jadhav and his Spokesman, Mr.Dallas Todywalla as also Trainer H.J.Antia. Having considered all papers and proceedings before them and having heard the submissions made by Jockey Sandeep Jadhav / his Spokesman as also Trainer H.J.Antia and having viewed the video of Race No.268, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Sandeep Jadhav and held him guilty of not permitting his mount SPEED BABY to run on its merits in Race No.268 and for being in touch with a warned off person. The Stewards noted that his Jockey’s license had been suspended w.e.f. 20th March, 2015 to 30th April, 2015 and he has not been granted a Jockey’s license for the Racing Season 2015/16. Whilst contemplating on the penalty, the Stewards considered the period of 6 months that Jockey Sandeep Jadhav has already suffered. Therefore, the Stewards decided to grant him a Jockey’s license for the Racing Season 2015/16 with immediate effect but it shall stand suspended up to and

 

inclusive of 30th October, 2015. Further, the Stewards decided to permit him to ride work with immediate effect

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “MYSTIC ROMANCE” IN RACE NO.96-THE Dr.P.K.SARDESHMUKH MAHARAJ TROPHY-DIV-I, RUN ON SUNDAY, 30TH AUGUST, 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Nirad Karanjawala in the above connection. He stated that MYSTIC ROMANCE had  decent racing form to go by, however, after her last run in Mumbai on the 15th of February 2015 the filly started getting tied up, hence he could not race the filly for 6 months. Thereafter, the filly recovered gradually and  she was kept on light work, she was only cantering and had one or two pace works and then raced on the 15th August 2015 in a Class III race. He explained that the filly  may have benefited after her first run at Pune and in the race in question on 30th August 2015 the filly ran in a lower class and one fancied runner was withdrawn and the favourite ROMAN FLAME ran a poor race, further the remaining field was weak, however, he had not fancied the filly.

 

Trainer Nirad Karanjawala’s attention was drawn to the Calendar Notification and also that his filly had shown improvement of over 12 lengths vis-a-vis the common horse, CHLOBUD in only 15 days time, to which he concurred, however, he reiterated that he had not fancied the filly and she was at longer odds.

 

After going through the Log Books, watching both the races and even considering that the filly was at longer odds on both occasions, Trainer Nirad Karanjawalla has been fined Rs.10,000/- for contravening of the Calendar Notification “Re : Comparative Performance of Horses winning within a short span of time:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “NAUMEE” IN RACE NO.106-THE RADIO ONE RUN,  RUN ON SATURDAY, 5TH SEPTEMBER, 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Nirad Karanjawala and Jockey S.Sunil  in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Nirad Karanjawala stated that this was only the second run of the filly and he had instructed the Jockey to take a good jump and sit well up and to do his best.  He said that the filly did not get an opening in the straight and thereafter the Jockey again rode her out and finished  fifth. He felt that in the absence of not getting an opening, at best, the filly could have finished third or fourth. He reiterated that this was only the second run of the filly and she was showing improvement after her first run. The Jockey confirmed the instructions and added that he received interference in the straight and thereafter he rode out and did his best.

 

After hearing the submissions made by the Trainer and the Jockey and considering that this was only the second run of the filly and she was still a maiden and that she had received interference in the straight and was at longer odds, the Stipendiary Stewards  decided to only caution the Trainer and the Jockey and informed them that a close watch would be kept on the future performance of the filly as they felt that the Jockey could have been more vigorous in the straight.

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Nirad Kranjawala and Jockey S.Zervan in the above connection. The video of the race in question as also the previous run of the horse were viewed and when questioned, Jockey S.Zervan stated that his instructions were to try and go to the front. The Trainer in his letter dated 17th September 2015 pointed out that the horse’s first run at Pune was over five furlongs where he was rushed to the front and he ran a poor race. Thereafter he had run the horse over six furlongs

 

where he got to go to the front comfortably without being hassled and gave up towards the end and finished second. After this run, the owner had asked him to again run the gelding over five furlongs as he felt that six furlongs was too long for the horse.

After hearing the submissions made by the Trainer and Jockey and going through the Work Log Book, the Stipendiary Stewards decided to caution the Trainer and informed him that a close watch would be kept on the future performance of the gelding and he was warned for the inconsistent running of his horses keeping in mind the common horse JOSHUA and Jockey S.Zervan was fined Rs.5,000/- for not riding out till the end of the race.

After hearing the submissions of the Trainer and the Jockey who had reported that the horse had wobbled which is evident in the video, viewing the video of both the races and considering the letter from the Trainer, the Stipendiary Stewards decided not to initiate any action in the matter. However, the Trainer was informed that a close watch would be kept on the future performance of the horse.

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNIN AND RIDING OF “SHE IS SPECIAL” IN RACE NO.132-THE JUVENTUS TROPHY DIV_I, RUN ON SUNDAY 13th SEPTEMBER 2015.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Altaf Hussain and Jockey Kishore Kadam in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Altaf Hussain stated that he had instructed the Jockey to take a good jump and sit 3rd ,4th or 5th  and to do his best this was confirmed by the jockey. The Trainer further stated the he had first requested Jockey T.S.Jodha to ride the filly however he declined the ride as he had to ride his stable runner NEVER SURRENDER in the same race, hence he declared Kishore Kadam on SHE IS SPECIAL. When questioned about his observation of the race, Trainer Altaf Hussain stated that the filly threw her head up at the start. Jockey Kishore Kadam stated that  the filly jumped out awkwardly and was throwing her head and he had lost his right  stirrup after a couple of strides.

 

Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Kishore Kadam, the matter was reported to the Stewards as the Stipendiary Stewards believed that the Jockey had not allowed the horse to jump out on an even keel.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 26th September, 2015 interviewed Trainer Altaf Hussain and Jockey Kishore Kadam and having heard them and viewing the video of the Race No.132 as also the previous run of the filly i.e. Race No.315 run during the Mumbai Meeting 2014/15, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Kishore Kadam and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on his mount. The Stewards noted that SHE IS SPECIAL was a fancied runner and Jockey Kishore Kadam was instructed to sit in 3rd/4th/5th position. However, he has not allowed the filly to jump out on an even keel and was lying last and thereafter

 

he did not make sufficient efforts to improve his position and sit closer. Also, his statement that he had lost his right stirrup soon after jump out, was not borne out on the video recording. Having viewed the previous run of the filly, it was noted that the filly took a fair start and did not show any erratic behavior on jump out.

 

Taking an over all view, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 11th October, 2015 till the end of Pune Meeting 2015 and for 15th November, 2015 for unsatisfactory riding on his mount (permitted to ride work/mock race).
