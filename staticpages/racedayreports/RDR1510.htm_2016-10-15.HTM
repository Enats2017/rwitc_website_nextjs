ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

RE: APPEAL FROM JOCKEY P.TREVOR AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 18th SEPTEMBER 2016 TO 25th SEPTEMBER 2016 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR RIDING AN INJUDICIOUS RACE ON “MARISKA” - ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNING AND RIDING OF “MARISKA” IN RACE NO. 68 – THE GOLDEN EDITION PLATE (DIV-II) – RUN ON 27th AUGUST 2016:

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 20th September 2016 considered the captioned Appeal. The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted and viewed the video recording of the Race several times, in normal as well as in slow motion and heard all the submissions made by Jockey P. Trevor in the matter.

 

The Board of Appeal was of the opinion that it was only the 2nd run of MARISKA in her career and she lacked experience and also the filly raced with Pacifiers for the first time. The Board felt that Jockey P. Trevor’s riding was not mala fide and that the filly had responded after he had put the whip down. The Board was of the unanimous view that he rode his best and did not agree with the Stewards’ finding that he rode an injudicious race. Therefore, the Board of Appeal, unanimously decided to allow the appeal and set aside the decision of the Stewards of the Club. His Deposit for the Appeal was ordered to be refunded.

RE: APPEAL FROM JOCKEY J.CHINOY AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 25th SEPTEMBER 2016 TO 21st OCTOBER 2016 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR DANGEROUS RIDING ON “GREGORIAN CHANTS” – STEWARDS’ ENQUIRY IN RACE NO. 107– THE RAMBUS PLATE (DIV-II) – RUN ON 10th SEPTEMBER, 2016:

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 20th September 2016 considered the captioned Appeal. The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted and viewed the video recording of the Race 107 and that of the interference several times, in normal as well as slow motion, and heard all the submissions made in the matter.

After having deliberated at length and having applied their minds to the entire facts of the case, the Board of Appeal, by majority, decided to reject the Appeal. Therefore, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands. His Deposit fee for the Appeal was forfeited.

RE : MERCY PETITION FROM JOCKEY J.CHINOY AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 25/09/2016 TO 21/10/2016 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR DANGEROUS RIDING ON “GREGORIAN CHANTS” – STEWARDS ENQUIRY IN RACE NO.107 – THE RAMBUS PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 10TH SEPTEMBER, 2016 :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 12th October, 2016 considered Mercy Petition from Jockey J. Chinoy in respect of the captioned subject matter.

 

The Board of Appeal having studied all documents and papers that were submitted and having heard the submissions made by Jockey J. Chinoy and having deliberated on the matter at length, unanimously decided to grant him mercy with immediate effect and he was free to accept rides on the Derby weekend. However, the Board of Appeal resolved to issue a note of caution to Jockey J. Chinoy that he should not flaunt rules and must ride without causing interference. Further, he must endeavour not to come before the Stewards of the Club for similar riding.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “MYRTLEWOOD” IN RACE NO.40 - THE EVE CHAMPION TROPHY (GR.3) (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUN ON 14th AUGUST, 2016 :

 

Trainer P. Shroff and Jockey P. S. Chouhan were questioned immediately after the race in the above connection. Jockey P. S. Chouhan stated that the filly was slightly slowly away and he immediately tried to catch up with the field, at which stage the filly got keen and he had to then try and settle her and it was unfortunate and under the circumstances, the filly ran a poor race.

 

When questioned, Tr. P. Shroff said that ideally he would have liked the filly to be settled alongside JIGSAW, however, as the filly was slowly away, “all the planning and strategy went up in the air” and the Jockey had to improve and ride the filly to the best of his ability. To a question, Tr. P. Shroff said that THE UNICORN is a genuine horse and likes galloping in front and he was definitely not expecting this result.

 

After reviewing all angles of the race in question as also the previous races of MYRTLEWOOD, Trainer P. Shroff was again questioned and it was brought to his attention that even though the filly was slightly slowly away, keeping in mind her past races where she has always come from the back, the Stipendiary Stewards felt that the Jockey need not have got her keen and later fought with her and then again try to improve, which led to her poor performance. Trainer P. Shroff agreed with the Stipes view and said that in future he will tell his Jockeys that in case the filly is slowly away then not to do what happened in this particular race.

 

Since the Club’s V.O. reported that MYRTLEWOOD had returned sore after the race, no action was initiated by the Stipendiary Stewards.
