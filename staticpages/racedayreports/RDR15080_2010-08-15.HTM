RACE NO 1 (69) - THE  SEVERRIANO PLATE :  (About) 1600 Metres. Runners - 5

 

PERFECT MOMENT (D.K.Ashish) was fractious and reluctant to be stalled and stumbled at jump out. AVON CREEK (Neeraj Rawal) was slow in the stride, losing about 4 lengths at the start. Trainers Imtiaz A.Sait and Dallas Todywalla have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and to show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, LIGHT MY WAY was found to be sound, having lower gum injury and PERFECT MOMENT had lost its left fore plate

 

RACE NO 2  (70) - THE  GAEA PLATE  : (About) 1600 Metres. Runners – 6

 

DESTINED PRINCE was withdrawn on Compassionate Grounds.

 

VICTOR IVANOV hung-in in the straight. Trainer Suresh Chavan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, ALWAYS & VICTOR IVANOV were found to be sound. ALWAYS had an abrasion on the right hind fetlock.

 

RACE NO 3 (71) – THE  SAINT EMILLION PLATE: (About)  1000 Metres.  Runners - 14

 

After being stalled, RIO DEL RAY (App.A.Sandesh) kept rearing up in the stall and took an awkward jump thereby losing about 2 lengths at the start. Trainer Vijay Kasbekar has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

About 100 metres after the jump, as ASHWA PRAHAR (Imran Chisty) came in on to the rails, HICKORY (B.Prakash) was steadied by its rider causing ORION STAR (J.Pradeep) to shift in, who in turn went onto MISCHEVIOUS Z (Suraj Narredu) who was hampered along the rails. Since the incident was accidental, no action has been taken again anyone.

 

Jockey N.S.Parmar (RISING GLORY) reported that at about 700 metres GOLDEN HEART (A.Imran Khan) shifted out and bumped him. Jockey A.Imran Khan has been severely cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.  

 

At the best of the Stipendiary Stewards, a Stewards’ Enquiry was held in this race. The reason being, near 200 metres, ENTICEMENT (C.S.Jodha, unplaced), shifted out due to which MACHO UNO (S.Sunil, placed first)  went on to WILD IMAGINATION (Dashrath Singh, placed fourth) who in turn shifted out and bumped CANDY FLOSS (C.Rajendra) who got unbalanced and fell, dislodging the Jockey. Due to the above, ORION STAR (J.Pradeep) who was racing right behind CANDY FLOSS, collided and fell. After considering the submissions made by Jockeys C.S.Jodha, S.Sunil and Dashrath Singh, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided that no change in the order of placing was called for. 

 

After the race, ENTICEMENT, RISING GLORY, GOLDEN HEART, CANDY FLOSS, ORION STAR, WILD IMAGINATION, RIO DEL RAY & MACHO UNO were found to be sound. WILD IMAGINATION had an abrasion on the left hind pastern and CANDY FLOSS had a bruise on the right nostril.

RACE NO 4 (72) – THE HIGHLAND RULE TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres. Runners - 7

 

ECSTATICA (Dashrath Singh) took an awkward jump.

 

CAIRNDHU (P.Kamlesh) was slow in the strides, losing about a length at the start.

 

The saddle on WEIZHOU (Suraj Narredu) was observed to have shifted to the front. The same was also reported by the Jockey. As such, Trainer Faisal A.Abbas has been fined Rs.2000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

After the race, CABRIOLET was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 5 (73) – THE SUB-AREA COMMANDER’S TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1200  Mts. Runners - 8

 

ASTON MARTIN was withdrawn on Compassionate Grounds.

 

GLORIA (A.M.Tograllu) took an awkward jump throwing her head up. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after start, SEA DIAMOND (S.P.Ranjane) shifted out and bumped RAJASTHAN ROYALS (Suraj Narredu) who in turn went on to NOTABILITY (A.Imran Khan), unbalancing both horses. Jockey S.P.Ranjane stated that his horse was lugging in and in trying to control his mount, the horse shifted out. Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.P.Ranjane, he has been fined Rs.3000/- for not making sufficient efforts to control his mount.

 

VISIONAIRE (D.K.Ashish) hung-in throughout the race and went onto NOTABILITY (A.Imran Khan) at the bend, unbalancing the latter. Trainer S.K.Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, VISIONAIRE & GLORIA were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 6  (74) - THE  PRA INDEPENDENCE MILLION:  (About) 2000 Metres.   Runners - 15

 

SATSUMA STAR (D.K.Ashish) maintained an erratic course from 600 metres causing inconvenience to SOLAR ANGEL (Imran Chisty) on a couple of occasions. Further, on entering the straight, Jockey D.K.Ashish (SATSUMA STAR) forced his way out and came onto WILNA nearly dislodging the rider. As such, Jockey D.K.Ashish (SATSUMA STAR) was reported to the Stewards for Dangerous Riding. The matter was deferred for next week.

 

After the race, WILNA was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The filly also had an abrasion on the right hind fetlock and right hind bulb of heel. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

After the race, KALINGA STAR was observed to have an abrasion on the right hind canon. SATSUMA STAR & BARBARA were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 7 (75) – THE  SUB-AREA COMMANDER’S TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1200  Mts. Runners - 7

 

At the request of Trainer H.J.Antia, SEA WONDER was examined at 0800 hours on 14th Aug ‘10 and was found to be sore. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

ROMASHKINSKAYA (Dashrath Singh) ran a detached last, throughout the race. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures.  

 

The Sr.V.O  reported that after the race STRONG FOUNDATION stopped suddenly on the track. On examination, the filly was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The filly was sent to the Equine Hospital in the Horse Ambulance for further management. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, JALSA was observed to have a broken tooth of lower jaw.

 

RACE NO 8 (76) - THE  SCARLET EMPEROR PLATE:   (About)  1200 Metres. Runners - 13

 

TRANQUILITY was withdrawn on compassionate grounds.

 

On start, EMERALD VALLEY (Ladjadj Stephane) jumped out awkwardly and bumped HEIRESS (P. Kamlesh). Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures and show the gelding to the Starter in morning gates before next accepting to race.

Travelling further, SET AFIRE (Neeraj Rawal) lugged out, taking out HEIRESS (P.Kamlesh) who was on his quarters, who in turn went out on to EMERALD VALLEY (Ladjadj Stephane) who came in because of the close proximity of STAR INVITATION (Satish Nayak) on his outside. In order to maintain their respective positions, they bumped into each other. Since the incident was accidental, no action has been initiated.

After the race, SCARLETTE was observed to have a cut injury on the right hind pastern.

 

RACE NO 9 (77) – THE  EAU DE VIE PLATE:   (About)  1000 Metres. Runners - 11

 

KISS AWAY (D.S.Rathore) played up in the stall. The mare was taken out of the stall and examined by the Club’s Sr.V.O. who certified her fit to race. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

BLAZING SPECTACLE (App.Kavraj Singh, dr.5) and CROWNED PRINCESS (D.K.Ashish, dr.6) jumped out awkwardly bumped each other and thereafter CROWNED PRINCESS went across BLESSINGS (L.Prashant)  who was inconvenienced and had to be checked by its rider. Jockey D.K.Ashish has been severely cautioned.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the improved performance of FLAMES OF FIRE (S.P.Ranjane) and a report will be submitted shortly. 

 

After the race, TRACK THUNDER was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FLAMES OF FIRE was found to be sound.

 

The post race urine sample of the winner of 4th, 6th, 7th & 8th race viz. ECSTATICA, FLOODS OF FORTUNE, CAUSTIC FINALE & SCARLETTE, as also that of SACRED RELIC & SECRET MAGIC (6th race) and the special samples of LIGHT MY WAY (1st race) and CABRIOLET (4th race), were collected for detection of prohibited substance.
