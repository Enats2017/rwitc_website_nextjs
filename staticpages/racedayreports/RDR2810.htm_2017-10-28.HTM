RACE NO.1(179) - THE ARTOIS PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres - 9 Runners

 

Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been fined Rs.5,000/- for failing to declare his charge JULIANO in this race after having accepted with him.

 

FROSTED (Raghuveer Singh) took a fly jump.  ISINIT (K. Nazil) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Passing the 1200 metres, LILAC TIME (Dashrath Singh) shifted in tightening CENTAURUS (App. M. Ayyar) along the rails who had to be checked thereby dropping back and inconveniencing SHIVALIK SKIES (Amyn Merchant). Jockey Dashrath Singh has been cautioned.

 

After the race, TAKE FIVE was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind cannon. LILAC TIME was found to be sound in the trot.

 

RACE NO.2(180) - THE NITROUS PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres - 8 Runners

 

GLORIOUS EYES (C. S. Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Passing the 600 metres, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (ZENON) shifted out thereby tightening GLORIOUS OPINION (Amyn Merchant) and inconveniencing JAGER BOMB (J. Chinoy). Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

RACE NO.3(181) – THE RED DRAGON PLATE (DIV.II): (About)  1000 Metres -  16 Runners

 

Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been fined Rs.1,000/- for accepting with his charge JULIANO in two races on the same day. As per the General Conditions published in the Prospectus, the horse was struck out from this race and accepted to run in THE ARTOIS PLATE.

 

ARC OF PASSION (Bhawani Singh) jumped out slow losing about 3 lengths at the start.

 

 

At the start, OLIVIA KASPEN (Nirmal Jodha, dr. 5) jumped outwards on to SARRAZIN (Amyn Merchant, dr. 6).

 

After the race, PROMINENCE was observed to have an abrasion at the left hind pastern and ALFONSO had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.4(182) – THE GENERAL RAJENDRASINHJI MILLION: (About)  1600 Metres - 5 Runners

 

At the start, BATELEUR (A. Imran Khan, dr. 1) jumped outwards on to GALLANT KNIGHT (Y. S. Srinath, dr. 2).

 

Passing the 300 metres, Jockey A. Imran Khan (BATELEUR) was observed to have lost his whip. This being his first offence, no action has been initiated.

 

ORION’S BELT (J. Chinoy) was placed fifth by the Judge, in this race.

 

RACE NO.5(183) – THE MAYOR BABURAO SANAS MEMORIAL MILLION powered by SRS Group: (About) 1000 Metres - 4 Runners

 

Soon after the start, DESERT PRIDE (Suraj Narredu) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening ICONIA (Neeraj Rawal) and ART MANOEUVRE (Dashrath Singh). After the race, on questioning, Jockey Suraj Narredu stated that being the first run of the filly she was green and was lugging in throughout the race.

 

After the race, ART MANOEUVRE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(184) – THE DELHI RACE CLUB TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres - 10 Runners

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that it was observed in the Saddling Area that the right hind plate on DEEP DIVER was splayed and the gelding was sore by the right hind leg. As such, DEEP DIVER was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

REMEMBER ME (App. S. A. Amit) SUPER SUNSHINE (Bhawani Singh) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths and 3 lengths respectively.

 

Soon after the start, NASCAR (App. Raghuveer Singh) shifted in and went across ABBEY (Suraj Narredu) forcing the latter to check and shift out thereby inconveniencing LADISLAUS (Neeraj Rawal) and BRYNHILL (App. Santosh G.). Apprentice Raghuveer Singh has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 900 metres, BRYNHILL (App. Santosh G.) shifted in inconveniencing UNDISPUTED (Dashrath Singh) and CHAMPAGNE (P. S. Chouhan) respectively.

 

Passing the 300 metres, BRYNHILL (App. Santosh G.) shifted in thereby tightening UNDISPUTED (Dashrath Singh) who had to be checked. Thereafter, BRYNHILL (App. Santosh G.) further shifted in thereby inconveniencing LADISLAUS (Neeraj Rawal). After the race, on questioning, Jockey Santosh G. stated that his mount hung in despite his efforts. He further added that the gelding has a tendency to hang-in, in view of this Trainer Nazak Chenoy has been asked to show her charge in a Mock Race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards before next accepting to race.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that BRYNHILL was examined at the stables in the morning on 29/10/2017 and was found to be sound in the trot.

 

RACE NO.7(185) – THE ARTOIS PLATE (DIV.I): (About)  1400 Metres– 11 Runners

 

KOAL (App. S. A. Amit) planted in the stall and took no part in the race. In view of above and a similar behavior of the mare in her last run, Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

AEOLIUS MAXIMUS (J. Chinoy) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, BIDSTONE HILL (S. S. Baria, dr. 11) jumped and shifted out and had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 1000 metres, MARVEL (App. R. Ajinkya), SUMAAQ (C. S. Jodha) and AQUARIUS (App. S. Joseph) were inconvenienced due to a tightly packed field.

 

Passing the 300 metres, WIND CRAFT (Bhawani Singh) shifted in tightening DIVINE MAGIC (Y. S. Srinath) along the rails, forcing the latter to be checked. After the race, on questioning, Jockey Bhawani Singh stated that his mount shied inwards due to the close proximity of TIMLESS DEEDS (T. S. Jodha). Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

After the race, WIND CRAFT and BIDSTONE HILL (examined on 29/10/2017) were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(186) - THE GOLFING PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres - 12 Runners

 

After being stalled, CLOUDBURST (Raghuveer Singh) and MAN OF WORD (A. Imran Khan) were restless in the stall and not allowing their riders to settle. Trainers Narendra Lagad and S. K. Sunderji have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

CLOUDBURST (A. Imran Khan) and IN VINO VERITAS (S. Jaykumar) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths each at the start.

 

At the start COSA NOSTRA (Bhawani Singh, dr.4) jumped inwards on to MAN OF WORD (A. Imran Khan, dr.3)

 

An inquiry has been initiated into the improved performance of COSA NOSTRA (Bhawani Singh) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, SANDALPHON and STAR OF THEA were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.9(187) - THE RED DRAGON PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres - 17 Runners

 

COMMANDPERFORMANCE (SandeepJadhav) jumped out slow losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainer Dallas Todywall has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start OUTSTANDING (A. Imran Khan, dr.11) jumped outwards on to ALYATIES (P. S. Chouhan, dr.12)

 

Soon after the start, ADAM’S CREATION (Satish Nayak) shifted in and made contact with MAGICAL SCRIPT (J. Chinoy) thereafter BEE QUIRKY (App. M. Ayyar) shifted in under urgings thereby bumping ADAM’S CREATION (Satish Nayak) who in turn shifted in and made contact with MAGICAL SCRIPT (J. Chinoy). Apprentice M. Ayyar has been cautioned.

 

Soon after the start, OUTSTANDING (A. Imran Khan) shifted in thereby starting a chain reaction due to which MOIRA (App. S. A. Amit), ADELE (Santosh. G) and HIGHLAND PRINCESS (App. Raghuveer Singh) were tightened and had to be checked. Jockey A. Imran Khan has been fined Rs. 2,000/-.

 

Passing the 800 metres, WHOOPSIDAISY (F. Irani) shifted out thereby inconveniencing MAKE IT SHINE (C. S. Jodha) and ROYAL EYES (App. Raj Pawar). Jockey F. Irani has been cautioned.

ADDITIONAL REPORT:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO A COMPLAINT MADE AGAINST APP. K. NAZIL BY TRAINER HOSIDAR DAJI

On a complaint received from Trainer Hosidar Daji against App. K. Nazil regarding irregularity and indiscipline at work, an enquiry was held into the same. After having questioned App. K. Nazil, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered. Taking into account that this is not the first such complaint made against App. K. Nazil, and also that he has been warned against such behavior on several occasions, his Apprentice Jockey’s license has been suspended for two race days i.e. from 19th November, 2017 to 24th November, 2017 (both days inclusive).

Pune : 3rd November, 2017
