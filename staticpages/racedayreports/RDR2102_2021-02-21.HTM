RACE NO.1(130) –THE BOLD MARCH PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  11   

 

At the request of Jockey K Nazil,  his mount ETERNAL DANCER was examined by the Club’s Sr.V.O. who certified the mare fit to race.

 

At the start, ON VA DANSER (App.P.S.Kaviraj, dr.7) jumped outwards and at the same time TANHAIYAAN (P.S.Chouhan,drawn-8) jumped inwards and they made contact.

 

Approaching the 150 metres, FLYING YODA (App Aniket Karande) was observed to have veered outwards thereby bumping and carrying GABBRIEL (T S Jodha) on to ETERNAL DANCER ( K Nazil). The Sr.V.O. reported that FLYING YODA stopped galloping just before the winning post.. The gelding was sore by the left fore leg and not able to bear weight on that leg. On X-Ray, it was found that there was a fracture of sesamoid bone, left fore. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey C S Jodha (PRIDE’S ANGEL) reported that his mount leaned inwards. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that PRIDE’S ANGEL was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SAFDAR was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(131) –THE PICASSO PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  5  

 

At the start, VENEZUELA (App P S Kaviraj) was observed to have jumped out awkwardly due to which App P S Kaviraj lost his stirrup which he regained shortly.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey P.S.Chouhan,  the rider of TIGRIO (Placed Second) against MAKHTOOB (C.S.Jodha, Placed First). The reason being,  in the last 300 metres, MAKHTOOB shifted out and took his ground. Further, in the last 100 metres, MAKHTOOB came on to him, bumped and unbalanced his horse, due to which he lost a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing as under:-

 

TIGRIO                                                  -               FIRST;                   VENEZUELA                        -               THIRD;

MAKHTOOB                                        -               SECOND;             ATHULYA                             -               FOURTH

 

Ater the race, Jockey C S Jodha (MAKHTOOB) reported that his mount hung out despite his efforts. Trainer S K Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures and show the gelding in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MAKHTOOB was trotted and found to be sound.

RACE NO.6(135) –THE HUNT FOR GOLD PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  12

 

SUPREME BEING (Raghuveer Singh) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Ivor Fernandes has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials,  before next accepting to race.

 

Soon at the start, ABOUT THE CLOUD (App.M.S.Deora) shifted out thereby starting a chain reaction, due to which DIVIJA (R Ajinkya), LA PEREGRINA (App.Kirtish Bhagat) and GALLOPING STAR (Vishal N Bunde)  were inconvenienced. App M S Deora was cautioned.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, ABOUT THE CLOUD (App M S Deora) shifted out thereby inconveniencing DIVIJA (R.Ajinkya).

 

Passing the 800 metres,  DOWSABEL (App A S Peter) shifted in due to which LA PEREGRINA (App.Kirtish Bhagat) shied inwards and collided with the railing.

 

After the race, LA PEREGRINA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

App Kirtish Bhagat has been fined Rs. 500/- for failing to dismount despite being told to do so since his mount LA PEREGRINA had bled .

 

After the race, SUPREME BEING was trotted and found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE BREAKING DOWN OF ch f “NOBLE QUEEN” (Yash Narredu) IN RACE NO..66 – THE GOOL & SOLI POONAWALLA INDIAN 1000 GUINEAS (Gr.1) RUN ON SUNDAY, 3RD JANUARY, 2021 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 21st February, 2021 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. The Stewards considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Tr.Deepseh Narredu/his spokesperson  Ms Anita Khalakdina at length. They also heard Jockey Yash Narredu who rode NOBLE QUEEN in Race No.66 as also Dr.P.T.Khambatta, the Equine Hospital Vets and the Stipendiary Stewards and having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards were  not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Tr.Deepesh Narredu and held him guilty of gross negligence in the management of his duties as a Trainer. In that, by his own admission, he had taken an X-Ray of the both fore legs of the filly on 24th  December, 2020 but did not see the X-Ray nor did he show the same to any practicing Vet and seek his view/advice before racing the filly in Race No.66. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Trainer’s licence for a period of 1 month i.e. w.e.f. 8th March, 2021 to 7th April, 2021 (both days inclusive) and to severely caution him for the future.

 

Further, as regards not maintaining his Medication Log Books properly, the Stewards noted that he had accepted his guilt and apologized for the same in front of them, hence they decided to fine him a sum of Rs.10,000/- for the same and warned him for the future.
