RACE NO 4. (329) THE JEHAN NUMA PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres - Runners – 8

 

STAR PRINCE (T.S.Jodha) was reluctant to be reach  the start  and had to be sent  in the opposite direction. Trainer Vinayak has been asked to school his charge to proceed to the start before next accepting with him to race.

 

JEN’S GLORY (App.A.Sandesh) was restless in the stall, not allowing the rider to settle. Trainer Altaf Hussain has ben asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Near 300 metres, FREANY (P.S.Chouhan) clipped the hind heels of OCEAN OF FAITH (D.K.Ashish) and stumbled.

 

Trainer Vinayak and Jockey I.Pasha were questioned with regard to the running and riding of MOONLIGHT BEAUTY. The instructions to the Jockey were to be in 4th or 5th position and to ride out hands and heels in the straight as the filly does not like whip. It was observed that Jockey I.Pasha had not ridden out the horse vigorously in the straight, as such the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards after the last race, interviewed Trainer Vinayak and Jockey I.Pasha in the above connection and having heard the submissions made and having viewed the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey I.Pasha and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on his mount. In that, early part of the race he sat behind and kept tracking the stable mate STAR PRINCE and after entering the straight he allowed STAR PRINCE to take the lead and thereafter switched his mount to the outside and he was not vigorous in his riding effort throughout the straight. Therefore, the Stewards decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f.  21st July, 2011 to 28th August, 2011(both days inclusive) for unsatisfactory riding on his mount MOONLIGHT BEAUTY (Not permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Further, the Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainver Vinayak in the subject case and held the view that the Jockey would not have ridden in this manner unless he had the Trainer’s tacit approval. Therefore the Stewards decided to fine Trainer Vinayak a sum of Rs.25,000/- for unsatisfactory running of his charge and to severely warn him for the future.

 

Two Objections were lodged in this race. First objection was lodged by App.A.Sandesh, the rider of JEN’S GLORY (Placed 4th) against Jockey T.S.Jodha, the rider of STAR PRINCE (Placed first). The reason being that at about 200 metres, STAR PRINCE shifted in and bumped his horse twice whereby he was unbalanced and could not do justice to his mount.

 

The second Objection was lodged by Jockey S Sunil, the rider of BLUE MELLON (Placed 2nd) against Jockey T.S.Jodha, the rider of STAR PRINCE (Placed first). The reason being that at around 450 metres, STAR PRINCE shifted out and came on to GOLDEN SPURS who in turn came on to him. Thereafter, again at 350 metres STAR PRINCE bumped him and his horse got unbalanced and at 150 metres, STAR PRINCE bumped  his horse again and unbalanced him whereby he could not ride out his mount and this cost him the race, the verdict being half length.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule both the objections.

 

The Stewards after the last race interviewed Jockey T.S.Jodha in the above connection and having heard the submissions made and having viewed the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and held him guilty of careless riding on his mount STAR PRINCE in the above race. Therefore, the Stewards decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f. 21st July, 2011 to 29th July, 2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that MOONLIGHT BEAUTY was examined at the stables at 0800 hours the next morning i.e. on 11th April, 2011 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg with swelling on the right knee. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that CHINGARI was examined at the stables at 0815 hours the next morning i.e. on 11th April, 2011 and was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FREANY & STAR PRINCE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 5. (330) THE CORNICHE PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 8

 

On arrival at the start,  DREAM TALK (App.Akshay) was examined by the Club’s V.O. and found to be sore from the right fore leg. As such, the mare was certified unfit to race and hence withdrawn on veterinary grounds, before coming under the Starter’s Order. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, SEA WARRIOR (Suraj Narredu, dr.8) jumped out awkwardly outwards and went on to GENERALISSIMO (Akash Agarwal, dr.9).

 

The saddle on STEELY DAN (Aadesh Kumar) shifted to one side. As such Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been fined Rs.2000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

Jockey S.Sunil (LAGO MEDIO) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his third offence with regard to the use of the whip, the matter was reported to the Stewards.  The Stewards after the last race interviewed Jockey S.Sunil in the above connection and having heard him and having viewed the video recording of the race held him guilty of using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Therefore, the Stewards decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2011/12, the same will be granted to him, however, his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f. 21st July, 2011 to 22nd July, 2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Jockey S.Sunil (LAGO MEDIO) has been reprimanded for improper use of the whip wherein he allowed his mount to shift outwards in the final 100 metres under the use of the whip.

 

Trainer Faisal A.Abbas was questioned regarding the comparative performance of LAGO MEDIO who won this race as compared to her previous runs where the gelding was unplaced. Trainer Faisal A.Abbas stated that the company this time was poor as compared to Race No.271 which was won by BESPOKE in a fast timing of 58.83 second. Further, although his horse had finished 11th in that race,  he was only 6 ½ lengths from the winner and possibly the extra distance of 200 metres this time suited him better. He said that he had not fancied the horse this time and he was at longer odds and the horse won after a gap of almost two and half years. The matter was reported to the Stewards, who, having heard Trainer Faisal A.Abbas noted the explanation tendered by him.

 

After the race, MARINE ZONE, LAGO MEDIO & STEELY DAN were found to be sound.

ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “SEA DIAMOND” IN RACE NO.318 RUN ON 9TH APRIL, 2011 WHICH THE MARE WON AS COMPARED TO HER PREVIOUS RUNS WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 10th April, 2011 interviewed Trainer  H.J.Antia in the above connection. Trainer Antia stated that this was a poor set of horses as compared to Race No.255, except  for UNSTOPPABLE who had placed second in its previous run, the other horses had no form.  Race No.255 was won by MYSTIC TEMPLE, clocking 59.50 seconds  and MYSTIC TEMPLE is a winner of two races this season. The horses who had finished 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 5th in Race No.255 viz. HOTTIE, GLORIOUS MISS, RISING GLORY and NATIONAL IKON have all won or placed in Class IV subsequently and even WILD IMAGINATION who had placed tenth has subsequently lost a race by half length in a higher Class  whereas his horse SEA DIAMOND ran in a lowest Class (0 to 19). Also, in Race No.255 it was big field of 20 horses and his horse was drawn-17. Further, his horse SEA DIAMOND had clocked 1 Minute 1.46 seconds in Race No.255 and today the horse has won in more or less same timing.

The explanation tendered by Trainer H.J.Antia in the subject case was noted. However, his attention was drawn to the Calendar Notification with regard to inconsistent running of horses and he was warned to be more careful in the future.
