RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED 6 METRES AWAY FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1600 METRES (MILE CHUTE) TO 1000 METRES, 8 METRES AWAY AT 800 METRES AND THEREAFTER 10 METRES AWAY UP TO THE WINNING POST. FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH, RAILS WERE PLACED FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1200 METRES LEADING TO 3 METRES AWAY AT 1100 METRES AND 6 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES.

 

Permission was granted to Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha to saddle the charges of Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha viz. DAZZLE N DAZE & YODHA SHAKTI (6th race) and MT TIANMEN (7th race), at his (Trainer Magansingh P Jodha’s) risk and responsibility.

RACE NO.1(253) - THE  RUNNING STAR PLATE: (About)1000 Metres.  Runners – 12

 

On a request from Trainer M.K.Jadhav, he was permitted to withdraw his charge TWO OCEANS in view of a spate of positives in his Yard. The Sr.V.O. was requested to sample the filly for analysis.

 

PEIZIERES (App.Santosh G) was reluctant to reach the starting point. Trainer Subhag Singh has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

PEIZIERES (App.Santosh G) jumped awkwardly outwards.

 

At about 700 metres, SPEAR OF TRIUM (App.Bhawani Singh) was inconvenienced whilst racing on the inside of RISING WAVE (R.Shelar) and lost position, and in doing so, GYAHANA (Neeraj Rawal) who was following, was steadied and in turn hampered MOUNTAIN TRESAURE (App.Akshay Gaikwad) who in turn dropped back on to OCEAN STAR ( S.Mosin).

 

Near 600 metres, OCEAN STAR (S.Mosin) was inconvenienced as PEIZIERES (App.Santosh G) shifted in.

 

Jockey Santosh G (PEIZEIRES) reported that his mount lugged out throughout the race. Trainer Subhag Singh has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, OCEAN STAR, ANI’’S V and MOUNTAIN TREASURE were found to be sound. GLORIOUS CROWN had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.2(254) – THE RADHA SIGTIA TROPHY: (About) 2400 Metres. Runners – 3

 

After the race, ZAZOU was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(255) – THE SOUTHERN EMPIRE TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 6

 

MINT APPROACH (Sandeep Jadhav) was reluctant to reach the starting point. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

Approaching 1000 metres, VISIONIST (App.R.Ajinkya) clipped the heels of ZANDER (App.Yash Narredu) and stumbled, due to which the Jockey lost his right stirrup which he regained after travelling some distance. 

 

RACE NO.4(256) – THE ROYAL DEEDS PLATE:(About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 8

 

Approaching 200 metres, HARVEY (App.Yash Narredu) was held up for a run and steadied as MAGNUM OPUS (A.Sandesh) shifted ground outwards. Jockey A.Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

Jockey S.Zervan (SENTIMENTAL) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Vishal Gaikwad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (ABBEY) reported that his mount ran green and lugged outwards whereby he could not ride out his mount in the straight. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

Trainer Hosidar Daji and Jockey Malcolm Kharadi were questioned regarding the running and riding of FLYING SHOW and having heard them and having viewed the recording of the race, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey. The matter will be reported to the Stewards shortly.

After the race, FLYING SHOW was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(257) – THE RONALDO PLATE:(About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 10

 

Near 200 metres, TEMPEST (App.Santosh G) was held up for a run for a few strides while racing behind INVINCIBLE (F.Irani).

 

Jockey S.G.Prasad (WINGS OF GLORY) was observed to have dropped his hands before passing the post thereby losing 4th place. As such, his Jockey’s license has been suspended w.e.f., 29th March to 4th April 2015 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, MOUNTAIN SONG was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(258) -THE  P.A.D’AVOINE TROPHY:(About) 1600 Mts. Runners– 13

 

On a request from Trainer M.K.Jadhav, he was permitted to withdraw his charge UNICO CORAZON in view of a spate of positives in his Yard. The urine sample of the filly was collected for analysis.

 

Jockey S.Zervan (SABOTAGE) reported that his mount lugged out while negotiating the bend. Trainer Vishal Gaikwad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

The saddle on RAPID GIRL (Imran Chisty) was observed to have slipped forward due to which the Rider lost control over his mount and the filly travelled wide while negotiating the bend and then could not be ridden out in the straight. As such, Trainer Vinesh has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, STRAIGHT SIX was observed to have broken a blood vessel. Being a second time bleeder, the gelding is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7(259) - THE A.HOYT PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres.  Runners -16

 

BLUE FLAME (S.G.Prasad) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

MR MORRISON (Amyn Merchant) reared up at the start and lost about 3 lengths.

 

At the start, ICE GATE (App.R.Ajinkya, dr.1) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to SALTANATH (App.G.Amit, dr.2) and at the same time, STAR (Dashrath Singh, dr.3) lugged in and went across SALTANATH and then made contact with ICE GATE.

 

At the start, GOLDSTORM (R.Shelar) was tightened due to lack of room.

 

App.Santosh G (ZIPPIT) reported that his mount jumped awkwardly at the start.

 

Soon after the start BLUE FLAME (S.G. Prasad) went on to EMRALD FORTUNE (Altaf Sayyed) who went on to ZIPPIT ( App.Santosh G), who in turn went to SILLY DUDE (N.Rawal).  Jockey S.G.Prasad has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

The Stipendiary Stewads will be looking into the running and riding of BLUE FLAME also also the running of the whole race and will submit a report shortly.

 

After the race, MR MORRISON and STAR (examined on 13/03/2015) were found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE INTERFERENCE THAT OCCURRED AT THE START OF RACE NO 246 – THE SINGAPORE TURF CLUB TROPHY (DIV-II) – RUN ON 8TH MARCH 2015

 

An enquiry was held into the interference caused at the start, wherein VENI VIDI VICI (Amyn Merchant) shifted in soon after the start and in the process hampered the running of WINDS OF CHANGE (S.P.Ranjane) who in turn went on to ABSOLUTE JEWEL (C.S.Jodha). On enquiring from both the Jockeys i.e. Amyn Merchant and Neeraj Rawal, it was established that MISS SENORITA (Neeraj Rawal) had shifted in and carried VENI VEDI VICI inwards which resulted in the interference to WINDS OF CHANGE and ABSOLUTE JEWEL. As such, Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.2000/-.

RE : POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF  gr f “GREY MIST” (WINNER)) IN RACE NO.172 – THE ROYAL CHALLENGE CUP (DIV.II) RUN ON 1ST FEBRUARY, 2015 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 12th March, 2015 interviewed Tr.M.K.Jadhav regarding the positive report dated 9thMarch, 2015  from Racing Laboratory, The Hong Kong Jockey Club, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge GREY MIST (Winner) in Race No.172 – The Royal Challenge Cup (Div.II) run on 1st February, 2015 which read as under :-

 

 “The analysis has shown the presence of O-desmethylvenlafaxine”

 

At this meeting, Trainer M.K.Jadhav was handed over a copy of the above report and was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, within five days of opening the envelope that identifies the sample, i.e. latest by 17th March, 2015, whether he wished to send the split sample of his charge GREY MIST to LGC, New Market, UK, or L C H, France, for Confirmatory analysis and also the name of the witnessing analyst, if any, failing which the Stewards shall treat the report of the primary laboratory as conclusive.

 

Tr.M.K.Jadhav was asked to conduct his own enquiries and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.V.O. of the Club, as and when required.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “BE SAFE” IN RACE NO.241 – THE OMKAR INDIAN TURF INVITATION CUP (GR.1) RUN ON SATURDAY, 07TH MARCH, 2015 :

Trainer M.Narredu and Jockey Suraj Narredu were questioned in the above connection and having heard them and having viewed the recording of Race No.232 as also the previous races of BE SAFE, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by them and held them guilty of unsatisfactory running and riding of BE SAFE, an On Money favourite. In that, it was noted that there was a change of running tactics of the horse in this race as compared to his run in the McDowell Indian Derby (Gr.1) where the horse was settled in a much better position and he was in a striking position at the top of the straight whereas in the race in question, he was settled too far behind and the Jockey did not bother to improve his position between the 1000 and 800 metres and thereafter on entering the straight he again waited for nearly 100 to 150 metres to make his move and allowed QUASAR (App.Yash Narredu) to take a long lead which has cost him the race. Despite this, Trainer M.Narredu was satisfied with the Jockey’s riding.

 

Therefore, the Stipendiary Stewards decided :-

 

i)              to suspend Mr.Suraj Narredu’s Jockey’s Licenses w.e.f. 26th March 2015 to 5th April 2015 (both days inclusive) (Permitted to ride work/ Mock race) for unsatisfactory riding of BE SAFE rand have referred the matter to the Stewards of the Club for further action as deemed fit;

 

ii)             to fine Trainer M.Narredu a sum of Rs.10,000/- as per the norms, for unsatisfactory running of his charge and and have referred the matter to the Stewards of the Club for further action as deemed fit.
