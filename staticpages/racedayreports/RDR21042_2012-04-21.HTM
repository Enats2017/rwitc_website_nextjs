RACE NO.1 (355) – THE SAMUEL NATHAN PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 8

 

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.6000/-  (Rs.3,000/- for each case)  for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charges CORDIALLY YOURS and ZORBA.

 

Near 200 metres, MARCIANO (Amyn Merchant) shifted in causing  Jockey T.S.Jodha (TIPPING POINT) to ease out to progress in the race and in the process caused SACRE COEUR (Neeraj Rawal) to travel wide.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal was questioned with regard to his riding on SACRE COEUR. The Jockey stated that he was not given any specific instructions how to ride the mare. He explained that he found the mare off the bridle from 1000 metres and she became disinterested coming into the straight. He added that as compared to her previous race, she was not keen at all to race and she is not a free sweater. The previous race was run late in the evening whereas this was the first race of the afternoon which she may not have liked. The explanation has been noted. When questioned, Trainer Adhiraj Singh Jodha stated that the filly didn’t sweat as well as she did last time, she might have felt the heat. He stated that he had instructed the Jockey to stay away from the rails and entering the straight to come from the extreme outside as she tends to get claustrophobic, as she did when she was crowded in her last run as well. Further, the mare was scoped at the Club’s Equine Hospital and was found to have suffered EIPH grade III. The mare has been stopped from racing for the stipulated period of four weeks and until passed fit to race by the Club’s VO.

 

After the race, TIPPING POINT was observed to have an abrasion at the left fore cannon. SACRE COEUR and MARCIANO were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (356)  – THE  STONE RIDGE PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 9

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (ISHPINGO) reported that his horse jumped out slowly and didn’t show initial speed.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey A.Sandesh, rider of DESTINED TO GLORY (placed second), against VISIONIST (C.Rajendra, placed first). The reason being, between the 300 and 200 metres, VISIONIST (C.Rajendra) came on to him whereby he had to stop riding and thereafter in the last 150 metres,  VISIONIST again interfered with him which cost him the race since he lost by half a length.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and viewing the video recording of the race, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the Order of Placing as under :-

DESTINED TO GLORY                     -               FIRST;                   LIMITLESS HILLS               -               THIRD;

VISIONIST                                            -               SECOND;             ISHPINGO                            -               FOURTH

 

An enquiry has been initiated in to the running and riding of ISHPINGO and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, GREATNESS, VISIONIST and DESTINED TO GLORY were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3 (357) – THE CHARON TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres - Runners – 12

 

At the request of Trainer Faisal A.Abbas, CLAIM TO FAME was examined at 1005 hours on 21/04/2012 and was found to have painful swelling on withers as a result of injury. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The Trainer has to obtain a fitness certificate for the horse from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting with him to race.

 

ZEKI (App.Nikhil Bhujbal) swayed her head and did not raise an effective gallop and in the process hampered the running of VELOCITY (N.S.Parmar) who was in the adjoining stall whereby both the horses lost considerable ground at the start. Since ZEKI had behaved in a similar manner in her previous outing, the filly has been banned from racing for a period of one month and thereafter the filly has to be shown to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials and also in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.3000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge MACHO UNO.

 

Jockey Akshay (PLAN B, dr.10) has been fined Rs.3000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker and in the process going across RAN BANKA (Shammi Khan, dr.9).

At the start, ZEKI (App.Nikhil Bhujbal) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to VELOCITY (N.S.Parmar) and thereafter stopped galloping. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to show the filly to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards in a Mock race, before next accepting to race.

 

Near 400 metres, THIS ONES FOR YOU (A.Sandesh) was steadied as RAN BANKA (Shammi Khan) shifted ground outwards.

 

Jockey A.Sandesh (THIS ONES FOR YOU) reported that near 200 metres, due to lack of room in front of him, he shifted out to make his bid but his mount shifted out more than he had anticipated and went on to RAN BANKA (Shammi Khan), who in turn went on to MIRACLE OF MERCY (T.S.Jodha).

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (WINCHESTER) reported that his horse did not respond throughout the race unlike his last outing. The Veterinary examination did not reveal any abnormality.

 

An enquiry has been initiated in to the running and riding of THIS ONES FOR YOU and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, ZEKI was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4 (358)  – THE  K.H.IRANI PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 11

 

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.3000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge BY THE WAY.

 

Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar (TOMORROWS DREAMS) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being the fourth offence with regard to the use of the whip, the matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Sunday, 22nd April, 2012 interviewed Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar in the above connection and having heard him and having viewed the video recording of the race, held him guilty of using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Therefore, the Stewards decided that if he applied for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2012/13, it will be granted to him. However, he shall be deprived of the use of the whip in races for ten race days of the Pune Meeting 2012 i.e. on 26th and 27th July, 2012 and 4th, 5th, 11th, 12th, 15tth, 19th, 25th and 26th August, 2012.

 

The saddle on BATHSHEBA (Neeraj Rawal) was observed to have slipped back during the race. This being the 3rd occasion of saddle slip during the Season, Tr.Dallas Todywalla has been fined Rs.10,000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

After the race, TO THE MOON was found to be sore by the left fore leg. TOMORROWS DREAMS  & HILLS AND DALES were both sore by the right fore leg. The above three horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.5 (359) – THE STOLEN KISSES PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 7

 

MARENGO (Nirmal Jodha) reared up in the gate and put his right fore leg on the top bar of the gate. The Horse was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. and was found to be having inco-ordination from the back, hence was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. BIG BLACK (S.Zervan) also reared up in the gate and fell backwards. The rear gate was opened but the colt got stuck under the gate and struggled to come out. As recommended by the Club’s V.O., BIG BLACK was also withdrawn from the race on veterinary grounds. VIVA CAPRI (A.Sandesh) who was in the adjoining  stall was also taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. who certified him fit to race. Trainers Narendra Lagad and P.Shroff have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.6000/-  (Rs.3,000/- for each case) for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charges MYLASTWISH and SOMETHING NEW.

 

At the start, SHEER ROMANCE (Neeraj Rawal) jumped out awkwardly.

 

SOMETHING NEW (A.Ashhad Asbar) hung out while negotiating the bend and in the straight and raced along the outer rail. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the filly and he reported that SOMETHING NEW was found to be sound, having a wolf tooth on the left side. Trainer Anil Kumar has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

VIVA CAPRI (A.Sandesh) kept an erratic course in the straight.

 

After the race, MYLASTWISH was observed to have an abrasion below both stifles. WINNING POST was  found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6 (360)  – THE CHARON TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 14

 

In the process of stalling, SPIRIT OF ECSTASY (Neeraj Rawal) brushed the rear flap of the gate, displacing the surcingle which was adjusted prior to re-stalling the filly.

 

SEIG FRIED (Ishwar Pardeshi) lost about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.3000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge DAIMONDZIP.

SPIRIT OF ECSTASY (Neeraj Rawal) lugged out in the straight. Trainer Nosher Cama has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly

 

Near 150 metres, GUNS N ROSES (Ikram Khan) was steadied as AFLAME (App.Nikhil Bhujbal) shifted inwards. App.Nikhil Bhujbal has been cautioned.

 

After the race, AFLAME was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CLOUD COVER was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7 (361) – THE JEHAN NUMA PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres–Runners –  11

 

At the start, INQUILAAB (App.Nikhil Bhujbal) veered out and lost ground. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Trainer Anil Kumar has been fined Rs.3000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge GIBRAN.

 

At the start, AFSOON (App.Prasad) jumped out awkwardly. MUMBAI KING (N.Rawal) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to AFSOON (App.Prasad, dr.5).

 

GIBRAN (Kavraj Singh) and VOYAGER (Dashrath Singh) lugged-in in the final stages of the race. Trainers Anil Kumar and H.J.Antia have been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race with their respective charges.

 

After the race, CHINGARI and VOYAGER were found to be sore by the left fore leg. Both the horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.
