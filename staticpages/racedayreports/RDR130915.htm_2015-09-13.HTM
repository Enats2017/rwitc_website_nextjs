THE IDAR GOLD TROPHY (GR.3) : (About) 2400 Metres – 6 Runners  (This Race was run before the first race of the day i.e. at 12.45 P.M.)

 

In consequence of this race having been postponed from 12/9/2015, the following change of riders were permitted as the Jockeys were declared to ride In The President of India Gold Cup (Gr.1) run at Hyderabad on this day :-

 

SHIVALIK SHOWERS from Jockey P.S.Chouhan to Jockey P.Kamlesh;

STARISTOCRAT from App.Yash Narredu to Jockey Neeraj Rawal                       

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that Jockey Suraj Narredu was declared to ride TATIANA in The Idar Gold Trophy (Gr.3) in place of Jockey S.Zervan (who was under suspension on this day), which was publicised. Subsequently, Trainer Todywalla informed that Jockey Suraj Narredu had declined to ride TATIANA and he wished to replace him with Jockey C.S.Jodha.

 

The Stewards interviewed Trainer Dallas Todywalla in the above connection and having heard him, decided to permit the change of Jockey on TATIANA to Jockey C.S.Jodha. However, Trainer Dallas Todywalla was fined Rs.1,000/- for declaring a Jockey (Suraj Narredu) without his consent.     

 

Approaching the 2000 metres, AQUAMARINE (D.K.Ashish) shifted in, forcing TATIANA (C.S.Jodha) and HIGHLANDER (T.S.Jodha) to check their mounts. Jockey D.K.Ashish has been fined Rs.2,000/-

 

Trainer P.Shroff was questioned with regard to the poor performance of his charge AQUAMARINE. He  opined that the Jockey rode a poor race on questioning the jockey he had no reply to offer  The Stipendiary Stewards will enquire further into the matter and submit a report at the earliest.

 

During the course of the questioning, Trainer P.Shroff also mentioned that he felt that SHIVALIK SHOWERS (P. Kamlesh) deliberately went on to TIGER TOPS (N. S. Parmar) at the start. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into this complaint and submit a report.

 

After the race, AQUAMARINE was found to be sound, but having a cut injury at the left hock. TATIANA had a cut injury at the left hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.1(126) – THE COL. KAIKHUSHROO MANECKJI BHARUCHA MEMORIAL TROPHY : (About) 1200 Metres – 7 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer Narendra Lagad, ROCK THE TURF was examined at 0730 hours on 12/09/2015 and was found to have suffered a cut injury at the right hind pastern and was sore from the right hind leg. As such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

GRAND MARCH (App.S.J.Sunil, dr.4) jumped out awkwardly thereby tightening MT TIANMEN (App.S. A. Amit, dr.3)

 

MR MORRISON (Amyn Merchant) hung in throughout the straight. Trainer Asad Siddiqui has been advised to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

GRAND MARCH (App. S. J. Sunil) shifted out in the last 100 metres under urging and made contact with MR MORRISON (Amyn Merchant) who was racing on his outside.

 

App. S. J. Sunil (GRAND MARCH) was observed to have used his whip more than the prescribed limit. However, since the gelding was hanging in and the whip was used as a corrective measure, no action was initiated against App.S.J.Sunil.

 

After the race, GRAND MARCH was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, PRETTY HOT and YARDSTICK (examined on 14/09/2015) were found to be sound

 

RACE NO.2(127) – THE ALGECIRAS PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres – 4 Runners

 

Approaching the 100 metre marker, it was observed that in an attempt to change the whip, the reins of  Jockey D.K.Ashish (GRENADINE) slipped out of his hands. Jockey D.K.Ashish has been asked to be more careful in the future. 

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that whilst returning after the race, MANAKEL collapsed near the 2200 metres marker. First Aid treatment was given but the gelding did not recover and died.

 

After the race, ZAZOU was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(128) – THE  JUVENTUS TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – 9 Runners

 

Trainer C. D. Katrak has been fined Rs.3,000/- for late Declaration in respect of his charge EXODUS.

 

MORAG (Vikram Jodha) played up in the stall due to which its head gear broke. Since all the horses were already stalled and MORAG was behaving unruly, change of the gear was not possible. As such, MORAG was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order.

 

HIGH AUTHORITY (Abhishek Habbu dr.2) jumped out awkwardly and made contact with SQUASH (App.N.B.Kuldeep, dr.1).

 

ISINIT (S.G.Prasad, dr.3) jumped out awkwardly and went on to PARADISE (App.Bhawani Singh, dr.4).

 

Passing 800 metres, CHARGING EMPRESS (J. Chinoy) shifted in sharply on to SQUASH (App.N.B.Kuldeep), who in turn went on to SHUKRAN (Kishore Kadam). Jockey J. Chinoy has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Considering the drift in odds an enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of VERBIER and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, ISINIT was observed to have an abrasion at the left hind pastern, PARADISE had a cut injury at the right hock and MORAG had a gum injury.  VERBIER and MORAG were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(129) – THE AIR STRIKE PLATE: (About) 1800 Metres – 11 Runners

 

BALCHANDRA (Kishore Kadam) planted losing a distance at the start and  did not take practical part in the race. Trainer Asad Siddiqui has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. 

 

Soon after the start, GERONIMO (C.S.Jodha) was observed to have dropped back due to a tightly packed field.

 

Coming into the straight, JAGER BOMB (Arshad Alam) was observed to have shifted out on to SANSA (Neeraj Rawal) who went on to AZADEGAN (App.G.Amit), who in turn went on to GAABRRIO (J.Chinoy). Jockey Arshad Alam has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, App.G.Amit (AZADEGAN) reported that his mount was hanging-in in the straight. Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been advised take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey Arshad Alam (JAGER BOMB) has been fined Rs.3,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount (Second Offence)

 

After the race, SANSA and AZADEGAN (examined on 14/09/2015) were found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE: APPEAL FROM TRAINER DALLAS TODYWALLA AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF A FINE OF RS.50,000/- IMPOSED ON HIM  - ELECTROCUTION OF “SOUL SPEAK” IN YOUR YARD

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 13th September 2015 considered the Appeal lodged by Trainer Dallas Todywalla.

The Board of Appeal having studied all the documents and papers that were submitted and having heard the submissions made by Tr.Dallas Todywalla, deliberated the matter at length and having applied their minds to the entire facts of the case, the Board noted that he should have examined the wiring at his stables when he had shifted to Pune and that he had admitted that the Electrical fitting was carried out by a private electrician which was in breach of the Notice published in the Racing Calendar. The Board observed that the safety of horses is of paramount importance and a Trainer should be able to pre-empt it. The Board was seized of the fact that the wire bitten by the horse was damaged due to it being too old and repaired many times which had weakened it.
Therefore the Board decided to reject his Appeal. However, looking to the peculiar nature of the case, the Board decided to refund his Appeal Deposit.
