RE: POSITIVE REPORT IN THE POST RACE BLOOD SAMPLE OF b. g. "FLASHMAN" (WINNER) IN RACE NO. 126-THE VINAYAK SALVER - RUN ON 12TH OCTOBER 2025:

 

Trainer Bezan Chenoy had meeting with the Stewards of the Club on Tuesday, 9th June 2026 in reference to the positive analytical finding reported in the post-race blood sample of b g FLASHMAN following Race No. 126 The Vinayak Salver run on 12th October 2025.

 

The Stewards considered all relevant documents, evidence, submissions, laboratory reports, and expert opinions placed before them, and in particular the findings relating to the presence of Lamotrigine detected in water samples collected from Pune TCH and the open well at Mumbai, being the water sources used for washing horses under the charge of Trainer Bezan Chenoy.

 

The Stewards also examined the scientific and factual material pertaining to the possible source and circumstances surrounding the analytical finding and termed it as contamination. Having considered these findings and being satisfied that the circumstances were beyond the Trainers control, decided that no action needs to be taken against the concerned Trainer and to close this matter.

 

Further, the presence of Lamotrigine in the post-race blood sample of b g FLASHMAN was confirmed by both the Primary and Confirmatory Laboratories and, therefore, the analytical finding itself remains established and in accordance with the applicable Medication Rules, the disqualification of b g FLASHMAN from the race concerned shall stand.

 

This decision was made strictly on the facts and evidence pertaining to this matter and shall not constitute a precedent, exemption, defense, entitlement, or basis for any claim in any future case. Each matter shall be considered and determined on its own merits, facts, and evidence.
