ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

PUNE MEETING 2018, SPECIAL RACE DAY, SATURDAY, 25TH AUGUST, 2018.

(PENETROMETER – 4.0; GOING: GOOD)

RACE DAY REPORTS

RAILS WERE PLACED 8 METRES AWAY FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1800 METRES UP TO THE WINNING POST.

 

RACE NO.1(63) –  THE WAR COMMAND PLATE (DIV.III): (About) 1400 Metres – 11 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer Subhag Singh, PARADISE was examined at the stables at 1100 hours on 22/08/2018 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg, as such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

Passing 800 metres, FOREVER FREE (Akshay) shifted in thereby tightening MAGICAL BLOSSOM (S.J.Sunil) who had to be checked. Jockey Akshay has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing 400 metres, SPINNING GOLD (S.A.Amit) shifted out thereby inconveniencing ANGEL COIN (S.G.Prasad). Jockey S.A.Amit has been cautioned.

 

Passing 250 metres, FOREVER FREE (Akshay) shifted in thereby inconveniencing SPINNING GOLD (S.A.Amit). Jockey Akshay has been cautioned.

 

After the race, ALLEGRIA was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind bulb of heel and fetlock. MAGICAL BLOSSOM and WHOOPSIDAISY were trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ANGEL COIN had right eye injury (mild corneal ulcer and opacity).

 

RACE NO.2(64) – THE TIMELESS BEAUTY PLATE: (About) 1800 Metres – 7 Runners

 

At the top of the straight, SMART N NOBLE (A.Imran Khan) shifted out under use of the whip thereby inconveniencing SAFDAR (A.Sandesh). After questioning Jockey A.Imran Khan, he  was fined Rs.2,000/- for failing to change his whip hand despite his mount shifting out .

 

After the race, SMART N NOBLE and KOMANDANT were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(65) – THE DR.K.VASUDEVAN TROPHY: (About) 1600 Metres – 8 Runners

 

SUMAAQ (Amyn Merchant) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start

 

After the race, BRABOURNE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(66) –  THE F.D.WADIA TROPHY (GR.3): (About) 1400 Metres – 7 Runners  - NO REPORT

 

RACE NO.5(67) –  THE WAR COMMAND PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – 12 Runners

 

LOVELY FAIRY (S.A.Amit) jumped out throwing its head thus  losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

After the race, Jockey S.Jaykumar (MASTER OF STUDIES) reported that his mount hung in throughout the race. Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, Jockey P.Trevor (BEWITCHED) reported that he found his mount uncomfortable in the last 300 metres.

 

After the race, Jockey K.Nazil (GANDALF) reported that at about 400 metres his whip got tossed from his hand which he managed to grab. In the process his mount shifted in and made contact with DANCE OF FIRE (Dashrath Singh) who got inconvenienced.

 

After the race, BEWITCHED, BOURBON LEGEND, ADVANCE TO CONTACT and MASTER OF STUDIES (examined at the stables) were trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ADVANCE TO CONTACT had left eye injury (mild corneal ulcer and opacity).

 

RACE NO.6(68) – THE WAR COMMAND PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – 12 Runners

 

On the arrival at the start, SIA (K.Nazil) was examined by the Club’s Sr VO and was certified fit to race.

 

Soon after the start,  LORRAINE (P S Chouhan, dr.7) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening and inconveniencing HUZZAH’S GRACE (D S Daman, dr.6), HANLON’S RAZOR (M Ayyar, dr.5) and LILIBETH (Dashrath Singh, dr.4). Due to this interference, HUZZAH’S GRACE dropped back inconveniencing BRAZOS (D A Naik) who was racing on its haunches. Jockey P S Chouhan has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

At 300 metres App D S Daman (HUZZAH’S GRACE) was observed to have shifted out and jostled SIA (App K Nazil).

App D S Daman has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, GOLDEN HORDE was observed to have an abrasion at the right hind fetlock. HANLON’S RAZOR, DRAGON MOSS and CRAY CRAY were found to be sound in the trot.
