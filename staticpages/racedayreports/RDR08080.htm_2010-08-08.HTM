ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

PUNE MEETING 2010, FOURTH DAY, SUNDAY, 8TH AUGUST, 2010.

(TRACK: MONSOON; PENETROMETER:  1.9; GOING: GOOD)

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

FALSE RAILS WERE PLACED 1.5 METRES WIDE FRON 1300 METRES TILL THE WINNING POST. FALSE RAILS WERE ALSO PLACED 4 METRES WIDE FROM 1200 METRES CHUTE TILL 1000 METRES. 

 

RACE NO 1 (50) - THE  EVATINA PLATE  (DIV.II) :  (About) 1400 Metres. Runners - 9

 

Approaching 200 metres, BLAU HIMMEL (M.Narredu) hung in and forced CULCRIFF (P.Kamlesh) to shift in who in turn caused interference to FASCINATION (R.Shelar). Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey Suraj Narredu (GOLDEN GRACE) reported loss of whip near 200 metres. The Jockey has been reminded of the notice with regard to Loss of Whip.

 

Jockey D.S.Rathore (DESERT DYNAMITE) reported that his mount was inclined to lug-in in the straight. Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

The bridle on FASCINATION (R.Shelar) was observed to have got displaced marginally as such Trainer M.K.Jadhav has been cautioned for the future.

 

After the race, BLAU HIMMEL and RED CLOUD were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 2  (51) - THE  WAVES OF GLORY PLATE : (About) 2000 Metres. Runners – 5

 

Jockey Ladjadj Stephane (LIVING THE DREAM) has been cautioned for looking back during the race.

 

After the race, LIVING THE DREAM was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 3 (52) – THE  HER MAJESTY TROPHY: (About)  1000 Metres.  Runners - 4

 

After the race, FOREVER YOUNG was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 4 (53) – THE MAYOR’S TROPHY : (About) 1800 Metres. Runners - 6

 

ZILLIONAIRE (Dashrath Singh) and TINTORETTO (B.Prakash) were reluctant to be stalled. Trainers Dallas Todywalla and Vinayak have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter during morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO 5 (54) – THE EVATINA PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1400  Metres. Runners - 9

 

At the start, MONARCHOS (Vikram Jodha) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside and made contact with GOLDEN KINGDOM (C.Rajendra). The incident was accidental, as such no action was initiated in the matter.

 

Near 300 metres, SURAJ (D.S.Rathore) shifted in and inconvenienced GOLDEN KINGDOM (C.Rajendra). Jockey D.S.Rathore has been cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

Approaching 200 metres, SUAVEMENTE (B.Prakash) shifted out and hampered the running of TYUMEN (Neeraj Rawal). Jockey B.Prakash has been cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.   

 

Jockey D.S.Rathore (SURAJ) has been fined Rs.1000/- using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Further, he has been reprimanded for using his whip with excessive frequency and force.

 

After the race, SUAVEMENTE was observed to be going short by the left fore leg and also lost its left fore shoe. The horse has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SOLID ROCK and MONARCHOS were observed to have lost their left fore plates. SOLID ROCK & SURAJ were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 6  (55) - THE  HAVOVI KHUSRO JALNAWALA MEMORIAL JUVENILE MILLION: (About) 1200 Metres.   Runners - 18

 

The saddle on ASHWA PRERNA and PALAZZO GRAZIOLI slipped back during the race, as such Trainers Uday Bhan Singh and Vinayak have been fined Rs.2000/ - each for faulty saddling of their respective charges.

 

Jockeys J.E.Pereira (STAR SYMPHONY) and A.M.Tograllu (STAR OF DECCAN) reported that their respective mounts lugged out in the straight, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (SET AFIRE) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight and Jockey Suraj Narredu (CRYSTAL) reported that his mount lugged out throughout the race. Trainers C.D.Katrak, Nirad Karanjawala have been asked to take remedial measures with their respective charges before next accepting to race. No action has been initiated against STAR OF DECCAN since the horse returned sore after the race.

 

STARRER (Dashrath Singh) hung-in in the straight. Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, STAR OF DECCAN was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The colt has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CRYSTAL, STAR SYMPHONY and STARRER were found to be sound.

 

ASHWA PRERNA (M.Narredu) was placed fifth and SUPREME FORCE (A.Imran Khan) was placed sixth by the Judge, in this race.

 

RACE NO 7 (56) – THE  F.D. WADIA TROPHY (GRADE 2) :  About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 11

 

At the start, ILLUSTRIOUS (Suraj Narredu) tried to veer to the outside. Trainer Imtiaz A Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

  

Passing 200 metres, SCIONIC (C.S.Jodha) lugged in and forced SUPERIOR QUEST (A.Imran Khan) to shift in who in turn tightened ASHWA PUSKAR (Neeraj Rawal) who was checked by its Rider. Trainer Altamash A.Ahmed has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

At the behest of the Stipendiary Stewards, a Stewards’ Enquiry was held in this race. The reason being that near 100 metres, CIEL INDIENNE (J.G.Sampson, placed first), shifted in and caused interference to SU CHALIYO (Ladjadj Stephane, placed 2nd) who was gaining ground on the inside and had to change course. The Stewards considered the submissions made by the Jockeys in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their own observation of the incident, decided to revise the Order of Placing as follows:

 

SU CHALIYO                – First;                          STAR WARRIOR - Third

CIEL INDIENNE           - Second                       SCIONIC          - Fourth

 

Jockey A.Imran Khan (SUPERIOR QUEST) has been cautioned for using his whip with excessive force.

 

Jockey J.G.Sampson (CIEL INDIENNE) reported loss of whip near 200 metres. The Jockey has been reminded of the notice with regard to the Loss of Whip.

 

After the race, ROYAL HIGHNESS was found to have a clip injury on the right hind canon and pastern.

 

BEAUTIFUL STRANGER (M.Narredu) was placed fifth and SERENITY (B.Prakash) was placed sixth by the Judge, in this race.

 

RACE NO 8 (57) - THE  MUSIC MAN PLATE (DIV.II) :   (About)  1200 Metres. Runners - 11

 

At the Start, GREY PHANTOM (A.M.Tograllu) lugged in and shifted inwards due to which TOMORROWS DREAMS (S.Shinde) shifted in and jostled with BRAVE GUN (Imran Chisty). Travelling some distance further, TOMORROWS DREAMS was again hampered while racing on the inside of GREY PHANTOM. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Near 700 metres, due to lack of room, LADY BLACK STAR (Vikram Jodha) shifted out and was steadied off the heels of DREAM TALK (Suraj Narredu) and caused TINTISA (S.S.Baria) to shift out, who went across LAMPARD (D.A.Naik) who was steadied.  

 

Near 600 metres, LAMPARD (D.A.Naik) was held up for a few strides.

 

TOMMORROWS DREAMS (S.Shinde) was held up for a clear run between 400 metres and 300 metres.

 

Jockey D.A.Naik (LAMPARD) reported loss of whip near 200 metres. The Jockey has been reminded of the notice with regard to the Loss of Whip.

 

After the race, TINTISA was found to have an injury on the right knee. GREY PHANTOM was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 9 (58) – THE  MUSIC MAN PLATE (DIV.I) :   (About)  1200 Metres. Runners - 11

 

MYSTIC TEMPLE (S.P.Ranjane) refused to be stalled inspite of all efforts, as such the filly was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Orders. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock race, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BONNY LIGHT (N.S.Parmar) fly jumped out of the Gates.

 

Near 500 metres, PERPETUATION (Suraj Narredu) lugged in and hampered the running of ENCHANTED (M Narredu). PERPETUATION continued to lug-in in the straight. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, THE RIGHT MAN was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit to race by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ENCHANHTED was found to be sound.

 

The post race urine sample of the winner of 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th and 9th race viz. CONSTANTINA, SURAJ, STARRER, SU CHALIYO and MANITOU SPLENDOUR as also that of PALAZZO GRAZIOLI & OCEAN PRINCESS (6th race), CIEL INDIENNE & STAR WARRIOR (7th race), were collected for detection of prohibited substance.
