RACE NO.5(92) – THE  KHEEM SINGH GOLD CUP: (About) 1400 Metres –Runners- 8

 

On going through the race day reports submitted by Dr. P. T. Khambatta, the Sr. V. O. noticed that DHISHOOM had been treated with Irga (Bute). On questioning, Trainer Imtiaz A. ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE : ENQUIRY RE-OPENED INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “DIWALI LIGHTS (VIKRAM JODHA) IN RACE NO.27 - THE FREE RADICAL PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 1st DECEMBER, 2016 AFTER THE HORSE WON IN RACE NO.52 – THE SNOW DEW PLATE , RUN ON 15TH DECEMBER, 2016 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 25th December, 2016 having interviewed Trainer H. J. Antia in the above connection, had adjourned the hearing since Jockey Vikram Jodha was unable for questioning on that day.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 1st January, 2017 interviewed Trainer H. J. Antia and Jockey Vikram Jodha in the above connection and having heard the submissions made and having viewed the video of Race No.27, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Vikram Jodha and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on his mount DIWALI LIGHTS in the above race. However, keeping in mind his recent clean record, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 19th January, 2017 to 5th February, 2017 (both days inclusive) for unsatisfactory riding. Further, he was cautioned that on a repetition of a similar offence, severe action may be initiated against him (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Further, the Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer H. J. Antia and held the view that Jockey Vikram Jodha would not have ridden in this manner unless he had the Trainer’s tacit approval. Therefore, the Stewards decided to fine Trainer H. J. Antia a sum of Rs.30,000/- for unsatisfactory running of his charge DIWALI LIGHTS in Race No.27. Further, he was severely cautioned for the future.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “ROYAL SAPPHIRE” (DRAWN 6) IN RACE NO 74 THE CONRAD PEREIRA TROPHY RUN ON 25th DECEMBER 2016 AS COMPARED TO RACE NO.58 - THE DHUN P KOTHAVALA TROPHY (1000 METRES) – RUN ON THURSDAY, 15th DECEMBER, 2016 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Narendra Lagad in the above connection. He said that in Race No.58. ROYAL SAPPHIRE was ridden by Jockey Bhawani Singh who had reported after the race in the presence of the Owners that the mare was loaded early and since the loading took time, the mare became very relaxed and when the start was given she was slow to begin. Thereafter, the mare covered ground very fast to finish 9 ¾ lengths behind the winner in a 1000 metres race. He further added that in her first start at Mumbai in Race No.12, the mare had finished a good 3rd and attributed the mare’s poor performance in Race No.58 to her getting left at the start after which he had given the mare sufficient Gate Practice as a result of which she took a flying jump in Race No.74. 

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad further added that Race No.58 was an open Class IV race as compared to Race No.74 which was confined to horses 5-years old and over and the horses that finished behind ROYAL SAPPHIRE had no great form to go by except MT TIANMEN who was the only common horse in the race and had run below form compared to his last run.

 

The Video recording of Race No.58 as also Race No.74 (all angles), was viewed and Trainer Narendra Lagad pointed out that in Race No.74 ROYAL SAPPHIRE was given a handler in the stall which may have helped in keeping her alert.

 

Having heard the submissions and viewing the video of both the races and keeping in mind that the mare had won in a short span of 9 days, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 1st January, 2017 interviewed Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey Bhawani Singh in the above connection and having heard them and having viewed the video of both the races, decided that no action was called for in the matter.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “DUSK N DAWN” (DRAWN 6)  IN RACE NO.190 - THE RED DRAGON PLATE DIV.II (1000 METRES) – RUN ON FRIDAY, 21st OCTOBER, 2016 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards had initiated the above enquiry in Pune and Trainer Mansoor Shah was sent call letters twice to appear before them for the enquiry. However, the first time Trainer Mansoor Shah informed that he would be out of station and will not be able to attend the enquiry. On the second occasion he cited medical reasons for not appearing before the Stipendiary Stewards for the enquiry and hence the delay in completing the enquiry.

 

Trainer Mansoor Shah and Apprentice S. A. Amit were questioned on 23rd December, 2016 in the above connection. App. S. A. Amit stated that he was instructed to sit in a forward position and to do his best. He said that soon after the start the mare did not show initial speed as such he had to brush her up to get into a forward position, however, she still did not respond. Thereafter, passing the 400 metres, he gave her a couple of reminders and rode her all out. To another question, he said that he had given similar instructions on both occasions i.e. in Race No.190 run at Pune and in Race No.69 at Mumbai where the mare won.

 

When asked to explain the poor performance of DUSK N DAWN in race No 190, Trainer Mansoor Shah said that she was a 5 year old mare who will soon turn 6 years old and she was a maiden until her win in Race No.69 on 22nd December, 2016. He said that the first time she ran 2nd in her career was at Pune during the just concluded Pune Meeting 2016 and that run had given them some hope but in her very next outing she ran a poor race and followed this pattern in her next three runs, which lead them to believe that she was not genuine. He said that her performance indicates that she is very inconsistent and they had decided to get rid of her along with a few other horses,  however, since she was in the lowest class, they were unable to find buyers for her. He added that the only reason he had accepted with her in Race No 69 was to avoid the race going void. Further, after the Acceptances Jockey Neeraj Rawal had called him up enquiring for a ride and since he had two runners in this race, he gave him the option to choose and Jockey Neeraj Rawal opted to ride SUPER BOLT, hence he had declared Jockey S. A. Amit on DUSK N DAWN. To another question, Trainer Mansoor Shah said that he had expected SUPER BOLT to run ahead of DUSK N DAWN as he was ridden by a superior Jockey and the win of DUSK N DAWN surprised him.

 

When confronted with regards to the drift in odds in Race No.190, Trainer Mansoor Shah said that the odds had also drifted when the mare won in Race No.69 in Mumbai.

 

Having heard the submissions made and viewing the video of Race No.190 and keeping in mind that the performance of the mare in relation to the common horse AMAZON CAT during the Pune Meeting was the same, the explanation tendered by Trainer Mansoor Shah and Jockey S. A. Amit was noted.
