RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT.

 

RACE NO.1(1) – THE P. B. AVASIA PLATE: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS -  7

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (PYRRHUS) is fined Rs.500/- for sending his Saddle without a saddle number cloth.

 

At the start, SILVER SPRINGS (P. Trevor, dr.2) jumped inwards onto LORD MURPHY

(Amyn Merchant, dr.1) who had to be steadied.

 

At the start, MIGHTY THUNDER (App. Mustakhim Alam, dr.6) jumped inwards and made contact with FREEDOM (C. S. Jodha, dr.5).

 

Passing the 500 metres due to a tightly packed field, SILVER SPRINGS (P. Trevor) racing along the rails was tightened for room and had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 300 metres, FREEDOM (C. S. Jodha) shifted out despite the riders efforts thereby inconveniencing JERUSALEM (Suraj Narredu). Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, on questioning Jockey Amyn Merchant (LORD MURPHY) stated that his mount was shying away from the other runners due to which it was inclined outwards. Tr. Shazaan Shah has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MIGHTY THUNDER was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The colt has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FREEDOM was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(2) – THE ROYAL CALCUTTA TURF CLUB TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  4

 

In the final stages of the race, SKY FALL (N. S. Parmar) was observed to have drifted out under pressure. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Sr. V.O. reported that ONE WISH was trotted and found to be sound thereafter the mare was scoped and found to have EIPHS Gr.II.

 

RACE NO.3 (3) – THE WELCOME TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS -  7

 

After being stalled, DRAGONLORD (P. S. Chouhan, dr.4) started lashing out at the Hind Gate. The gelding was unloaded and examined by the Clubs Sr. V.O. and found to be sound, however, the gelding having suffered a bleeding injury on the left hind gaskin and hock inside and hence was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn before coming under Starter order. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, EMRYS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4 (4) – THE BOOKMYSHOW LIVE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES –RUNNERS- 7

 

At the start, ARCANA (App. Kirtish Bhagat, dr.7) jumped inwards and made contact with LORD VADER (Bhawani Singh, dr. 6). 

 

Approaching the 800 metres, RODRIGO (P. Trevor) leaned inwards thereby tightening and inconveniencing NORTHERN SINGER (App. Mustakim Alam). Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

After the race, ENLIGHTENED was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SOUP AND SANDWICH was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5 (5) – THE GATEWAY OF INDIA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS -  11

 

REGAL COMMAND (Bhawani Singh, dr.3) jumped out awkwardly throwing its head resulting in losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, MAJESTIC WARRIOR (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.8) jumped outwards at the same time LETO (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.9) leaned in and they made contact. Thereafter MAJESTIC WARRIOR further shifted out and at the same time FORTUNATE SON (N. S. Parmar, dr. 11) leaned inwards onto EMPEROR RODERIC (P. S. Chouhan, dr.10) who in turn shifted in. Due to this incident LETO was tightened for room and had to be checked.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (FORTUNATE SON) reported loss of whip passing the 200 metres. This being the first instance during the current Mumbai meeting 22/23, no action has been initiated.

 

From the top of the straight, EMPEROR RODERIC (P. S. Chouhan) was observed to be inclined outwards. After the race, Jockey P. S. Chouhan reported that his mount hung out throughout the race despite his efforts and in the process he lost his whip passing the 300 metres. Jockey P. S. Chouhan was informed that this being his first instance of loss of whip during the current Mumbai meeting 22/23 no action has been initiated against him. In view of colt’s behavior, he was subjected to a Veterinary examination and was found to have returned with a Sore on its left fore and has to be passed fit by club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(6) – THE TRUEFITT & HILL FOUNDERS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 11

 

INTENSE BELIEF (N. S. Parmar, dr.8) planted thereby losing considerable grounds at the start. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching 1000 metres, CAPRIFLA (App. P. S. Kaviraj) leaned inwards thereby inconveniencing THE PRESIDENT (Vishal N. Bunde) who had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 300 metres, CAPRIFLA (App. P. S. Kaviraj) shifted out under urgings and went across THE PRESIDENT (Vishal N. Bunde) causing the latter to clip the former and stumble. After questioning App. P. S. S. Kaviraj was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 22/23, he has being fined Rs.3,000/- .

 

After the race, Jockey Amyn Merchant (MARIELLA) reported that his mount hung out in the straight, despite his efforts. On being pointed out to him that he had used the whip in an improper manner, he stated that he had done so as a corrective measure, however, not being satisfied with the explanation, he has been fined Rs.2,000/- for improper use of whip.  Further, Tr. Shazaan Shah has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting race the filly.

ADDITIONAL REPORT:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO RIDING OF JOCKEY J. CHINOY ON “GOLDEN NEIL” (DRAWN 1) IN RACE NO.83 – THE S. A. POONAWALLA MILLION (GRADE 3) – RUN ON SUNDAY, 18TH SEPTEMBER 2022:

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey J. Chinoy were questioned on 17th October 2022 in the above connection.

 

Jockey J. Chinoy was informed that Trainer Narendra Lagad had reported immediately after the race that you had not followed your riding instruction i.e. considering that the gelding is a free running horse you were instructed to go to the front.

 

Jockey J. Chinoy having confirmed the same stated that he was drawn 1 and despite the horse not taking a smart jump he made all efforts and went to the front thereafter i.e. about 150 metres after the start he saw EMPEROR RODERIC coming from outside with his mouth open he felt that either he would cause him to check or if he continued to go along with him he would use his horse too much up the hill also considering that he was running an additional furlong he felt that he should wait and not fight with him. The trainer pointed out that on the contrary EMPEROR RODERIC had come on his outside and passed him only because he had slowed the pace in fact it is very apparent the rider of EMEROR RODERIC did not want to go to the front and was trying to settle him.

 

On being asked to explain Jockey J. Chenoy stated that he taken a call at that moment and had not done anything deliberately to disrupt the horse and added that he may have misjudged it at that point. Race no.51 run during the current Pune Meeting was viewed where the gelding had won in the hands of with same Jockey.

 

The trainer once again stated that since the jockey has ridden the horse before he knew it and based on his winning race they had planned and accordingly he was instructed to go to the front.

 

Having viewed the race and heard the submissions made by both trainer and jockey  the Stipendiary Stewards are of the view that Jockey J. Chenoy had all the opportunity to follow his riding instructions, which he failed to do hence reported to the Stewards of the Club for failing to follow his riding instructions. The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 20th November 2022 having viewed the race in question, considered note from Stipendiary Stewards and heard the submissions made by Trainer and Jockey, decided to fine Jockey J. Chinoy Rs.5,000/- for failing to follow his riding instructions and to caution him for future.

 

Mumbai: 21st November 2022.
