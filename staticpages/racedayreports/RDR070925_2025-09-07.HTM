RACE DAY REPORT

RACE NO.1 (66) – THE WORLD'S OLDEST BARBERSHOP TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

PRINCE IGOR (App. Avinash Paswan, dr.2) jumped out awkwardly.

 

STAR ROMANCE (S. G. Prasad, dr.3) lost about two lengths at the start.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, EMPOWER (T. S. Jodha) leaned in thereby tightening THE ATHABASCA (R. Ajinkya) causing the latter to be steadied. Jockey T. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 500 metres, MOONLIGHT KISS (A. Sandesh) shifted out thereby tightening MAJESTIC WARRIOR (K. Nazil) thereafter passing the 400 metres, STAR ROMANCE (S. G. Prasad) shifted in and went across MAJESTIC WARRIOR causing the latter to be checked. After questioning, Jockeys A. Sandesh and S. G. Prasad have been cautioned.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the running and riding of THE ATHABASCA (R. Ajinkya) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, MAJESTIC WARRIOR was trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that PRINCE IGOR had a left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.2 (67) – THE LEMON TREE HOTELS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 5

RACE NO.3 (68) – THE DINKOO N. CHENOY SALVER  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

ENSKY (Vivek G., dr.1) was fractious, refused to be stalled despite substantial efforts and was permitted to be withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s order. As such, Trainer Faisal A. On arrival at the starting gates, on a request from Jockey A. Prakash, the saddle used on his mount SAKANA was checked and re-adjusted.

 

RUNNING AWAY (App. Haridas Gore, dr.8) lost about two lengths at the start.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, EKATARINA (Neeraj Rawal) racing on the haunches of EAGLE EYE (K. Sai Kiran) leaned out onto RUNNING AWAY (App. Haridas Gore).

 

After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh (SHE’S MY GAL) reported that his mount was running green throughout the race. As such, Trainer Vinesh has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ALLEZ L’ETOILE & SHE’S MY GAL were trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, RUNNING AWAY was observed to have an injury on its right hind coronet.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that SHE’S MY GAL had a right eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.4 (69) – THE AMJAD KHAN TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

Soon after the start, LIAM (App. A. Omkar) leaned in thereby bumping OPUS DEI (C. Umesh) who in turn shifted in onto ZARAK (K. Pranil).

 

Soon after the start, VINCERO (App. Aditya Waydande) shifted in and went across LIAM (App. A. Omkar) & OPUS DEI (C. Umesh) causing both horses to be steadied. After questioning and not being satisfied, App. Aditya Waydande has been fined Rs.2,000/- for shifting in before the red marker.

 

Approaching the 100 metres, CHARDIKALA (Vivek G.) shifted in under pressure thereby tightening and inconveniencing LORD AND MASTER (Santosh G.) along the rails causing the latter to be checked. Jockey Vivek G. has been cautioned.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the running and riding of AXLROD (T. S. Jodha) and a report will submitted shortly.

 

After the race, FLAMING LAMBORGINI & OPUS DEI were trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

On a request from Trainer Nazzak B. RE: LOOKING INTO JOCKEY ANTONY RAJ S. LOSING HIS STIRRUP ON “CHARLIE BROWN “ IN RACE NO.21 RUN ON 2ND AUGUST 2025:

 

In connection with the above captioned subject matter.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards viewed  the replay of the Race in the presence of Trainer Imtiaz A Sait and Jockey Antony Raj S  from all angles and the blow up, at which time it was noted that the gelding had jumped out awkwardly which resulted in unbalancing its rider due to which Jockey  Antony Raj S. lost his left stirrup. Subsequently, during the Race whilst Jockey Antony Raj S.  had momentarily gained his stirrup however his heel was lodged in the iron the wrong way which once again slipped passing the 400 metres, as such he was not  able to ride out his mount to his full advantage, in view of the same the explanation tendered by Jockey Antony Raj S. was noted.

XXXXX

 

RE: LOOKING INTO JOCKEY ANTONY RAJ S. LOSING HIS WHIP ON “SIEGE COURAGEOUS” IN RACE NO.35 RUN ON 10TH AUGUST 2025:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stipendiary Stewards viewed the replay of the race along with Trainer Prasanna Kumar P. and Jockey Antony Raj S.  from all angles along with the blow up, slow motion of the whip drop, and having considered the explanation tendered by Jockey Antony Raj S. wherein he stated that in the last furlong the whip had got entangled in the reins and fell. On review, it was found that the reins had indeed got entangled which may have caused the whip to fall.

 

Keeping in mind the fact that the horse was a favorite and the Jockey lost his whip at a crucial time of the race, he is being severely cautioned and has been asked to be more careful in the future. This being the first instance of whip loss during the current Pune Meeting 2025, the same has been noted.

XXXXX

 

RE: LOOKING INTO THE APP. AVINASH PASWAN’S RIDING ON “HIGH SPIRIT” IN RACE NO.45 RUN ON 16TH AUGUST 2025:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stipendiary Stewards having viewed the replay of the Race no.45 along with App. Avinash Paswan and his Master Trainer Narendra Lagad, considering that this was App. Avinash’s first ride of his career in which it was observed that he was sitting back in the saddle which caused the horse to throw its head up and took an awkward jump thereby loosing considerable ground. Hence, the Apprentice has been asked to be alert in the gates and also practice with the riding instructor on the horse simulator and improve his riding skills.

RE: JOCKEY VIVEK G. (ARKADIAN) JOSTLING WITH JOCKEY A. SANDESH (POSITANO) IN RACE NO 64. THE IDAR GOLD TROPHY (2400 MTRS.) RUN ON 31ST AUGUST 2025:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, we refer to the meeting of the Stipendiary Stewards with Jockeys Vivek G. and A. Sandesh on 7th September 2025, having heard the submissions made by Jockey A. Sandesh (POSITANO) and Jockey Vivek G. (ARKADIAN) and  having viewed the video recording of the Race in question, the Stipendiary Stewards  were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Vivek G. wherein passing the 400 metres, Jockey Vivek G. (ARKADIAN) shifted out under urgings in an effort to force a passage, thereby bumping POSITANO (A. Sandesh), thereafter, Jockey A. Sandesh in an attempt to hold his ground leaned in which resulted in both the riders jostling for room  which could have caused an accident. Therefore, keeping all the circumstances in mind, it was decided to suspend the Jockeys licence of Mr. Vivek G. for One Race Day i.e. on 27th September 2025 (permitted to ride work/mock race) and caution Jockey A. Sandesh  for the same.
