RACE NO.5(28) – THE  M.H.AHMEDBHOY TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners – 6

 

HAY DAY (C.S.Jodha) lugged out in the straight. Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey A.Sandesh,  rider of  NEGOTIATOR (Placed Third) against AMISTAD (S.Zervan, Placed Second). The reason being, AMISTAD had tightened him before the bend and near 500 metres came on to him and knocked him out thereby breaking his horse’s stride totally and then again came on to him before the winning post, which cost him the second place.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection and forfeit the deposit.

 

After the race, MOUNTAIN FORCE, AMISTAD and HAY DAY were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(29) – THE  ASTANA PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 15 

 

Jockey C.S.Jodha (MZURI, dr.15) shifted in sharply after the start and went across the entire field on his inside and in the process carried BY THE WAY (Altaf Sayyed) inwards. Jockey C.S.Jodha has been fined Rs 3,000/-.

 

Trainer Karthik G and Jockey Zameer Sayyed were questioned regarding the running and riding of SPLENDID LIGHT. The Trainer stated that the Instructions were to be in mid-bunch, improve gradually and to ride out in the straight. The Jockey stated that he was asked to be in 5th or 6th or 7th position and to do his best. He added that he had done his best. The race was viewed and on questioning, the Trainer stated that the Jockey was not vigorous in the straight. The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards interviewed the Trainer Karthik G and Jockey Zameer Sayyed and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of the race, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Zameer Sayyed. The Stewards noted that he did not make sufficient effort to sit in mid bunch as instructed and coming into the straight he did not give the horse a clear run and made his move very late when the race was nearly over and he was not vigorous in his riding effort. However, considering his plea that he had injured his foot in the starting gate, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided to give him the benefit of the doubt for the charge of “not allowing his mount to run on its merits” and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 15th December, 2013 to 26th December, 2013 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work). Further, he was warned to be more careful in the future.

 

Trainer M.K.Jadhav and Jockey Vikram Walkar were questioned regarding the running and riding of MOHEGUN SUN and not being satisfied with the riding effort of Jockey Vikram Walkar, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards interviewed Tr.M.K.Jadhav and Jockey Vikram Walkar and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of the race, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Vikram Walkar.  The Stewards noted that he was not vigorous in his riding effort in the straight. However, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided to give him the benefit of the doubt for the charge of “unsatisfactory riding” and held him guilty of incompetent riding. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 15th December, 2013 to 19th December, 2013 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work).

 

Jockeys R.Shelar (HIGHWAY EXPRESS) and Altaf Sayyed (BY THE WAY) reported the loss of  their whip near 600 metres.

The filly is not to race for the stipulated period of four weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HIGHWAY EXPRESS, RUN LIKE THE WIND, MOHEGUN SUN, ZARAR, MIDNIGHT LADY and SPLENDID LIGHT were found to be sound.
