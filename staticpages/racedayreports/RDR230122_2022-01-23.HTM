ADDITIONAL REPORT:

 

RE:  GALLOPING ON INNER GRASS TRACK:

 

On a report that on 17th January 2022, Jockey A. Imran Khan had galloped BRAVE EAGLE on the inner portion of the grass track instead of the outer portion.

 

As such, he has been fined Rs.2,000/- for the laps on his part and repetition of a similar offence will be reported to the Stewards of the Club for such action as they deemed fit.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “UNTITLED” (DRAWN 6) (DIST. 1000 MTRS.) IN RACE NO. 36 RUN ON 7TH JANUARY 2022 WHERE IT WON AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS RUN IN RACE NO. 19 (DRAWN 10) (DIST. 1000 MTRS.) RUN ON 23RD DECEMBER 2021 WHERE IT WAS UNPLACED :

 

In connection with the captioned matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd January, 2022 heard Jockey D R Shubham and Trainer H.J.Antia and having viewed the video of the races in question i.e. race no.19 & 36.The Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey D R Shubham as also Trainer H.J.Antia and held Jockey D R Shubham guilty of “indifferent riding” on UNTITLED in Race No.19. In that it was noted that despite the poor start Jockey D R Shubham had all the opportunity to improve his position however, he had made no effort to do so due to which the gelding was positioned at the backend of the field contrary to the running pattern in race no 36 where the gelding won. Further the Stewards also took exception to his riding efforts in the straight as the same were found wanting in race no.19. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence from 13th February, 2022 to 20th February, 2022 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE IMPROVED PERFORMANCE OF “UNTITLED” (DRAWN 6) (DIST. 1000 MTRS.) IN RACE NO. 36 RUN ON 7TH JANUARY 2022 WHERE IT WON AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS RUN IN RACE NO. 19 (DRAWN 10) (DIST. 1000 MTRS.) RUN ON 23RD DECEMBER 2021 WHERE IT WAS UNPLACED :

 

In connection with the captioned matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd January, 2022. The Stewards considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Trainer H J Antia and Jockey D.R. Shubham. Having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer H J Antia and held him guilty of inconsistent running / improved performance of his charge UNTITLED in a short span of time.

In that,

i)              The pattern of running of horse UNTITLED was different in both the races, i.e. Race No.19 the gelding was settled at the backend of the field and ran unplaced. Thereafter, in Race No.36 the gelding went to the front and won the race.

ii)             The gelding had raced in a span of 15 days and had  shown a vast  improvement of about (9 lengths from LAGERTHA, 15 lengths from WILD FIRE & 12 lengths from SPARKLING GLORY) all three common horses in Race No. 19;

iii)            In Race No. 19, the eventual winner was a stable mate DILBAR and the comparative odds were DILBAR opened at 7/1 and backed down to 5/1 whereas UNTITLED opened 15/1 and drifted to 30/1;

iv)            In Race No. 36 where the gelding won, the opening odds were 8/1 and got backed down to 6/1;

 

The Stewards also noted his past record wherein action was initiated against him several times for similar offences of Improved/Comparative performance of his horses and keeping in mind the betting trend of both the races, the Stewards of the Club decided to fine Trainer H J Antia a sum of Rs.1,00,000/- for the improved performance of his charge UNTITLED in Race No.36 run on 7th January, 2022 as compared to its previous run in Race No.19 run on 23rd December, 2021.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SUSSING” (DRAWN 10) IN RACE NO. 37  – THE TASAWWUR PLATE (DIV.II) (DIST. 1600 MTRS.) RUN ON 7TH JANUARY 2022 :

 

In connection with the captioned matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd January, 2022. The Stewards considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Jockey D R Shubham and Trainer H.J. Antia. Having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey D R Shubham and held him guilty of“unsatisfactory riding” on SUSSING. In that, when he entered the straight, he was not vigorous and his riding was found wanting thus depriving his mount the chance to obtain the best possible placing in the race. The Stewards noted that since his Jockey’s license already stands suspended w.e.f. 13th February, 2022 to 20thFebruary, 2022 for indifferent riding on UNTITLED. Therefore, his Jockey’s license will stand further suspended with effect from 27th February, 2022 to 20th March, 2022 (both days inclusive) for unsatisfactory riding on SUSSING in Race No.37 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SUSSING” (DRAWN 10) IN RACE NO. 37– THE TASAWWUR PLATE (DIV.II) (DIST. 1600 MTRS.) RUN ON 7TH JANUARY 2022 :

 

In connection with the captioned matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd January, 2022. The Stewards considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Trainer H J Antia and Jockey D.R. Shubham. Having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer H J Antia and held him guilty of unsatisfactory running of his charge SUSSING. In that, the Stewards relying on their own observation of the race in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards note in connection with captioned matter vis-à-vis the submissions made by him in his defense, were in agreement with the findings of the Stipendiary Stewards and were of the opinion that Jockey D.R.Shubham would not have ridden in the manner he did without Trainer H J Antia approval thus depriving his charge the chance to obtain the best possible placing in the race.

 

The Stewards having taken an overall view with regards to the running and riding of his horses in the past, were of the strong view that despite severe disciplinary action taken against him , the same has not deterred him from transgressing the Rules of Racing & Calendar Notifications repeatedly therefore, decided to suspend his Trainer’s licence from 7th February 2022 to 8th March 2022 and also to fine him a sum of Rs.5,00,000/- for unsatisfactory running of his charge SUSSING in Race No.37 run on 7th January, 2022.
