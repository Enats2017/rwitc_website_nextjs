RACE NO.1 (79) – THE PRADEEP VIJAYAKAR PLATE: (ABOUT) 1800 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

On arrival at the starting gate FLOYD (T. S. Jodha) was observed to have a gum injury. The gelding was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., who certified him fit to race.

 

GOODFELLOW (K. Nazil, dr.9) planted and lost considerable ground at the start. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to race.

 

KIMIKO (A. Sandesh, dr.8) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, NOSTALGIA (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in thereby tightening OH KAY (Amyn Merchant) racing along the rails who had to check and change course thereby bumping and carrying KIMIKO (A. Sandesh) outwards. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (NOSTALGIA) reported that passing the 400 metres he was held up for room until passing the 200 metres.

 

Jockey T. S. Jodha (FLOYD) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, FLOYD & ATATURK were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (80) – THE MATHRADAS GOCULDAS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 3

 

After the race, BABY BAZOOKA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3 (81) – THE ROYAL BARBERSHOP TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS – 7

 

PRINCE IGOR was not declared to start after accepting with the gelding due to late realization of medication that had been logged. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been fined Rs.5,000/- for his lapse and the gelding was subjected to sampling.

 

At the request of Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain, ARIYANA STAR was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. at 11:00 a.m. on 21st January 2025, at the stables and was found to be sore by right fore with painful swelling on fetlock, as such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was permitted to be withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, MILA (Vivek G., dr.8) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

At the start, SENTINEL (Bhawani Singh, dr.5) jumped outwards at the same time MILLI (K. G. Likith Appu, dr.7) jumped inwards thereby inconveniencing COIN EMPRESS (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.6) drawn between them, who had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, MILA (Vivek G.) leaned inwards thereby tightening JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe) racing along the rails, who had to be steadied. Jockey Vivek G. has been cautioned.

 

It was observed that Jockey Bhawani Singh (SENTINEL) maintained an erratic course in the straight wherein approaching the 400 metres, SENTINEL shifted out under urgings and went across DEMETRIUS (S. Zervan) who had to check and change course resulting in COIN EMPRESS (App. Mustakim Alam) to shy outwards. Thereafter, approaching the 200 metres, SENTINEL leaned inwards onto WALTER (N. B. Kuldeep) who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Bhawani Singh has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

In the final stages of the race, DEMETRIUS (S. Zervan) shifted in thereby tightening and bumping SENTINEL (Bhawani Singh) who in turn shifted in thereby bumping and unbalancing WALTER (N. B. Kuldeep) racing along the rails who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey S. Zervan has been fined Rs.10,000/-.

 

It was observed that passing the 200 metres, MILA (Vivek G.) shifted out under the use of whip thereby carrying JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe) and MILLI (K. G. Likith Appu) outwards. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Vivek G. has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that MILA was declared to run without bandages on both fore but ran with bandages. Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy has been fined Rs.1,000/- for her lapse.

 

Jockey K. G. Likith Appu (MILLI) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, COIN EMPRESS was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by left fore. The gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SENTINEL was observed to have an injury on right fore fetlock.

 

After the race, WALTER was trotted and found to be sound.

RACE NO.6 (84) – THE ROYAL BARBERSHOP TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

At the start, NORTHERN SINGER (Shrikant Kamble, dr.1) took an awkward jump and stumbled thereby dislodging the rider.

 

At the start, ART COLLECTOR (A. Sandesh, dr.5) jumped inwards and made contact with HOUSE OF LORDS (App. S. Saba, dr.4).

 

At the start, THE GENERAL (Bhawani Singh, dr.9) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, JET TYPHOON (Amyn Merchant) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing ETOILE (Vishal N. Bunde) racing along the rails, who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Amyn Merchant has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “LIAM” (DRAWN 1) IN RACE NO.56 - THE INDIAN AIR FORCE TROPHY (DIST. 1200 MTRS.) RUN ON 5TH JANUARY 2025:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 23rd January 2025, having heard the submissions made by App. Bharat Singh, his spokesperson Mr. Bajrang Singh, Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race in question and the previous races of the gelding i.e. Race nos.81 & 140 and having deliberated in the matter at length, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and held him guilty of riding an incompetent race. In that, his efforts were found wanting in the initial part of the race wherein he was sitting four lengths off the second last horse thereby placing his mount at a disadvantage. The Stewards considering the fact that this is his first year as an Apprentice Jockey, took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for one race day i.e. on 9th February 2025 (permitted to ride work/mock race).
