RACE NO.2(70) – THE DAMODARDAS C.SHAH TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 8

 

After being stalled, EXCELLENT TIMES (A.Sandesh) reared up and flipped over and came out from the underneath of the rear gate and bolted. As such, the gelding was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s order.  FURIOSA  (App.Niranjan S.Parmar) who was in the adjoining stall was also taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr.V.O. who certified the mare fit to race.  Trainer S.S.Shah has been asked to give his charge EXCELLENT TIMES more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to race.

 

HODSON’S HORSE (S.S.Baria) was reluctant to be stalled and thereafter was restless in the stall thereby not allowing the rider to settle.  AUGURY (App.Gagandeep Mann) jumped out slow losing about 4 lengths at the start. Trainers Adhirajsingh Jodha and Altaf Hussain have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching 400 metres, FLAMMEUS (App. K. Nazil) shifted in thereby inconveniencing PANARAEA (S.A.Amit). On being questioned after the race, App. K. Nazil stated that his mount hung in despite his efforts. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

Passing 300 metres, ANNIVERSARY GIRL (Sunny Chinoy) who was racing between FLAMMEUS (App. K. Nazil) and HODSON’S HORSE (S. S. Baria), was held up for room and had to be steadied. After questioning all concerned, Jockey S. S. Baria has been cautioned.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that HODSON’S HORSE showed signs of distress after returning from the race. After cold water hosing, the gelding recovered.

 

After the race, EXCELLENT TIMES (withdrawn at the start), was observed to have a deep cut injury on the right fore fetlock. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FLAMMEUS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(71) – THE JEHANGIR P.DUBASH TROPHY:(About) 1400 Metres–Runners - 8 

 

Passing 600 metres, AURODEN (Akshay) weakened and dropped back, due to which QUESTIONABLE (P. S. Chouhan) who was racing on its haunches, had to be checked and change course thereby carrying ZANZIBAAR (App.A.S.Peter) outwards.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPLAINT LODGED AGAINST TRAINER SHAZAAN SHAH BY PERMISSION BOY SARFARAZ :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Friday, 21st December, 2018  interviewed Trainer Shazaan Shah in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club having heard Trainer Shazaan Shah and Mr.Sarfaraz (Permission Boy), were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer Shazaan Shah and held him guilty of gross misconduct under Rule 114 (b) of the Rules of Racing. In that,

 

1)            As stated by Riding Boy Sarfaraz, this was not the first instance that Trainer Shazaan Shah has manhandled him.

 

2)            The Stewards did not accept his explanation that he felt that the Permission Boy was faking illness to avoid work and his only intention was to discipline him. He was aware that in such a scenario his recourse was to lodge a written complaint and he had no right to take law into his own hands.

 

3)            The Stewards noted this was Trainer Shazaan Shah’s second case of gross misconduct, in the earlier incident that took place on 3rd January, 2017, the Stewards had suspended his Trainer’s license w.e.f. 7th January, 2017 to 22nd January, 2017 and had imposed on him a suspended sentence of 1 month over the next five years and he was told that if he was found guilty of any act of misbehavior / misconduct within the next five years, then the aforesaid suspended sentence of 30 days would immediately come into force  in addition to any other punishment which the Stewards may impose on him for any subsequent act of his as regards the aforesaid.

 

Taking an overall view and keeping in mind that Trainer Shazaan Shah had admitted his guilt and apologized for the same and assured the Stewards that he would never misbehave in the future, the Stewards decided :-

 

a)            For the present case, to suspend his Trainer’s license for 8 Race Days from  24th December, 2018 to 24th January, 2019 for gross misconduct under Rule 114 (b) of the Rules of Racing;

 

b)            To invoke the suspended sentence of one month imposed on him by the Stewards of the Club on 5th January, 2017 and to further suspend his Trainer’s license from 25th January, 2019 up to and inclusive of 24th February, 2019. 

 

c)             Further, he was cautioned to control his temper and that any acts of misbehavior / misconduct by him in the future, will be severely dealt with by the Stewards which may include withdrawal of his license.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd December, 2018  considered letter dated 22nd December, 2018 from Trainer Shazaan Shah requesting for a stay on the above punishment imposed on him for gross misconduct and to start his punishment from March, 2019. The Stewards of the Club having deliberated on the matter regretted their ability to accede to his request.

RE : APPEAL FROM  JOCKEY A.SANDESH AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE FOR 2 RACE DAYS I.E. FROM 30TH DECEMBER, 2018 TO 3RD JANUARY, 2019 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR BEING GUILTY OF RIDING AN ILL-JUDGED RACE ON HIS MOUNT “EYES FOR YOU” IN RACE NO.164 – THE GERMAN CHARITY DAY TROPHY (DIV.I) RUN ON 27TH OCTOBER, 2018 AT THE PUNE RACE COURSE : 

 

The Board of Appeal at their meeting held on Thursday, 20th December 2018, considered Appeal dated 14th December 2018 from Jockey A.Sandesh in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal having studies all documents and papers that were submitted including Jockey A.Sandesh’s Grounds of Appeal, and having heard the submissions made by Jockey A.Sandesh and his spokesman, Mr.Narendra Lagad and having viewed the video tape recording of the race in question i.e. Race No.164 as also other races, deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds, gave him the benefit of doubt and decided to allow his appeal and refund the deposit.
