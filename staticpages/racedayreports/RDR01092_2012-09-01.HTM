RACE NO.1 (97) – THE  AIR DASH PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 7 Runners

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that whilst approaching the starting gates, VICTOR IVANOV was observed to be coming sore, On examination, the gelding was confirmed to be sore from the right fore leg. As such VICTOR IVANOV was withdrawn on veterinary grounds before coming under the Starter’s Order. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar (BY THE WAY, dr.8) has been fined Rs.2000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker and in the process hampered the running of SURPRISE GIFT (J.E.Pereira).

 

Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar reported that his mount “BY THE WAY” started to drop out near about 600 metres and did not respond to his urgings and became uninterested in the race. Trainer Anil Kumar when questioned stated that he had no explanation to offer for the poor performance of his charge. The Trainer has opted to scope the horse.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that at the request of the Trainer, BY THE WAY was scoped at the Equine Hospital after the race and  reported to have EIPH Grade-III.  Also, the gelding had a cut injury at the right hind coronet. BY THE WAY is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, XENIA was observed to have a cut injury at the right hock and AMAZING SMILE had a cut injury at the left hind pastern.

RACE NO.6(102) – THE RADIO ONE RUN: (About) 1000 Metres – 9 Runners

 

At the start, SMART STRIDER (B.Sreekanth, dr.7) jumped awkwardly inwards and caused STRINGSOFMYHEART (Imran Chisty, dr.6) to shift in and at the same time VICTORIOUS MARCH (A.Sandesh, dr.5) shifted out after an awkward jump and made contact with STRINGSOFMYHEART.

 

SMART STRIDER (B.Sreekanth) lugged-in in the final 200 metres and caused VICTORIOUS MARCH (A.Sandesh) to shift in, who in turn hampered the running of STRINGSOFMYHEART (Imran Chisty). Trainer Karthik G. has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

  

An objection was lodged by Jockey Imran Chisty, rider of STRINGSOFMYHEART (Placed Fourth) against VICTORIOUS MARCH (A.Sandesh, Placed Third). The reason being, at the start, VICTORIOUS MARCH (A.Sandesh) took him out and again in the last 250 metres he kept coming in when his horse was full of running, whereby he had to stop riding and check his mount which cost him a certain place.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule the objection.

 

After the race, VICTORIOUS MARCH was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind fetlock and pastern. LAKE PARADISE and FORTUNER were found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “BLUSH” IN RACE NO.21 – THE AGNI PLATE (DIV.II) (1200 METRES RUN ON SATURDAY, 4TH AUGUST, 2012 WHICH THE MARE WON AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS RACES DURING THE MUMBAI MEETING 2011/12 :

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad was questioned in the above connection. He said that BLUSH ran eleven times during the Mumbai Meeting 2011/12, having won one race and finished fourth twice including his last run i.e. Race No.378. It was pointed out to Trainer Narendra Lagad that there was a common horse viz. ARABIAN CHIEF who in Race No.378 had finished 2nd whereas BLUSH had finished fourth, more than 8 ½ lengths behind ARABIAN CHIEF, however, in Race No.21 BLUSH had beaten ARABIAN CHIEF by 3 ½ lengths, thereby showing an improvement of over 12 lengths. Trainer Narendra Lagad reiterated that BLUSH ran eleven times in Mumbai and it was the mare’s eleventh run in No.378 and she may have been tired at the end of the season. Also, the mare has won just one race in Mumbai and she enjoys Pune Weather and she was looking good and working well and was racing after three months. He added that BLUSH was a six year old mare and she was an ordinary Class V mare and he did not expect phenomenal improvement from older horses. When asked as to on what basis he had fancied the mare in Race No.21, Tr.Narendra Lagad said that the field was weak and BLUSH was working well and looking good as she always does at the beginning of the Pune Season and therefore he had fancied her in this race. To another question, he said that there was no change of equipment.

 

The explanation was noted.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “STEP UP” IN RACE NO.57 – THE GOODNESS GRACIOUS PLATE (1600 METRES) RUN ON SUNDAY, 12TH AUGUST, 2012 :

 

Trainer Dallas Todywalla and Jockey C.Rajendra were questioned in the above connection. Trainer Todywalla said that STEP UP is no great filly, she has won only one race over 1600 metres earlier beating NIGELLA and MZIMA who have both not performed subsequently. However, he admitted that the filly had performed poorly in this race and therefore, he had the horse scoped by Dr.Khambatta the same evening as he normally does with his other runners who run below form. He said that as per the Endoscopy Report the horse was found to have Gr.II Mucopus GP, thick white in the Pharynx which had to be flushed out the next day. He said that he had instructed the Jockey to be well up and if nobody hits the front then to make the running and to do his best. He said that the horse was travelling well till about 500 metres at which time the Jockey started pushing the horse but instead of improving, the horse started dropping back at the 400 metres. Trainer Dallas Todywalla opined that the only reason that comes to his mind for this poor performance, may be the Mucopus GP in the Pharynx, the filly may possibly have chocked at that stage and dropped back. He said that the filly had worked well and she had not coughed nor was there any nasal discharge before the race to give an indication that something was wrong with her or else he would not have run her.

 

Jockey C.Rajendra confirmed the instructions and added that the filly travelled well till 600 metres and when he started pushing her near about 500 metres she dropped back suddenly and he felt that something may have been wrong with her and after the race had informed the Trainer to have her examined. He said that he had worked the filly and she had worked well.

 

The explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted..

RE ; ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPLAINT LODGED BY TRAINER P.SHROFF AGAINST JOCKEY P.TREVOR FOR NOT FOLLOWING HIS INSTRUCTIONS ON “STROMBOLI” IN RACE NO.42 – THE CABRIOLET PLATE RUN ON 11TH AUGUST, 2012 :

 

Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey P.Trevor were questioned in the above connection.  Trainer P.Shroff said that everyone on the racecourse is aware that if AGNI BAAN hits the front and takes the rails, it is very difficult  horse to beat. He said that he had studied the opposition and discussed the riding strategy with Jockey P.Trevor so as to get the best out of his filly and he had instructed Jockey P.Trevor to take a good jump and first 100 to 200 metres, to get into her and try to take over the running and if some other horse goes faster then try to be in 2nd position and do his best.

 

Jockey P.Trevor confirmed the instructions. He said that the filly   jumped out well but threw her head up and he received slight interference and therefore he settled her and before the final bend he improved to third position and did his best.

 

Trainer P.Shroff confirmed that Jockey P.Trevor had informed him after the race that the filly threw her head up at the start. He said that this filly has the habit to throw her head up slightly on jump out and she did the same thing in her subsequent run (Race No.89) also.  However, there was no attempt from Jockey P.Trevor to hit the front or sit second as instructed despite having discussed the riding strategy with him in detail. Trainer P.Shroff added that in Mumbai this filly used to be ridden from the back but with a change in her temperament and mental attitude,  he felt that the best way to ride her now was to allow her to run with the flow. He opined that the filly did not deserve to be favourite in this race as he felt that AGNI BAAN was better and yet STROMBOLI was the public choice.

 

Jockey P.Trevor had no further explanation and assured that in future he would follow the instructions.

 

The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Saturday, 1st September, 2012 interviewed Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey P.Trevor and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of the race, were not entirely satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey P.Trevor. However,  keeping in mind that at the start the horse threw its head up and thereafter he was covered up by other horses, the Stewards decided to give him the benefit of the doubt in this case. However, he was cautioned to make greater efforts to follow his instructions in the future.
