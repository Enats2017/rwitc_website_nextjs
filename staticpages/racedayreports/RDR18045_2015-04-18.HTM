RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “LOVEABLE LOCKS” RIDDEN BY VIKRAM JODHA IN RACE NO 328 : THE RUSTOMJI BYRAMJI TROPHY – DIV II RUN ON 12TH APRIL 2015

 

Trainer H.J.Antia and Jockey Vikram Jodha were questioned in the above connection. It was pointed out to Jockey Vikram Jodha that in the final 300 metres he had unnecessarily shifted out under the use of the whip and did not attempt to take the gaps that came his way and instead continued to shift out and lost valuable ground, considering the short verdict of ¾ of a length between LOVEABLE LOCKS (Placed 6th) and the 4th Place horse POLAR ZONE. The Trainer concurred with the observation made by the Stipendiary Stewards. After enquiring into the matter and not being satisfied with the explanation of the Jockey, he was charged with Incompetent Riding. Considering his request that he did not wish to appeal against this decision and that his suspension may start early, it was decided to suspend his Jockey’s License for 26th April 2015.     

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF UN-NAMED 3-YEAR-OLD b g “EX - BE FRIENDLY” THE URINE SAMPLE WAS COLLECTED DURING THE RANDOM SAMPLING FOR THE MONTH OF DECEMBER, 2014 WHICH WAS REPORTED POSITIVE FOR “BOLDENONE” – UNER THE CHARGE OF TRAINER ARIF HUSSAIN :

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 18th April, 2015 interviewed Trainer Arif Hussain in the above connection. Having thoroughly enquired into the matter into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and also having permitted him to make submissions in the matter, the Stewards found that he had no mitigating circumstances to offer in the subject case and held him vicariously responsible for breach of the Medication Rules governing the finding of BOLDENONE, an anabolic steroid, in the urine sample of unnamed 3-year-old Ex – Be Friendly.

                                                                                                                                                                               

Taking an overall view and keeping in mind that this was his first infringement of the Medication Rules, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided :-

a)       to suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 90 days i.e. Should he apply for a license for the Season 2015/16, it will be granted to him, however, his license will stand suspended w.e.f. 23rd July, 2015 to 20th October, 2015 (both days inclusive).

b)       To fine him a sum of Rs.10,000/-.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ARMEL” IN RACE NO.285 – THE JEHAN NUMA PLATE RUN ON THURSDAY, 26TH MARCH, 2015 – TRAINER H.J.ANTIA AND JOCKEY HANUMANT SINGH :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on Sunday, 5th April, 2015 and thereafter on Saturday, 18th April, 2015 interviewed Trainer H.J.Antia and Jockey Hanumant Singh in reference to the captioned subject matter.

 

The Stewards considered the submissions made by you and viewed the video recording of the race in question as also the horse’s previous run i.e. Race No.197 where the horse had finished a short-head second. By the Trainer’s own admission, the normal style of running of ARMEL was to hit the front or be well  up, however, in Race No.285 the horse was ridden contrary to his running pattern, which, according to him was one of the reasons for the horse performing poorly. The trainer stated that the instructions were given by the Owner and he does not go against his owners even if he was not in agreement with them.

 

The owner confirmed to the Stipendiary Stewards that the instructions were given by him in Race No.285 to sit in 4th or 5th position since the horse carried top weight and in the previous race he went to the front and stopped in the last part. Keeping in mind the drift of odds and the poor performance of the horse, the Stewards took exception to this and expressed their dissatisfaction.

 

Attention of Trainer H.J.Antia was drawn to Rule 167 ( c ) of the Rules of Racing of the Club and he was warned that in future, if he wishes to change the running tactics of a horse, he must inform the Stipendiary Stewards before the race.

 

Further, attention of the Owner was also drawn to Rule 25 of the Rules of Racing of the Club and he was advised that if he wished to change the running tactics of his horse, the same should be brought to the notice of the Stipendiary Stewards before the race.
