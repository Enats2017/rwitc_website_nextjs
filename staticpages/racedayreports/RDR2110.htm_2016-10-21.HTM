ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RIDING OF APP.S.A.AMIT ON “SINDBAAD” IN RACE NO.103 – THE GIRONDE PLATE RUN ON 10th SEPTEMBER, 2016 :

 

Trainer Nirad Karanjawala complained against App. S. A. Amit, stating that he was dissatisfied with the riding of the Apprentice on his charge “SINDBAAD”.

 

Trainer Nirad Karanjawala stated that he had instructed the Apprentice to go to the front if he can or else sit second, take over the running once in the straight, keep the whip in the left hand as the gelding has the tendency to drift out and to ride out using the whip to good measure. App. S. A. Amit confirmed the instructions.

 

The race in question was viewed in the presence of all concerned.  Trainer Karanjawala pointed out that he only took exception to the fact that the Apprentice did not use the whip as instructed, other than that he could not find fault with his riding efforts. Further, he stated that the Apprentice has ridden the gelding in a race during the last Mumbai Meeting while he was yet a 5 kg allowance claimer and not permitted the use of whip. After the said race, the Apprentice had informed him that the gelding does not respond without whipping. The Trainer therefore felt that despite knowing the horse’s habit, not using the whip was very suspicious. Trainer Karanjawala went on to add that in the very next race on the same day App. S. A. Amit rode a horse called “OLLY BOY” on whom he used the whip 8 times, thus establishing the fact that he is competent with the whip. When questioned about the same, App. S. A. Amit stated that he thought he had won the race not realizing he had a horse on his outside. To a specific question App. S. A. Amit stated that he had every intention to win the race.

 

After hearing all concerned and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. S. A. Amit, his Apprentice Jockey’s license has been suspended for a period of 4 days for unsatisfactory riding.

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING  OF “PALATIAL” (DRAWN.11) IN RACE NO.104 - THE ROSMINI PLATE, RUN ON SATURDAY 10th SEPTEMBER, 2016:

Trainer C. D. Katrak and Jockey Amyn Merchant were questioned regarding the running and riding of his charge PALATIAL in the above race. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned on 15th September, 2016 Trainer C. D. Katrak said that he had instructed Jockey Amyn Merchant to sit wherever he could and to do his best. He said that he had fancied the horse in Place and would have liked the Jockey to have been a little more vigorous in the last 100 metres as he felt that he was a little weak in his riding effort which possibly cost him the third place. He added that due to the wide false rails, the track was a bit narrow at the start and had placed his horse at a disadvantage since he was drawn on the outside.

Jockeyk Amyn Merchant had fractured his collar bone and was not available for questioning.

Jockey Amyn Merchant, when questioned on 7th October, 2016, confirmed the instructions and stated that his mount had to be ridden from the word go as such he was a little tired in the straight. Also, the kick back may have affected the horse.

The video of the race was viewed and Jockey Amyn Merchant was asked as to why he travelled wide after the start, to which he replied that he was drawn on the outside and he was not able to bring the horse in. Thereafter, coming into the straight, he had clipped the front runner and shifted in. It was pointed out to Jockey Amyn Merchant that he had ridden his horse into the front horse causing the horse to clip. Trainer C. D. Katrak and Jockey Amyn Merchant concurred with the Stipes view in this regard.

After hearing the submissions and viewing the video of the race and keeping in mind the Trainer’s statement that the horse had lost a certain third place, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Amyn Merchant and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for two race days for incompetent riding. The suspension will take effect from the first Race Day of the Mumbai Meeting 2016/17 (permitted to ride work/mock race).
