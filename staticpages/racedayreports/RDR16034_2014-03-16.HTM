RACE DAY REPORTS

RACE NO.1(265) – THE P.R.MEHTA TROPHY: (About) 2400 Metres – Runners  - 4

 

SNOWING (A.Sandesh) was inclined to lug-in in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

RACE NO.2(266) –THE  PRONTO PRONTO TROPHY: (About) 1800 Metres –Runners – 5 

 

Trainer Nazak Chenoy and Jockey Dashrath Singh were questioned regarding the poor performance of COMMANDER. Trainer Nazak Chenoy stated that the Instructions to the Jockey were to sit in a forward position if  the pace was slow, otherwise ride from a comfortable position. She added that the horse had a lot of weight to give around and that the horse had not sweated after the race as the gelding may have felt the heat. She also opined that the horse lacked the condition coming into this race. She added that the gelding has not liked the Mumbai Track and factually the horse has won only one race on this Track that too during the current Mumbai Meeting otherwise the gelding has done well on the Pune Track. She further added that she was satisfied with the Jockey’s riding. Jockey Dashrath Singh confirmed the instructions and added that he had done his best. Explanation was noted.

 

After the race, COMMANDER was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(267)– THE  COLOURS OF HOLI TROPHY:(About) 1600 Metres – Runners – 7

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that on the way to the starting gate, ICE COOL was observed to be trotting sore by the left fore. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and hence withdrawn on veterinary grounds before coming under the Starter’s Order. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey S.Mosin (VIXEN) has been cautioned for allowing his mount to shift-in in the final 100 metres.

 

Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed was questioned regarding the poor performance of his charge INFALLIBLE. The Trainer stated that he himself was disappointed with the gelding’s poor run. He said that the instructions to the Jockey were to sit in 3rd / 4th position and to do his best in the straight. He added that the horse has soundness problems. He further said that the gelding ran poorly in Race No 90 also and hence he had changed the equipment i.e. he removed the Tongue Straps and put on the Blinkers. The explanation was noted.

 

After the race, VIXEN, MUMBAI KING, WORD OF HONOUR and INFALLIBLE were found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORT/S:

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RIDING OF JOCKEY S.ZERVAN ON “APACHE” IN RACE NO.261 – THE SAHACHARI FOUNDATION CUP RUN ON 13TH MARCH, 2014 – AFTER A STEWARDS ENQUIRY, APACHE WAS DECLARED A NON STARTER :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 16th March, 2014 interviewed Jockey S.Zervan in the above connection. They considered all papers and documents and having heard all the submissions made by Jockey S.Zervan, including his letter dated 15th March, 2014 in reply to the Show Cause and after viewing the video recording of Race No.261 as also the incident of the whip fall, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.Zervan. The Stewards noted he waited far too long to make his move in the straight, which he himself admitted. Even when JACK FROST came alongside up to his quarters, he still kept waiting and thereafter lost his whip  at a crucial part of the race and the fall of the whip looked suspicious. The Stewards noted that Trainer P.Shroff also concurred with the Stewards view that Jockey S.Zervan had made his move late which may have cost him the race. The Stewards noted Jockey S.Zervan’s past record wherein action was taken against him for similar offences.

However,  taking an overall view and keeping in mind that there was no drift in the odds of APACHE, the Stewards decided to give him the benefit of grave doubt for the charge of “unsatisfactory riding” although the horse was declared a Non-starter and held him guilty of injudicious and over confident riding which may have cost him the race. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 5th April, 2014 to 27th April, 2014 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: POSITIVE FINDING IN RESPECT OF “HIDDEN ASSET” (WINNER) IN RACE NO 177 – THE ROYAL CHALLENGE ‘GAME FOR LOVE’ CUP (DIV –II) RUN ON 2ND FEBRUARY 2014 – REPORTED POSITIVE FOR “PENTAZOCINE”:

 

As per the Medication Rules, Trainer Mansoor Shah was required to inform to the Sr.Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, within five days of opening the envelope that identifies the sample i.e. latest by 11th March, 2014 whether he wished the split sample of your charge HIDDEN ASSET to go to The Hong Kong Jockey Club Lab or L.C.H., France,, for Confirmatory Analysis and also the name of the Witnessing Analyst, if any, failing which the Stewards shall treat the report of the Primary Laboratory as conclusive. However, Trainer Mansoor Shah failed to do so as stated above.

 

In view of the above, as per the Medication Rules,  the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 16th March, 2014 decided to treat the report dated 28th February, 2014 from the Primary Laboratory i.e. HFL Sport Science, England, in respect of the sample of urine taken from HIDDEN ASSET (Winner) in Race No.177 – The Royal Challenge ‘Game For Life’ Cup (Div.II) run on 2nd February, 2014, as conclusive.

 

Further, as per the Medication Rules,  the Stewards of the Club decided to disqualify HIDDEN ASSET (Winner) in Race No.177– The Royal Challenge ‘Game For Life’ Cup run on 2nd February, 2014 and to revise the order of placing of the above race as under :-
