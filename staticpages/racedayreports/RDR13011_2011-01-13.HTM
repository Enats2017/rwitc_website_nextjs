RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “GAMBA” IN RACE NO.103 – THE NEW YEAR TROPHY (DIV.II) RUN ON SUNDAY, 2ND JANUARY, 2011 WHERE THE MARE FINISHED SECOND, AS COMPARED TO HER PREVIOUS RUNS I.E. RACE NOS.17, 43 & 61 WHERE THE MARE WAS UNPLACED :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Faisal A.Abbas in the above connection.  He said that he had given similar instructions in all the races i.e. to sit well up and to do the best. He explained that in the very first outing in Race No.17 GAMBA had finished fourth and thereafter the mare ran off Board in the next two outings. He said that in his opinion the mare had not shown improvement in Race No.103 but she looked good as the set of horses in this race was mediocre, except for STERLING SILVER, the other horses had no form and RIVER GEM who was also fancied had finished about 15 lengths behind the winner in its earlier run whereas GAMBA had finished only 6 lengths behind in Race No.61.. Also, if STERLING SILVER had not hung out in the straight, GAMBA would have finished behind the actual verdict. He added that if this race had not been divided, than the winner STERLING SILVER and GAMBA may not have finished in the first six. Further,  if GAMBA was in Div.I, she may not have figured on Board as the time clocked by STERLING SILVER was  1 Min 13.51 seconds and the winner of Div.I CAPE FERRAT clocked 1 Min 12.52 seconds and the horse who finished 6th in Div.I clocked 1 Min 13.42 seconds whilst GAMBA clocked 1 Min 13.54 seconds. He said that he has run GAMBA over different distances to get the best out of the mare but she has not shown any form and she was available at longer odds in this race.

 

The explanation tendered by Trainer Faisal A.Abbas was noted.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “LADY GENEREUSE” IN RACE NO.115 – THE  FRIDA KAHLO PLATE (1200 METRES)  RUN ON FRIDAY, 7TH JANUARY, 2011 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Adil Dajee and Jockey Amyn Merchant in the above connection. Trainer Adil Dajee said that LADY GENEREUSE is a well bred filly, however, she has no past form, she ran six times in Pune, in most races finishing in the rear bunch and her best performance was in her last run in Pune over 1000 metres where she had finished fifth, about 10 lengths from the winner. However, after coming from Pune the filly had become much fitter and was working well and on the basis of her work outs and considering that the field was very poor with no horse having any past form, he as also the Jockeys who worked her, had fancied her to win this race and had informed the Owner and their friends and even the Time Keepers had tipped her to win this race and possibly that was the reason she ran an On Money favourite. However, the filly ran a very disappointing race, she did not produce her work outs in the race and even Jockey Amyn Merchant was shocked at her performance. Tr.Dajee said that looking at her breeding he now feels that probably she may need a longer trip. To a further question, he said that on the advice of his Jockeys, he had used a Tongue Strap on this filly since she has the habit of playing with the bit and had also used Blinkers since she had worked well with Blinkers. He said that the filly is absolutely sound, however, after the race in question he felt that the filly was slightly sore and had requested the Club’s Sr.V.O. to examine her, however, the Sr.V.O.  has certified the filly sound.

When questioned, Jockey Amyn Merchant said that the filly had shown no form in Pune, however, after coming to Mumbai she had worked well with Tongue Strap and Blinkers and considering that the field in this race was very poor, he had expected her to win this race. He said that the filly ran a disappointing race, she did not reproduce her work outs in this race and he has no explanation as to why she ran  poorly.

 

The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 13th January, 2011 considered the above report and decided to keep the enquiry open.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ENDLESS HORIZON” IN RACE NO.116 – THE  M.D.PETIT PLATE (DIV.II) (1000 METRES)  RUN ON FRIDAY, 7TH JANUARY, 2011 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey Stephane Pasquier in the above connection and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Stephane Pasquier with regard to the handling of his mount in the straight, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 13th January, 2011 considered the above report and interviewed Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey Stephane Pasquier.

The video recording of the race was viewed in the presence of Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey Stephane Pasquier.

When questioned, Jockey Stephane Pasquier said that the Trainer had told him that this filly has the habit of jumping out slow from the gates and he should try to take a good start and get a good position and to do his best. He said that the filly was slightly slow out of the gate and near 800 metres she suddenly became keen and he did not wish to travel wide at that stage as it was too early, therefore, he settled her behind the other runners and after entering the straight pulled her out at the 400 metres and did his best. When again asked to explain his riding effort in the straight and why he had not use the whip, Jockey Stephane Pasquier said that he had followed the good horses into the straight and pulled the filly out at the 400 metres and ridden her out. He said that he uses the whip very sparingly on horses as he believes that whipping is not the only medium to get the best out of the horse and he uses the whip mainly as a corrective measure on horses who tend to hang in a race.

When questioned, Trainer P.Shroff confirmed that Jockey Stephane Pasquier uses his whip sparingly and concentrates more in pushing. He said that the Jockey has ridden out the filly and also tapped her on the shoulder felt that the filly was possibly choking under pressure as she has soft pallet displacement problem and he had even had her scoped after the race. He added that she was a well bred filly but she has shown nothing and he was planning to retire her.  He said that he was satisfied with the Jockey’s riding.

The explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was accepted.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “MAGICAL MANTRA” IN RACE NO.117 – THE  WARSAW PACT PLATE (1600 METRES)  RUN ON FRIDAY, 7TH JANUARY, 2011 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha and Jockey C.S.Jodha in the above connection and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey C.S.Jodha with regard to the handling of his mount in the straight, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 13th January, 2011 considered the above report and interviewed Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha and Jockey C.S.Jodha.

The video recording of the race was viewed in the presence of Trainer Magansinhg P.Jodha and Jockey C.S.Jodha. Tr.Magansingh P.Jodha stated that he had instructed the Jockey to settle the horse in 3rd/4th position and coming into the straight to make his move from the outside and do his best. He said that the Jockey has followed his instructions in the early part, however, entering the straight he has remained on the inside instead of pulling the horse out due to which he was pocketed and the horse was pulled out near 200 metres and thereafter the Jockey has ridden out the horse and also DISTANT PROMISE who was in front kept an erratic course. Tr.Magansingh Jodha felt that if the Jockey had made his move from the outside at the right time probably he would have run closer but may not have been able to beat the winner. He said that MAGICAL MANTRA was a 6-year-old horse and he was at longer odds

Jockey C.S.Jodha confirmed the instructions and added that after entering the straight there was a horse on his outside who he felt will drop back and he would get an opening, however, the outside horse kept going and therefore he had no option but to pull his horse out near 200 metres and thereafter he did his best.

When asked as to why he did not take the gap along the rails near 200  metres, Jockey C.S.Jodha said that he was aware that his horse was pocketed and  he had already made up his mind to pull him out at that stage so as to give him a clear run. Also, when the gap appeared the outside horse was inclined to shift in and therefore he made his move from the outside. When asked as to why he did not make use of the whip in the last 50 metres, he said that he did change his whip hand but before he could use the whip the outside horse shifted in and he had no place to go.

The explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “HIGHLAND MUSIC” IN RACE NO.119 – THE  T.M.GOCULDAS PLATE (1000 METRES)  RUN ON FRIDAY, 7TH JANUARY, 2011 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer H.J.Antia and Jockey R.Hughes in the above connection and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey R.Hughes with regard to the handling of his mount in the final stages of the race, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 13th January, 2011 considered the above report and interviewed Trainer H.J.Antia and Jockey R.Hughes.

The video recording of the race was viewed in the presence of Trainer H.J.Antia and Jockey R.Hughes.

When questioned, Jockey R.Hughes said that he rode the horse in the same way as he did in the previous run i.e. Race No.62 which he won. He pointed out that in Race No.62 HIGHLAND MUSIC carried 55.5 kgs. and clocked 59.70 seconds whereas in Race No.119 the horse carried 61.5 kgs. and yet bettered his own time having clocked 58.87 seconds thus showing improvement over his previous run. Further, he pointed out that the course record stands in the name of ARES who carried 50 kgs. and clocked 57.84 seconds but HIGHLAND MUSIC carried 11.5 kgs. extra which in theory mean almost 1.5 seconds off the Track record. Jockey R.Hughes said that he believes that a Jockey cannot make a horse go faster and has to concentrate more on balancing the horse. Also, if he feels that the horse is giving his 100%, there was no need to use the whip. He said that when he first rode the horse in Race No.62, Trainer Antia had told him before the race that this horse has the habit of drifting out and in that race he had to use his whip 2 or 3 times to prevent him from going out but in this race the horse was travelling straight and he was giving his 100%, therefore he did not use the whip and the horse got beat only in the last 50 metres. He further pointed out that his horse had done last 200 metres in 11.13 seconds and he felt that his horse could not have done better than that. He said that he had done his best in Race No.119.

When questioned, Trainer H.J.Antia said that his horse had bettered his own performance considerably from the last start and he felt that the horse had run such a good race only because of Jockey R.Hughes’ handling. He said that he took no exception to the Jockey’s riding.

The explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was accepted.

RE : APPEAL FROM JOCKEY S.SUNIL IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 16TH JANUARY, 2011 TO 27TH MARCH, 2011 FOR NOT ALLOWING HIS MOUNT “ARSHAVIN” TO RUN ON ITS MERITS IN RACE NO.90 – THE VICTORY MARCH PLATE RUN ON SUNDAY, 26TH DECEMBER, 2010 :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 12th January, 2011 considered Appeal dated 3rd January, 2011 from Jockey S.Sunil in the above connection and interviewed him along with his Spokesman, Mr.Y.S.Srinath.

 

The Board of Appeal having considered all documents and papers that were submitted and having heard the submissions made by Jockey S.Sunil and his Spokesman and having  heard Trainer Faisal A.Abbas and having viewed the video tape recording of the race in question, and having deliberated on the matter at length felt that the Jockey had ridden out to the best of his ability and he had stopped whipping when the horse shifted wide out to correct  the horse. The Board was convinced that in the endeavor to win the race that too when he was in the close proximity to the winning post, the jockey did not want to change the whip hand at this crucial juncture and jeopardize his move especially since his contention was that he was not very comfortable in using the whip with his left hand. The Board felt that  there was no mala-fide intention on the part of the jockey. The Board felt that at no stage did the Jockey stop riding and that he had in fact ridden out throughout the race. The Board of Appeal therefore decided to allow his appeal and return his deposit.
