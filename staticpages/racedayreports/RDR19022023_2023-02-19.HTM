ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB.

MUMBAI MEETING 2022/23, TWELFTH DAY, SUNDAY 19TH FEBRUARY 2023

 

(PENETROMETER: 3.9 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WILL BE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES (MILE CHUTE) LEADING TO 2 METRES AWAY AT 1200 METRES, 4 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES, 6 METRES AWAY AT 800 METRES AND THEREAFTER 7 METRES AWAY UP TO THE WINNING POST.

 

FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WILL BE PLACED FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES LEADING TO 2 METRES AWAY AT 1200 METRES AND 4 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES.

 

On a request from Trainer M. K. Jadhav, permission was granted to Trainer A. M. Jadhav to saddle his

charges AME in the 1st race, MOONSHOT in the 3rd race and MUFAZA in the 7th race at his own (Trainer M. K. Jadhav’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO.1 (108) THE DR. A. H. SAYED PLATE DIVISION II  (ABOUT) 1200 METRES. - RUNNERS - 13

 

POWER OF BLESSINGS (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.4) jumped out slowly losing about 4 lengths at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

DEMETRIUS (Bhawani Singh, dr.2) took a fly jump at the start and had to be steadied.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (AME) reported that he was not ready even though he had called out to the Starter and the start was affected. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the matter.

 

After the race, POWER OF BLESSINGS and COUNT THE WINS were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (109) THE INDIAN AIR FORCE TROPHY (ABOUT) 1600 METRES - RUNNERS - 6

 

At the start, LETO (S. Saqlain, dr.4) veered out and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, SIERRA DELA PLATA (Neeraj Rawal) was observed to have stumbled for no apparent reason. However, when questioned the Jockey Neeraj Rawal he felt that he got cramped for room.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, SANDMAN (Vishal N. Bunde) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing SAN SALVATORE (K. Nazil) racing along the rails causing the latter to be checked. After the race on questioning Jockey Vishal N. Bunde stated that his mount was hanging-in in the straight despite his efforts. As the gelding had behaved in a similar manner before, the Stipendiary Stewards wish to interview Trainer Nirad Karanjawala before deciding if the gelding needs to be shown in a mock race.

 

After the race, App. Haridas Gore (NELSON RIVER) reported that his mount was inclined outwards from the 1400 metres up to the 1200 metres, despite his efforts. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to look into the same and take corrective measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Sr. V.O. reported that Tr. Nirad Karanjawala informed Veterinary officer that SANDMAN coughed in the stables in the morning. In view of the same endoscopy was performed on gelding at 9.50 a.m. on 19th February 2023 and after the race no abnormalities were found.

 

RACE NO.3 (110) THE AMATEUR RIDERS' CLUB TROPHY (ABOUT) 1200 METRES  - RUNNERS - 11

 

At the start, LORD VADER (Bhawani Singh, dr.9) was observed to have stumbled thereby dislodging its rider. After the race on questioning Jockey Bhawani Singh confirmed that his mount had stumbled due to which he had got dislodged. The same has been noted.

 

At the start, SOVEREIGN MASTER (S. Saqlain, dr.5) jumped outwards due to which OUTLANDER (App. Haridas Gore, dr. 6) shied out and brushed the haunches of MOONSHOT (N. S. Parmar, dr.7).

 

After the race, MOONSHOT was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.4 (111) THE SONNY BRAR TROPHY  (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

After the race, not being satisfied with the riding of Jockey R. Ajinkya on JERUSALEM, a preliminary enquiry was held, where the Trainer expressed his dissatisfaction of the Jockeys riding. The Stipendiary Stewards are holding a detailed enquiry into the running and riding and the matter will be reported to the Stewards of the Club.

 

After the race, ALPINE STAR and JERUSALEM were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5 (112)  THE LT. COL. GOVIND SINGH (VRC) TROPHY (ABOUT) 1400 METRES - RUNNERS - 9

 

Soon after the start, it was observed that App. Kirtish Bhagat (CAPUCINE) went across (AXLROD) causing the latter to be steadied. App. Kirtish Bhagat was cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockeys S. Zervan (AXLROD) and A. Sandesh (STAR GALLERY) reported that their mounts drifted out in the last 100 metres. After reviewing the race it was found that both had drifted out under the use of the whip. Jockeys S. Zervan and A. Sandesh have been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey Y. S. Srinath (WINTER AGENDA) reported that ALEXANDROS (Neeraj Rawal) kept causing him interference throughout the race. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

RACE NO.6 (113) THE SION F. NESSIM PLATE (ABOUT) 1600 METRES -  RUNNERS -  14

 

Jockey Y. S. Srinath (FREEDOM) informed Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait in the paddock that the saddle on his mount was not the saddle which he weighed out. On investigating it was found that his saddle had got exchanged with the saddle of FANCY NANCY of race no.7, as such both the Jockeys were made to weigh out again. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

SILVER STEPS (H. Zeeshan, dr.13) reared up as the start was effected thereby losing considerable ground at the start. Trainer Shazaan Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at the morning starting trails, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BETWEEN FRIENDS (Amyn Merchant, dr.12) jumped out and thereafter did not raise an effective gallop. Trainer S. S. Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at the morning starting trails, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, STUNNING VISUAL (K. Nazil, dr.14) jumped out slowly losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

App. A. S. Peter (SHADOWS) reported the loss of whip passing the 500 metres.  The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, Sr. V.O. reported that FASHION ICON was declared to run with all aluminum plates. However, on examination before the race the filly was found to have aluminum high heels on both fore. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been fined Rs.1,000/- for the lapse.

 

After the race, SHADOWS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7 (114) The Dr. A. H. Sayed Plate Division I  (ABOUT) 1200 METRES - RUNNERS - 13

 

After the race, Jockey S. Zervan (ARBITRAGE) reported that his mount was held up for room momentarily passing the 400 metres, the same was noted.

 

After the race, Jockey R. Ajinkya (BOMBER) reported that his mount was hanging-in in the straight. The gelding was subjected to veterinary examination and found to be sound. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, PERFECT WIN and BOMBER were trotted and found to be sound, ATTAINED was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.
