RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED 3 METRES AWAY FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES (MILE CHUTE) TO 1200 METRES, 4 METRES AT 1000 METRES AND THEREAFTER 5 METRES AWAY UPTO THE WINNING POST.

 

FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WERE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES LEADING TO 2 METRES AWAY AT 1200 METRES AND 4 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1(160) – THE PONTA DELGADO PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  9

 

At the start, NUSRAT (App. A. S. Peter, dr.6) jumped inwards onto AURELE (M. Ayyar, dr.5) who inturn shifted in thereby forcing MARIELLA (S. Zervan, dr.4) inwards. App A S Peter has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Soon after the start, DAULAT MAI (Akash Agarwal) shifted outwards and made contact with DOWSABEL (App. A. Prakash). Jockey Akash Agarwal has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, DAULAT MAI (Akash Agarwal) shifted in under urging onto ALLIED ATTACK (App. P. Shinde). After questioning Jockey Akash Agarwal has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, App. A. Prakash reported that his mount DOWSABEL hung-in in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After The race, ALLIED ATTACK & DOWSABEL were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(161) – THE M. M. T. PANDOLE PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  3

RACE NO.3(162) – THE RACING JOURNALISTS TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  6

After the race, DESERT FIRE was trotted and found to be sound.

RACE NO.5(164) – THE TAPPED TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  10

 

At the start, COLD PURSUIT (App. A. S. Peter, dr.4) leaned out at the same time MOONSHOT (N. S. Parmar, dr.6) leaned inwards due to which FASSBINDER (R. S. Bhati, dr.5) drawn between them was tightened for room and dropped back.

 

It was observed that RUBIK STAR (C. S. Jodha, dr.9) jumped out awkwardly throwing its head. After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha reported that his mount was trying to veering in after the start and had to be steadied. In view of the behavior, Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey T. S. Jodha rider of MAJORELLA BLUE was observed to have dropped his hands before passing the winning post thereby losing a certain second place. After questioning and not being satisfied with his explanation tendered, as such, he has been suspended for 2 race days i.e. first two race days of Pune Meeting 2022 (permitted to ride work/mock race) (both days inclusive) (dates for the same will be intimated in due course).

 

After the race, FULL OF GRACE (S. Zervan) was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SANDMAN & COLD PURSUIT were trotted and found to be sound and SUNRISE found to be in distress.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “KING’S RANSOM” (DRAWN 4) IN RACE NO. 130 – THE FORBES BREEDERS’ JUVENILE FILLIES’ CHAMPIONSHIP (GR.3) RUN ON 20TH MARCH 2022 :

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 17th April 2022, viewed the video recording of Race No. 130 run on 20th March 2022. They also considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Jockey P. S. Chouhan through Video calling and Trainer P. Shroff. Having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards have decided to fine Jockey P. S. Chouhan a sum of Rs.10,000/- for riding an ill-judged race.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO INVOLVEMENT IN BETTING AND PASSING ON STABLE INFORMATION/TIPS TO PUNTERS FOR PERSONAL GAIN – APP. A. K. ANIKET:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 17th April 2022, considered all the papers and proceedings before them and having heard the submissions made by App. A. K. Aniket, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. A. K. Aniket and held him guilty of improper practice under Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing of the Club wherein he admitted before the Stipendiary Stewards and the Stewards of the Club that he had not only indulged in betting but were also sharing tips for monetary gains. Further, scrutiny of his Google pay account revealed that certain cash deposits had been made into his account, by the persons he was in contact with and he confirmed at the meeting that these cash deposits received by him were related to betting.

 

In view of the above, the Stewards of the Club were of the view that the above breach of Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing warranted a punishment of Fifteen Calendar Months. However, keeping in mind that he was co-operative and forthcoming in the enquiry and had admitted his guilt, the Stewards of the Club took a lenient view and decided to impose on him a punishment covering Twelve Calendar Months. Therefore, should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the season 2022/23, it shall not be granted to him.

 

Please note that during the period of his punishment :-

 

i)              He is not permitted to ride work;

ii)             He is not permitted to attend racing, on course or off course.

iii)            All other disabilities applicable to a person suspended/disqualified shall apply to him.
