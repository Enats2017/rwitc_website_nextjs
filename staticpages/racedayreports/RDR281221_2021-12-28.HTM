RACE NO.1(22) – THE CASTLEBRIDGE PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  10

 

Passing the 300 metres, TURMERIC TOWER (Dashrath Singh) shifted in under urgings and went across GUSTY GIRL (App. P. Vinod) causing the later to check and change course. After questioning, Jockey Dashrath Singh has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, App. Jockey P. S. Kaviraj (LITTLE MORE) reported that his mount was inclined outwards from the 600 metres and thereafter hung out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

ZARRIYA (S. Mosin) was observed to have run detached from the field. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, GUSTY GIRL had a gum injury.

 

After the race, LA PEREGRINA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(23) – THE NAWABZADA RASHIDUZZAFAR KHAN TROPHY: (About) 1800 Metres – Runners -  3

 

No report

 

RACE NO.3(24) – THE PHOENIX TIGER PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  13

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, FUHRER (App. Aniket Karande) shifted in onto SAFDAR (S. Mosin) who in turn shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing PEPPOLI (App. P. Vinod) forcing it to check and drop back thereby inconveniencing RED CARNATION (App. P. S. Kaviraj) racing on his haunches. After questioning, App. Aniket Karande has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, RECIPROCITY (S. A. Amit) weakened and dropped back due to which SAFDAR (S. Mosin) racing on its haunches was held up for room and had to be checked.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (JUICED) reported that his mount was slowly off and hung-in under pressure. Trainer A. M. Jadhav has been asked to remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, JUICED was trotted and found to be sound but had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.4(25) – THE FAIZ JASDANWALLA TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres –Runners- 6

 

Passing the 300 Metres, ZARAK (Suraj Narredu) shifted out under pressure and carried TREASURE GOLD (T. S. Jodha) outwards. Jockey Suraj Narredu has been cautioned.

 

In the final stages of the race, CIRCLE OF LOVE (A. Sandesh) shifted out under pressure and carried AIRA (Bhawani Singh) outwards. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

Jockey Bhawani Singh (AIRA) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

  

RACE NO.5(26) – THE M N NAZIR TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  8

 

SHARAREH (Bhawani Singh, dr.8) jumped out throwing its head.

 

At the start, BABY BAZOOKA (S. Zervan, dr.7) jumped inwards and made contact with CELLINI (T. S. Jodha, dr.6) who in turn shifted in on to EXCELERATOR (A. Sandesh, dr.5).

 

Passing the 350 metres, BABY BAZOOKA (S. Zervan) leaned outwards thereby tightening and inconveniencing SPRING GROVE (N. S. Parmar). Jockey S Zervan has been cautioned

 

In the final stages of the race, ENDEAVOUR (App. Kirtish Bhagat) was observed to have drifted out and raced on the extreme outside despite rider’s efforts. Trainer Imtiaz A Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race..

 

After the race, BABY BAZOOKA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, EXCELERATOR was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The colt has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(27) – THE ARION HORSE COMPANY INDIAN 2000 GUINEAS (GRADE 1): (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 13

 

PURE (A. Sandesh, dr.4) reared up as the start was effect due to which he lost about 4 lengths at the start. Trainer A. M. Jadhav has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter in morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, SOUZA (App. P. S. Kaviraj) shifted out and carried A STAR IS BORN (Suraj Narredu) and MAJORELLA BLUE (S. Zervan) outwards.

 

Passing the red marker, A STAR IS BORN (Suraj Narredu) shifted in and went across ZUCCARELLI (P. S. Chouhan) who had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 1000 metres, MAJORELLA BLUE (S. Zervan) leaned inwards at the same time STOCKBRIDGE (Antony Raj S.) leaned outwards as a result A STAR IS BORN (Suraj Narredu) and ZUCCARELLI were tightened and had to be steadied. Jockeys S. Zervan and Antony Raj S. have been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, PRESIDENTIAL (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out and went across COMMANDMENT (T. S. Jodha) and tightened ALASTAIR (C. Umesh) who had to be checked. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Passing the 300 metres, ZUCCARELLI (P. S. Chouhan) shifted in under urging and went across PRESIDENTIAL (Neeraj Rawal) who had to check and change course. Jockey P. S. Chouhan has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

RACE NO.7(28) – THE MACHIAVELLIANISM PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  8

 

At the request of Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait, MEMORABLE MOMENTS was examined at the stable area at 11.45 a.m. on 28/12/2021 and was observed to have insect bite at angle of mouth on lower lips which had developed into an abscess as such the filly was unable to take a bit. Hence the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

BLACK CHERRY took awkward fly jump thereby losing considerable ground at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter in morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, BLACK CHERRY had a gum injury.
