ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

PUNE MEETING 2025, SPECIAL RACE DAY, SATURDAY, 27TH SEPTEMBER, 2025.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 4.7 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD TO YIELDING)

 

RACE DAY REPORT

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THEIR ORIGINAL POSITION FROM 1600 METRES TO 1400 METRES 2 METRES WIDE AT 1200 METRES. 4 METRES WIDE AT 1000 METRES, 6 METRES WIDE AT 800 METRES, 7 METRES WIDE AT 600 METRES, 8 METRES WIDE AT 400 METRES, THEREAFTER RAILS WERE PLACED 9 METRES WIDE UP TO THE WINNING POST. RAILS WERE PLACED 5 METRES WIDE IN THE CHUTE.

 

On a request from Trainer Dr. Anil Kumar, permission was granted to Trainer Hosidar Daji to saddle his charge LIBAN in the 1st race, at his own (Trainer Dr. Anil Kumar’s) risk and responsibility.

 

On a request from Trainer S. Waheed, permission was granted to Trainer Vijay Kasbekar to saddle his charge GOLD CAVIAR and WE STILL BELIEVE in the 2nd race, at his own (Trainer S. Waheed’s) risk and responsibility.

 

On a request from Trainer J. E. Mckeown, permission was granted to Trainer Subhag Singh to saddle his charge MEADOW SPRINGS in the 7th race, at his own (Trainer J. E. Mckeown’s) risk and responsibility.

 

On a request from Trainer P. S. Chouhan, permission was granted to Trainer Subhag Singh to saddle his charge GOLDEN DEGREE in the 7th race, at his own (Trainer P. S. Chouhan’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO.1 (99) – THE MID-DAY TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 4

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey N. S. Parmar, the saddle used on his mount MIDNIGHT EXPRESS was checked and re-adjusted.

 

At the start, ESPERANZA (App. Ramswarup dr.3) was observed to have lost its footing and stumbled.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, ESPERANZA (App. Ramswarup) shifted in gradually and went across MIDNIGHT EXPRESS (N. S. Parmar) racing along rails causing the latter to check and change course thereby carrying CELLINI (Antony Raj S.) outwards. App. Ramswarup has been cautioned.

 

After the race, MIDNIGHT EXPRESS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (100) – THE INDIARACE.COM CUP: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

GOLD CAVIAR (D. R. Shubhan, dr.1) took fly a jump at the start.

 

TAABIIR (App. Ramswarup, dr.5) jumped outwards onto LORD MURPHY (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.6) who had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, ARBITRAGE (A. Sandesh) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing TAABIIR (App. Ramswarup) racing on its haunches causing the latter to be steadied. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

ALPHA GENE (R. Shelar) was observed to have trailed the field. Trainer Ivor Fernandes has been asked to ensure that his charge puts up a reasonable performance in the future.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

LORD MURPHY & GOLD CAVIAR were trotted and found to be sound.

 

BALENO was observed to have an injury on its right hind fetlock and coronet.

 

WE STILL BELIEVE was observed to have an injury on its left hind fetlock.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that TAABIIR had right eye injury (sore) with ulcer.

 

RACE NO.3 (101) – THE RACINGPULSE.IN TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS – 8

 

Approaching the 700 metres, MAYSARA (K. Nazil) leaned in onto BETWEEN FRIENDS (Prashant P. Dhebe) causing the latter to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 100 metres, LIGHTNING BLAZE (A. Prakash) shifted out thereby tightening and inconveniencing BETWEEN FRIENDS (Prashant P. Dhebe) racing on its haunches causing the latter to check and change course. After the race on questioning Jockey A. Prakash stated that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight, despite his efforts. As such, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MAYSARA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

ETOILE was observed to have an injury on its left hind fetlock.

 

MIRAE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

LIGHTNING BLAZE was trotted at the stables and was found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its right hind pastern.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ETOILE had right both eye injuries (sore) with ulcer in right eye.

 

RACE NO.4 (102) – THE TIMES OF INDIA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 5

 

Soon after the start, SON OF A GUN (A. Prakash) shifted in and went cross IT’S MY TIME (P. Trevor), SHAMBALA (Antony Raj S.) and MOJITO (D. A. Naik). As such, Jockey A. Prakash has been cautioned for shifting in before the Red Marker.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

MARKET KING & IT’S MY TIME were trotted and found to be sound.

 

SHAMBALA was observed to have a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.5 (103) – THE HINDU TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 11

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey P. Trevor, the saddle used on his mount EL MORAN was checked and re-adjusted.

 

SKY FULL OF STARS (D. R. Shubham, dr.11) was observed to be restless in the stall and was not allowing its rider to settle. As such, Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, SHE’S MY GAL (Bhawani Singh, dr.8) jumped outwards and made contact with TRAIL BLAZER (App. N. Bhosale, dr.9).

 

Soon after the start, RIPTIDE (Antony Raj S.) jumped outwards and SKY FULL OF STARS (D. R. Shubham) jumped inwards and they made contact due to which Jockey Antony Raj S. had lost his right stirrup momentarily which he re-gained.  

 

Approaching the 700 metres, RIPTIDE (Antony Raj S.) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing AGATHA (S. Mosin) racing along the rails who had to check and drop back. Jockey Antony Raj S. has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

In the final stages of the race, DESERT CHARM (A. Prakash) was observed to have shied away from the railing.

 

After the race, Jockey D. R. Shubham (SKY FULL OF STARS) reported that his mount was running erratic throughout the race. Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (LIGHTNING MCQUEEN) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that SKANDHA was observed to have run with bandages on all four, permission for which was not obtained. As such, Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy has been fined Rs.1,000/- for her lapse.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that AGATHA had both eye injuries (sore).

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

SKANDHA & SKY FULL OF STARS was trotted and found to be sound.

 

TRAIL BLAZER was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its right fore tendon, left hind fetlock and left nostril.

 

DESERT CHARM was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its left hind coronet.

 

HEAVEN’S RHYTHM was observed to have an injury on its left hind fetlock.

 

RACE NO.6 (104) – THE FREE PRESS JOURNAL TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

BRAVE BEAUTY (Bhawani Singh, dr.6) planted and lost considerable ground at the start. As such, Trainer Vinesh has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race. 

 

At the start, OPUS DEI (Antony Raj S., dr.5) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

Jockey S. Mosin (LORD AND MASTER) was observed to have used the whip in an improper manner on his mount. As such, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, OPUS DEI & ROSARIO were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7 (105) – THE RACE MIRROR TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 12

  

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey P. Trevor, the saddle used on his mount PHANTASMIQUE was checked and re-adjusted.

 

At the start, MEADOW SPRINGS (C. Umesh, dr.10) jumped inwards onto ELEVEN ELEVEN (S. Mosin, dr.9) causing the latter to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 1300 metres, REGAL GALLERIA (S. J. Sunil) shifted in onto JACKSON (A. Sandesh) who further shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing SEMURG (Amyn Merchant) racing along the rails causing the latter to check and drop back. Jockey S. J. Sunil has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey A. Sandesh (JACKSON) reported that he was held up for room approaching the 200 metres.

 

In the final stages of the race, ADEYA (App. Ramswarup) shifted out and went across SOUTH BEACH (N. S. Parmar) causing the latter to be steadied. App. Ramswarup has been cautioned.

 

Jockey Antony Raj S. (MIRACLE OF HANUKAH) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Pune Meeting 2025, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, GOLDEN DEGREE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

PHANTASMIQUE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

SAKANA was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

ADEYA was observed to have an injury on its right fore coronet.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ADEYA had left eye injury (sore).
