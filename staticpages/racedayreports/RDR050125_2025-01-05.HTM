RACE NO.1 (52) – THE KAZAKH PLATE: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 5

 

At the start, SILVER BRAID (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2) jumped outwards and went across LEO THE LION (A. Prakash, dr.3), who had to be steadied.

 

After the race, WILLY WONKAA was observed to have an injury on right hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.2 (53) – THE KAILASHPAT SINGHANIA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

SINGER SARGENT (Vivek G., dr. 1) lost about five lengths at the start. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey Bhawani Singh (CHARLIE) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his second offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, he has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

RACE NO.3 (54) – THE DR. I. C. NAGREE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1800 METRES – RUNNERS - 5

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, ARDAKAN (A. Sandesh) shifted in onto BOOSTER SHOT (Neeraj Rawal) who had to be steadied.

 

After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (BOOSTER SHOT) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Vivek G. (BUGATTI) reported his mount hung-in in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, BOOSTER SHOT was trotted and found to be sound. BUGATTI was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on right hind fetlock. ZARAK was observed to have an injury on left hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.4 (55) – THE LT. COL. GOVIND SINGH (VRC) CUP: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

At the start, AEON FLUX (Amyn Merchant, dr.3) jumped inwards and made contact with BEAT THE HEAT (S. A. Amit, dr.2).

 

It was observed that MIRACLE OF HANUKAH (Akshay Gaikwad) was running green throughout the race.

 

After the race, BEAT THE HEAT & MINISTRY OF TIME were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5 (56) – THE INDIAN AIR FORCE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

At the start, CHAMPAGNE SMILE (A. Prakash, dr.2) jumped inwards and made contact with LIAM (App. Bharat Singh, dr.1) who had to be steadied.

 

At the start, DAULAT MAI (S. Zervan, dr.6) jumped outwards and made contact with THE GENERAL (Bhawani Singh, dr.7).

 

Approaching the 950 metres, DAULAT MAI (S. Zervan) leaned inwards onto CORDELIA (C. Umesh) who in turn shifted in and made contact with JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe). Jockey S.Zervan has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, GOOMAH (A. Sandesh) shifted out under urgings thereby inconveniencing WALTER (R. Ajinkya) who had to steadied. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 200 metres, CORDELIA (C. Umesh) shifted out under urgings thereby bumping and inconveniencing DAULAT MAI (S. Zervan) who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey C. Umesh has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that LIAM was declared to run with Aluminium shoes but ran with Aluminium Both Fore Speed Toe. As such, Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy has been fined Rs.1,000/-.

 

After the race, DAULAT MAI was observed to have a gum injury. GOOMAH & WALTER were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6 (57) – THE RAVISHANKAR GUPTA SALVER: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

AMADEO (Vivek G., dr.3) lost about a length at the start.

 

After the race, Jockey A. Prakash (BALENO) reported loss of whip approaching the 250 metres and thereafter his reins slipped momentarily. This being the third instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2024/25, Jockey A.Prakash has been fined Rs.10,000/-.

 

After the race, AMADEO was trotted and found to be sound. AXLROD was observed to have an injury on left hind fetlock.

 

RACE NO.7 (58) – THE A. HOYT PLATE: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

At the start, TRILLIONAIRE (A. Prakash, dr.9) jumped inwards and went across CAREGIVER (S. Mosin, dr.8) who was marginally slow to begin and had to be steadied.

 

It was observed that CAREGIVER (S. Mosin) drifted out in the straight despite the riders efforts. Trainer Ivor Fernandes has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, App. Bharat Singh (GOLDEN GOOSE) reported that his mount hung out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, GOLDEN GOOSE was trotted and found to be sound. SUPERLUMINAL was observed to have an injury on right hind pastern. CAREGIVER was trotted and found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “JULIETTE” (DRAWN 1) IN RACE NO.49 – THE MAHARAJA JIWAJIRAO SCINDIA TROPHY (GRADE 3) (DIST. 1800 MTRS.) RUN ON SUNDAY, 29TH DECEMBER 2024:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 5th January 2025, having heard Jockey C. S. Jodha, his spokesperson Mr. P. S. Chouhan and Trainer Karthik Ganapathy and having viewed the video recording of the race in question and the previous races of the JULIETTE, the Stewards of the Club were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey C. S. Jodha. In that, he was instructed to be mindful of the mares early positioning and not to sit too far behind the field but in the early part of the race he positioned his mount at a disadvantage whereas he could have sat in a better position. Further, coming into the straight he travelled on the wide outside thereby losing considerable ground. Therefore, the Stewards of the Club by majority held Jockey C. S. Jodha guilty of riding an ill-judged race and decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for four race days i.e. on 23rd January, 1st, 2nd & 9th February 2025 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: ENQUIRY INTO RACE NO.31 THE K. M. MUNSHI TROPHY RUN ON SUNDAY, 22ND DECEMBER 2024, WHEREIN “FAST PACE” RACED SPORTING A RED HOOD:

 

The Stewards of the Club having considered the report submitted by the Stipendiary Stewards into the captioned subject matter, having heard both the Starters as also the Stipendiary Stewards took note that the Asst. Starter Mr. J. E. Pereira had admitted to his mistake that led to the mare racing with the red hood, an equipment that should have been removed at the gates.

 

The Stewards noted with displeasure the lapse on part of the Asst. Starter and decided to fine him for the same. Further, he was asked to be more careful in the future.
