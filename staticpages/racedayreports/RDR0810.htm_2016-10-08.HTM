RE: APPEAL FROM JOCKEY DASHRATH SINGH AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB WITH REGARD TO SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 8th OCTOBER 2016 TO 22ND OCTOBER 2016 FOR UNSATISFACTORY RIDING – ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “DIVINE MAGIC” IN RACE NO:84 – THE ALGECIRAS PLATE (DIV-I) – RUN ON 28TH AUGUST 2016 

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 3rd October 2016 considered Appeal dated 23rd September 2016 from Jockey Dashrath Singh.

 

The Board of Appeal studied all documents and papers that were submitted and having considered the submissions made by Jockey Dashrath Singh and his Spokesman, Mr. Narendra Lagad and having viewed the Video recording of the race, in normal as well as in slow motion, deliberated on the matter at length and applied their minds to the entire facts of the case. The Board also noted that his ride was in accordance with the Trainer's instructions. The Board did not agree with the finding of the Stewards that Jockey Dashrath Singh had unbalanced the horse in the final 100 metres. The Board also did not agree with the Stewards on the charge of Unsatisfactory Riding. The Board felt that his ride on DIVINE MAGIC was not mala-fide. However, the Board found Jockey Dashrath Singh guilty of Improper Riding on his mount DIVINE MAGIC.   

 

In view of the above, the Board unanimously decided to partially allow the Appeal and modify the charge to Improper Riding and hence, his Jockey’s License will now stand suspended w.e.f. 8th October 2016 to 9th October 2016 for Improper Riding on his mount DIVINE MAGIC in Race no 84 run on 28th August 2016. His Deposit for the Appeal was ordered to be refunded.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “POLLYANA” IN RACE NO.93 – THE ISHPINGO PLATE, RUN ON 4th SEPTEMBER, 2016 WHICH THE FILLY WON VIS-À-VIS HER RUN IN RACE NO.52 – THE BRUNELLO PLATE,  RUN ON 15th AUGUST, 2016 WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED:

 

Trainer C. D. Katrak and Jockey R. Shelar who rode the filly in Race No.52, were questioned in the above connection. It was pointed out to Trainer C. D. Katrak that in Race No.52 run on 15th August, 2016 POLLYANA was ridden by Jockey R. Shelar and was available at 20/1 and ran unplaced and his other runner PRISTINA, who was the favourite in that race, won the race. Thereafter, in Race No.93 run on 4th September, 2016 he declared Jockey Amyn Merchant and the filly won as an On Money favourite at 90/100 in less than three weeks time.

 

The video recording of Race No.52 and Race No.93 were viewed.

 

Trainer C. D. Katrak said that in Race No.52 he did not give the filly much of a chance and had instructed Jockey R. Shelar  to sit in a comfortable position and to do his best. He said that in Race No.93, POLLYANA was an On Money favourite, as such, he instructed Jockey Amyn Merchant to go to the front or to sit in 2nd position and to do his best. He said that in Race No.52 the filly ran in open Class-V race whereas in Race No.93 she ran in a race for maiden 3-year-olds who have run and not placed 2nd or 3rd.

 

Tr. Katrak was informed that this enquiry was more so regarding the running and riding of POLLYANA in Race No.52 where she was ridden by Jockey R. Shelar and was available at 20/1 and ran unplaced and in less than three weeks time POLLYANA was backed to the core in Race No.93 and won as a On Money favourite, therefore, his explanation was not acceptable especially since his other runner PRISTINA was fancied in Race No.52 and won that race.

 

Trainer C. D. Katrak accepted that Jockey R. Shelar was feeble in his riding effort in the straight or else the filly would have finished closer. He said that he was not satisfied with the riding of Jockey R. Shelar but that was the calibre of the Jockey. He said that Jockey R. Shelar rides work for him and therefore he gave him this ride in Race No.52, however, after this race he has stopped giving rides to Jockey R. Shelar.

 

Jockey R. Shelar confirmed the instructions and added that unfortunately one horse was fractious in the gate and was withdrawn and another horse was also playing up so he turned around to look what was happening and just then the start was given and he was not ready. Thereafter, he found it difficult to ride due to the kick back and it was only when he switched the filly to the outside in the straight that she started responding and he did his best. He said that the filly ran a good race and next time out he asked for this ride in Race No.93 but the Trainer refused to give him the ride.

 

The video of Race No.52 was viewed and it was pointed out to Jockey R. Shelar that after a good start he had fallen back and then made no effort in the early part to improve his position and he was feeble in his riding effort in the straight.

 

Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer C. D. Katrak and Jockey R. Shelar, the matter was reported to the Stewards for unsatisfactory running and riding of POLLYANA in Race No.52.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 8th October, 2016 interviewed Trainer C. D. Katrak and Jockey R. Shelar in the above connection. Having heard them and having viewed the video of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by them, and decided:-

 

i)              to suspend Jockey R. Shelar’s license for four Race Days for unsatisfactory riding on his mount POLLYANA in Race No.52. The suspension will take effect from the first Race Day of the Mumbai Meeting 2016/17 (permitted to ride work/mock race);

ii)             to fine Trainer C. D. Katrak a sum of Rs.50,000/- for unsatisfactory running of his charge POLLYANA in Race No.52.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ARAKAWAH” (DRAWN 3 ) IN RACE NO.131 - THE ENAKSI PLATE (DIV.I) (1200 METRES) - RUN ON 18th SEPTEMBER, 2016 :

 

Tr. Altaf Hussain and Jockey Kavraj Singh were questioned in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Trainer Altaf Hussain said that Jockey Kavraj Singh was engaged to ride this horse by the Owner and he gave the instructions in the presence of the Owner. He said that ARAKAWAH is a speedy horse and he had instructed the Jockey to take a quick jump and go to the front but if somebody else rushes to take over the running, then to sit in 2nd position and to do his best. He said that he had also told the Jockey that the horse was carrying top weight of 61.5 kg. and the going along the inner rails is soft so he should avoid going in.  

 

The video of the race was viewed and when asked for his observation, Trainer Altaf Hussain said that when the gates opened, the horse’s mouth was open and in his opinion the Jockey had made no effort to go to the front as instructed. Further, coming into the straight the Jockey had taken the horse in despite being specifically told to avoid coming along the rails. He said that he was not satisfied with the Jockey’s riding and the Owner too was not satisfied and had asked him to report the matter to the Stipes.

 

Jockey Kavraj Singh said that he was instructed to go to the front but if anyone fights for the lead then to sit in 2nd/3rd position and to do his best. He confirmed that he was also told not to come along the rails in the straight. He said that at that start he received interference from LADISLAUS (P. Trevor) and could not hit the front, therefore, he settled the horse in 2nd position and coming into the straight the horse on his own shifted in and he had ridden out the horse. Also, the horse wobbled in the last 100 metres. When asked as to why he did not report to the Stipes that the horse wobbled, he said that he had noticed the wobble subsequently on the replay. To another query, Jockey Kavraj Singh said that he was engaged to ride this horse by the Owner and that he had cantered the horse once over a mile, before this race.

 

Having heard the submissions made and viewing the video of Race No.131 as also the previous run of the horse in Race No.65, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Kavraj Singh, as such the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit for the under-mentioned reasons :-

 

i)              For no apparent reason Jockey Kavraj Singh has not let the horse jump out fluently and take the forward position he likes and was instructed to do.

 

ii)             He was specifically instructed not to go on the inside in the straight, which he failed to follow.

 

iii)            He is known to be more vigorous in the straight.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 8th October, 2016 interviewed Trainer Altaf Hussain and Jockey Kavraj Singh who was accompanied by his Spokesman, Mr. P. Trevor. Having heard them and having viewed the video of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Kavraj Singh and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for a period of Twelve Race Days for unsatisfactory riding on his mount ARAKAWAH in the above race. The suspension will take effect from the first Race Day of the Mumbai Meeting 2016/17. (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “COMMANDER” IN RACE NO.56 - THE GAMBLE FOR LOVE TROPHY (1600 METRES) - RUN ON 20th AUGUST, 2016 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that an enquiry was initiated into the running and riding of COMMANDER in Race No.56 – The Gamble For Love Trophy run on 20th August, 2016. The horse was trained by Trainer Nazak Chenoy and ridden by Jockey Santosh G.  In the said enquiry, Jockey Santosh G. had stated that he was instructed by the Trainer not to use the whip. Trainer Nazak Chenoy had denied giving the Jockey specific instructions not to use the whip, however, she said that she never instructs her Jockeys to be “whip happy” and that she would have loved to have won the race. She opined that the Jockey had done his best and if he had tried to pull out his whip and used it, he may have lost valuable time of pushing the horse and that she was very satisfied with this ride and had no complaints. She had further added that a whip is a corrective instrument and she did not feel that the horse needed to be corrected at any stage as a horse like this does not get to where he is if he needs a lot of correcting. It was brought to the attention of Trainer Nazak Chenoy that a whip when used judiciously, is also an aid to encourage the horse to perform better. To this, Tr. Nazak Chenoy had stated that she had scientific evidence that a whip does not help the horse to perform better.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey since they felt that the horse would have run better with a little more encouragement and better riding and as such reported the matter to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 4th September, 2016 having heard the Trainer and Jockey and having viewed the race, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Santosh G. and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for one race day for riding an incompetent race and no action was initiated against the Trainer.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards pointed out that COMMANDER raced again in Race No.146 on 25th September, 2016 which he won and it was observed that Jockey Suraj Narredu has used the whip 12 times, contradicting the statement made by Trainer Nazak Chenoy in her previous enquiry.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 8th October, 2016 considered the above report and directed the Stipendiary Stewards to write to Trainer Nazak Chenoy informing her to be consistent and specific with her instructions to Jockeys in the future.

To a specific question, he said that the mare had not responded in the early part and after the race the Jockey had informed him that the mare was very uncomfortable on her right leg, however, she had returned sound after the race.
