RACE NO.1(127) – THE IRISH BOSS PLATE  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  12

 

At the start, CHAMPAGNE SMILE (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.4) jumped inwards and made contact with VICTORY CHANT (M. S. Deora, dr.3).

 

Passing the 100 metres, it was observed that VICTORY CHANT (M. S. Deora) stumbled and fell along with the rider, the gelding was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to have a swelling due to a fracture on its right shoulder, as such, the gelding was euthanized on humanitarian grounds.

 

It was observed that MARIELLA (H. Zeeshan) was travelling wide throughout the race for no apparent reason. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey H. Zeeshan has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Jockey S. Saqlain had failed to give his Breathalyzer Test before his first ride of the day. Subsequently his test was taken immediately after his ride in the first race and the report was negative. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey S. Saqlain has been fined a sum of Rs.3,000/-.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the improved performance of HOUSE OF LORDS (A. K. Aniket) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ZIP ALONG was found to have sore left eye and HOUSE OF LORDS was found to have sore right eye.

 

RACE NO.2(128) – THE DOPAHAR TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS -  11

 

REMY RED (Shrikant Kamble, dr.9) swayed his head as the start was effected resulting in the gelding losing about two lengths at the start.

 

Passing the 800 metres, ZUKOR (S. Saqlain) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing ART COLLECTOR (Neeraj Rawal) causing the latter to be checked. Jockey S. Saqlain has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 200 metres, ART COLLECTOR (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in and went across JETFIRE (Prashant P. Dhebe) causing the latter to check and change course. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

After the race, TYRONE BLACK was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by left fore, as such, the gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ART COLLECTOR was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(129) – THE BETBRICKS7 SPRINT: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  12

 

At the start, FORTUNE TELLER (Amyn Merchant, dr.7) jumped outwards and went across ASHWA MAGADHEERA (A. S. Peter, dr.8) who had to be steadied. Jockey Amyn Merchant has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 700 metres, MAJESTIC WARRIOR (App. Mustakim Alam) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing SUPERLATIVE (P. Shinde) along the rails causing the latter to be checked. App. Mustakim Alam has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, FORTUNE TELLER (Amyn Merchant) shifted out thereby tightening and inconveniencing DILBAR (P. Vinod) causing the latter to be checked. Jockey Amyn Merchant has been cautioned.

 

After the race, ESPERANZA was observed to have broken a blood vessel (2nd time) and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ASHWA MAGADHEERA was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that MAJESTIC WARRIOR was found to have sore right eye.

 

RACE NO.4(130) – THE DOPAHAR TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES –RUNNERS- 12

 

On the arrival at the start, RED MERLOT was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O who certified the gelding fit to race.

 

After being stalled, ULTIMO (R. Ajinkya, dr.8) reared up, hooked his fore in the adjoining stall, the gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, ULTIMO (R. Ajinkya, dr.8) jumped outwards thereby bumping and forcing ATTAINED (P. Trevor, dr.9)  onto MONARCHY (Shahrukh Khan, dr.10) and MY PRINCESS (P. Vinod, dr.11) who were tightened for room and had be checked. Jockey R. Ajinkya has been cautioned.

 

At the start, RED MERLOT (A. S. Peter, dr.3) jumped outwards awkwardly thereby bumping MOJO (Amyn Merchant, dr.4) who had to be steadied.

 

At the start, SIM SIM (S. Saqlain, dr.6) jumped inwards and made contact with WALSHY (S. J. Sunil, dr.5) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, MAYSARA (App. Haridas Gore) leaned inwards and went across MY PRINCESS (P. Vinod) causing the latter to be steadied. App. Haridas Gore has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Saqlain (SIM SIM) reported loss of whip passing the 200 metres. This being his second instance during the current Pune Meeting 2023, he has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, ULTIMO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ATTAINED was found to have sore right eye and MOJO was found to have sore left eye.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE: APPEAL BY MR. VISHAL GAIKWAD AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB ARISING OUT OF THE ENQUIRY INTO THE CONDUCT AND PENDING ENQUIRIES WHERE HE HAS BEEN DECLARED A DISQUALIFIED / WARNED OFF PERSON BY THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB ON 3RD APRIL 2022:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Board of Appeal at their meeting held on 2nd November 2023, noted with grave displeasure that Mr. Vishal Gaikwad, had not only failed to submit his grounds of appeal as required under the Rules, but also he did not appear before the Board of Appeal on the day of the meeting without any intimation or informing the reason of his absence. All the members of the Board of Appeal along with officials waited for 30 minutes before concluding that he was not going to appear.

 

The Board of Appeal had decided to declare Mr. Vishal Gaikwad as a disqualified person until such time that he appears before the Board of Appeal and furnish a satisfactory and valid reason for his non – appearance. As such, all the disabilities under the Rules of Racing for Disqualified Person, will be applicable to him till then.
