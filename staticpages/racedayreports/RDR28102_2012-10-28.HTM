RACE NO.1(244) – THE  CLASSICAL ACT PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 10 Runners

 

EQUIANO (App.Prasad) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Amjad Khan has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials,  before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, ZEKI (Shrikant Kamble) jumped awkwardly.

 

Trainer H.J.Antia was asked if he could explain the apparent poor performance of WIND OF THE NORTH. Tr.Antia said that the gelding had raced poorly last time at his only other Pune start. He added that WIND OF THE NORTH has had a hoof problem. He has been working well but has not reproduced his track work in his two starts.

 

After the race, WIND OF THE NORTH was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SERENA, FOLLOW ME & JOSEPHINE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(245) – THE  DELHI RACE CLUB TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres  – 5 Runners

 

Near 900 metres, YUVILLE (Imran Chisty) shifted in and inconvenienced SEA TREASURE (Akshay). Jockey Imran Chisty has been cautioned.

 

Passing 800 metres, MOUNTAIN REGIMENT (App.Tushar Nemane) shifted in and hampered the running of SAINTS N SINNERS (A.Sandesh), who in turn, while slowing down, hampered the running of YUVILLE (Imran Chisty). App.Tushar Nemane has been cautioned.

 

Near 200 metres, SAINTS N SINNERS ( A.Sandesh) shifted inwards and closed the gap available to YUVILLE (Imran Chisty) who had to change course outwards to make his bid. Jockey A.Sandesh has been cautioned. 

 

After the race, MOUNTAIN REGIMENT and SAINTS N SINNERS were found to be sound. MOUNTAIN REGIMENT had a cut injury at the right hind fetlock and SAINTS N SINNERS had a cut injury above the right hock.

 

RACE NO.3(246) – THE  KEJRIWAL TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, AFSOON ((App.Nikhil Bhujbal) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Approaching 200 metres, AFSOON (App.Nikhil Bhujbal) was eased off the heels of IRRESISTIBLE HUNK (Dashrath Singh) and in the process ducked out.

 

Near 100 metres, GOLDEN MEMORY (Akshay) was carried in as INTEQUAM (A.Ashhad Asbar) shifted inwards at that stage. Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar has been cautioned.

 

MONZA (C.Rajendra) hung out through the race thereby placing its Rider at a disadvantage. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to show the gelding in a Mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race him.

 

After the race IRRESISTIBLE HUNK, MONZA, MAXIMUS, & AFSOON were found to be sound. VICTORIOUS MARCH and IRRESISTIBLE HUNK  had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.4(247) – THE  DEMOCRATICUS PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, SAMI (Amyn Merchant) and BLUE MELLON (S.Sunil) were slow to move, losing ground.

 

Soon after the start, SONGLARK (P.Trevor) was tightened and lost position.

 

Near 900 metres, SAMI (Amyn Merchant) weakened and dropped back due to which SONGLARK (P.Trevor) who was following, shifted in to avoid colliding and jostled with BLUE MELLON (S.Sunil) wherein both the horses became unbalanced. 

 

Jockey S.Sunil (BLUE MELLON) has been fined Rs.3000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

 

Jockey B Sreekanth (PHOENIX FORCE) reported that his mount lugged in during the race, Trainer Karthik G has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Trainer P.Shroff was questioned regarding the poor performance of his charge SONGLARK. The Trainer stated that he had instructed the Jockey to be in 2nd, 3rd or 4th position and do his best. He added that his filly received interference soon after the start was checked and lost ground. Thereafter, the Jockey was unable to improve his position. Near 900 metres, the filly came in contact with BLUE MELLON and became unbalanced. Jockey P.Trevor was forced to travel wide at the bend to improve his position. The explanation was noted.

 

After the race, SAVAGE GARDEN was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind pastern. PHOENIX FORCE & SONGLARK were found to be sound.

RACE NO.7(250) – THE IRISH THOROUGHBRED MARKETING & GOFFS R.W.I.T.C., LTD. GOLD CUP (GR.2) : About) 2000 Metres– 12 Runners

Jockey Neeraj RAWAL (ALMA MATER) reported loss of his whip near 50 metres from the Winning Post.

 

Trainer Altamaash Ahmed and Jockey A.Sandesh were questioned regarding the poor performance of ASTAPI. The Jockey stated that he was instructed to be in 5th or 6th position and do his best. The Jockey added that the gelding was a bit slow out of the Gates and he had tucked him behind horses and during the race his mount had clipped the hind heels of BERLUSCONI on a few occasions and thereafter lost interest in the race and when he asked for an effort, the gelding did not respond. The Trainer stated that the gelding has lost condition after running the Indian St Leger, and was not looking good in the Paddock. When asked whether the use of Tongue Strap had a bearing on the performance, he replied in the negative and added that he uses the Tongue Strap in work and on an advise from his Work Rider he has used it in the race. The explanation was noted.  

 

After the race, ALMA MATER was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind fetlock, ONASSIS had an abrasion at the left hind fetlock and BERLUSCONI had a gum injury. ASTAPI, NADIA & HILLS AND STARS were found to be sound.

RE :APPEAL FROM JOCKEY A.ASHHAD ASBAR IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 28TH OCTOBER, 2012 TO 28TH DECEMBER, 2012 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) FOR DANGEROUS RIDING ON HIS MOUNT “CRÈME DE LA CRÈME” IN ACE NO.203 – THE PHENOMENALE PLATE (DIV.I) RUN ON 13TH OCTOBER, 2012 – JOCKEY A.ASHHAD ASBAR WAS GIVEN A STAY ON THE PUNISHMENT TILL 28TH OCTOBER, 2012 :

 

The Board of Appeal at their meeting held on Saturday, 27th October, 2012 considered appeal dated 17th October, 2012, from Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar, on the captioned subject.

 

The Board of Appeal having considered all the documents and papers that were submitted and having heard Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar and his Spokesman and having viewed the video recording of the race and having deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds decided to reject the appeal and forfeit the deposit.

 

Further, the Board of Appeal felt that the punishment meted out to Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar appeared to be lenient considering the gravity of the offence. The Board of Appeal felt that the punishment meted out to him should be enhanced and therefore he was given a show cause asking him to show cause as to why the punishment meted out to him should not be enhanced.

 

After again hearing Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar and his Spokesman and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered, the Board of Appeal decided to enhance the punishment i.e. his Jockey’s license now stands suspended w.e.f. 29th October, 2012 to 29th January, 2013 (both days inclusive) (not permitted to ride work/mock race).
