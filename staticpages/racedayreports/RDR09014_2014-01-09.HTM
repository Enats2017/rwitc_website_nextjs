RACE NO.3(130) – THE   WARSAW PACT PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 14000 Metres – Runners – 10

 

Trainer B.Prakash was questioned regarding the comparative performance of his charge MALKIA in this race which the filly won as compared to her previous run in Race No 90 run on 22 December 2013 where she was unplaced. The Trainer stated that the previous run was disappointing. This time there was change of two equipments i.e. Tongue Strap and Cross Nose Band, which has helped the filly. Also, in the previous race, the filly found herself up in the field which may have not suited her and therefore this time he had specifically asked the Jockey not to be up and ride her from rear position and let her race on her own which suited her.  Whilst noting the explanation of the Trainer, he has been cautioned for running his charge within a short span of time.

 

After the race, ICE COOL and LAURUS PRIDE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(131) – THE  RAMNIWAS RAMNARAIN RUIA GOLD CUP (GR.2): (About) 2000 Metres – Runners - 3

 

MURIOI (B.Sreekanth) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer S.Ganapathy has been asked to give the colt more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MARS was observed to have a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.5(132) – THE  PERCEIVED VALUE PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 14

ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “NIGELLA” IN RACE NO.109 - THE 10TH WORLD ZOROASTRIAN CONGRESS TROPHY (1600 METRES) RUN ON 29/12/2013 WHICH THE FILLY WON AS COMPARED TO HER PREVIOUS RUN IN RACE NO.51 (1400 METRES) RUN ON 8/12/2013) WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer D.J.Surti and Jockey Zameer Sayyed in the above connection. Trainer D.J.Surti said that NIGELLA had previously won two races over 1400 metres and 1600 metres, however, he was advised by the sellers that the filly is bred to stay and therefore he had accepted with her in Race No.74 – The PRA Independence Million  over 2000 metres and had fancied her strongly but she ran a disappointing race and so he switched her back to 1400 metres but she kept performing poorly in Class IV (rated 20-46) during the Pune Season, except in her last run at Pune in Class V (1 – 26) where she finished sixth but was only 2 lengths from the winner. Thereafter, he accepted with her in Race No.8 over 1400 metres during the current Mumbai Meeting but this being the first run of the Season and she was racing after a long gap, he did not give her much chance and she again performed poorly.  He then accepted with her in Race No.51 again over 1400 metres where the field was strong and in this race the filly received interference or else she may have finished on board. Subsequently, he gave the filly a Mock Race where the filly showed improvement and on the basis of that Mock Race he gave her a slight chance in Race No.109 over 1600 metres and was very happy to win this race. Trainer D.J.Surti further explained that NIGELLA is an unsound filly and she is not genuine, she may perform well in one race and then perform poorly in the subsequent runs. He said that most of the times the filly comes back unsound after a race and has to be treated, she had come back unsound even after her Mock Race and had to be treated. When questioned why he did not give the ride to the same Jockey Zameer Sayyed in Race No.109, Trainer D.J.Surti said that keeping in mind that the filly had gone a good Mock Race, he gave her a slight chance this time and wanted to put up Apprentice S.Mosin and claim allowance but he was not available so he approached other Jockeys and since

nobody was available he reluctantly gave the ride to Jockey Prabhu Naidu. He opined that NIGELLA has limited ability and all her three wins are in Class V, she has always performed poorly in Class IV. Jockey Zameer Sayyed said that NIGELLA is an unsound filly and whilst riding her, if one holds her she runs better but when pushed she starts dropping back. He said that in Race No.51 he had received slight interference or else he may have finished a little better.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 9th January, 2014 considered the above report and the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted.
