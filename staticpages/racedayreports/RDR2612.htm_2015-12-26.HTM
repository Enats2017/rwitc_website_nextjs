RACE NO.3(80)  – THE POLISH PATRIOT PLATE(DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 10

 

At the request of Tr.A.M.Jadhav, SAMURAI was examined   on 26/12/2015 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg with swelling at the right tendon. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey A.Sandesh, rider of TENNESSEE (Placed Fourth) against COMMITMENT (App.S.J.Sunil, Placed Second). The reason being, at about 350 metres, COMMITMENT (App.S.J.Sunil) shifted out and bumped him whereby he had to check his mount or else he would have fallen and this incident cost him the second place as he would have beaten COMMITMENT.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing as under:-

 

AZADEGAN                         -               FIRST;                   TENNESSEE                       -               THIRD;

LIMEKILNS                           -               SECOND;             COMMITMENT                    -               FOURTH             

The Stewards after the last race interviewed App.S.J.Sunil, who was accompanied by his Master, Tr.Sanjay Kolse, for dangerous riding on COMMITMENT. Having heard them and viewing the video of the race, the Stewards  were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App.S.J.Sunil. Taking an overall view the Stewards of the Club decided to suspend his Apprentice Jockey’s license w.e.f. 14th January, 2016 to 6th February, 2016 (both days inclusive) for dangerous riding on COMMITMENT (permitted to ride work/mock race).  In that, at approximately 350 metres he pulled his mount out to make his move and in doing so, bumped TENNESSEE (A.Sandesh) who in turn went on to KOAL (App.D.S.Daman) and both the horses lost position and this incident nearly caused an accident. Thereafter, COMMITMENT again shifted out and made contact with TENNESSEE (A.Sandesh) who in turn went on to KOAL. Further, App.S.J.Sunil has been told to work harder to improve his riding skills under the guidance of the Riding Instructor, Mr.Aslam Kader.

 

At the start, COMMITMENT (App.S.J.Sunil, dr.7) shifted in on to AZALEA (App.Akshay Gaikwad, dr. 6) who was tightened for room and dropped back.

 

Passing 1000 metres, LIMEKILNS (App.Raghuveer Singh) shifted in, as a result COAT OF ARMS (App.K.Nazil) had to be steadied.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the improved performance of AZADEGAN and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, COMMITMENT was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind fetlock. KOAL was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(81) – THE CONRAD PEREIRA TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1800 Metres – Runners -  10

 

At the start, ZEPHIR (P.Trevor) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Passing the 600 metres, JAGER BOMB (J. E. Pereira), racing behind RAJASTHAN ROYALTY (R. Vaibhav), was forced to check his mount due to the later dropping back.

 

Approaching 300 metres, FLASHING HONOUR (Shrikant Kamble) was observed to have shifted out, going on to ZEPHIR (P.Trevor) and thereafter Jockey Shrikant Kamble persisted to use the whip on the right despite his mount shifting out. Jockey Shrikant Kamble has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 100 metres, DANSE DEBONAIRE (App.Bhawani singh) shifted out on to STREETJAMMER (App.Yash Narredu). App.Bhawani Singh has been cautioned.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the riding of Jockey Neeraj Rawal on MARVEL and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, RAJASTHAN ROYALTY was found to be sound, but having a cut injury at the left hind coronet and bulb of heel.

 

RACE NO.5(82) – THE WHITE METAL PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 10

 

At the start, LADY DANEHILL (S. Zervan dr. 2) and SNOW CRYSTAL (A. Sandesh dr. 3) were observed to have shifted in.

After the race, FIREBOLT was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(83) – THE CONRAD PEREIRA TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1800 Metres – Runners - 11

 

At the start, PURE BLISS (App.R.Ajinkya, dr.7) shifted out and at the same time HURACAN (C.S.Jodha, dr.9) shifted in, as a result FIREPROOF (App.S.J.Sunil, dr.8) was jostled for room.

 

Passing 700 metres, SABIQ (Dashrath Singh) shifted in thereby tightening SUMAAQ (Y.S.Srinath).

 

Passing 200 metres, SABIQ (Dashrath Singh) shifted in, as a result Jockey S.Zervan (MAJESTIC)  had to steady his mount.  Jockey Dashrath Singh has been cautioned

 

In the last 100 metres,  CHEF D’OEUVRE (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out and went across  SUMAAQ  (Y.S.Srinath). On questioning, Jockey Neeraj Rawal stated that his mount shifted out under pressure.

 

After the race, App.R.Ajinkya (PURE BLISS) reported that his mount was trying to run out passing the 600 metres and thereafter did not raise a gallop. Trainer S.K.Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that after completion of the race, FIREPROOF was observed to be sore from the left fore leg. First aid treatment was administered on the track and the filly was sent to the Equine Hospital by Horse Ambulance for further management. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, PURE BLISS was found to be sound, but having a bruise on the left quarter.
