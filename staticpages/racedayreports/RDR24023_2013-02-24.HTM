RACE NO.1(217)  – THE  BLURR TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 12

 

Trainer Karthik G. has been fined Rs.1,000/- for giving Clearance to two Trainers to declare his Apprentice S.Mosin, which resulted in the Apprentice being declared to ride on two horses in the same race.

 

At the start, PRINCE ARIUS (Akshay) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Jockeys Daniel M.Grant (STAR BLESSINGS) and Akshay (PRINCE ARIUS) have been advised not to shift in before the Red Marker.

 

Jockey R.K.Mahesh (RADICAL MACKIE) has been cautioned for allowing his mount to shift outwards under the use of the whip in the straight.

Jockey Ishwar Pardeshi (AMAZING SMILE) has been reprimanded for allowing his mount to shift inwards under the use of the whip in the straight.

 

App.Prasad (VINCERO) has been cautioned for allowing his mount to shift outwards in the straight.

 

Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed and Jockey Shrikant Kamble were questioned regarding the poor performance of CRRISP.  The Jockey said that his Instructions were to go to the front. He stated that his mount did not respond to his urgings and after the race he had informed the Trainer that his mount was sluggish while proceeding to the Gates. Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed was informed that the odds on the gelding drifted and he had performed poorly. The Trainer stated that the Instructions were to go to the front and  if he could not do so, then to be in 2nd position. He said that there was no common horse and that the gelding had no form coming into this race, hence he was surprised at the short odds though he did give the horse a chance to race well.  He added that the gelding today raced over 1200 metres which was 400 metres shorter than in Race no 161 where the gelding had performed decently. He also cited the shorter time gap between today’s race and the previous race i.e. Race no 206 may be the reason for poor performance. He added that the horse has broken a blood vessel on previous occasions, which was also the reason for the Owners to have sold this horse. The Trainer opted for endoscopy for the gelding. On a request, the Sr. VO reported that CRRISP had returned sore by the right fore leg. Also, on Endoscopy, the horse was found to have bled internally (Gr.II EIPH).  The explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey has been noted.   

 

After the race, CRRISP was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, STAR BLESSINGS, RADICAL MACKIE, CONZTANZA and VINCERO were found to be sound. ATHOS had a gum injury.

RACE NO.6(222) –THE  POONAWALLA BREEDERS’ MULTI-MILLION (GR. I): (About) 1400 Mts– Runners - 17

 

After being stalled, Jockey S.Zervan (MARIINSKY) informed that the bit on his mount had slipped. The filly was taken out and the bit was readjusted and the filly was re-stalled.

 

GEROME (R.Hughes) was fractious in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. The Trainer has been asked to give the colt more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Two objections were lodged in this race. The first objection was lodged by Jockey David Allan, rider of STARRY EYES (Placed Second), against Jockey B.Sreekanth (MURIOI, placed First). The reason being, in the last furlong, MURIOI carried him to the right and made his horse hit the horse on the inside and lose balance and momentum at a vital stage of the race which cost him the race as his filly was regaining towards the finish.

 

The second objection was lodged by Jockey D.K.Ashish, rider of FALCON (Placed Fifth), against DANCING PRANCES (Dashrath Singh, Placed Fourth). The reason being, DANCING PRANCES came out sharply on to him when his horse was full of running, due to which his horse shied at about 20 metres from the winning post, which cost him the fourth place.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule both the objections.

 

The Stewards after the last race, interviewed Jockey Dashrath Singh for careless riding on his mount DANCING PRANCES and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 14th March, 2013 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Near 400 metres, ALBERTINO (Paul Mulrennan) received interference as STARRY EYES (David Allan) shied away and shifted in due to MURIOI (B.Sreekanth) lugging inwards.

 

Jockey David Allan (STARRY EYES) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

MURIOI lugged-in in the straight.  Trainer S.Ganapathy has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the colt. 

 

After the race, GEROME was observed to have an abrasion at the right fore pastern,  MURIOI had an abrasion at the left hock, STARRY EYES had a cut injury at the right hock and ALBERTINO had a gum injury.  DANCING PRANCES, HAIL THE KING and MURIOI were found to be sound. 

 

FALCON (D.K.Ashish) was placed fifth and DANCING PHOENIX (J.Fortune) was placed sixth by the Judge, in this race.

RACE NO.7(223) – THE  HONG KONG JOCKEY CLUB TROPHY:(About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 11

 

INTESAR (Imran Chisty) and MUNTASIR (Neeraj Rawal) were restless in the stall.  ANCIENT WARRIOR (D.K.Ashish) reared up and put his fore legs on to the side partition of the stall and got cast. The colt was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. and found to have suffered cut injury on the left stifle and an abrasion on the left

fore cannon. As such, ANCIENT WARRIOR was certified unfit to race and hence withdrawn on Veterinary grounds, before coming under the Starter’s Order. Trainers H.J.Antia, Rehanullah Khan and M.Narredu have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. Further, Trainer M.Narredu is required to obtain a Fitness Certificate for ANCIENT WARRIOR from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, MUNTASIR (Neeraj Rawal, dr.7) jumped awkwardly inwards and went on to GLORIOUS REWARD (Valdir R. De Sousa, dr.6) who in turn went on to DYNAMO (R.Hughes, dr.6).

 

INTESAR (Imran Chisty, dr.10) shifted in before the Red marker and hampered the running of BROWN DYNAMITE (M.A.Roushan). Jockey Imran Chisty has been fined Rs.2,000/-

 

Jockey P.Trevor (SILVER STREAK, dr.11) has been fined Rs.2,000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker.

 

ZARAR (S.S.Baria) hung outwards throughout the race and at the start shifted out and went across ZAAGROS (Shrikant Kamble) who was steadied and drifted outwards and went on to SILVER STREAK (P.Trevor) who had also shifted inwards at that stage. ZARAR drifted outwards while negotiating the bend and then raced along the outer rails. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that ZARAR was found to be sound, but having wolf tooth on the left side. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Approaching 400 metres, GLORIOUS REWARD (Valdir R. De Sousa) drifted outwards for a brief period.

 

After the race, DYNAMO was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind pastern..

 

RACE NO.8(224) – THE  LE GRIS CHEVAL TROPHY:(About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 10

 

The Owners of STORMY WIND informed that since they have removed all the horses to another Trainer, they did not wish to race STORMY WIND with the new Trainer as she was accepted to race by the previous Trainer. As such, the filly was permitted to be withdrawn.

 

Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha informed that he had erroneously accepted ALKAZI while the horse was medicated and therefore requested permission not to declare the horse, which was granted and Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

At the start, GRANDE DAME (S.Zervan) shifted in as the filly was settled and went on to HOME FACTOR (Neeraj Rawal) who in turn went out on to ARCTIC MINSTREL (J.Chnoy) and at the same time ZIDAAN (P.Trevor) shifted ground inwards, due to which ARCTIC MINSTREL was tightened and lost ground.

 

At the start, HOME FACTOR  (Neeraj Rawal, dr.8) shifted in and tightened BUCHAREST and then took the running of latter, who clipped the hind heels and stumbled. Jockey Neeraj Rawal was reported to the Stewards for careless riding.

The Stewards interviewed Jockey Neeraj Rawal and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, held him guilty of careless riding on his mount HOME FACTIOR.  The Stewards noted that Jockey Neeraj Rawal’s license already stands suspended w.e.f. 14th March, 2013 to 17th March, 2013 for an earlier whip offence on SENOR HILL. Therefore, the Stewards decided to further suspend his Jockey’s license for 21st March, 2013 for careless riding on HOME FACTOR (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Near 900 metres, DREAMLAND (D.K.Ashish) was eased up while racing on the inside of GRANDE DAME (S.Zervan) and then shifted out and went on to ICE ZONE (Kishore Kadam), who in turn went out on to HOME FACTOR (Neeraj Rawal).

 

ICE ZONE (Kishore Kadam) lugged-in in the straight. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that ICE ZONE was found to be sound, but having wolf tooth on the left side. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey J.Chinoy (ARCTIC MISTREL) has been cautioned for incompetent handling of his mount in the race wherein he travelled wide while negotiating the bend and in the straight for no apparent reason.

 

After the race, ARCTIC MINSTREL and DREAMLAND were found to be sound.
