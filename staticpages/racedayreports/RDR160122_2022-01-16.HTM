RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT.

 

RACE NO.1(45) – THE MARCHETTA PLATE  - DIVISION I: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  11

 

At the start, MASCARA (Yash Narredu, dr.11) jumped inwards and made contact CONNAUGHT (App. P Shinde, dr.10)

 

Passing the 300 metres, SUNLORD (App. Kirtish Bhagat) shifted out under the whip and went onto ON VA DANSER (P. S. Kaviraj). App Kirtish Bhagat has been severely reprimanded.

 

In the final stages of the races, VIKRAMADITYA (Antony Raj S.) was inclined outwards. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

It was observed that ON VA DANSER (App. P. S. Kaviraj) hung out in the final stages of the race despite rider’s effort. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar reported that his mount HELLBENT was not galloping fluently passing the 1200 metres and thereafter the gelding stopped galloping near 800 metres. On examination it was found that the gelding was sore by the right hind leg. He was given first aid treatment and evacuated to Equine Hospital for further treatment and investigation. Subsequently, the Sr. V.O. reported that the gelding was found dead on 17th January 2022 due to multiple fracture fragments of wings of ilium right hind limb.

 

After the race, MASCARA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(46) – THE HOMI MODY TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  5

 

No report

 

RACE NO.3(47) – THE THUMBELINA PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 7

 

After being stalled, TYRONE BLACK (A. Sandesh, dr.3) played up in the stall as such the colt was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

HAWK OF THE WIND (S. Zervan, dr.1) and HOOVES OF THUNDER (Yash Narredu, dr.4) were reluctant to stall. Trainers Vishal Gaikwad and M. Narredu have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, TYRONE BLACK (A. Sandesh, dr.3) jumped out onto HOOVES OF THUNDER (Yash Narredu, dr.4) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, SUPERNATURAL (P. S. Chouhan) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing HOOVES OF THUNDER (Yash Narredu) causing the later to check and drop back. After questioning, Jockey P. S. Chouhan has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race Jockey A. Sandesh (TYRONE BLACK) reported that his mount was running green.

 

RACE NO.4(48) – THE UTTAM SINGH TROPHY: (About) 2400 Metres –Runners- 6

 

No Report.

 

RACE NO.5(49) – THE RAMNIWAS RAMNARAIN RUIA GOLD CUP (GRADE 3): (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  6

 

At the start, DARWIN (Y. S. Srinath, dr.3) was observed to have stumbled thereby dislodged its rider.

 

RACE NO.6(50) – THE P. D. AVASIA TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 9

 

At the start, PETRONIA (P. Trevor, dr.3) jumped inwards and brushed the haunches of BAKHTAWAR (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2)

 

Approaching the 200 metres, MINX (App. Kirtish Bhagat) shifted in under urgings and went across STEPPENWOLF (R. Shelar) who had to be steadied. After questioning, App. Kirtish Bhagat has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

RACE NO.7(51) – THE VILLOO C. POONAWALLA INDIAN OAKS (GRADE 1): (About) 2400 Metres – Runners -  5

 

Passing the 400 metres, Jockey T. S. Jodha (SONG SONG BLUE) was observed to have lost his whip. This being his first instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021-22, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, ANIMOUS had a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.8(52) – THE TRICUMDAS DWARKADAS TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  14

 

On the arrival at the start, at the request of Jockey H. Zeeshan his mount SPIRIT BAY was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified her fit to race.

 

GUSTY GIRL (P. Vinod, dr.10) took an awkward fly at the start.

 

RHYTHM OF NATURE (M. Ayyar, dr.4) jumped and veered inwards thereby cutting across EMERALD (App. P. S. Kaviraj, dr.3) who was marginally slow to begin. Trainer S. S. Shah has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching the 650 metres, DRAGONESS (N. S. Parmar) racing on haunches of SPIRIT BAY (H. Zeeshan) and LAMBRETTA (Bhawani Singh) was denied a clear passage as a result overreached the front runners thereby losing its footing and had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 600 metres, RECIPROCITY (S. A. Amit) weakened and dropped back due to which RHYTHM OF NATURE (M. Ayyar) racing on his haunches had to check and change course and in the process made contact with REMUS (Antony Raj S.) and thereafter shifted in as a result TURMERIC TOWER (T. S. Jodha) was tightened and had to be checked.

 

After the race, RECIPROCITY was trotted and found to be sound but had a cut injury on left hind fetlock.

 

RACE NO.9(53) – THE MARCHETTA PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  11

 

It was observed that TOUCH OF FAITH (N. S. Parmar) was sporting with Cross Nose Band for which permission was not obtained the same was removed at the starting gates. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the equipment without seeking prior permission.

 

MAGICAL RAYS (N. Nadeem, dr.7) was reluctant to reach the starting point and thereafter was reluctant to stall. Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly satisfaction of the Starter, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 400 metres, POLARIS (Bhawani Singh) shifted out at the same time FULL OF GRACE (S. Zervan) shifted in due to which CHARMING STAR (Dashrath Singh) racing between them was tightened for room and dropped back. After questioning, Jockey Bhawani Singh and S. Zervan have been fined Rs.3,000/- each.

 

After the race, Jockey N. Nadeem reported that his mount MAGICAL RAYS hung in throughout the race. Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MAGICAL RAYS was trotted and found to be sound and POLARIS had a cut injury on right fore fetlock.

ADDITIONAL REPORT:

 

RE:  ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “JULIETTE” IN RACE NO.13 – THE ZAVARAY S POONAWALLA INDIAN TURF INVITATION CUP (GR.1) (2400 METRES) RUN ON 19th DECEMBER, 2021 :

 

In connection to the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 16th January 2022, heard Trainer Karthik Ganapathy and Jockey A. Sandesh along with his spokesperson Trainer Narendra Lagad and having viewed the video of the race in question, as also the previous races of JULIETTE i.e. Race no. 82 & 145 run at Hyderabad Race Club. Keeping in mind that during the course of the enquiry Jockey A. Sandesh pleaded guilty and that it was an error of judgment on his part and despite the dissatisfaction expressed by the trainer in his riding the Stewards decided to give him the benefit of the doubt for the charge of “not permitting your mount to run on its merits” and held him guilty of “unsatisfactory riding” on JULIETTE in Race No.13. It was noted that in the early part of the race he has dropped-out and positioned the mare more than 30 lengths behind the field thus denying the mare a fair chance in the race by failing to follow the specific riding instructions given to him by the trainer. Further, he has made no effort to improve his position due to which the mare was at complete disadvantage and had a daunting task to cover the lost ground. The Stewards also took exception to his riding efforts in the straight as the same were found wanting. Therefore, the Stewards of the Club decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for Twelve Race Days i.e. from 5th February, 2022 to 17th April, 2022 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).
