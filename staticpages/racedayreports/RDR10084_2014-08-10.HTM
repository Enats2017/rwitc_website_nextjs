ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

PUNE MEETING 2014, SPECIAL RACE DAY, SUNDAY, 10TH AUGUST, 2014.

(PENETROMETER: 5.2; GOING: GOOD TO YIELDING)

RACE DAY REPORTS

THERE WERE NO FALSE RAILS.

 

On a request from Trainer Dallas Todywalla, permission was granted to Trainer Nirad Karanjawala to saddle his charges OCEAN DREAM, ANTONIUS MAXIMUS, ATHENS, DANCING PHOENIX & SILVER STREAK, in the 3rd, 4th, 5th and 6th races respectively, at his (Trainer Dallas Todywalla’s) risk and responsibility.

-------------------------

RACE NO.1(44) – THE  HURRICANE PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – 9 Runners

 

At the start, MINSTREL SON (H.A.Sachin, dr.4) jumped awkwardly inwards and went on to WITHOUT FEAR (App.D.S.Daman, dr.3).

 

Near 200 metres, TWO OCEANS (A.Sandesh) put in an awkward stride and was unbalanced and shifted-in, due to which ANNABETH (C.S.Jodha) who was following, was steadied momentarily.

 

DIOR (App.Santosh G.) lugged-out in the straight and went across ZAAGROS (App.Akshay Gaikwad) who had to change course inwards. Trainer Bezan Chinoy has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, WITHOUT FEAR was observed to have a cut injury at the left fore bulb of heel and AMAYA had a cut injury at the left fore fetlock.  TWO OCEANS, ANNABETH and MINSTREL SON (examined on 11/08/14) were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(45) – THE HURRICANE PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres – 10 Runners

 

At the start, STORMYDAY (CS Jodha) jumped out awkwardly.

 

STORMYDAY (CS Jodha) leaned inwards in the straight. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that the gelding was found to be sound, but having wolf tooth on either side. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

SKYWALK (Anil Baandal) lugged-out while negotiating the bend. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, NEGREDO and SKYWALK were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(46) – THE  ARTS PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 7 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer P.Shroff, THE UNICORN was examined at 1240 hours on 08/08/2014 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg, as such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

At the start, SECRET OATH (S.Sunil) jumped out awkwardly.

 

SECRET OATH (S Sunil) lugged-out in the straight. Trainer Faisal A.Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, ULRIKA was observed to have a cut injury on the left hind bulb of heel. SECRET OATH was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(47) – THE GOODNESS GRACIOUS PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 9 Runners

 

Near 500 metres, DICTATOR (D.K.Ashish) shifted-in on to MONZA (Kavraj Singh), who in turn went on to SET TO FLY (P.Kamlesh). Jockey D.K.Ashish has been cautioned.

 

Jockey P.Trevor (PAPAKURA) reported that his saddle had slipped to the left side, as such, Trainer S.S.Shah has been fined Rs 2,000/-.

 

MONZA (Kavraj Singh) was held up for a clear run in the final stages of the race.

  

Trainer S.S.Shah was questioned regarding the comparative performance of his charge PAPAKURA in this race which the gelding won as compared to its earlier run in Race No 23 run on 2nd August 2014 where he was unplaced, about 12 lengths behind the winner. The Trainer stated that after the last run during the Mumbai Meeting, the gelding was sore from the right fore leg and had undergone treatment. He added that he had not given any pace work to the gelding coming into its first run in Race No 23. Further, considering the unsoundness of the gelding, he was unsure whether he should run the horse or not, however, he took a chance to race him over 1200 metres. Trainer S.S.Shah said that since the gelding returned sound after the first race, he decided to race him today as it was a restricted race for horses 5 year old over, over 1400 metres, which was the only such race during the current Pune Meeting. In his opinion, the favourite, MOON SHADOW, was too good and he had not fancied the gelding and he was available at longer odds. The explanation tendered by Trainer S.S.Shah was noted.

 

After the race, IN THE LIMELIGHT was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MOON SHADOW and SET TO FLY were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(48) – THE MAYOR’S TROPHY: (About) 1800 Metres – 5 Runners

 

After the race, ATHENS was observed to have an abrasion above the right eye and nostril and a cut injury above the left eye.

 

RACE NO.6(49) – THE PUNE BOOKMAKERS’ ASSOCIATION TURF CLUB TROPHY (GR.3):

                                                                                                                                 (About) 1400 Metres – 9 Runners

At the request of Trainer P.Shroff, AMELIA was examined at 1245 hours on 08/08/2014 and was found to be sore by the right hind leg, as such, the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

Near 200 metres, SUPREME STAR (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out and went on to HEATWAVE (C.S.Jodha), who shifted out and tightened STARRY EYES (P.Kamlesh) on to COMMANDER (N.S.Parmar), who itself had leaned inwards at that stage. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.3,000/- and severely cautioned for the future.

 

RACE NO.7(50) – THE MASTER OFTHE ROLLS PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 13 Runners

 

Near 500 metres, ZOLTAN (App. Yash Narredu) lugged inwards and instead of correcting his mount, App.Yash Narredu rode his mount while being placed on the outside of DARK WILLIAM (Shrikant Kamble), which resulted in ZOLTAN making contact with DARK WILLIAM twice. As such, App.Yash Narredu has been fined Rs.3,000/- and severely cautioned for the future. Also, Trainer Nina M. Lalvani has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey P.Trevor (ZUMI ZUMI) reported that his mount was inclined to lug-out during the race. Trainer S.S.Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, ZUMI ZUMI was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(51) – THE MOOSA M.HOOSEIN TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 15 Runners

 

Soon after the start, MOUNTAIN SONG (Hanumant Singh) was steadied as YARDSTICK (S.P.Ranjane) shifted inwards. Jockey S.P.Ranjane has been cautioned.

 

Passing 400 metres, PALACE GIRL (V.Chandrakant) shifted in and hampered the running of YARDSTICK (S.P.Ranjane). Jockey V.Chandrakant has been reprimanded.

 

Near 200 metres, SHAUGHNESSY (A.Sandesh) was inconvenienced as ACT OF VALOUR (S.Mosin) shifted ground inwards as the gelding lugged in and continued to do so in the straight. Trainer S.K.Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, YARDSTICK was observed to have lost its left hind plate. VICTOIRE, AFFIRMATION, ACT OF VALOUR (examined on 11/08/2014) and PALACE GIRL (examined on 11/08/2014), were found to be sound. 

 

RACE NO.9(52) – THE MOOSA M.HOOSEIN TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 14 Runners

 

MAQSAD (Vikram Walkar) was restless in the stall. Trainer M.K.Jadhav has been asked to give the gelding more Gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, MAQSAD (Vikram Walkar, dr.2) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to PRETTY IN PATPONG (Hanumant Singh) who in turn made contact with FEISTY LASS (J.Chinoy) who in turn went on to MONTERAY (Kishore Kadam, dr.5).   COME ALIVE (H.A.Sachin) jumped out awkwardly.

 

THUNDER STAR (S.Mosin) stumbled at the start.

 

At the start, LUCKY CHARM (Dashrath Singh) travelled wide.

 

Passing 1000 metres, PRETTY IN PATPONG (Hanumant Singh) travelled wide, due to which OCEAN STAR (Akash   Agarwal) travelled wide. Also, IN TIME (App.R.Ajinkya) travelled wide, due to which DIVINE WISH (A.Sandesh) travelled wide. At this stage, LUCKY CHARM (Dashrath Singh) shied away from the horses on its inside and travelled wider.

Approaching 600 metres, LUCKY CHARM (Dashrath Singh) shifted in and tightened DIVINE WISH (A.Sandesh) on to IN TIME (App.R.Ajinkya). On questioning, Jockey Dashrath Singh reported that his mount ran green during the race and had shifted in on its own. The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards considered the above report and having heard Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey Dashrath Singh and having viewed the video recording of this race as well as the horse’s previous race i.e. Race No.12 during the current Pune Meeting, and keeping in mind that the horse had run green in the previous race as well, decided to give Jockey Dashrath Singh the benefit of a grave doubt. However, Jockey Dashrath Singh was told that he should have been aware that there was a fancied runner on his inside and should have made more efforts to control the horse and he was severely cautioned for the future. Further, the Stipendiary Stewards were directed to inform Trainer Narendra Lagad to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, LUCKY CHARM was observed to have an abrasion at the left hind fetlock. DIVINE WISH was found to be sound.
