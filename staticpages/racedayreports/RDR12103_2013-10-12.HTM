RE: APPEAL AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS IN RESPECT OF (1) SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENSE W.E.F. 12/10/2013 TO 13/10/2013 FOR HAVING BEEN FOUND GUILTY OF USING HIS WHIP IN EXCESS OF THE PRESCRIBED LIMIT ON HIS MOUNT ‘BOMBARDIER’ IN RACE NO 172 – THE S.A.POONAWALLA MULTI-MILLION (GR.2) RUN ON 22ND SEPTEMBER, 2013 AND  (2) WITHDRAWAL OF HIS WHIP FOR HAVING FOUND IN BREACH OF THE NORMS FOR USE OF THE WHIP ON HIS MOUNT ‘SUPREME STAR’ IN RACE NO 180 – THE NIRMAL RUIA MILLION RUN ON 28TH SEPTEMBER, 2013 - JOCKEY D.K. ASHISH :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on Monday, 7th October, 2013 considered two appeals from Jockey D.K.Ashish in the above connection. The Board of Appeal, having studied all documents and papers that were submitted, including the Grounds of Appeal and having considered the submissions made by Jockey D.K.Ashish and having viewed the video recording of the races in question, deliberated on the matter at length and having applied their minds, decided to reject both the appeals and forfeit the deposits. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands.

 

Further, the Board of Appeal felt that the punishment meted out to Jockey D.K.Ashish by the Stewards of the Club needs to be enhanced especially since he has repeated the offence despite the fact that when he was punishment for his third offence, the Chairman had informed him that should he commit another whipping offence during the current Pune Meeting 2013, he shall be deprived of the use of the whip in races for the minimum of 10 days. The Board therefore asked Jockey D.K.Ashish to show cause as to why the punishment imposed on him by the Stewards should not be enhanced further by the Board.

 

The Chairman of the Board of Appeal explained to Jockey D.K.Ashish the abovementioned decision of the Board of Appeal in detail and gave him time to come before the Board of Appeal with his submissions / response to the show cause. However, Jockey D.K.Ashish indicated that he did not require any further time and he would like to reply to the show cause immediately.

 

When Jockey D.K.Ashish was ready with his response, he was called and his submissions were heard by the Board of Appeal. The Board having deliberated on the matter, took a lenient view, and having applied their minds decided not to enhance his punishment, but to severely warn him to be careful in the future.

RE : RACE NO.201 – THE OCTOBER HANDICAP RUN ON 6TH OCTOBER, 2013 – JOCKEY P.KAMLESH, RIDER OF “RECKLESS” – REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR CARELESS RIDING :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 12th October, 2013 interviewed Jockey P.Kamlesh in the above connection and having heard him and having viewed the video recording of Race No.201 as also the previous race of the filly i.e. Race No.136, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and held him guilty of careless riding on his mount RECKLESS.  The Stewards noted that rounding the final bend RECKLESS first shifted in and jostled with SILSILA (R.Vaibhav) who in turn tightened BEFOREMEMORYFADES (Neeraj Rawal) and thereafter again shifted in twice in the straight and caused interference to BEFOREMEMORYFADES and Jockey P.Kamlesh made no apparent effort to correct his mount. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 17th November, 2013 to 24th November, 2013 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

--------------------------------

RE : RACE NO.161 – THE INGLENOOK PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON 21AT SEPTEMBER, 2013 – JOCKEY S.ZERVAN, RIDER OF “MIDNIGHT WIND” – REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR CARELESS RIDING:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 12th October, 2013 interviewed Jockey S.Zervan in the above connection and having heard him and having viewed the video recording of Race No.161 as also the previous two races of the horse i.e. Race No.20 and Race No.43, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and held him guilty of careless riding on his mount MIDNIGHT WIND.  The Stewards noted that near the 1000 metres

 

Jockey S.Zervan allowed his mount to shift in without making any effort to correct the horse and caused severe interference to OCEAN OF FAITH (App.Sandeep Jadhav). Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 17th November, 2013   (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “MIDNIGHT WIND” IN RACE NO.161 – THE INGLENOOK PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON SATURDAY, 21ST SEPTEMBER, 2013 :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 21st September, 2013 had directed the Stipendiary Stewards to conduct an enquiry into the poor performance of MIDNIGHT WIND in Race No.161 and to submit a report.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 12th October, 2013 having considered a report from the Sr.Veterinary Officer that MIDNIGHT WIND was scoped after the races at the Club’s Equine Hospital and was found to have suffered EIPH Grade-III and in view of the same decided to close the matter.
