RACE NO.3(301) – THE PRIME TIME PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 14

 

Soon after the start, NAME OF THE GAME (A.Ashhad Asbar) shifted in and carried SIMPLY SHINING (N.Rawal) inwards, who tightened SENTOSA COVE (D.K.Ashish), who in turn went on to AURELIO (Dashrath Singh). Jockey A.Ashhad Asbar has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Jockey N.Rawal (SIMPLY SHINING) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker.

  

Soon on entering the straight, AURELIO (Dashrath Singh) leaned inwards and then shifted out as the Jockey used /his whip as a corrective measure and carried SENTOSA COVE (D.K.Ashish) outwards. Passing 200 metres, AURELIO hung out suddenly and jostled with SENTOSA COVE (D.K.Ashish) and carried SENTOSA COVE outwards and then continued to hang out and finished racing along the outer rails. Even though the gelding had returned sore after the race, Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards in a Mock race before next accepting to race,.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey D.K.Ashish, rider of SENTOSA COVE (Placed Fifth) against AURELIO (Dashrath Singh, Placed Third). The reason being, in the final 200 metres, AURELIO bumped his horse twice and took him out twice, due to which he had to stop riding and this cost him a certain place.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing of the race as under :

MERAKI                                 -               FIRST;                   NAME OF THE GAME       -               THIRD;

ITSCARO                              -               SECOND;             SENTOSA COVE                                -               FOURTH.

 

After the race, AURELIO was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ESPADA was found to be sound.

ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “BALCHANDRA” IN RACE NO.284: THE SIMPLY GORGEOUS PLATE RUN ON 26TH MARCH 2015 – THE OWNER HAD LODGED A COMPLAINTTHAT HE WAS NOT SATISFIED WITH THE RIDING OF JOCKEY I.PASHA :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 4th April,  2015 considered Note dated 28th March, 2015 from the Stipendiary Stewards. They interviewed Jockey I.Pasha who was accompanied by his Spokesman, Mr.Narendra, as also Trainer Asad Siddiqui. Having viewed the video of Race No.284 as also Jockey I.Pasha’s winning ride on DOUBLE DIAMOND in Race No.42, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey I.Pasha and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on his mount BALCHANDRA. The Stewards noted that when he pulled his horse out at about 300 metres, the horse was responding well and he was full of running, however, the Jockey did not ride out his mount but rather restrained the horse by his left hand thereby not allowing the horse to gallop freely. Accordingly, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 19th April, 2015 to 26th April, 2015 (both days inclusive). Thereafter, should he apply for a license for the Season 2014/15, it will be granted to him, however, his license shall stand suspended for Nine Race Days commencing from the first day of the Pune Meeting 2015. (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Further, the Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer Asad Siddiqui. The Stewards noted that despite him (Tr.Asad Siddiqui) and Jockey I.Pasha being fined for unsatisfactory running and riding of the same horse in Race No.180 during the current Mumbai Meeting, he still chose to declare the same Jockey. Further, he also made contradictory statements in the enquiry. Therefore, the Stewards decided to severely caution him for the future.

Jockey I Pasha (BALCHANDRA) was observed to have used his whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount BALCHANDRA in Race No.284. RE :  CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS REPORT IN RESPECT  OF ch f “MAISHA” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.17 – THE MAHARAJA OF MORVI TROPHY RUN ON 23RD NOVEMBER, 2014 :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Saturday, 4th April, 2015 interviewed Tr.P.Shroff regarding the Confirmatory Analysis Report dated 1st April, 2015 from LCH, France, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge MAISHA (Winner) in Race No.17 – The Maharaja of Morvi Trophy run on 23rd November, 2014, which read as under:-

 

“…..It is hereby confirmed that the sample, which has been analysed as set out above, contains O-DESMETHYLVENLAFAXINE”.

 

A copy of the above report was handed over to Trainer P.Shroff and he was asked to continue his own enquiries and to lead evidence, if any, to prove mitigating circumstances, within 15 days and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.Veterinary Officer of the Club, as and when required.
