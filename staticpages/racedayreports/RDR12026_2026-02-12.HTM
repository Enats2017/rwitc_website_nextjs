RACE NO.2 (107) THE M. D. MEHTA TROPHY : (ABOUT) 1000 METRES - RUNNERS - 6

 

Approaching the 300 meters LATIOS (Yash Narredu) veered out and went across AVANTE (Neeraj Rawal) and DREAMS COME TRUE ( A.Prakash) at the same time DREAMS COME TRUE (A.Prakash) shifted in sharply thereby carrying AVANTE (Neeraj Rawal ) inwards, Jockey A Prakash has been cautioned.

 

In the final stages of the race LUCKY DIAMOND (K. Nazil) leaned out thereby tightening DREAMS COME TRUE (A.Prakash) racing on its haunches causing the latter to be checked.  After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation Jockey K. Nazil has been fined Rs 3,000/-

 

After the Race the VO reported the following:

 

AVANTE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

DREAMS COME TRUE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3 (108) THE P. D. BOLTON TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES  - RUNNERS -  8

 

MR BRAINWASH was examined by the Clubs VO at the stables at 11;45 am on 11th February 2025 and was found to be sore by Left Fore , As such , the Horse  was permitted to be withdrawn on Veterinary Grounds and has to be passed fit by the Clubs VO before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, FOURTH WING (Yash Narredu, dr. 5) jumped inwards at the same time DESTROYER (S.G.Prasad, dr 2) jumped outwards onto FORTUNATE SUN (N.S.Parmar, dr 3 ) who in turn shifted in thereby tightening and inconviencing THUNDERING PHEONIX( Vivek.G d r 4 ) drawn between them causing the latter to be checked

After the Race the VO reported the following :

 

BASHIR was trotted and found to be sound

 

FOURTH WING was trotted and found to be sound

 

BIG RED was trotted and found to be sound

 

FORTUNATE SON was trotted and found to be Sore by Right Fore , As such the Gelding has to be passed fit by the Clubs VO before next accepting to Race.

 

At the behest of the Stipendiary Stewards an Endoscopy was performed on FOURTH WING and no abnormality was found.

RACE NO.5 (110) THE W. BUCKLEY PLATE : (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

In the final stages of the race BRIGHT BUTTON (Neeraj Rawal ) shifted in thereby tightening and inconviencing VIKING (Akshay Kumar ) racing on its haunches causing the latter to  check and change  course.  After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs 5,000/-

 

An objection lodged by Jockey Vivek G ,rider of MA CHERIE, placed 4th by the judge  against Jockey Akshay Kumar, rider of VIKING , placed 2nd by the judge in that “In the last 200 Meters , when my horse was full of running VIKING shifted out and when I tried to come from inside , he again shifted in front of me where I had to stop riding and switch my horse outside which cost  me a certain 2nd place’’ The Stewards after hearing the submissions made by both the Jockeys and viewed  the video recording of the Race from all angles, in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards and acting on their own observations of the race decided to overrule the objection and the placing remain unchanged as declared by the judge

 

The Stipendiary Stewards after questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation decided to fine Jockey Akshay Kumar Rs 5,000/-
