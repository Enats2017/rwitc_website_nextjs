RACE NO.1(163) –THE SONNY BRAR TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners - 6 

 

Passing 150 metres, DAFFODILS (David Probert) shifted out and at the same time ETERNAL SUNSHINE (S. Zervan) shifted in, inconveniencing GLORIOUS EYES (Neeraj Rawal).

 

After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (GLORIOUS EYES) informed that he did not get a clear passage in the straight.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Zervan reported that his mount ETERNAL SUNSHINE Hung-in in the straight.

 

An Enquiry has been initiated into the riding of ETERNAL SUNSHINE (S. Zervan) in this race and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of GLORIOUS EYES (Neeraj Rawal) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, SQUARE MOON, ODESSA and ETERNAL SUNSHINE (examined on 11/02/2018) were trotted and found to be sound. ETERNAL SUNSHINE had an abrasion at the left fore fetlock.

 

RACE NO.2(164) – THE NATIVE KNIGHT PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 8

 

At the start, UNIMAGINABLE (David Probert, dr. 5) jumped outwards onto GOLD MEDALIST (P. S. Chouhan, dr. 6).

 

Approaching 1400 metres, UNIMAGINABLE (David Probert) leaned outwards on to BOURBON PRINCESS (P. Trevor) who had to be steadied.

 

Passing 1200 metres, UNIMAGINABLE (David Probert) was observed to have taken an awkward stride.

 

Approaching 200 metres, BOURBON PRINCESS (P. Trevor) shifted in onto AMAZING ART (Yash Narredu) who had to be checked. After the race Jockey P. Trevor reported that his mount was inclined to run in, despite his efforts. As such, Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, BOOMERANG, UNIMAGINABLE and BOURBON PRINCESS were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(165) –THE DELTIN CASINOS ECLIPSE STAKES OF INDIA (GR.2): (About) 2000 Metres – Runners- 2 

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of TEMERITY (Neeraj Rawal) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, TEMERITY was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(166) –THE  KOKU – AL TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres–Runners- 11

 

On the arrival at the start, CRYSTAL BALL (B. Nikhil) and ABERLOUR (S. Jaykumar) were examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified them fit to race.

 

REGAL SHOT (S. Sunil), ABERLOUR (S. Jaykumar) and JACKPOT (App. M. Ayyar) were reluctant to reach the starting point. Trainers Karthik Ganapathy, Altamaash A. Ahmed and Vinesh have been asked to take remedial measures with their respective charge and ensure that they reach the starting point on time in the future.

 

At the start, ABERLOUR (S. Jaykumar) was observed to have stumbled and had to be steadied.

 

Passing the 150 metres, OPTIMUM (App. H. Zeeshan) was observed to have veered out thereby bumped and carried RAIN DANCE (D. R. Shubham) outwards. After the race, App. H. Zeeshan reported that his mount had shied away from the whip. Trainer Shazaan Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey S. Zervan, the rider of HOPEILICIOUS (Placed Fourth) against CHAPLIN (David Allan, Placed Second). The reason being, coming into the straight, near the one furlong marker, his horse HOPEILICIOUS was full of running and gaining momentum at which time CHAPLIN (David Allan) came from the outside and bumped his horse, cut across him badly and took his ground, due to which he had to completely stop riding and check his horse. Thereafter, his horse again ran on to place fourth and this cost him a certain 2nd/3rd place. 

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgement, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection.

 

Passing the 150 metres, HOPEILICIOUS (S. Zervan) shifted in under pressure and made contact with CELESTIAL LIGHT (P. Trevor) and ricocheted outwards, at the same time CHAPLIN (David Allan) shifted in, due to which they made contact.

 

After the race, ABERLOUR was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CHAPLIN, RINCON, REGAL SHOT and JACKPOT were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(167) – THE DELTIN CASINOS TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres –Runners- 10 

 

JARAMA (App. M. Ayyar) jumped out slow losing about 6 lengths at the start. Since the mare had behaved in a similar manner in her previous start and was subsequently passed in a Mock Race, in view of her repeated behavior, Trainer S. Waheed has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter in two Mock Races, before next accepting with her to race.

 

At the start BOUNTY QUEEN (S. A. Amit, dr. 6) jumped out awkwardly and shifted in brushing the haunches of ETERNALINSPERATION (Yash Narredu, dr. 5)

 

Passing the 150 metres, due to a tightly packed field ROOSEVELT (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in thereby tightening LADY IN RED (P. S. Chouhan) who had to be checked.

 

After the race, JARAMA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ROOSEVELT, BOUNTY QUEEN and TIMELESS DEEDS were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(168)–THE DR.S.C.JAIN SPRINTERS’ CHAMPIONSHIP (GR.2): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 10

 

At the start, ST. ANDREWS (Santosh G dr.6) jumped inwards on to RUFFINA (David Allan dr.5).

 

After the race, CAPTAIN COURAGE, DANCING PRANCES and LINCOLN were trotted and found to be sound. CAPTAIN COURAGE had an abrasion at the left hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.7(169) –THE  KOKU – AL TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres–Runners- 11

 

At the start, SNOW BLAST (G. Amit, dr.5) jumped inwards on to GOOD THING (J. Pradeep, dr.4).

 

Passing the 250 metres, SNOW BLAST (G. Amit) shifted in thereby tightening PORVI (S. A. Amit) along the rails. Jockey G. Amit has been cautioned.

 

After the race Jockey Neeraj Rawal (EBONY) reported that his mount hung out in the straight. Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, GOOD THING was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, DIVINE MAGIC, EBONY, TESSA and TRANSFORM were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(170) – THE DR.JAGJIT SINGH TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 14 

 

EPIPHANY (P. Trevor) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Nazak Chenoy has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 700 metres, STUNNER (P. S. Chouhan) shifted in on to ALSACE (App. H. Zeeshan) who in turn shifted in onto WIND CRAFT (Bhawani Singh) forcing it to be checked. Jockey P. S. Chouhan has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 250 metres, CHERRY BLOSSOM (S. Zervan) shifted in onto ANNIVERSARY GIRL (App. Raghuveer Singh) who had to be steadied. Jockey S. Zervan has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 150 metres, SHINNING NOON (G. Amit) shifted out and carried BRONX (S. Sunil) outwards. Jockey G. Amit has been severely reprimanded.
