RACE NO.1(154): THE TRAINERS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES - RUNNERS - 7

 

No report

 

RACE NO.2(155): THE DADY ADENWALLA TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES - RUNNERS - 9

 

LIGHT OF LIFE (S.J.Sunil, dr.3) jumped outwards onto EL GRECO (P. Trevor, dr.4) causing the latter to be steadied.

 

After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh (OPUS DEI) reported that his mount hung-out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, EL GRECO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(156): THE RACE OF HOPE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES  - RUNNERS -  8

 

Passing the 800 metres, CHENEVIX TRENCH (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out and went across BRAMHOS (Amyn Merchant) who had to be steadied.

 

After the race, App P. S. Kaviraj (SUPREME SPIRIT) reported that his mount was inclined outwards throughout the race. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (ALOYSIA) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.4(157): THE JOCKEYS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 14

 

In the process of backing-in M’SARRAT (App. Haridas Gore, dr.1), the filly leaned on the left front flap due to which the spring holder was damaged and was left unserviceable. As such the horses which were loaded were unloaded and the damaged part was replaced with the new part. The gates were tested and thereafter the horses were loaded. Due to the said incident the race was delayed.

 

After being stalled, MYRCELLA (H. Zeeshan, dr.5) ducked the front gate and came out as such the mare was withdrawn before coming under Starters orders. Trainer Shazaan Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to be shown to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

In the process of stalling, TABRIZ (App. A. S. Peter, dr.15) brushed the rear gate as such the mare was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O and was certified fit to race.

 

MYSTERIOUS GIRL (K. Nazil, dr.6) was reluctant to stall. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to be shown to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 400 metres, BALENCIAGA (P. Trevor) shifted out carrying KIRKINES (Neeraj Rawal) outwards.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, MYSTERIOUS GIRL (K. Nazil) shifted in and went across STUNNING VISUAL (Amyn Merchant) causing the latter to check and change course. Jockey K. Nazil has been cautioned.

 

After the race, the Sr. V.O. reported that CHIEFTAIN cast both his fore legs on the side bar, the gelding was unloaded, examined and was found to be sound.

 

After the race, STUNNING VISUAL was observed to have broken a blood vessel (2nd time) and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s Sr. V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CHIEFTAIN, TABRIZ & STUNNING VISUAL were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(158): THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 13

 

After being stalled, RHYTHM OF NATURE (App. N. Bhosale, dr.11) reared up and fell in the stall and was taken out. As such, the mare was withdrawn before coming under Starter’s order. Trainer S. S. Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

DALASAN (K. Nazil, dr.9) jumped out slowly thereafter stopped galloping after the start. In view of the geldings behavior, Trainer S. Waheed has been asked to show his charge in two mock races to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, TYRONE BLACK (App. A. K. Aniket, dr.13) jumped out tardily and tried to veer out thereby losing about two lengths.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, KARDASHIAN (Amyn Merchant) was observed to have stumbled thereby fell along with the rider. After the race, Sr. V.O. reported that the mare died and did not respond to the resuscitation efforts to revive her.

 

In the final stages of the race, SIM SIM (App. Haridas Gore) shifted out thereby inconveniencing WALSHY (S. J. Sunil). App. Haridas Gore has been cautioned.

 

After the race, WALSHY was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s Sr. V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(159): THE DADY ADENWALLA TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES -  RUNNERS -  8

 

At the start, TESS (N. S. Parmar, dr.6) jumped and veered outwards and had to be steadied.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (TESS) reported that he was denied a clear passage in the final stages of the race.

 

After the race, BUBBLY BOY was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(160): THE INDIAN ASSOCIATION OF EQUINE PRACTITIONERS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES - RUNNERS - 6

 

Trainer S. S. Attaollahi has been fined Rs.3,000/- for putting in late declaration in respect of his charge BELLATOR.

 

After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh (FLYING VISIT) reported that he was denied a clear passage in the final stages of the race.

 

RACE NO.8(161): THE WIRHOA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES - RUNNERS - 10

 

Passing the 300 metres, RAFFAELLO (A. Sandesh) shifted out at the same time GAZINO (S. Zervan) shifted in due to which BABY BAZOOKA (K. Nazil) racing between them was tightened for room and had to be steadied.

 

After the race, App. A. K. Aniket (ARCANA) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CELLINI was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.9(162): THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES - RUNNERS - 12

 

At the request of Trainer Vijay Kasbekar, WINSTAR was examined at 09.40 a.m. on 26/03/2023 and found to be lame by right fore, as such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 200 metres, REMY RED (N. Nadeem) shifted in at the same time BOMBER (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out due to which PERFECT MAN (App. P. Shinde) racing on its haunches clipped the heels of the latter and stumbled. Jockey N. Nadeem has been cautioned.

 

After the race, PERFECT MAN was observed to have broken a blood vessel (2nd time) and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s Sr. V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Sr. V.O. reported that at the request of Jockey, TANHAIYAAN was examined at the starting gates and found to be sound, the gelding came back sound after the race also.

 

After the race, DAULAT MAI, BLAZE CARSON & BOMBER were trotted and found to be sound.
