RE : MERCY PETITION FROM TRAINER M.K.JADHAV :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 4th February, 2016, considered Mercy Petition dated 22nd January, 2016 from Trainer M.K.Jadhav in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal considered the matter and having applied minds to the entire facts of the case and having heard Tr. M. K. Jadhav, was of the opinion that his Mercy Petition with regard to the penalty imposed on him for the positive findings of Venlafaxine in the horses under his charge lies with the Board of Appeal whereas penalty imposed on him for the positive finding of Flurbiprofen, which was not appealed against, did not lie with the Board of Appeal, therefore the matter was not under the realm of the Board of Appeal.

 

Since Trainer M. K. Jadhav had already undergone a suspension of beyond 60 days when the Board of Appeal considered his Mercy Petition, therefore, no action was called for in the matter.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE AWKWARD JUMP OUT OF “GOOD MEMORY” IN RACE NO.109 – THE BLUE RIBBON PLATE RUN ON 7TH JANUARY, 2016 :

 

Jockey S. Mosin was questioned in the above connection. He stated that his instructions were to take a good jump and sit 1st or 2nd. He said that the gelding took a fly jump and he could not control his mount. To a specific question, he stated that his reins were loose and he had not tried to hold the gelding in the gates.

 

The video recording from the starting gate cameras was viewed and it was pointed out to Jockey S. Mosin that it does appear that he was holding his reins tight just before jump out, which was primarily the reason for the horse taking an awkward jump. It was also pointed out to him that GOOD MEMORY subsequently won in his very next outing wherein he showed no signs of bad behavior at the gates and the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him. However, considering that the horse was at longer odds, the Stipendiary Stewards took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 2nd March, 2016 for incompetent handling of his mount (permitted to ride track work and mock race).

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SINDBAAD” IN RACE NO.141 – THE KEJRIWAL TROPHY (DIV.I) RUN ON 17TH JANUARY, 2016 :

 

Trainer Nirad Karanjawala and App. Yash Narredu were questioned in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and it was pointed out to Apprentice Yash Narredu that he had not made an effort after the start to get into position as instructed. Apprentice Yash Narredu stated that though he was asked to jump and sit well up, the gelding, in its previous outing was too keen after the start and had bolted with the rider.  Therefore, as a precautionary measure, he elected to jump out smartly and give the gelding a chance. However, keeping in mind that the horse was at longer odds and that Apprentice Yash Narredu had reported his error, the Stipendiary Stewards took a lenient view and decided to fine him Rs. 5,000/- for ill-judged riding and not following the Trainer’s instructions.

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND  RIDING OF “SHINING MELODY” IN RACE NO.164-THE YALE UNIVERSITY TROPHY, RUN ON SATURDAY 30th  JANUARY  2016.

 

Trainer S. K. Sunderji and Apprentice R. Ajinkya were shown the video of the race and thereafter on questioning, App. R. Ajinkya stated that he was instructed to go to the front or else to sit 2nd. Trainer S. K. Sunderji, while confirming the instructions, added that he had instructed the Apprentice to preferably go to the front, if necessary, to push the horse and go to the front. Tr.S. K. Sunderji added that he took exception to the riding of App. R. Ajinkya in the initial part of the race, wherein he has pushed the horse and then sat next to the front horse instead of going to the front.

 

Apprentice R. Ajinkya stated that soon after the start KOROL (App. G. Amit) had already taken over the running and when he pushed his horse and came alongside KOROL, App. G. Amit further made a forward move, therefore he decided to sit behind KOROL and not to fight him. However, as soon as he had settled his mount in the 2nd position, App. G. Amit slowed the pace down.

 

When questioned about his ridding effort in the straight, App. R. Ajinkya stated that he had worked the filly twice and in the first workout he had carried the whip and after the work out he had informed the trainer that the filly needs to be alerted to get going. He said that the race in question was a “no whip race” and coming into the straight the filly was not responding initially as she was all by herself. Thereafter, as soon as the other horses came on to her haunches, she started moving. Trainer S. K. Sunderji stated that he had planned to race the filly in this particular ‘No Whips Race”, hence he had worked her without the whip twice, both the times ridden by App. R. Ajinkya and he was satisfied with both her workouts without the whip.

 

After considering the submissions made and keeping in mind that the Trainer took exception to the Jockey’s riding as he had not followed his riding instructions and he could have been more vigorous in his ridding efforts  in the straight, the Stipendiary were not satisfied with the explanation tendered Apprentice R. Ajinkya and decided to suspend his Apprentice Jockey’s license w.e.f. 20th February, 2016 to 21st February 2016 (both days inclusive) for riding an incompetent race (permitted to ride work/mock race).
