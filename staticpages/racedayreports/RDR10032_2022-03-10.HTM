ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

MUMBAI MEETING 2021/22, TWELFTH DAY, THURSDAY, 10TH MARCH, 2022.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 3.9 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED 2 METRES AWAY FROM ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES (MILE CHUTE)  TO 1200 METRES, 3 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES AND THEREAFTER 4 METRES AWAY UPTO THE WINNING POST.

 

FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WERE PLACED AT ITS ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES LEADING TO 3 METRES AWAY AT 1000 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1(112) – THE SION F. NESSIM PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  9

 

At the start, RED DUST (S. Zervan, dr.2) jumped inwards at the same time EYES ON THE PRIZE (R. Rupesh, dr.1) jumped outwards and they made contact.

 

At the start, INTENSE BELIEF (App. P. S. Kaviraj, dr.4) jumped outwards and brushed the haunches of NUSRAT (S. A. Amit, dr.5).

 

Passing the 1000 metres, SEMINOLE (App. Kirtish Bhagat) leaned inwards inconveniencing ZACAPA (App. A. K. Aniket)

 

Approaching the 500 metres, EYES ON THE PRIZE (R. Rupesh) shifted out due to which ZACAPA (App. A. K. Aniket) racing on his haunches had to be check. Jockey R. Rupesh has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 250 metres, SPIRITUAL ROCK (Akshay Gaikwad) shifted in under the use of whip thereby tightening INTENSE BELIEF (P. S. Kaviraj) who in-turn shifted in onto NUSRAT (S. A. Amit) who had to be check and drop back. Thereafter, approaching the 150 metres, SPIRITUAL ROCK (Akshay Gaikwad) further shifted in and went across RED DUST (S. Zervan) who had to be steadied. After questioning, Jockey Akshay Gaikwad’s licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on 3rd April 2022 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, Jockey Akshay Gaikwad was questioned with regard to his riding on his mount SPIRITUAL ROCK in presence of Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi. After hearing both, and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Akshay Gaikwad his Jockey’s Licence has been suspended for two race days i.e. from 10th April 2022 to 17th April 2022 for incompetent riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, INTENSE BELIEF and SPIRITUAL ROCK were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(113) – THE J. P. VAZIFDAR TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  3

 

No report.

 

RACE NO.3(114) – THE T. M. GOCULDAS PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  6

 

At the start, MAJESTIC WARRIOR (App. A. K. Aniket) jumped outwards onto MULTISTARRER (C. S. Jodha).

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar rider of FLAMING FIRE reported that approaching the 400 metres in the process of changing the whip his reins had slipped momentarily. As such, Jockey N S Parmar has been cautioned. He also reported that his mount lugged out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer M Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, EVANGELINE (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out under the use of whip and made contact with POLYNEICES (S. Zervan) causing the latter shift outwards. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 150 metres, MULTISTARRER (C. S. Jodha) shifted in went across FLAMING FIRE (N. S. Parmar) who had to be checked. Jockey C. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

After the race, MULTISTARRER was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, EVANGELINE and FLAMING FIRE were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(115) – THE SION F. NESSIM PLATE  - DIVISION I: (About) 1200 Metres –Runners- 10

 

POWER OF BLESSING (R. Shelar, dr.1) took an awkward fly jump resulting in the gelding losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, WELL SPEAKING (App. A. Prakash, dr.4) jumped and veered out and made contact with TAIL EVENT (Akshay Gaikwad, dr.6) due to which C’EST L’AMOUR (App. A. K. Aniket, dr.5) who was drawn in between had to be checked and drop back. In view of above and a similar behavior of the gelding in his last start, Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to show his charge in 2 mock races to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, MOONSHOT (N. S. Parmar) shifted in thereby starting a chain reaction due to which TAIL EVENT (Akshay Gaikwad), WELL SPEAKING (App. A. Prakash) and JUGHEAD (Bhawani Singh) were interfered with and had to be checked. On questioning, Jockey N. S. Parmar informed that his mount shifted in despite his efforts. Considering the behavior of MOONSHOT, Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching the 250 metres, TAIL EVENT (Akshay Gaikwad) shifted out and made contact with C’EST L’AMOUR (App. A. K. Aniket). Jockey Akshay Gaikwad has been cautioned.

 

FALDO (C. S. Jodha) was observed to have lugged out in the straight. Trainer S. S. Shah has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, App. Kirtish Bhagat reported that his mount CHERISHED lugged out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer P Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

BRILLIANT LIGHT (App.A.S.Peter) was observed to have finished the race on the outer railings after the race, App. A. S. Peter reported that his mount hung out in the straight despite his efforts as such Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FALDO was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.5(116) – THE S. A. WAHID PLATE: (About) 1800 Metres – Runners -  15

 

CAPRIFLA (C. S. Jodha, dr.13) refused to stall despite substantial efforts as such the gelding was withdrawn before coming under starter’s order. In view of the above Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start MASCARA (P. S. Kaviraj, dr.3) jumped inwards onto SCOTLAND (App. A. S. Peter, dr.2) who in-turn shifted onto IAMSTORM (N. S. Parmar, dr.1) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

At the start ALPHA GENE (R. Shelar, dr.4) jumped outwards and made contact with LORD BYRON (App. Kirtish Bhagat) who in-turn shifted onto WILLY WONKAA (Akshay, dr.6).

 

At the start AIR POWER (S. Mosin, dr.7) jumped outwards and at the same time VERDANDI (Neeraj Rawal, dr.9) jumped inwards due to which ANGELS TRUMPET (N. B. Kuldeep, dr.8) drawn in between was tightened and inconvenienced. 

 

At the start ABISHOLA (App. A. Prakash, dr.12) jumped out throwing its head.

 

Soon after the start, ABISHOLA (A. Prakash) was keen and overreached the hinds of FAIRMONT (S. Zervan).

 

Passing the 900 metres, WILLY WONKAA (Akshay) shifted in under urging onto SCOTLAND (App. A. S. Peter) who was tightened. Further, passing 800 metres WILLY WONKAA shifted out due to which FAIRMONT (S. Zervan) racing on its haunches had to be steadied thereafter approaching the 600 metres WILLY WONKAA shifted in under urging onto SCOTLAND and AIR POWER (S. Mosin) causing both the horses to check and drop back. After questioning, Jockey Akshay and not being satisfied with his explanation his Jockey’s Licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on Sunday, 3th April 2022 for careless riding on his mount WILLY WONKAA (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, Jockey S Zervan reported that his mount FAIRMONT lugged out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Nosher Cama has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar reported that his mount IAMSTORM lugged-in in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Mosin rider of AIR POWER reported the loss of whip near 400 metres. This being his first instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, no action has been initiated.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (VERDANDI) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. After the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal stated that his mount was lugging-in in the straight and the whip was used as a corrective measures and the same was borne out from the race video as such no action has been initiated against Jockey. However, Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

GHALEB (Shahrukh Khan) was observed to have run detached from the field. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to ensure that his charge puts up a reasonable performance in the future

 

After the race, IAMSTORM found to be sore by the right hind leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, LORD BYRON was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(117) – THE BYRAM N. JEEJEEBHOY TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 7

 

DISTINCTION (P. S. Chouhan) was observed to lugged-out in the straight despite Jockey’s efforts. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 400 metres, ROYAL MANOR (K. Nazil) shifted out under the use of whip. Jockey K. Nazil has been cautioned.

 

RACE NO.7(118) – THE RED BUTTONS PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  14

 

NORTHERN SINGER (App. A. K. Aniket) jumped out slowly losing about 3 lengths at the start. In view of above, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, PERIWINKLE (App. A. Prakash) jumped inwards and went across BEEMER (App. P. S. Kaviraj) who was marginally slow to begin and thereafter made contact with ON VA DANSER (Neeraj Rawal). As such, App. A. Prakash has been fined Rs.3,000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker.

 

JETFIRE (D. S. Daman, dr.10) jumped inwards onto POWER OF INFINITY (Akshay Gaikwad, dr.9) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Passing the 350 metres, ENLIGHTENED (App. P. Shinde) went across under urgings due to which DOWSABEL (App. A. S. Peter) had to be checked and drop back. After questioning and not being satisfied by the explanation, App. P. Shinde licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on Sunday, 3rd April 2022 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, Jockey Akshay Gaikwad reported that his mount POWER OF INFINITY lugged out in the straight despite his efforts. Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey M. Ayyar rider of HONOURABLE EYES reported the loss of whip near 450 metres. This being his first instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, no action has been initiated.

 

Passing the 200 Metres, PERIWINKLE (App. A. Prakash) shifted out and brushes the haunches of MAGIC IN THE WIND (N. S. Parmar). App A Prakash has been cautioned.

 

After the race, App. A. K. Aniket had informed that the surcingle used on his mount NORTHERN SINGER had broken in the race. On questioning, App. A. K. Aniket stated that the surcingle used on his mount was the stable equipment and not his. In view of the same, it was pointed out to Trainer Narendra Lagad that the Jockey is required to weigh out once again if any changes were made to the saddlery. As such he has been fined Rs. 1,000/- for the lapse on his part.

 

After the race, DREAMS was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.
