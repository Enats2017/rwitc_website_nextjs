RE: ENQUIRY IN TO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “QUANDO QUANDO” IN RACE NO 317 (WINNER) RUN ON 6TH APRIL 2014 AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS STARTS IN RACE NO 277 RUN ON 20TH MARCH 2014 & RACE NO 239 RUN ON 6TH MARCH 2014 – TRAINER DJ SURTI

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer D.J.Surti was questioned in the above connection. Tr.Surti gave the veterinary status of the mare and stated that the mare ran in Pune 2013 on 18th October 2013 carrying 62 kg and finished 4th after which she was treated with painkillers. On 19th October 2013, she was x-rayed and painkillers were given. On 27th November 2013 she was given intra-splint injections in both the fore legs but did not get much relief and was finally operated on 16th December 2013 and she was rested after that till 5th January 2014. Thereafter on 16th February 2014 she was entered for a race which went void and she finally got to race on 6th March 2014 where she finished 10th. She again ran on 20th March 2014 and had finished 1 ¼ length behind ICE RINK who had finished 4th in Race no 277 and ICE RINK went on to win later. The mare suffered colic on 26th March 2014. She was given pick-ups till 3rd April 2014 and she ran on 6th April 2014 in Race No 317 and won beating mediocre field in which there were 2 winners only including his charge QUOANO QUANDO and the majority of the field had not even placed in any of their runs.

 

The Trainer further added that vis-à-vis the common horse STAR BENEVOLENT, QUANDO QUANDO was better placed since he had claimed 3 ½ kg allowance this time as compared to the last run and therefore the result is justified also considering that the mare had not run badly in the previous start. He had given the mare a chance to do well but she was not a certainty. The Trainer was informed that he has contravened the Calendar Notification Comparative performance of horses winning in short span of time.

Jockey Zameer Sayyed who rode the mare on the previous occasions when questioned stated that his Instructions have been to be in front bunch and do his best. He stated that in all her runs the mare has been showing improvement and that she has soundness issues. He added that the mare appeared to be ‘heavy’ in her previous runs which may be due to the rest.

 

Jockey S Mosin in Race No 317 was instructed to ‘sit and ride her out from the outside’. When Jockey Zameer Sayyed was questioned with regard to the Instructions he stated that in Race no 277, the mare was well placed and since he had room along the rails in the straight, therefore he did not feel the need to ride out the mare from the outside.  

The matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on, 30th April, 2014 interviewed Trainer D.J.Surti in the above connection and having heard him, the Stewards were not entirely satisfied with the explanation tendered by him. The Stewards noted that there was a set pattern wherein if he fancied a horse he declares a better calibre Jockey and there is betting on the horse and he was warned to be more careful in the future.

RE:: POSITIVE FINDING IN RESPECT OF ch m “MATRIOSHKA” (PLACED 3RD) IN RACE NO 359 – THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY (DIV – II) RUN ON 26TH APRIL 2014 – IN CHARGE OF TR.S.SHAIKH:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 12th June 2014 interviewed Trainer S Shaikh regarding the Positive Report dated 27th May 2014 from LGC, Newmarket, UK, in respect of the urine sample taken from his charge MATRIOSHKA (Placed 3rd) in Race No 359 – The Racing Officials Trophy run on 26th April 2014, which read as under:

 

“THE CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS WAS PERFORMED USING DOCUMENTED LGC METHODS, WHICH IDENTIFIED THE PRESENCE OF 16B-HIDROXYSTANOZOLOL, A METABOLITE OF STANOZOLOL, IN THE URINE SAMPLE”.

 

At this meeting, Tr.S.Shaikh was handed over a copy of the above report. At this time, Tr.S.Shaikh confirmed that he did not wish to send the “B” Sample of his charge MATRIOSHKA for Confirmatory Analysis and therefore the Stewards treated the positive finding of the Primary Sample of his charge as final and conclusive.  Under the Rules, the Stewards decided to disqualify MATRIOSHKA and revise the Order of Placing as under:

 

                DANCING SPLENDOUR  -               First;                      DOUBLE OR QUITS          -               Third;

                SNOW ANGEL                    -               Second;                                 SEVEN SEAS                       -               Fourth.

 

Further, as per the Clause of the Medication Rules, MATRIOSHKA has been banned from Racing for a period of 6 months i.e. till 12th December 2014.

 

Tr.S.Shaikh was asked to conduct his own enquiries and to lead evidence, if any, to prove mitigating circumstances, within 15 days and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club as and when required to.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 24th July, 2014 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards/Sr.V.O. in the above enquiry and interviewed Mr.S.Shaikh.

 

The Stewards after having thoroughly enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings, reports and records and submissions made in the matter and also having permitted Mr.S.Shaikh to make submissions in the subject matter, found that he had no mitigating circumstances to offer in the subject case and as such, held him guilty of breach of the Medication Rules.

 

Further,  the circumstantial evidence in the enquiry leads the Stewards  to believe that :-

 

1)   Mr.S.Shaikh was unable to give a reasonably satisfactory explanation as to how the drug 16B-hydroxystanozolol, a metabolite of stanozolol was found in the urine sample of MATRIOSHKA. However, the fact remained that the mare was administered STANOZOLOL.  Also, STANOZOLOL, an anabolic steroid, is a banned substance for racing horses as per the notification received from the Government of India and its presence in the urine sample of MATRIOSHKA is also a  breach of the Medication Rules and therefore more serious than the mere finding of a prohibited substance;

 

2)            By Mr.S.Shaikh’s own admission, the security in his stable was good and one of his staff was always present in the stable to keep security of the horses and it was not possible for an outsider to enter his stable and administer Stanazolol to MATRIOSHKA. Also, Mr.S.Shaikh stated that he has full faith in his staff and he was confident that they were not involved in administering STANOZOLOL to MATRIOSHKA.

 

3)            Mr.S.Shaikh stated that MATRIOSHKA came to his charge about three weeks before the race. However, he had the choice of having the mare tested before racing her, which he failed to do.

 

4)            Mr.S.Shaikh declared a good calibre Jockey on MATRIOSHKA in the race in question and she was backed down from 12/1 to 5/1. Further, the mare looked in good condition before the race in the paddock and she was fractious in the paddock as well as while going towards the start. Also, she raced very keen during the race and finished a good third.

 

Taking an overall view  and in view of the circumstantial evidence as stated above,   the Stewards held Mr.S.Shaikh guilty of administering the prohibited substance Stanozolol to MATRIOSHKA.

 

The Stewards noted that the positive report in respect of MATRIOSHKA was Mr.S.Shaikh’s second infringement of the Medication Rules pertaining to anabolic steroids and sex hormones, the earlier one being that of URBAN WARRIOR on 21st August, 2010 who was found positive for Boldenone. Also, in the enquiry into the positive finding of URBAN WARRIOR the Stewards had suspended Tr.Shaikh’s license for a period of one year and imposed on him a suspended sentence of one year i.e. if he was again found guilty of breach of the Medication Rules within the next three years after he started training again, then the aforesaid suspended sentence of punishment of one year would ipso facto come into force and effect.

 

The Stewards noted that vide letter dated 6th June, 2014 Mr.S.Shaikh had already surrendered his Trainer’s license.

 

Taking an overall view and keeping in mind that 16B-HYDROXYSTANOZOLOL is an Anabolic Steroid, which is banned by the Government of India and this being Mr.S.Shaikh’s second infringement of the Medication Rules in respect of anabolic steroids and sex hormones, the Stewards of the Club decided :-

 

a)       For the positive report in respect of MATRIOSHKA, to exclude Mr.S.Shaikh for a period of three years i.e. w.e.f. 25th July, 2014 to 24th July, 2017;

b)       As intimated to Mr.S.Shaikh in  the previous order in respect of URBAN WARRIOR enquiry, the suspended sentence of one year ipso facto comes into effect i.e. he was further excluded for a period of one year i.e. from 25th July, 2017 to 24th July, 2018. 

 

Therefore, Mr.S.Shaikh stands excluded for a total of four years i.e. from 25th July, 2014 to 24th July, 2018 from the premises of the racecourse, Mumbai/Pune and all other places wherever the Rules of Racing of this Club are in force.

 

 Further, all the disabilities under the Rules of Racing for Warned Off /Excluded/Disqualified Person, shall be      applicable to him.

 

Also, since he has been excluded,  the permission granted to him to own and race horses under the  Rules of this Club stands revoked. 

 

c)       After Mr.S.Shaikh exhausts his punishment, should he decide to re-apply for a Trainer’s license in the future, the same shall not be granted to him.

RE : APPEAL FROM TRAINER P.SHROFF IN RESPECT OF THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB – RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT OF THE COMPULSORY SAMPLE OF URINE TAKEN FROM  dk b f “BULLSEYE” ON 6TH DECEMBER,  2013 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE FILLY HAD RETURNED TO HIM FROM THE STUD FARM :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 19th May, 2014 interviewed Tr.P.Shroff in the above connection. The Board of Appeal having heard Tr.P.Shroff and in view of his request to allow certain persons who were not interviewed by the Stewards of the Club, as his witnesses before the Board of Appeal and in view of the fact that the Report dated 12th May, 2014 from the AWBI Enquiry Committee on BULLSEYE was received after the Stewards of the Club had given a decision in the BULLSEYE case, the Board of Appeal considered it to be fresh evidence and therefore, under Rule 47 (b) of the Rules of Racing, decided to refer the matter back to the Stewards of the Club and to stay the order of the Stewards of the Club in the matter until the Stewards of the Club hear the matter. His deposit was ordered to be refunded.
