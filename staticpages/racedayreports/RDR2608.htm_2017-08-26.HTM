RACE NO.1(62) – THE STAR WISH PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – 11 Runners

 

SHINNING NOON (D. A. Naik) was brought in to the paddock sporting a Tongue Strap. Permission to use the same was not obtained. As such the Tongue Strap was removed before the filly left the paddock and Trainer C. Rajendra has been fined Rs.1,000/- for the lapse.

 

POWERHAUS (Dashrath Singh) was brought in to the paddock sporting a Tongue Strap. Permission to use the same was not obtained. As such the Tongue Strap was removed before the filly left the paddock and Trainer Narendra Lagad has been fined Rs.1,000/- for the lapse.

 

ABBEYROAD (S. J. Sunil) jumped out slow, losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, POWERHAUS (Dashrath Singh, dr. 10) jumped inwards and went across MEDELLIN (C. S. Jodha, dr. 9) who was slow to begin.

 

Passing the 800 metres, SHINNING NOON (D. A. Naik) was observed to have hung out despite the Jockey’s efforts. Trainer C. Rajendra has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

Passing the 200 metres, HUZZAH’S GRACE (Prashant P. Dhebe) was observed to have hung in despite the Jockey’s efforts. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait  has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, Jockey Yash Narredu (FANTASY STAR) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha (MEDELLIN) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh (POWERHAUS) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight and passing the 100 metres his whip came in contact with the railing and fell. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the above incident and will submit a report shortly.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the running of this race.

 

After the race, KNIGHT’S QUEST was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that SMART VISION had right eye injury with lacrimation.

 

After the race, SMART VISION, FANTASY STAR, SHINNING NOON, HUZZAH’S GRACE, POWERHAUS and MEDELLIN were trotted and found to be sound. GENERAL’S SONG had an abrasion at the right hind fetlock and MEDELLIN had a cut injury at the right fore coronet.

 

RACE NO.2(63) – THE PICASSO PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 7 Runners

 

TAR HEEL (A.Imran Khan) reared up and dislodged its rider as he was rolling behind the starting gate and got lose. The colt was caught within the vicinity of the starting point.

 

Passing the 800 metres, HANLON’S RAZOR (J. Chinoy) was observed to have brushed the rails.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that HANLON’S RAZOR was examined at the stables in the morning on 27/08/2017 and was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race MAGIC DUST was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(64) – THE  HER MAJESTY TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – 6 Runners

 

MULTIGLORY (C. S. Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

After the race, SEVERUS was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, WINTER RENAISSANCE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(65) – THE  NANA RAGHUNATH TROPHY:(About) 1000 Metres – 9 Runners

 

At the request of Jockey T. S. Jodha, his mount STAR WARS was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race.

 

STAR WARS jumped out throwing its head thereby losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, STALLONE (C. S. Jodha, dr. 7) jumped outwards on to FINE TUNE (R. Shelar, dr. 8).

 

Passing the 600 metres, AURELIA (Santosh G.) shifted out and carried PANDEMONIUM (N. S. Parmar) outwards.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, STALLONE (C. S. Jodha) shifted in on to ASCENSION (Neeraj Rawal) who had to be steadied. After the race, on questioning, Jockey C. S. Jodha stated that his mount hung in despite his efforts. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, STAR WARS was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that STAR WARS had right eye injury (corneal ulcer and opacity).

 

After the race, STALLONE and WHOOPSIDAISY were trotted and found to be sound. STALLONE had lost its right fore shoe.

 

RACE NO.5(66) – THE STAR WISH PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – 12 Runners

 

VOULEZ VOUS (N. B. Kuldeep) jumped out slow losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race

 

At the start, GOOD THING (Dashrath Singh, dr. 10) jumped inwards on to HARMONY OF THE SEA (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr. 9).

 

Soon after the start, KING OF KILLEN (Neeraj Rawal) jumped inwards on to KISS FROM A ROSE (K. Nazil, dr. 11) who in turn shifted in and made contact with GOOD THING (Dashrath Singh).

 

Passing the 200 metres, WAY AHEAD (A. Imran Khan) lugged in on to RAPID GIRL (A. Sandesh). On questioning Jockey A. Imran Khan stated that his mount hung in despite his efforts. Trainer C. D. Katrak has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, HARMONY OF THE SEA and ICE ANGEL were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(67) – THE  PUNE CITY GOLD CUP (GR.3): (About) 1600 Metres – 8 Runners

 

MRS. PATMORE was permitted to be withdrawn.

 

On arrival at the gates, TRAHERNE (C. S. Jodha) was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race.

 

JEFFERSON (Dashrath Singh) jumped out slow losing about a length at the start.

 

ALAINDAIR was declared to run with bandages, however, the gelding raced without the bandages. Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed has been fined Rs.1,000/- for the lapse.

 

After the race, JEFFERSON was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The horse has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7(68) – THE  GOLDEN EDITION PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 13 Runners

 

At the start, LILAC TIME (N. B. Kuldeep, dr. 3) jumped out awkwardly throwing its head.

 

Passing the 500 metres, LILAC TIME (App. N. B. Kuldeep) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening EMILIA ROMAGNA (N. S. Parmar) along the rails. App. N. B. Kuldeep has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, App. N. B., Kuldeep (LILAC TIME) reported that his reins slipped passing the 200 metres. App. N. B. Kuldeep has been cautioned and asked to be more careful in the future.

 

After the race, Jockey R. Ajinkya (MULTISTAR) reported that his mount hung-in throughout the race. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

After the race, Jockey D. S. Daman (FENCING) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that after finishing the race, EMILIA ROMAGNA showed signs of distress. After First Aid treatment, the filly was sent to the Equine Hospital for further management.

 

After the race, FENCING, ADELE and MULTISTAR were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(69) – THE  JAIDEV PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 13 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer Faisal A. Abbas, CRACKLIN’ ROSIE was examined at 1115 hours on 26/08/2017 and was found to be sore by the left hind leg. As such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

WIND CRAFT (N. B. Kuldeep) and DANCING PRINCE (M. Ayyar) were reluctant to be stalled. SENSEX (P. Trevor) was restless in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. Trainers Sangramsinh N. Joshi, M. Narredu and Imtiaz A. Sait have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

HIROHAMA (T. S. Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, DANCING PRINCE (App. M. Ayyar, dr. 3) shifted out and made contact with INDIAN SERENADE (Dashrath Singh, dr. 4).

 

Passing the 700 metres, THE CIVILIAN (S. A. Amit) shifted in on to STREETJAMMER (J. Chinoy) who in turn shifted in tightening WIND CRAFT (N. B. Kuldeep) along the rails. Jockey S. A. Amit has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, STREETJAMMER (J. Chinoy) shifted out forcing THE CIVILIAN (S. A. Amit) outwards on to SENSEX (P. Trevor) who had to be checked. Jockey J. Chinoy has been fined Rs. 3,000/-.

 

After the race, App. S. A. Amit (THE CIVILIAN) reported that his mount hung in throughout the race. On viewing the replay it was observed that the Apprentice had not changed his whip hand despite his mount hanging in. App. S. A. Amit has been cautioned, further Trainer A. M. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of STREETJAMMER (J. Chinoy) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that HIROHAMA had right eye injury with lacrimation. MASTER OF TRINITY had right eye injury (corneal opacity).

 

After the race, THE CIVILIAN, INDIAN SERENADE, SENSEX and WIND CRAFT were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.9(70) – THE  MARIINSKY PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – 17 Runners

 

After being stalled, LORD ARAZAN (P. S. Chouhan) rushed the front gates. The gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race.

 

ROYAL CLASSIC (Nirmal Jodha) and RARE SILVER (J. E. Pereira) were restless in the stall and not allowing their riders to settle. Trainers Adhirajsingh Jodha and Imtiaz A. Sait have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

RICH N RARE (N. B. Kuldeep) jumped out slow losing considerable ground at the start.  ELSA (T. S. Jodha) jumped out and thereafter did not raise an effective gallop. Trainers Narendra Lagad and Subhag Singh have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

JARAMA (S. A. Amit) and D’ACCORD (Neeraj Rawal) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths each at the start.

 

At the start, AINRA (App. M. Ayyar, dr. 1) and ZABISCO (App. K. Nazil, dr. 2) jumped outwards and inwards respectively and made contact.

 

At the start, LORD ARAZAN (P. S. Chouhan, dr. 4) jumped outwards on to ROYAL CLASSIC (Nirmal Jodha, dr. 5).

 

At the start, SUDARSHAN CHAKRA (Bhawani Singh, dr. 8) jumped outwards on to JACK DAN (App. Vishal N. Bunde, dr. 9).

 

Soon after the start, D’ACCORD (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out onto JARAMA (App. S. A. Amit) who had to be checked. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

After the race, RARE SILVER was trotted and found to be sound.
