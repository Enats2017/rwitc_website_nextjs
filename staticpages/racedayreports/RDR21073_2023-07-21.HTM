RACE NO.1(1) – THE BEYOND EXPECTATION PLATE  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS -  14

 

On the arrival at the start, at the request of App. N. Bhosale (DIVINE SOUL), the saddle on his mount was adjusted.

 

At the start, PRINCE IGOR (P. S. Kaviraj, dr.6) jumped outwards at the same time ZIP ALONG (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.8) jumped inwards and made contact with LADY DI (S. A. Amit, dr.7).

 

Soon after the start, RECIPROCITY (P. Vinod) shifted in at the same time NAIROBI (S. G. Prasad) leaned outwards and they made contact. Jockey P. Vinod has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, Jockey N. S. Parmar (PRINCE O’ WAR) was observed to have shifted in thereby inconveniencing NAIROBI (S. G. Prasad) who had to be steadied. Jockey N. S. Parmar has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, RECIPROCITY (P. Vinod) racing between NAIROBI (S. G. Prasad) and TARZAN (Bhawani Singh) was tightened for room and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, SILVER STEPS (T. S. Jodha) shifted out and went across LIGHTNING BLAZE (App. A. K. Aniket) causing the latter to be checked. After questioning, Jockey T. S. Jodha has been severely reprimanded.

 

It was observed that the saddle used on HABIBI (S. Saqlain) shifted forward during the race. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Vinod (RECIPROCITY) reported loss of whip approaching the 200 metres. This being his first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2023, no action has been initiated. However, he has been instructed to practice the use of whip under the supervision of the Riding Instructor.

 

After the race, LIGHTNING BLAZE was trotted and found to be sound and ANOUSHKA was found to have a gum injury.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that LADY DI had left eye injury (sore) and ZIP ALONG had both eye injuries (sore).

 

RACE NO.2(2) – THE SEASON OPENER PLATE  - DIVISION II: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS -  10

 

In the process of saddling FANCY NANCY (App. Haridas Gore) reared up and flipped over, as such, the filly was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O., in the saddling enclosure and also on the arrival at the start and was certified fit to race.

 

FANCY NANCY (App. Haridas Gore) was reluctant to reach the starting gates thereby delaying the start of the race. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to school his charge in a mock race before next accepting with it. Thereafter on the arrival at the start, at the request of App. Haridas Gore, the saddle on his mount was adjusted.

 

At the start, SMILES OF FORTUNE (Yash Narredu, dr.7) jumped outwards onto AMEERAH (Neeraj Rawal, dr.8) who in turn shifted out and made contact with ATTAINED (App. P. Shinde, dr.9).

 

DIVINE INTUITION (Lalkar Singh, dr.4) reared up as the start was affected thereby losing 3 lengths.

 

Passing the 1200 metres DIVINE INTUTION (Lalkar Singh) shifted out thereby forcing SMILES OF FORTUNE (Yash Narredu) outwards. Jockey Lalkar Singh has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, SMILES OF FORTUNE (Yash Narredu) shifted in thereby bumping and inconveniencing FLOYD (T. S. Jodha) who in turn shifted in and made contact with CHARMING STAR (App. Mustakim Alam). After the race on questioning, Jockey Yash Narredu stated that his mount hung-in and that he had immediately taken corrective measures, as the same was borne out, no action was initiated against the Jockey. However, Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race

 

After the race, Jockey S. Saqlain (GOLDIVA) reported loss of whip passing the 300 metres. This being his first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2023, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, App. Haridas Gore (FANCY NANCY) reported that his saddle had slipped during the race, considering that the saddle was adjusted at the gates no action has been initiated against the Trainer. However, the Starter has been asked to be more cautious in the future.

 

After the race, FANCY NANCY was trotted and found to be sound but found to have bruises on both hock.

 

After the race, STREET SENSE was trotted and found to be sound.

At the request of Trainer Dallas Todywalla, MI ARION was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. in the stable area at 08:00 pm on 20/07/2023 and was found to have colic, as such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was permitted to be withdrawn on Veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey P. S. Chouhan, rider of DEMOCRACY placed third by the Judge against Jockey Bhawani Singh, rider of THE GENERAL placed first by the Judge, reason being in the last 200 metres, Jockey Bhawani Singh hit on his whip due to which Jockey P. S. Chouhan lost his whip at a crucial stage, thereafter in the last 50 metres, the winner came onto him and tightened him thereby taking his ground which cost him a certain race and if he wouldn’t have stopped riding, he could have fallen.

 

After reviewing the race from various angles and considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with the views of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their own judgment, the Stewards decided to uphold the objection and revised the order of placing as under:-

RE: MATTER REMANDED BACK TO THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB BY THE BOARD OF APPEAL-ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF DREAM ALLIANCE IN RACE NO.172-THE BOMBAY CLUB MULTI MILLION (DIST.1400 METRES RUN ON 2 APRIL 2023- DE-NOVO ENQUIRY – JOCKEY A. SANDESH:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Monday, 15th May 2023, having considered all submissions made by Jockey A. Sandesh and his spokesperson Mr. Geoffrey Nagpal and Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait. The Stewards also provided him with the opportunity to bring in witnesses, namely Mr. P. S. Chouhan and Mr. S. Zervan. Additionally, the Stewards also took note of the email dated 15 May 2023, from Mr. P. Trevor, who had expressed his inability to attend the meeting but provided his observations.

 

Furthermore, the Stewards reviewed the races as requested by his spokesperson i.e. race numbers 44, 94, 126 and 172 (Runs of DREAM ALLIANCE) and race numbers 38,141,166 and 176. After thoroughly investigating the matter and carefully considering all the facts, circumstances, papers, proceedings, reports, records, evidence and annexures, the Stewards had concluded that they were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey A. Sandesh. Consequently, they found him guilty of not permitting his mount (DREAM ALLIANCE) to run on its merits.

 

The Stewards decided to Suspend his Jockey's Licence for a period of one year i.e. from 30th May 2023 to 30th April 2024 and further, should he apply for grant of ‘A’ Jockey's licence for the Racing Season 2024/25, the same shall not be granted to him upto and inclusive of 29th May 2024. Also to impose on him a suspended sentence of 6 months over the next three years ie. if he is found guilty of a similar offence within the next three years, commencing from 30th May 2024 i.e. after the expiry of the suspension of his Jockey's License, then the aforesaid suspended sentence of 6 months would immediately come into force and effect and which aforesaid punishment would be in addition to any other punishment which the Stewards may impose on him for any subsequent act of yours as regards the aforesaid Please note that during the period of his suspension i.e. upto 29th May 2024, he shall not be permitted to:-

 

a)    Ride work;

b)    Attend racing, on course or off course;

c)    Enter the stable area at Mumbai and Pune

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SNOWFALL” (DRAWN 13) IN RACE NO. 170 – THE STAR FORTUNE MILLION (DIST. 1200 MTRS.) RUN ON 2ND APRIL 2023 :

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Wednesday, 12th July 2023, having considered all the papers and proceedings before them and heard the submissions made by Jockey A. Sandesh and Trainer Altaf Hussain  as also the Stipendiary Stewards and having viewed the video recording of race in question as also Race Nos. 61, 110 and 176 run during the Mumbai Meeting 2022/23 of the filly, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey A. Sandesh and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on his mount SNOWFALL in the above race.

 

As such, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s Licence for a period of 8 race days for unsatisfactory riding. Since his appeal in respect of the running and riding of DREAM ALLIANCE in Race No. 172 run on 2nd April 2023 is pending with the Board of Appeal, the dates of his suspension will be intimated after his appeal has been heard and disposed of by the Board.
