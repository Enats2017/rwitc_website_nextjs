RACE NO.1 (115) – THE  GIACOSA PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 10

 

At the start, AURARIA (Dashrath Singh, dr.6) jumped awkwardly inwards and went on to SEA WONDER (Imran Chisty, dr.5) who in turn tightened STUD OF GOLD (S.Zervan, dr.4) on to FIREMISS (R.Rupesh, dr.3) who itself had shifted out and in the process STUD OF GOLD lost position.

 

Near 900 metres, STYLISH STROKES (C.S.Jodha) rolled in and caused AURARIA (Dashrath Singh) to shift in and make contact with SEA WONDER (Imran Chisty). 

 

After the race, VICTOR IVANOV was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FIREMISS and SEA WONDER were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (116)  – THE  GIACOSA PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 10

 

At the start, CASPIAN ZONE (Nirmal Jodha) jumped awkwardly inwards and ASHWA SAKIRA (M.A.Roushan) jumped awkwardly outwards.

 

Approaching 800 metres, MYPETHONEY (Shammi Khan) shifted in and jostled with QUICK RETURNS (Akash Agarwal) who went in on to LAKE ONTARIO (R.Shelar) who stumbled. Jockey Shammi Khan was reported to the Stewards for Careless riding. The Stewards interviewed the Jockey and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him found him guilty of Careless riding and decided to suspend his Jockey’s License for 22nd January 2012 (permitted to ride work/ Mock race).

 

Jockey M.A.Roushan (ASHWA SAKIRA) has been fined Rs.3000/- for allowing his mount to shift in the final 100 metres thereby hampering the run of MOUNTAIN LION (Imran Chisty) who shifted in and went on to MYPETHONEY (Shammi Khan).

 

Jockey T.S.Jodha (ENDLESS HORIZON) has been cautioned for allowing his mount to shift-in in the straight. 

 

After the race, MYPETHONEY was observed to have an abrasion at the right hind pastern.

 

RACE NO.3 (117) – THE  THUNDER BIRD PLATE (DIV.II): 1400 Metres - Runners – 10

 

TZIGANE was fractious in its Stall. As the start was affected, TZIGANE (App Prabhu Naidu), while jumping out, hit the Gate of its Stall and knuckled under, due to which App.Prabhu Naidu lost his right stirrup which he could not regain for quite some distance. Due to no assistance from the saddle, the horse lost considerable ground. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to give the gelding more Gate Practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

  

ADAMS PRINCE (C.S.Jodha) hung out in the straight and carried BLUSH (P.Trevor) outward, who in turn forced DAUGHTEROFDESTINY (Shammi Khan) outward. Since the ADAMS PRINCE had returned sore,  no action was initiated in the matter.

 

While straightening for home, DAUGHTEROFDESTINY (Shammi Khan) shifted out and inconvenienced INNER HARMONY (Shrikant Kamble). Jockey Shammi Khan has been cautioned.

 

Jockey R.Shelar (OCEAN SENSATION) reported loss of his whip near 400 metres.

After the race, DETROIT DEMON and ADAMS PRINCE were found to be sore by the right fore leg and INNER HARMONY was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The above three horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ALASKA was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind bulb of heel. TZIGANE was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4 (118)  – THE  MAGIC TOUCH PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres - Runners – 7

 

OCTOPAUL (Dashrath Singh) lugged out in the straight whereby the Jockey could not ride out to complete advantage. Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey Stephane Pasquier (ZAKYNTHOS) was observed to have eased/relaxed his mount momentarily at about 150 metres from the winning post which may have effected the result of the race, considering that the verdict was a short-head. As such, the matter was reported to the Stewards who interviewed the Jockey Stephane Pasquier and Trainer P Shroff. After having heard the submissions made by them and having viewed the video recording of the race, the Stewards decided to give Jockey Stephane Pasquier the benefit of the doubt.

 

ASTAPI (A.Sandesh) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Trainer Altamaash Ahmed and Jockey A.Sandesh were questioned regarding the running and riding of ASTAPI. The Jockey was instructed to settle his mount and keep him covered and gradually improve from the 800 metres mark. The instructions were changed this time as in the 2000 Guineas the horse had been in front and had fizzled out. The jockey confirmed the instructions and stated that he was unable to make his move from the 800 metres as the front runners didn’t progress and he was asked to keep the horse covered. The Trainer took no exception to the Jockey’s riding and was satisfied with the performance of the horse. The explanation was noted.

 

After the race, ZAKYNTHOS was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

RE : SHOW CAUSE ISSUED TO JOCKEY R.HUGHES, THE RIDER OF “STAR TRAINER” FOR FOUL RIDING IN RACE NO.112 – THE MORELLATO BY GITANJALI LIFESTYLE MILLION RUN ON SUNDAY, 1ST JANUARY, 2012 :

 

The  Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 5th January, 2012 interviewed Jockey R.Hughes who was accompanied by Interlocutor, Mr.Ivor Fernandes.

 

The Stewards heard all the submissions made by Jockey R.Hughes/the Interlocutor, including his reply to the Show Cause Notice. The Stewards also heard his Witnesses viz. Jockeys C.S.Jodha (BEAUTIFUL STRANGER), S.Sunil (WEIZHOU) and David Allan (ANCIENT WONDER) and the Starter as also Jockey S.Zervan, rider of ELIZABETH. Having heard all the submissions made and having viewed the video recording of the blow up picture of the incident (in normal as well as slow motion – all angles), the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey R.Hughes in the subject case and therefore decided to confirm the charge of foul riding against him. In that, even conceding that ELIZABETH (S.Zervan) had leaned on to his horse about 150 metres from the start, the Stewards gave him the benefit of the doubt for the first instance of elbowing S.Zervan, that it could have been an act of self defense to prevent Jockey S.Zervan from shifting in further. However, the Stewards held the view that the second instance of more aggressively elbowing Jockey S.Zervan was intentional and without justification, especially from a rider of his stature. The Stewards were not convinced that the “elbowing” was necessary to protect him or his horse or other riders in the race. The Stewards did not accept his contention that it was an act of self defense since there was room for him along the rails.

 

However, taking into consideration Jockey R.Hughes’ defense and his clean past record and giving consideration to the explanation tendered by him, the Stewards took a lenient view on the punishment and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 22nd January, 2012 to 26th February, 2012 (both days inclusive) for foul riding on his mount STAR TRAINER in Race No.112. (Not permitted to ride work/mock race).
