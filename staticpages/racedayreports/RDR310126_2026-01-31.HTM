RACE NO.1 (83) – THE FOREST FLAIR TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

On a request from Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi, GIBSON KNOTT was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. at 11:30 a.m. on 31st January 2026 at the stables and was found to be sore by right fore with a painful swelling on its right fore tendon. As such, the mare was certified unfit to race, was permitted to be withdrawn on veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

BETWEEN FRIENDS (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.6) lost about a length at the start.

 

At the start, EKLA CHOLO (P. Trevor, dr.3) jumped inwards onto SHE’S A TEASER (B. Nikhil, dr.2) who in turn shifted in thereby tightening  ARBITRAGE (App. Haridas Gore, dr.1) racing  along the rails. Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, ANANYA (App. Raswarup) shifted in under urgings thereby starting a chain reaction due to which LICKETY SPLIT (A. Prakash), SUSSING (S. A. Amit), SHE’S A TEASER (B. Nikhil), BETWEEN FRIENDS (Prashant P. Dhebe) and ARBITRAGE (App. Haridas Gore) were inconvenienced and had to be checked, due to this incident BETWEEN FRIENDS racing on the haunches of SUSSING had to check and change course. After questioning all concerned, not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. Ramswarup, his Apprentice Jockeys licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on 22nd February 2026 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

LICKETY SPLIT (A. Prakash) was observed to have drifted out under pressure in the straight, despite its rider’s efforts. As such, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to show his charge in a Mock Race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

SHE’S A TEASER was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

SUSSING was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

LICKETY SPLIT was trotted and found to be sound.

RE: POSITIVE REPORT IN THE POST RACE URINE AND BLOOD SAMPLES OF b c  “BUCKINGHAM” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.46 – THE LT. COL. GOVIND SINGH (VRC) TROPHY - RUN ON 28TH DECEMBER 2025:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 29th January 2026, interviewed Trainer Karthik Ganapathy, in connection with the Positive Report dated 27th January 2026 from Quantilab, Mauritius, in respect of the post-race urine and blood samples of b c BUCKINGHAM (Winner) in Race No.46 – The Lt. Col. Govind Singh (VRC) Trophy run on 28th December 2025, which states as follows:­­­-

 

“THE ANALYSIS OF THE ABOVE SAMPLE HAS SHOWN THE PRESENCE OF LAMOTRIGINE”

 

At the said meeting, Trainer Karthik Ganapathy was provided with a copy of the aforementioned reports as per rules as also a copy of the quantification of this substance received from Quantilab, Mauritius. He was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, whether he wish to send the “B” Samples for Confirmatory Analysis within 48 hours of opening the envelope identifying the samples i.e. no later than 31st January 2026 and also indicate his choice of Laboratory for the Confirmatory Analysis of the split samples of his charge BUCKINGHAM.

 

The available Laboratories for such analysis are: The Hong Kong Jockey Club Laboratory, Hong Kong; LGC, Newmarket, UK; Laboratoire Des Courses Hippiques (LCH), France; Laboratory of Racing Chemistry (LRC), Japan. Failure to comply within stipulated period shall result in the Stewards treating the report of the Primary Laboratory final and conclusive.

 

Additionally, he is required to ensure the presence of the Witnessing Analyst, if he so desires, at the Laboratory conducting the Confirmatory Analysis for blood within 7 days and for the urine within 30 days of the nomination. In the event, he is unable to arrange for the presence of the witnessing analyst within the said period, notwithstanding the nomination, the Confirmatory Analysis shall proceed in the absence of the Witnessing Analyst.

 

Further, in accordance with the Medication Rules presently in force, he is hereby informed that 50% of his runners shall be subjected to follow-up samples.
