RACE NO.1(126) – THE LAHINCH PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  11

 

Jockey P. Trevor rider of SIM SIM has been fined Rs. 1000/- for weighing out late due to which SIM SIM came late into the Paddock.

 

It was observed that RED RIOT was sporting with Snaffle Bit for which permission was not obtained. Trainer H J Antia has been fined Rs.1,000/-.  

 

At the start, DIVINE SOUL (M. Ayyar, dr.6) jumped inwards onto MYSTERIOUS GIRL (Akshay, dr.5) at the same time SAFDAR (Shahrukh Khan, dr.9) shifted in thereby forcing COUNT THE WINS (Raghuveer Singh, dr.8) and PRINCESS SNOW (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.7) inwards. At this juncture DIVINE SOUL shifted out and bumped PRINCESS SNOW and COUNT THE WINS thereafter due to the impact DIVINE SOUL shifted in onto MYSTERIOUS GIRL forcing it inwards onto REGAL PRINCE (Akash Agarwal). After questioning Jockey M. Ayyar and not being satisfied with his explanation, his Jockey’s licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on Sunday, 10th April 2022, for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race) and Jockey Shahrukh Khan has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 900 metres, HILMA KLINT (App. A. K. Aniket) shifted in under urging and went onto SUITED ACES (Neeraj Rawal) forcing inwards thereby tightening SIM SIM (P. Trevor) along the rails causing the later to check and drop back. After questioning App. A. K. Aniket and not being satisfied with his explanation, his Jockey’s licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. Sunday, 10th April 2022, for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race Jockey Raghuveer Singh rider of COUNT THE WINS reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor reported that the saddle on his mount SIM SIM shifted back. As such, he was unable to ride out his mount to complete advantage. Trainer Faisal Abbas has been fined Rs.5,000/- being second offence of faulty saddling of his charge, during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22.

 

After the race, Jockey Akshay rider of MYSTERIOUS GIRL reported that in process of using the whip he accidently hit the whip of Jockey Shahrukh Khan rider of SAFDAR due to close proximity near 400 metres as such both the Jockeys lost their whips.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, MYSTERIOUS GIRL (Akshay) lugged out and carried SAFDAR (Shahrukh Khan) outwards. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SAFDAR was observed to have broken a blood vessel second time during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, COUNT THE WINS was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.
