ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

PUNE MEETING 2025, SPECIAL RACE DAY, SUNDAY, 19TH OCTOBER, 2025.

 

 (PENETROMETER: 4.4 (LONGCHAMP METHOD); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORT

 

RAILS WERE PLACED 3 METRES WIDE FROM 1600 METRES TO 1400 METRES. THEREAFTER, 5 METRES UPTO THE WINNING POST. RAILS WERE PLACED 4 METRES WIDE IN THE CHUTE.

 

On a request from Trainer Dr. Anil Kumar, permission was granted to Trainer Hoisdar Daji to saddle his charge LIBAN in the 4th race, at his own (Trainer Dr. Anil Kumar’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO.1 (127) – THE GOLD BAG PLATE: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

ALEXANDRIA (S. Zervan, dr.1) lost about three lengths at the start. As Such, Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, MA CHERIE (S. J. Sunil, dr.7) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

At the start, FLOYD (T. S. Jodha, dr.6) jumped inwards onto THE FLUTIST (C. S. Jodha, dr.5) who in turn shifted in thereby inconveniencing LEO THE LION (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.4) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, SPEAK THE BREED (Antony Raj S.) shifted out and made contact with MA CHERIE (S. J. Sunil). Jockey Antony Raj S. has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 500 metres, Jockey Antony Raj S. (SPEAK THE BREED) was observed to have used the whip in an improper manner on his mount. After questioning Jockey Antony Raj S. and not being satisfied with the explanation also keeping in mind his past offence of a similar nature, he has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

Passing the 400 metres, BALENO (S. A. Amit) and THE FLUTIST (C. S. Jodha) were observed to have jostled. Jockey’s S. A. Amit and C. S. Jodha have been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, SPEAK THE BREED (Antony Raj S.) was observed to have dropped back and thereafter stopped galloping. As such, the filly was subjected to veterinary examination and was found to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

MA CHERIE was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its left hind cannon.

 

BALENO was trotted and found to be sound but was observed to have an injury on its right hind coronet.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that ALEXANDRIA had left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.2 (128) – THE ENIGMA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 4

 

At the start, MOJITO (N. S. Parmar, dr.2) jumped inwards and made contact with MADAM RICH (Vivek G., dr.1)

 

At the start, SHUBANKAR (App. Bharat Singh, dr.4) was observed to have lost its footing and stumble.

 

After the race, Jockey Vivek G. (MADAM RICH) reported that his mount was not galloping fluently. As such, the mare was subjected to veterinary examination and was found to be sore by right fore, the mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that MADAM RICH had right eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.3 (129) – THE OAKWOOD RESIDENCE PUNE TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

At the start, PRINCE IGOR (App. Avinash Paswan dr.3) jumped inwards at the same time MOJO (App. Ritesh Gochhi, dr.1) jumped outwards thereby bumping and tightening ETOILE (S. Mosin, dr.2) drawn between them causing the latter to be checked.

 

Approaching the 500 metres, EMPOWER (T. S. Jodha) racing on the haunches of ARIYANA STAR (Antony Raj S.) was denied a clear passage and had to check and drop back as a result ETOILE (S. Mosin) racing directly behind EMPOWER also had to be checked.

 

After the race, App. Avinash Paswan (PRINCE IGOR) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ETOILE was observed to have an injury on its right fore cannon.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that PRINCE IGOR had left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.4 (130) – THE DAKSHIN MAHARASHTRA & GOA SUB-AREA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey Amyn Merchant the saddle used on his mount DESTROYER was checked and re-adjusted.

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey C. Umesh the saddle used on his mount EMPEROR RODERIC was checked and re-adjusted.

 

At the start, RAMBLER (P. Vinod, dr.5) jumped outwards onto EMPEROR RODERIC (C. Umesh, dr.6).

 

At the start, BASHIR (Antony Raj S., dr.8) jumped inwards and made contact with DESTROYER (Amyn Merchant, dr.7).

 

Jockey N. S. Parmar (FIGHTON) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Pune Meeting 2025, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

DESTROYER was trotted at the stables and was found to be sound.

 

EMPEROR RODERIC was observed to have lost its left hind shoe.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that BASHIR had both eye injuries (sore).

 

RACE NO.5 (131) – THE ROYAL BARBERSHOP TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 13

 

After being stalled, DISCIPLINED (Neeraj Rawal, dr.6) was fractious and reared up thereby injuring its rider who was declared unfit to ride. As such, the gelding was permitted to be withdrawn before coming under starter order. Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.   

 

RED DUST (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.8) lost about a length at the start.

 

SEINA (C. Umesh, dr.11) lost about a length at the start.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, RIPTIDE (Antony Raj S.) leaned outwards and went across OTELLO (K. Nazil) causing the latter to be checked thereafter RIPTIDE shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing PRECIOSO (T. S. Jodha) who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation, Jockey Antony Raj S. has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, due to a tightly packed field MARCUS (App. Mustakim Alam) was denied a clear passage and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, RED DUST (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.8) shifted out onto PRECIOSO (T. S. Jodha, dr.1). App. Aditya Waydande has been cautioned.

 

After the race, ARLINGTON HEIGHTS was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

ASHWA GYPSY was observed to have an injury on its left hind pastern.

 

LA DOLCE VITA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

PRECIOSO was observed to have an injury on its right fore arm.

 

RACE NO.6 (132) – THE DR. P. K. SARDESHMUKH MAHARAJ TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 11

 

At the start, ESTEBAN (Neeraj Rawal, dr.7) jumped outwards and went across REGAL GALLERIA (App. Haridas Gore, dr.8) who was marginally slow to begin and inconvenienced AR FIRST STAR (S. J. Sunil, dr.9).

 

Soon after the start, SANTANA ROW (C. S. Jodha) shifted in and went across TOUCH OF MYSTIC (S. A. Amit) who had to be checked thereafter SANTANA ROW further shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing AGATHA (S. Mosin) who had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation, Jockey C. S. Jodha has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe (SOUTH BEACH) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey C. Umesh (EPIC ENDEAVOUR) reported that his mount was running green throughout the race. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SOUTH BEACH was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7 (133) –THE D. N. ZODGE PLATE: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

FLAMING LAMBORGINI (App. A. Omkar, dr.1) planted in the stall and did not take any practical part in the race. As Such, Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the rig in a Mock Race to the satisfaction of the Starter, before next accepting to race.

 

SUBSTANTIAL (A. Sandesh, dr.6) jumped out brushing the gates.

 

Approaching the 700 metres, KAVYA (N. S. Parmar) shifted out and made contact with BLACK THUNDER (App. Aditya Waydande). Jockey N. S. Parmar has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 500 metres, MANSA MUSA (A. Prakash) shifted out and carried BLACK THUNDER (App. Aditya Waydande) outwards. Jockey A. Prakash has been cautioned.

 

After the race, App Aditya Waydande (BLACK THUNDER) reported loss of whip approaching the 200 metres. This being his third instance during the current Pune Meeting 2025, the matter has been reported to the Stewards for such action as they deem fit.

 

After the race, SUBSTANTIAL was trotted and found to be sound. Further, at the behest of the Stipendiary Stewards, endoscopy was performed on the filly at the saddling enclosure and found to have EIPH (Gr. II).

 

The Equine Hospital reported that SHIRSA had left eye injury (sore).
