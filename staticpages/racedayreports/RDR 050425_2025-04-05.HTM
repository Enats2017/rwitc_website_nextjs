RACE NO.1 (174) – THE ARKTOUROS MILLION: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been fined Rs.500/- for failing to put in a request to use a red hood on his charge CRYSTAL CLEAR before the race day.

 

While approaching the starting gate, CRYSTAL CLEAR was observed to have a gum injury and on being examined was certified fit to race.

 

On arrival at the starting gate, at the request of Jockey Vivek G., OPERATION FINALE was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds before coming under Starters order and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

BOHEMIAN RHAPSODY (K. Nazil, dr.5) jumped out slowly and lost about two lengths at the start.

 

At the start, ZACAPA (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.6) jumped out awkwardly outwards onto CONTINENTAL DRIFT.

 

Approaching the 900 metres, CONTINENTAL DRIFT (A. Sandesh) shifted in and went across CRYSTAL CLEAR (Neeraj Rawal) causing the latter to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey A. Sandesh has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Approaching 800 metres, ZACAPA (App. Aditya Waydande) was observed to have lost its footing, stumbled and fell along with the rider. The Club’s Sr. V.O. reported that the gelding had Fractured its left fore fetlock and its right fore shoulder joint. As such, with due consent of Trainer Shazaan Shah the gelding was euthanized on humanitarian grounds.

 

After the race, Jockey A. Sandesh (CONTINENTAL DRIFT) reported that his mount was not galloping fluently. On being subjected to veterinary examination, the gelding was observed to have lost its right hind shoe.

 

RACE NO.2 (175) – THE OPEN SPACES MILLION: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 12

 

At the start, GIANT KING (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.1) jumped out slowly and lost about two lengths.

 

At the start, BLACK THUNDER (App. Bharat Singh, dr.3) jumped inwards and made contact with ALEXANDROS (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2) who had to be steadied.

 

At the start, DOCTOR DOLLY (A. Sandesh, dr.10) jumped outwards at the same time UZI (T. S. Jodha, dr.12) jumped inwards thereby inconveniencing LUCA (David Allan, dr.11) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Passing the 200 metres, ALEXANDROS (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in and went across BEYOND STARS (K. Nazil) causing the latter to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, GIANT KING was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by left fore. As such, the gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ALPHA GENE was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was found to be sore by right fore. As such the gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, LA DOLCE VITA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.3 (176) – THE MYSTICAL MILLION: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

On arrival at  the starting gate at the request of Jockey P. Trevor, ANGELO was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. before the race and was certified fit to race.

 

Soon after the start, ANGELO (P. Trevor) shifted out thereby bumping and boring GOLDEN GLOW (Antony Raj S.) thereby inconveniencing the latter. Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 1200 metres, MIGHTY SPARROW (Vivek G.) shifted out thereby forcing CHAGALL (App. S. Siddharth) outwards due to which SUBSTANTIAL (A. Sandesh) had to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, Jockey Vivek G. has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

Passing the 1200 metres, CHAGALL (S. Siddharth) shifted in and went across GREY BEAUTY (David Allan) causing the latter to be checked. After questioning and not being satisfied, App. S. Siddharth has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Approaching the 500 metres, GREY BEAUTY (David Allan) was held up for room and had to be steadied.

 

After the race, Jockey Vivek G. (MIGHTY SPARROW) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight, despite his efforts. As such, Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey P Trevor (ANGELO) reported that his mount was not galloping fluently. On being subjected to veterinary examination, the gelding was found to be sore by right fore. As such the gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

App. S. Siddharth (CHAGALL) was observed to have worn his  racing goggles in the straight, this being his second instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 24/25, he has been fined Rs.1,000/- for his lapse.

 

After the race, MIGHTY SPARROW was trotted and  found to be sound.

 ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “MOONLIGHT KISS” (DRAWN-12) IN RACE NO.170 - THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY DIV-II RUN ON 30TH MARCH 2025.

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 5th April 2025, having heard the submissions made by App. Mustakim Alam and Trainer Narendra Lagad, as also the Stipendiary Stewards and reviewing the video footage of the race in question, the Stewards having deliberated extensively on the matter noted that upon entering the straight, he failed to get into his mount despite a clear passage, and in the final furlong, he did not ride out. As a result, he failed to take all reasonable and permissible measures to secure the best possible placing in the race which could amount to not permitting his mount to run on its merits.

 

However, considering his past record, and that the mare was at longer odds, keeping in mind his admission of guilt, the Stewards gave him the benefit of doubt for not permitting his  mount to run on its merits, instead held him guilty of riding an unsatisfactory race and decided to suspend his Jockeys licence for a period covering 15 race days i.e. Should he apply for grant of a Jockey's licence for the racing season 2025/26, and the same is granted to him, it shall stand suspended from the first day of the Pune Meeting 2025 upto and including a period covering 15 race days.
