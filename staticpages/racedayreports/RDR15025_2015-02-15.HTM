RACE NO 1 (193). THE PLATINUM WORLD CUP  - DIVISION II  

(About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 12                                           

 

At the request of Trainer Narendra Lagad, ADMIRAL GRAY was examined at 1010 hours on 15/02/2015 and was found to be sore by left hind leg as such the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The gelding has to be passed fit to race by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

The Saddle on GOLDEN WAVE (Kishore Kadam) was observed to have slipped forward during the race and the jockey had to pull up his mount near 400 metres. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been fined Rs.2000/-.

 

SUPER BOLT (S.G. Prasad) lugged-in in the straight Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

NAZRANA (Darren Williams) lugged in in the straight near 400 metres went on to SUPER BOLT (S.G. Prasad). Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (DHISHKYAON) reported that he found his mount uncomfortable during the race. The Sr. VO reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey Darren Williams (NAZRANA) were questioned with regard to running and riding of NAZRANA.  The trainer stated that the instructions were to sit up in 5th, 6th position wherever comfortable and do his best in the straight. Jockey confirmed the instructions and stated that after the start he gave his horse a squeeze but the gelding did not muster enough speed and therefore he tucked him in and waited till the straight. He added that he did not want to travel wide around the bend therefore he continued to ride from where he was already positioned, and in the straight, the gelding lugged in and added that he could not have beaten the winner.  The trainer expressed his dissatisfaction with regard to the riding of his charge.  The matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards interviewed Jockey Darren Williams and Trainer Narendra Lagad and not being satisfied with the explanation of the Jockey, held him guilty of Unsatisfactory Riding on his mount whereby he did not follow the Instructions of the Trainer.

 

Taking an overall view, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s License w.e.f. 8th March 2015 to 18thApril 2015. (permitted to ride work/ Mock race).

RACE NO 4 (196). THE DR GOVIND R GURBAXANI MEMORIAL TROPHY 

(About) 2000 Metres. Runners –6

 

SHINING PATH whipped around after the start and took no part in the race. Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been asked to give the gelding more Gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter before next accepting to race the gelding.  

 

The Saddle on PAPPILLON (A. Ashhad Asbar) was observed to have slipped forward during the race whereby the jockey could not ride out his mount in the straight. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been fined Rs.2000/-.

 

Jockey Imran Chisty (MYSTIC ROMANCE) reported that his mount did not continue to gallop effectively in the straight and had changed his legs on a few occasions.  The Sr. Veterinary Officer was requested to examine the filly who reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

An enquiry was held into the running and riding of ELLIPSIS. When questioned trainer Zadmal Singh stated that the Jockey had not followed the Instructions which were to settle the filly wherever comfortable in the early part and near 1400 metres to judge the pace and if it was slow then to progress to 2nd position and ride his own race from 800 metres. The trainer observed that the jockey did not improve his position as instructed near 1400 metres and he rode the filly from the inside whereas she gallops well on the outside. When questioned jockey Dashrath Singh confirmed the instructions and added that it was an error of judgment on his part near 1400 metres not to have brushed up and sat well up. He argued that if he had to brush up at that stage he would have caused the filly to travel more ground. The trainer contented that it was not so and rather the jockey has travelled wide while negotiating the bend. He further added that the jockey had tugged at the filly on two occasions near 1400 metres and further that the filly has gained ground towards the end and if the jockey had positioned his mount as instructed then surely the filly could have won.  On further questioning the jockey admitted there was an error of judgment on his part as far as the pace of the race at that stage was concerned.

After viewing the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh has been given benefit of grave doubt since it was left to his judgment of pace in the race and he chose to do what appeared to him to be right.  Further, a close watch will be kept of the running of the horse in the future.

After the race, MYSTIC ROMANCE and ELLIPSIS were found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF b g “STROGANOFF” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.221 – THE GOEL GANGA GROUP SUPER SPRINT RUN ON SATURDAY, 18TH OCTOBER, 2014 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 15th February, 2015 interviewed Trainer Faisal A.Abbas in the above connection. Having thoroughly enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and also having permitted him to make submissions in the matter, the Stewards found that he had no mitigating circumstances to offer in the subject case and held him vicariously responsible for breach of the Medication Rules governing the finding of BOLDENONE, an anabolic steroid, in the urine sample of STROGANOFF.

                                                                                                                                                                               

Taking an overall view and keeping in mind that this was his first infringement of the Medication Rules, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided :-

 

a)                   To suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 120 days i.e. from 2nd March, 2015 till the end of the Mumbai Meeting 2014/15 (60 days). Thereafter, should he apply for a license for the Season 2015/16, it will be granted to him, however, his license will stand suspended for a further period of 60 days starting from the first day of Pune Meeting 2015;

b)                   To fine him a sum of Rs.15,000/-.

RE : CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS REPORT IN RESPECT OF UN-NAMED 3-YEAR-OLD b g “SERIOUS SPENDER / BE FRIENDLY”. THE URINE SAMPLE WAS COLLECTED DURING THE RANDOM SAMPLING FOR THE MONTH OF DECEMBER, 2014 – UNDER THE CHARGE OF TRAINER ARIF PATEL:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 15th February, 2015 interviewed Trainer Arif Patel regarding the Confirmatory Analysis Report dated 11th February, 2015 from LGC, Newmarket, UK. in respect of the random sample of urine taken from his charge un-named 3-year-old Serious Spender / Be Friendly for the month of December, 2014   which read as under :-

 

“…..The confirmatory analysis was performed using documented LGC methods, which identified the presence of boldenone sulphate in the urine sample”

 

A copy of the above report was handed over to Trainer Arif Patel and he was asked to continue his own enquiries and to lead evidence, if any, to prove mitigating circumstances within 15 days and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.Veterinary Officer of the Club, as and when he would be required to.

 

Further,  as per the Clause of the Medication Rules, un-named 3-year-old Serious Spender / Be Friendly has been banned from Racing for a period of six months i.e. up to and inclusive of 15th August, 2015.
