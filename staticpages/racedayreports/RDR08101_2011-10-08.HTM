RE: ENQUIRY IN TO THE RIDING OF JOCKEY SURAJ NARREDU (STAR OF VENUS) AS ALSO THE INCIDENT OF THE LOSS OF WHIP IN RACE NO 159 RUN ON SATURDAY, 24TH SEPTEMBER 2011

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday,  8th October, 2011 interviewed Jockey Suraj Narredu as also Trainer C.D.Katrak  in the above connection and having heard the submission made by them and having viewed the video recording of Race No.159 in a loop, (all angles – normal as well as slow motion) as also the blow up picture of the incident of the loss of whip, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Suraj Narredu with regard to his riding on STAR OF VENUS as also the manner in which he lost the whip. The Stewards noted :-

 

1)         That Jockey Suraj Narredu had ridden this horse once before during the Mumbai Meeting 2010/11 and won on him, therefore he knew the horse. Further,  he admitted that in the race which he won, he had used the whip and the filly had responded. Hence, inspite of being aware that the filly responds well to the whip, he did not use the whip even once in the straight and thereafter lost his whip at a crutial part of the race in the last 100 metres, which may have cost him the race.

 

2)         The fall of the whip, as apparent on the blow up picture of the incident of the whip fall, did not suggest an accidental fall and further there was no reaction on Jockey Suraj Narredu’s part to grab the whip back which happens in most cases where a Jockey loses his whip accidentally during the race.

3)         Jockey Suraj  Narredu stated before the Stewards that he wanted to give the filly a backhander at which stage he lost the whip, in the motion. However, the position of the whip in his hand at the time when the whip fell out of his hand, as apparent on the blow up picture of the incident of the whip fall, did not give any indication that he was attempting to give a backhander and he had therefore tried to mislead the Stewards.

4)         The Stewards also noted that just before the loss of whip, Jockey Suraj Narredu had peeped under his arm and was aware that there was a horse moving fast from his outside and thereafter the whip fell out of his hand in a doubtful manner.

5)         Trainer C.D.Katrak confirmed in the Jockey’s presence that STAR OF VENUS responds well to the whip and the loss of whip had cost the filly the race.

6)         There was prior information that STAR OF VENUS was not going to win this race and Jockey Suraj Narredu had been warned by the Stipendiary Stewards in the paddock.

 

Taking an overall view and the circumstantial evidence as stated above, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Suraj Narredu. The Stewards decided to give him some benefit for the charge of deliberately dropping his whip and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 13th November, 2011 to 1st December, 2011 for unsatisfactory riding on his mount (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: ENQUIRY IN TI THE INCIDENT IN THE STABLE IN RESPECT OF “STAR OF VENUS” PRIOR TO HER RUN IN RACE NO 159 – THE PEGASUS TROPHY – RUN ON SATURDAY, 24TH SEPTEMBER 2011 – TRAINER CD KATRAK:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 4th October 2011 considered the enquiry conducted by the Stipendiary Stewards and Sr. VO of the Club and interviewed Trainer C.D.Katrak as also his stable staff. The Stewards after having thoroughly enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and also having permitted Trainer Katrak to make submissions in the subject matter, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and as such, held him guilty of breach of the Medication Rules. It was noted :-

 

1)         His Jamadar admitted that Amino Speed Excell paste was given to the filly two hours before the race and he had forgotten to give it in the morning as instructed by him (Trainer Katrak).

 

2)         Tr.katrak was well aware that as per Clause VII of the Rules for “Medication Control For Horses In Training And Racing” as published in the Club’s Racing Calendar “No substance, other than those used in the normal feeding of a horse should be administered to the horse within 48 hours before the start of the race” The Stewards did not accept his contention that Amino Speed Excell paste was similar to hygain pre-mix and it was part of the normal feed of a horse, and therefore, it was a breach of Calendar Notification. The Stewards took into consideration Trainer Katrak’s contention that Amino Acid was a nutritional supplement, however, by his own admission, STAR OF VENUS was the only horse to whom the Amino Speed Excell paste was given on race days, therefore, it could not be considered as part of the normal feed of a horse, as also the recommended dosage was to give the dose two hours before the race.

 

3)         It was established by the Manufacturer’s literature, that amino acids have the benefit of improving the athletic performance of a horse.

 

4)         There was contradiction in the statement given by Trainer Katrak in the enquiry, in that, he informed that STAR OF VENUS was highly strung and overly sweating and had therefore to be given amino acids. However, the Syce of STAR OF VENUS denied that the filly was highly strung and overly sweats. Further, his Jamadar during the enquiry before the Stipendiary Stewards also denied that the filly was highly strung and overly sweats and thereafter he kept on changing the statements before the Stewards.

 

5)         Tr.Katrak’s Jamadar initially confirmed that several of his horses were given Amino Excell paste, a statement which he later retacted. By his admission, he had given this paste to STAR OF VENUS each time she ran on the morning of the race day, which was in breach of the Calendar Notification.

 

Taking an overall view of the facts of the case and taking into consideration the fact that Trainer Katrak has come up repeatedly before the Stewards for Medication and other Infringements, and this being his fourth case within one year, the Stewards decided :-

 

a)       To suspend his Trainer’s license for a period of 90 days;

b)       To impose on him a suspended sentence of 90 days for next two years.

In view of the above and taking note that Trainer Katrak’s license has already been suspended up to and inclusive of 26th December, 2011 for an earlier offence, his Trainer’s license will now stand suspended w.e.f. 27th December, 2011 to 25th March, 2012 (both days inclusive). As also to impose a suspended sentence of 90 days over the next two years, i.e. if he was found guilty of a similar offence within the next two years commencing from 26th March, 2012, then the aforesaid suspended sentence of punishment of 90 days suspension would immediately come into force and effect and which aforesaid punishment would be in addition to any other punishment which the Stewards may impose on him for any subsequent act of his as regards the aforesaid.

 

The Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer C.D.Katrak’s Jamadar Sohansingh and held him guilty of tendering false and misleading evidence in the enquiry.

Taking an overall view of the facts of the case, the Stewards decided to withdraw Mr.Sohansingh’s Jamadar’s license and to warn him off from the premises of the racecourses at Mumbai and Pune and all other places under the control of the Stewards for the purpose of race meeting which would include the days on which betting is taken at Mumbai and Pune in respect of races held at any other centre/s in India and of the premises of the R.W.I.T.C., Ltd. where the powers of the Stewards are in force, w.e.f. 19th October, 2011 till 16th January, 2012.
