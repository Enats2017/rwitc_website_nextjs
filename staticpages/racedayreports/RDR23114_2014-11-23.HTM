RACE NO.2(16) – THE ROTARY CLUB OF AMBARNATH CUP : (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 5

 

Soon on straightening for home, MOUNT RUSHMORE (A.Sandesh) shifted out behind SPIRITED TOUCH (Neeraj Rawal) and went on to COSTA DEL SOL (D.K.Ashish) who travelled wide. Jockey A.Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

SPIRITED TOUCH (Neeraj Rawal) was inclined to lean inwards in the straight. Trainer Karthik G has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, GOLDEN BELLE was observed to have lost its right hind plate.

 

RACE NO.3(17)  – THE MAHARAJA OF MORVI TROPHY : (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 3 – NO REPORT

 

RACE NO.4(18) – THE WOCKHARDT HOSPITAL & ROTARY CLUB OF BOMBAY WORLI CUP:

                                                                                                                         (About) 1800 Metres – Runners -  9

 

Near 800 metres, FLEETWOOD MAC (P.Trevor) was tightened for room and steadied as NIGELLA (App.S.Joseph) shifted in due to the proximity of BURLESQUE (D.K.Ashish) on its outside.

 

DOUBLE OR QUITS (Neeraj Rawal) did not get a clear run in the early part of the home straight.

 

DANSE DEBONAIRE (Dashrath Singh) lugged out in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, ZABISCO, ZAAN and REPLICA were observed to have broken a blood vessel and are not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The three horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ZABISCO, BURLESQUE, ZAAN, DANSE DEBONAIRE, REPLICA and NIGELLA were found to be sound. BURLESQUE had an abrasion at the left hock and NIGELLA had a cut injury at both hind tendon and right hind fetlock and had lost its right hind shoe.

 

RACE NO.5(19) – THE  ROTARY CLUB OF BOMBAY PIER – KRYFS CUP: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 3

 

The Sr. VO reported that ROSIE SUNSHINE & MYKONOS were treated with Ringer Lactate by Dr. P.T.Khambatta within 48 hours of the start of the first race of the Day. As per the Medication Rules, both the horses were not permitted to race and the matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards interviewed Trainer Adil Dajee and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, found him in breach of the Medication Rules and fined him a sum of Rs.5,000/- for each horse, for his lapse.

 

At the start, DYNAMO (D.K.Ashish) jumped awkwardly outwards and went on to MAUNA KEA (C.S.Jodha) who was checked.  

 

At the start, MINT APPROACH (A.Sandesh) jumped awkwardly outwards.

 

Jockey A.Sandesh (MINT APPROACH) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

 

RACE NO.6(20) – THE  ROTARY CLUB OF BOMBAY CHARITIES CUP: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 7

 

REMEMEBR ME (Neeraj Rawal) lost about 2 lengths at the start.

 

SHANIA (C.S.Jodha) lugged-in in the straight. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the filly and he reported that SHANIA was found to be sound but having a wolf tooth on the right side. Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that after completion of the race,  WAR COMMAND stopped at the 2000 metres mark. the colt was observed to be sore by the right fore leg. First Aid treatment was given and the colt was sent to the Equine Hospital by Horse Ambulance, for further management. WAR COMMAND has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7(21)– THE  ROTARY CLUB OF BOMBAY QUEENS NECKLACE CUP:

                                                                                                                             (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  9

 

The Sr.VO reported that SHIVALIK SHOWERS was treated with Ringer Lactate by Dr. PT Khambatta within 48 hours of the start of the first race on this Day. As per the Medication Rules, the horse was not permitted to race and the matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards interviewed Trainer Adil Dajee and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him found him breach of the Medication Rules and fined him a sum of Rs.5,000/- for his lapse.

 

Jockey P.Trevor (RULE DOWNUNDER) has been reprimanded for Improper use of the whip wherein he has the whip in his right hand as his mount was shifting out thus aggravating the outward movement of his mount. The Jockey explained that he was unable to change his whip hand as the whip was entangled with the reins and per force he had to use the whip with his right hand.  

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of CONGRESSIONAL and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, TOP COMMANDER was observed to have an abrasion at both the hind fetlocks. HAIL THE KING and FRIBOURG were found to be sound.

 

Due to Public agitation following the running of the 7th race, the Stewards decided to cancel the last race of the Day. The Stewards further decided not to permit Jockey A.Sandesh to ride at Mumbai on Thursday, 27th November, 2014 pending enquiry.

RE : POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF  gr f “MAHAAKAUSHAL” (NOW NAMED “GREY TYPHOON”) WHO WAS COMPULSORILY SAMPLED ON 9TH JULY, 2014 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED TO YOU FROM TRAINER ARTI DOCTOR, R.C.T.C.:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 23rd November, 2014 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry and interviewed Trainer Subhag Singh.

 

The Stewards of the Club having thoroughly enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings, reports and records and submissions made in the matter and also having permitted Trainer Subhag Singh to make submissions in the subject matter, held him guilty of breach of the Medication Rules, consequently he became vicariously liable.

 

Taking an over all view and considering the circumstances of the case, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to fine him a sum of Rs.25,000/- . Further, he was severely cautioned to be more careful in the future.

​RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “RUNNING SUN” IN RACE NO 211 RUN ON 17TH OCTOBER 2014 – TRAINER SUBHAG SINGH

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Subhag Singh in the above connection. The Trainer stated that all the runs during the Pune Meeting have been in upper Class and the gelding ran in a stepped up distance i.e. over 1800 metres which he felt that the gelding was not able to handle, also the gelding carried top weight of 62 kg at an age of 7 year old.

He said that his instructions were to ride from the front in the earlier runs and in this Race also the gelding comfortably went to the front. He opined that since the gelding was carrying the top weight, he would have liked if the gelding could be saved a little bit with better control from the Jockey. To another question, the Trainer stated that he had to declare Jockey J.Chinoy as the Jockey who usually rides the horse was not available.

 

The explanation was noted.

--------------------------

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “CELTIC KING” IN RACE NO 219 RUN ON 18TH OCTOBER 2014 – TRAINER ADIL DAJEE

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Adil Dajee in the above connection. Trainer Dajee stated that CELTIC KING was a stubborn and erratic horse and this time he had declared a 5 kg claiming Rider as he wanted to take advantage of taking the weight off and the Instructions were to go to the front. He added that the gelding could not go to the front and the Rider had hustled up the horse in the early part of the race and also the gelding ran in patches and kept losing position and then gained on in the straight.

The Trainer stated that the gelding is a rogue and behaves inconsistently even during the Track work and would keep stopping at different places on the Track. To another question, the Trainer stated that the gelding has reached his mark by placing on Board and not winning races that he should have won. To another question, the Trainer stated the horse has not won a race for a long time and has been up for sale but isn’t getting sold as well.

The explanation has been noted.

-------------------------

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “INCENTIO” IN RACE NO 212 RUN ON 17TH OCTOBER 2014 – TRAINER NARENDRA LAGAD

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Narendra Lagad in the above connection. Trainer Lagad stated that when INCENTIO was to run in this Race, the gelding was not too comfortable but not of such a concern that it would warrant withdrawing the gelding from the race. Also the gelding was put under a shower before the race as it was too hot, which he opined was the main reason for the gelding to have performed poorly. To another question, he stated that the gelding was reported to have broken a blood vessel earlier. The Trainer further added that the gelding did not enjoy the new Monsoon Track, but reiterated that the main reason may have been the heat on that day. 

 

The explanation was noted.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “WITHOUT FEAR” IN RACE NO 221 RUN ON 18TH OCTOBER 2014 – TRAINER NARENDRA LAGAD

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Narendra Lagad regarding the captioned subject matter despite the fact the filly had run in a Terms Maiden race in Race no 221 as compared to a Handicap race in Race no 180, considering the manner in which the filly fizzled out in the straight. The Trainer stated that when the filly placed 2nd in Race no 180 over 1400 metres, it was a Class V race with poor set of horses where only ADAMS WING had some form and the filly was able to place as compared to the Terms Maiden race which had a better set of horses. He opined that WITHOUT FEAR is a mediocre filly.

The explanation was noted.
