WAR OF ATTRITION (H. Zeeshan) jumped out slow losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, BIG BEN (N. S. Parmar, dr. 9) jumped out awkwardly throwing its head.

 

At the start, IN CONTENTION (P. Trevor, dr. 2) jumped outwards on to PATRIOTS DAY (App. P. S. Kaviraj, dr. 3). At the same time, SHE IS ON FIRE (Santosh G., dr. 4) jumped inwards, going across PATRIOTS DAY and on to IN CONTENTION, causing both the horses to be steadied.

RACE NO.2(94) –THE GREAT INVESTMENT PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  12

ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO MR.ALI AKBAR ABUTALIBI’S ENTRY TO THE STABLE AREA WITH AN UNAUTHORIZED PERSON / PROMOTING SALE OF ILLICIT SUBSTANCES ON THE RACE COURSE :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 26th December, 2020 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry and having heard the submissions made by Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi /  his Spokesman Mr. Haren Malkani / his witnesses as also the Stipendiary Stewards, the Stewards decided to adjourn the hearing to 9th January, 2021.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 9th January, 2021 again heard Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi and his Spokesman Mr. Haren Malkani as also the Stipendiary Stewards. They considered all papers, proceedings, reports and records including the documents submitted by Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi and his Spokesman Mr. Haren Malkani and having deliberated the matter at length, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi.

 

The Stewards noted that Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi was provided a Stable Entry pass only to help people to buy/sell horses and arrange for their transport. However, he had broken the trust by entering the stable area at the Mumbai Race Course on 13th December, 2019 along with an unauthorized person viz. Mr. Abdul Razak alias Vinod.  Further, he had broken the Rules as it was evident from the WhatsApp chats that he was knowingly or unknowingly exchanging information pertaining to medicines and illicit substances. The Stewards noted that the delay in concluding this enquiry was from Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi’s side and not the office as claimed by him. Also, from the audio recording of the enquiry conducted by the Stipendiary Stewards, it was evident that he had handed over his Mobile Phone to the Stipes voluntarily and not under pressure.

 

 

In view of the above, the Stewards of the Club were contemplating taking action against Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi. However, considering that he had tendered a written apology vide his letter dated 6th January, 2021 and assured the Stewards that henceforth he would give no cause for complaint and would abide by all the Rules of the Club and also keeping in mind that he had already suffered for almost one year due to him being refrained from entering the stable area to carry on his business, the Stewards of the Club decided not to initiate any further action in the matter and he was permitted to enter the stable area during the prescribed timings only to help people buy/sell horses and arrange for their transport and other related work.  Mr. Ali Akbar Abutalibi was warned that if he was again found transgressing the limited permission so granted to him, the permission so granted to him will be revoked with immediate effect and he will be liable for appropriate action under the rules.
