RACE NO.1(8) – THE GOOL S. POONAWALLA MILLION (GRADE 3): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  9

 

At the start, SON OF A GUN (Dashrath Singh, dr.8) jumped outwards onto FORTUNATE SON (N. S. Parmar, dr.9).

 

Soon after the start, VENUS (C. Umesh, dr.5) leaned inwards due to which DAIANNE (Bhawani Singh, dr.4) shifted in onto MEHR (P. S. Chouhan, dr.3).

 

Soon after the start, SON OF A GUN (Dashrath Singh) shifted in and went across LIAM (T. S. Jodha) causing the latter to check and drop back. After questioning, Jockey Dashrath Singh has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey P. S. Chouhan, the rider of MEHR placed second by the Judge against Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe the rider of O HANSINI placed first by the Judge. The reason being, in the last one furlong, O HANSINI (Prashant P. Dhebe) started to shift out, at 100 metres he used his whip from the right when MEHR (P. S. Chouhan) was on his quarters & shifted out further thereby crossed him in last 50 metres, whereby he had to stop riding to save a serious accident & lost a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule the objection. 

 

In this connection, the Stewards of the Club held Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe guilty of careless riding on his mount O HANSINI and it was decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for one race day i.e. on 8th January 2022, for careless riding, (Permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, LIAM was observed to have a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.2(9) – THE SPARTAN POKER SPRINTERS' CUP (GRADE 1): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  6

 

After being stalled, Jockey C. S. Jodha reported that his mount CORFE CASTLE had dropped his Tongue Strap as such the horse was unstalled. Considering the time to be taken to tie the said equipment all the other horses which were already stalled were also unstalled. Tongue Strap was tied on CORFE CASTLE and accordingly all the horses were reloaded.

 

At the start, GOLDEN OAKS (S. A. Gray, dr.3) jumped outwards at the same time ENIGMA (P. S. Chouhan, dr.4) jumped inwards and they made contact.

 

Passing the 400 metres, CORFE CASTLE (C. S. Jodha) drifted out despite the riders efforts and raced on extreme outside. Trainer L D’Silva has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

In the final stages of the race, MULTIFACETED (Suraj Narredu) shifted out under pressure.

 

After the race, MULTIFACETED was observed to have a cut injury on left hind cannon and hock.

 

RACE NO.3(10) – THE MAJ. P. K. MEHRA MEMORIAL SUPER MILE CUP (GRADE 1): (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  8

   

At the request of Trainer Laxman Singh, SAFFRON ART was examined at the stable area at 10.00 a.m. on 19/12/2021 and was found to be sore from left fore leg, as such the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

TAIMUR (C. S. Jodha, dr.4) was slow to begin at the start.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Antony Raj (TRULY EPIC) reported that he was denied a clear passage in the straight as such he had to steady his mount and had to shift out.

 

After the race, NORTHERN ALLIANCE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(11) – THE SURESH MAHINDRA MULTI-MILLION (GRADE 2): (About) 1400 Metres –Runners- 6

 

No Report

 

RACE NO.5(12) – THE VILLOO C. POONAWALLA INDIAN 1000 GUINEAS (GRADE 1): (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  9

 

MARVELLOUS (App. Kirtish Bhagat, dr.5) jumped and veered out and thereafter did not raise an effective gallop. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 200 metres, TERESITA (Akshay Kumar) shifted out onto QUEEN O’WAR (A. Sandesh) who in turn shifted out thereby inconveniencing ANIMOUS (Bhawani Singh). After questioning, Jockey Akshay Kumar has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe pointed out that the saddle on this mount LIT had slipped during the race. Trainer A. M. Jadhav has been fined Rs.2,000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

After the race, LIT was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, QUEEN O’WAR was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.6(13) – THE ZAVARAY S. POONAWALLA INDIAN TURF INVITATION CUP (GRADE 1): (About) 2400 Metres – Runners - 7

 

At the start, JULIETTE (A. Sandesh, dr.3) jumped inwards and had to be steadied.

 

After the race, SOVEREIGN LEGEND (S. A. Gray) was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The colt has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, JULIETTE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

An enquiry has been initiated in running and riding of JULIETTE (A. Sandesh) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

RACE NO.7(14) – THE DR. M. A. M. RAMASWAMY STAYERS' CUP (GRADE 1): (About) 3000 Metres – Runners -  7

 

Approaching the 400 metres, SALAZAAR (A. Sandesh) shifted in and went across MY OPINION (Akshay Kumar) who had to be steadied. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.
