RACE NO.1 (73) – THE VOX POPULI PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres, Runners – 5

 

Approaching 600 metres, RODRIGO (App.P.Trevor) shifted out due to FEMENIST (Zameer Sayyed) weakening and carried OUR LITTLE ANGEL (N.G.McCullagh) on to MONK’S FERRARI (Neeraj Rawal). Since the incident was accidental, no action has been initiated against anyone.

Near 600 metres, RODRIGO (App.P.Trevor) shifted in and tightened LIGHTNING CAVALRY (C.S.Jodha) who in turn tightened FEMENIST (Zameer Sayyed). App.P.Trevor has been reprimanded for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

LIGHTNING CAVALRY (C.S.Jodha) lugged-in in the straight for a brief period. Trainer Magansingh P.Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

After the race, RODRIGO was found to be sound. LIGHTNING CAVALRY was found to have an abrasion on the right fore bulb of heel.

RACE NO.2 (74) – THE AZTEC PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres, Runners – 8

 

Near 1000 metres, TINTISA (S.S.Baria) brushed the rails.

Soon on entering the straight, ARABIAN POWER (T.S.Jodha) shifted in and at the same time HOUDINI (Dashrath Singh) shifted out as TINTISA (S.S.Baria) who was racing in front, weakened and dropped back.

Near 100 metres, GOLD VALUT (D.K.Ashish) shifted in and at the same time HOUDINI (Dashrath Singh) shifted out and made contact with each other. Both the Jockeys, D.K.Ashish and Dashrath Singh, have been cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over their respect mount.

After the race, TINTISA was found to be sound.

RACE NO.3 (75)  – THE AZTEC PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metrrs, Runners - 9

 

STAR BLAZE (App.M.A.Roushan) jumped out slowly and thereafter stopped galloping. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been asked to give the filly more gate practice before next accepting to race.

 

Soon on entering the straight, MYSTIC TEMPLE (B Prakash) shifted in and made contact with GEISHA (D.S.Rathore) on a couple of occasions and travelling some distance further shifted in and made contact with MARUDHARA RATAN (Dashrath Singh) who was steadied. The matter was reported to the Stewards. The Stewards interviewed Jockey B Prakash and having heard him and viewing the video recording of the race, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to fined him a sum of Rs.10,000/- for not keeping proper control over his mount. Further, he was warned to be more careful in the future.

Trainer Narendra Lagad has been fined Rs.1000/- for bringing his charge EMERALD QUEEN in the Paddock with Blinkers, an equipment  for the use of which no prior permission was obtained. The filly ran without the Blinkers. 

After the race, TYSEA was found to have a cut injury on the right fore canon. MYSTIC TEMPLE, GEISHA and AGAIN GALLOP were found to be sound.

RACE NO.4 (76)  – THE ASTANA PLATE (DIV.II):   (About) 1000 Metres, Runners - 10

 

Jockey Zameer Sayyed (WELLS FARGO) has been cautioned for not being vigorous on his mount in the straight.

After the race, CAVALRYMAN was found to be sound

RACE NO.6 (78)  – THE EDGAR DeSYLVA CUP:  (About) 1000 Metres, Runners - 7

RACE NO.7 (79) – THE SNOW DEW PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres -  Runners - 7

 

Trainer P Shroff reported that Jockey S.A. De Sousa, the declared Rider for FLOODS OF FORTUNE would not be able to fulfill his riding engagement. Jockey S.A.De Sousa was replaced by Jockey Stephane Pasquier. An enquiry has been initiated into the matter and a report will be submitted shortly.

Jockey Paul Mulrennan (SET AFIRE) has been cautioned for shifting out under the use of the whip and inconveniencing FLOODS OF FORTUNE (Stephane Pasquier), in the last 100 metres.

RE : STEWARDS’ ENQUIRY – RACE NO.54 – THE JACQUELINE TROPHY RUN ON SUNDAY, 12TH DECEMBER, 2010 – JOCKEY SHRIKANT KAMBLE, RIDER OF ARES – DANGEROUS RIDING :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 23rd December, 2010 interviewed Jockey Shrikant Kamble in the above connection and having heard the submission made and viewing the video recording of Race No.54, the Stewards were not  satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Shrikant Kamble and held him guilty of dangerous riding on his mount ARES in the above race. RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “GREAT WHITE” IN RACE NO.45 – THE FREE RADICAL PLATE RUN ON 9TH DECEMBER, 2010 :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 23rd December, 2010 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. They interviewed Trainer N.B.Chenoy who was accompanied by her Spokesman, Mr.Imtiaz A.Sait as also Jockey V.Chandrakant and having heard the submissions made by them and viewing the video recording of the race :-

 

i)          the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey V.Chandrakant and held him guilty of  riding an incompetent race. In that he had unnecessarily check his mount and lost his position in the early part of the race and coming into the straight he did not take the gap available and did not give his mount a clear run.Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 7th January, 2011 to 20th January,  2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race). Further, he was severely cautioned for the future.

ii)         The Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Tr.N.B.Chenoy in the subject case and she was warned to be more careful in the future.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “AMORE MIO” IN RACE NO.46 – THE SIR RAHIMTOOLA CHINOY TROPHY RUN ON 9TH DECEMBER, 2010 :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 23rd December, 2010 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. They interviewed Trainer P.Shroff and Jockey S.Zervan and having heard the submissions made by them and viewing the video recording of the race :-

 

i)          the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.Zervan and held him guilty of  not permitting his mount AMORE MIO to run on its merits in the above race. In that, he waited for too long to make his move in the straight and allowed ANGELO to take a big break and thereafter made his move at 200 metres when it was too late and lost by a Head. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 7th January, 2011 to 27th March, 2011 (both days inclusive) (not permitted to ride work/mock race).


ii)         The Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Tr.P.Shroff. They held the view that since he had two fancied runners in this race, he should have given explicit instructions to Jockey S.Zervan when to make his move, which he failed to do and also tried to defend the Jockey’s riding in this race. Further, the Stewards also held him guilty of giving misleading evidence in the enquiry. Therefore, the Stewards decided to fine him a sum of Rs.25,000/-

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “LOYAL TYCOON” IN RACE NO.37 – THE  POWERHOUSE EASTERN GERMANY TROPHY (1000 METRES)  RUN ON SUNDAY, 5TH DECEMBER,  2010 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey H. G.Rathod  in the above connection.  Trainer Narendra Lagad said that LOYAL TYCOON was a five year old horse who was still a maiden. He said that because of unsoundness, he cannot work the horse on the Track and kept him on swimming and the horse comes up race to race. He said that in Race No.37 he had instructed the Jockey to ride his own race, however, he had also told him that the horse has soundness problem, therefore, to try and give him support in the early part and ride out in the straight. Jockey H.G.Rathod confirmed the instruction and added  that  the horse was lugging-in in the early part and therefore he held the left rein tight and rode out in the straight.

The video recording of Race No.37 was viewed and it was pointed out to Jockey H.G.Rathod that this was a 1000 metres race and the horse took a good start but thereafter, he had held the horse back by holding the left reins tight instead of allowing the horse to run free. Jockey H.G.Rathod reiterated that the horse was lugging-in soon after the start and therefore he held the left reins tight. Also, it was at the back of his mind that the horse has soundness problem and he wanted to give him support in the early part as instructed by the Trainer. He said that he had not  worked the horse prior to the race and this was the first time he rode the horse. Also, the horse did not have early speed and had warmed up towards the end and he had advised the Trainer to run him over a longer trip.

Trainer Narendra Lagad  confirmed that the Jockey had advised him after the race to run the horse over a longer trip. He said that the horse returned sound after Race No.37, however, he came back sore after Track Work this morning (18th December, 2010).

After scrutinizing the betting sheets and keeping in mind that the horse has soundness problem  and he will soon turn a 6-year-old and was still a maiden, the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted.

RE : COMPLAINT LETTER FROM TRAINER UDAY BHAN SINGH AGAINST MR.RAVINDER PAL SINGH CHAUHAN AND OTHERS FOR FORCIBLE REMOVAL OF HORSES FROM HIS YARD :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 23rd December, 2010 interviewed Trainer Sangramsinh N.Joshi in the above connection. The Stewards considered all the submissions made by him. They considered all papers and documents and having heard all the submissions  made, including his letter dated 6th October, 2010 in reply to the Show Cause,  and having deliberated the matter at length, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him in the subject case and held him guilty of gross misconduct. However, taking an overall view, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and instead of suspending his Trainer’s license, decided to fine him a sum of Rs.50,000/- and warned him for the future.
