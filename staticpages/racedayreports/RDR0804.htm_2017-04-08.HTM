RACE NO.1(265) – THE H.R.SHANTIDAS TROPHY : (About) 2000 Metres – Runners – 4                                            

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that SABIQ stopped galloping during the race at about 200 metres from the Winning Post. The mare was observed to be sore by the left fore leg and was given First Aid treatment on the track and sent to the Equine Hospital in a Horse Ambulance for further management. ADDITIONAL REPORTS :

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “FIRENZE” IN RACE NO.199 – THE NARAYANDAS J.DAVE PLATE – RUN ON 1st MARCH,  2017 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 5th April, 2017 interviewed Trainer Sanjay Kolse and Jockey Santosh G. in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club having considered the submissions made by them and having viewed the video of Race No.199 and the previous race of FIRENZE i.e. Race No.152, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Santosh G. and  held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on FIRENZE.  In that, he was specifically instructed to sit in the front bunch. However, he made no effort to sit in the front bunch thereby riding contrary to the instructions and allowed the filly to drop back to the tail end of the field. Further, his statement that the filly had thrown her head up on her own accord was not borne out in the video recording wherein it was observed that the filly had thrown her head up under restrain as she was fighting for her head. The Stewards noted that the Trainer had also expressed his dissatisfaction with the riding. Taking an overall view, the Stewards of the Club decided that should Mr. Santosh G. apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2017/18, the same will be granted to him, however, his license shall stand suspended for Eight Race Days commencing from the first Race Day of the Pune Meeting 2017. (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Jockey Santosh G. was further informed that in future if he wished to submit written submissions, he must do so well in advance.

RE :  ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ZOOM ZOOM” IN RACE NO.185 – THE OTTERS’ CLUB TROPHY – RUN ON 23rd FEBRUARY,   2017 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 5th April, 2017 interviewed Trainer A. M. Jadhav and Jockey Akshay Gaikwad in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club having considered the submissions made by them and having viewed the video of Race No.185, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Akshay Gaikwad and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on ZOOM ZOOM.  In that, entering the straight when he used the whip with the left hand, his mount shifted in and instead of changing your whip hand he again used the whip from the left aggravating the inward movement of the horse and thereafter changed his whip hand by which time you had lost valuable ground thereby placing his horse at a disadvantage, which possibly cost him the race. Therefore, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for Eight Race Days. This punishment will commence after you complete your suspension of Fifteen Months imposed on you for breach of Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing.

SUB : ENQUIRY INTO JOCKEY G. AMIT’S INVOLVEMENT IN BETTING AND PASSING ON STABLE INFORMATION/TIPS TO PUNTERS FOR PERSONAL GAIN:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 5th April, 2017 interviewed Jockey G. Amit in the above connection and having heard him as also the Stipendiary Stewards, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey G. Amit and held him guilty of improper practice under Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing of the Club wherein he admitted before the Stipendiary Stewards and the Stewards of the Club of passing racing information/tips to his friend/s on WhatsApp/ Facebook Messanger for monetary gains. (Further, scrutiny of his Bank Pass Book, revealed that certain cash deposits had been made into his account, three of which were cash deposits made from Hyderabad by the person he was in contact with and he confirmed at the meeting that these cash deposits were money received by him for having passed on racing information / tips and / or shares in bets.)

 

In view of the above, the Stewards of the Club were of the view that the above breach of Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing warranted a punishment of Fifteen Calendar Months. However, keeping in mind that Jockey G. Amit was co-operative and forthcoming in the enquiry and had admitted his guilt,  the Stewards of the Club took a lenient view and decided to impose on him a punishment covering Twelve Calendar Months. Therefore, his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f. 20th April, 2017 till 30th April, 2017 and thereafter should he apply for a Jockey’s license it shall not be granted to him up to and inclusive of 19th April, 2018.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO JOCKEY AKSHAY GAIKWAD’S INVOLVEMENT IN BETTING AND PASSING ON STABLE INFORMATION/TIPS TO PUNTERS FOR PERSONAL GAIN:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 5th April, 2017 interviewed Jockey Akshay Gaikwad in the above connection and having heard him as also the Stipendiary Stewards, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Akshay Gaikwad and held him guilty of improper practice under Rule-101 (iii) & (iv) of the Rules of Racing of the Club and for having given misleading evidence during the enquiry. In that, he admitted before the Stewards of passing racing information/tips to his friend/s but denied doing so for monetary gain. However, when confronted with his WhatsApp/Facebook Messenger chat history which revealed beyond doubt that he had passed on racing information/tips for monetary gain and also scrutiny of his Bank Account Pass Book which revealed that certain cash deposits had been made into his account, for which he failed to offer a plausible explanation.

 

In view of the above, the Stewards of the Club decided to impose on Jockey Akshay Gaikwad a punishment of Fifteen Calendar Months i.e. his Jockey’s license shall stand suspended w.e.f. 20th April, 2017 till 30th April, 2017 and thereafter should he apply for a Jockey’s license it shall not be granted to him up to and inclusive of 19th July, 2018.
