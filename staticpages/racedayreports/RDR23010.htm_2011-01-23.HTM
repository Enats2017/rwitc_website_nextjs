ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

MUMBAI MEETING 2010/11, NINETEENTH DAY, SUNDAY, 23RD JANUARY, 2011.

 (PENETROMETER: 2.0; GOING: GOOD)

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT.

 

On a request from Trainer Vijay Kasbekar, permission was granted to Trainer Himmat Singh to saddle his charges ARABIAN EMPIRE and DOCTOR NO in the 7th and 11th race respectively, at his (Trainer Vijay Kasbekar’s) risk and responsibility.

 

On a request from Trainer Shyam S Habbu, permission was granted to Trainer P Shroff to saddle his charge CAROUSEL in the 10th race, at his (Trainer Shyam S Habbu’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO 1. (151) THE SWEEPING SUCCESS TROPHY (About) 1600 Metres. Runners – 9

 

TINTISA (AM Tograllu) was reluctant to reach the Start. Trainer Imtiaz A Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare and ensure that the mare reaches the Start without causing any delay to the start of the race.

 

FLUENT VICTORY (Akash Agarwal) and HI DOLLY (Neeraj Rawal) were slowly out losing about 6 lengths and 4 lengths respectively. Trainers Narendra Lagad and DJ Surti have been asked to give their respective charges more gate Practice and show to the satisfaction of the Starter before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, TINTISA (AM Tograllu) lugged in and made contact with MAGICAL MANTRA (YS Srinath). TINTISA lugged out in the straight. Trainer Imtiaz A Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

On questioning, Jockey Imran Chisty (TOMORROWS DREAMS) stated that his mount lugged-in in the straight and as he tried to correct his mount, the gelding shifted out and carried MAGICAL MANTRA (YS Srinath) outwards who was steadied. Trainer HJ Antia has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, BRILLIANT MOON, TOMORROWS DREAM & TINTISA were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 2. (152) THE HARRISH MEHRA TROPHY - DIVISION II (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 10

 

Near 100 metres, GOLDEN ORRA (Towfeeq R Shaikh) shifted in as the mare lugged in and at the same time HOUDINI (Imran Chisty) shifted out and were involved in a jostle. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures with regard to GOLDEN ORRA and Jockey Imran Chisty has been cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

After the race, INSITE SOUL was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SEA WONDER was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 3. (153) THE P.D. AVASIA TROPHY (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 7

 

At the start, SEA TREASURE (R Hughes) jumped out awkwardly and went on to ASHWA PAARTH (Dashrath Singh). Since the incident was accidental, no action has been initiated against anyone.

 

Near 1000 metres, ILLUSTRIOUS (C Rajendra) shifted in as the filly was being settled when she became keen as SUPERIOR QUEST (NG McCullagh) came alongside and went on the ASHWA PAARTH (Dashrath Singh) who in turn tightened SEA TREASURE (R Hughes) who was steadied and in doing so lost position and inconvenienced DEMONSTRATOR (YS Srinath). Jockey C Rajendra has been cautioned for not exhibiting proper care in handling of his mount.

 

After the race, SEA TREASURE was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 4. (154) THE SATINELLO TROPHY (About) 1600 Metres.  Runners – 6

 

Trainer S Mangalorkar has been fined Rs.1000/- for accepting with his charge SNOW QUEEN without meeting the requirement of showing her to the Starter before accepting to race her. The filly was struck out at the Acceptance stage.

 

In the final 100 metres, FORWARD CHARGE (C Rajendra) lugged in and caused GREAT WHITE (Dashrath Singh) to shift in, who tightened DEEP PURPLE (App MA Roushan) along the rails. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, FLOODS OF FORTUNE was found to have lost its left fore plate. FLOODS OF FORTUNE and ARISTOCRATIC were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 5. (155) THE RAJPIPLA TROPHY (About) 1600 Metres. Runners – 7

 

At the request of Jockey Paul Mulrennan his mount, NATIVE KNIGHT, was re-saddled after reaching the start.

 

Jockey CD Hayes (STAR ALLIANCE) has been fined Rs.1000/- for using his whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Further, he has been cautioned for shifting out under the use of the whip whereby NATIVE KNIGHT (Paul Mulrennan) was inconvenienced.

 

RACE NO 6. (156) THE HARRISH MEHRA TROPHY  - DIVISION I (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 11

 

At the start, THE RIGHT MAN (A Sandesh) jumped out awkwardly and lost about 3 lengths.

 

Jockey Zameer Sayyed (DANCING MONEY) has been reprimanded for not being vigorous in his riding effort in the straight.

 

Jockey R Shelar and Trainer Faisal A Abbas were questioned with regard to the running of DEVILITTO. The Trainer stated that the gelding had suffered an eye injury during the last Pune Meeting and was racing after a long gap. The Jockey stated that the gelding hung-in in the straight and he had done his best. Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Trainer, he has been cautioned and told to ensure that his charges put in better performance in future.  

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal (GAME OF POWER) reported that his mount lugged out in the straight. Trainer Nana Ragunath has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey R Biramne (WARRIOR PRINCE) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer CD Katrak has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey S Zervan (DISTINGUISHED) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Nina M Lalvani has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding. 

 

After the race, SEA DIAMOND, WARRIOR PRINCE, THE RIGHT MAN, DEVILITTO, DISTINGUISHED & DANCING MONEY were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 7. (157) THE TRICUMDAS DWARKADAS TROPHY  - DIVISION I (About) 1000 Metres. Runners – 13

 

At the Start, APPALINA (DK Ashish) shifted in and carried SILVER SOUL (Neeraj Rawal) inwards who was steadied and at the same time SEEN IT ALL (SP Ranjane) shifted out when carried outwards by SEA BUCANEER (R Shelar). Since the incident was accidental, no action has been initiated against anyone.

 

Approaching 200 metes, ARABIAN EMPIRE (TS Jodha) shifted in and weakened due to which SEA BUCANEER (R Shelar) who was following was forced to shift out and in the process went on to SILVER SOUL (Neeraj Rawal), who in turn went on to APPALINA (DK Ashish) who inconvenienced CRÈME DE LA CRÈME (Akash Agarwal). Since ARABIAN EMPIRE was reported to have broken a blood vessel, no action has been taken in the matter. The gelding has been stopped from racing for the stipulated period of 4 weeks and until passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

App A Sandesh (MAJOR WAGER) reported that his mount lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey DK Ashish (APPALINA) reported loss of whip near 300 metres.

 

After the race, PRINCE OF TROY was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SILVER SOUL was found to be sound. ALBUURZ was found to have a cut injury on the right fore cannon.

 

RACE NO 8. (158) THE GOOL S. POONAWALLA MILLION (GRADE 3) (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 9

 

At the start, MOUNTAIN RULER (Amyn Merchant) fly jumped.

 

Jockey CD Hayes (CLASS APART) reported that his mount kept an erratic course in the straight.

 

ARACELI (C Alford) and SONEEYO (Dashrath Singh) were placed 5th and 6th by the Judge.

 

RACE NO 9. (159) THE LT.COL. GOVIND SINGH VRC TROPHY (About) 1400 Metres. Runners - 9

 

Trainer Sangramsinh N Joshi has been fined Rs.1000/- for failing to produce the registered racing colours in respect of his charge ASHWA PRABIR. The Jockey wore Emergency Colours i.e. All Black.

 

Jockey R Hughes (HILL RANGER) reported that his mount charged the Gates prematurely.

 

MAJESTIC FORCE (AM Tograllu) lugged out in the final 100 metres and went across FREE FOR ALL (R Shelar). Trainer Karthik G has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, HILL RANGER was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 10. (169) THE HDIL INDIAN OAKS (GRADE 1) (About) 2400 Metres. Runners - 6

 

Trainer JS Dhariwal has been fined Rs.3000/- for putting in late Declaration in respect of his charge SIACHEN. 

 

CAROUSEL & SUN ELEGANCE were placed 5th and 6th by the Judge.

 

RACE NO 11. (161) THE TRICUMDAS DWARKADAS TROPHY  - DIVISION II (About) 1000 Metres. Runners – 13

 

SHABDEEZ (Amyn Merchant) lugged out in the straight. Trainer Asad Siddiqui has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.
