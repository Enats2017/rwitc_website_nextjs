RE : ENQUIRY INTO RUNNING AND RIDING OF “SET TO FLY” IN RACE NO.88 – THE SANS PAREIL PLATE (1400 METRES) RUN ON SUNDAY, 26TH AUGUST, 2012 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer S.K.Sunderji and Jockey J.Chinoy in the above connection. Trainer Sunderji said that in the first run of the horse during the current Pune Meeting in Race No.35 the horse was well up and had fizzled out in the straight. He said that in the race in question, the horse was drawn-12 and he had instructed the Jockey to take a jump and settle the horse in mid bunch, gradually improve his position at the bend, have horses in front of him to challenge and to do his best in the straight. Jockey J.Chinoy confirmed the instructions.

 

The video recording of Race No.88 was viewed and when asked for his observation, Tr.Sunderji opined that the Jockey probably sat a little further back than he would have liked and travelled a little wide from the 400 metres and he came too quick. Also, the Jockey used the whip in the wrong hand (right hand) whereby the horse drifted out and all this had cost him the race. Tr.Sunderji said that he had told the Jockey in the paddock that he should keep the whip in his left hand and on entering the straight when he saw the whip in the Jockey’s right hand, he was hoping that the horse would run straight but he went out. He said that this was only the third run of the horse and he was running a little green and Pune Race Course has a short straight whereby if a Jockey commits a mistake he has no time to correct the horse. He said that after the race the Jockey informed him that the horse was lugging in slightly and therefore he had switched the whip to the right hand which he (Jockey J.Chinoy) had later realized was a mistake and the same had cost him the race.

 

Jockey J.Chinoy said that he settled the horse in mid bunch as instructed and he had his whip in the left hand all along. However, on entering the straight the horse started lugging in slightly and he felt that COMMANDER (Dashrath Singh) was racing too close to him, therefore, he switched the whip to the right hand because it was at the back of his mind that in the earlier race also the horse had hung in slightly.  Jockey J.Chinoy said that on hindsight, he felt that it was a mistake to change the whip hand and the same had cost him the race and he had informed this to the Trainer after the race. When asked as to why he did not switch the whip to the left hand again when he realized that the horse was drifting out, Jockey J.Chinoy said that when he used the whip twice, the horse showed no signs of drifting out but thereafter suddenly went out and gave him no time to correct the horse.

The matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as deemed fit.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Saturday, 15th September, 2012 interviewed Tr.S.K.Sunderji and Jockey J.Chinoy in the above connection and having heard the submissions made by them and having viewed the video recording of the race, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey J.Chinoy and held him guilty of injudicious riding on SET TO FLY wherein he had allowed the horse to drift out under use of the whip in the straight and made insufficient effort to correct the horse, which, by his own admission, had cost him the race. However, keeping in mind that Jockey J.Chinoy had admitted his mistake, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and instead of suspending his Jockey’s license, they decided to fine him a sum of Rs.10,000/- for injudicious riding.

RE: APPEAL AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB FOR EXCLUDING HIM FROM THE PREMISES OF THE RACECOURSE AT MUMBAI/ PUNE AND ALL OTHER PLACES WHERE THE RULES OF RACING OF THE CLUB ARE IN FORCE, W.E.F 6TH MARCH 2013 TO 5TH MARCH 2018 – ALLEGED ACCEPTANCE OF ILLEGAL RACE BETS BY HIM DURING THE MYSORE RACES HELD ON 30TH MAY 2012 – MR. MOHAMMAD SALIM NAIK:

 

The Board of Appeal at their meeting held on Saturday, 15th September, 2012 considered appeal dated 13th August, 2012, from Mr.Mohammad Salim Naik, on the captioned subject.

 

The Board of Appeal having studied all documents and papers that were submitted including his grounds of appeal and having heard Mr.Mohammad Salim Naik and his Spokesman, Mr. Gopal, decided to reject his appeal and forfeit the deposit. Accordingly, the decision of the Stewards of the Club stands.

 

Further, the Board of Appeal felt that the punishment meted out to Mr.Mohammad Salim Naik appeared to be lenient considering the gravity of the offence as also the fact that he had given misleading statements to the Board of Appeal. The Board of Aooeal felt that the punishment meted out to him should be enhanced and therefore he was given a show cause asking him to show cause as to why the punishment meted out to him should not be enhanced.

 

After again hearing Mr.Mohammad Salim Naik and his spokesman and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered, the Board of Appeal decided to enhance the punishment and accordingly decided not to admit him at the Mumbai/Pune Race Courses and all other places under the Rules of Racing of the Club, up to and inclusive of 5th March, 2023.

SHROFF:

 

The Board of Appeal at their meeting held on Saturday, 15th September, 2012 considered Appeal dated 11th August, 2012, from Trainer P.Shroff, on the captioned subject.

 

The Board of Appeal having considered all documents, papers and proceedings, including his “Grounds of Appeal” and the submissions made by Tr.P.Shroff and after having applied their minds to the matter, considered that, since the Stewards have accepted the findings of the Government Analyst, Drug Control Laboratory, Maharashtra State and have already expunged the suspension from his record and refunded the fine of Rs.10,000/- that was paid by him, the Board of Appeal has decided to remove the condition placed on him by the Stewards in their order of 7th August, 2012.

 

The Board of Appeal decided to remove the condition placed by the Stewards in order of 7th August, 2012 since the Stewards of the Club are entitled to re-open any matter if they are satisfied that fresh evidence is forthcoming.
