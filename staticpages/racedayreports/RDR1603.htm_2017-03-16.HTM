RE : WITHDRAWAL OF “DHISHOOM” FROM RACE NO.92 – THE KHEEM SINGH GOLD CUP – RUN ON 1ST JANUARY, 2017 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 9th March, 2017 interviewed Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait and Dr.P.T.Khambatta in the above connection and having considered all the submissions made by them as also the Club’s Sr.V.O., Dr.S.K.Kulkarni and having scrutinized the relevant documents/log books, the Stewards of the Club were convinced that there was a genuine clerical error in recording the treatment given to DHISHOOM, however, such incidents casts a doubt on the integrity of racing.  The Stewards held the view that  there was negligence on the part of Dr.P.T.Khambatta wherein he had failed to exercise the requisite degree of care in accurately recording/logging  the treatment given to the horse and that he was directly accountable for the actions of his employees. Taking an overall view, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to fine him a sum of Rs.5,000/-.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE LOSS OF WHIP BY JOCKEY SURAJ NARREDU ON HIS MOUNT “MAGICAL MEMORY” IN  RACE NO.121 – THE SIR H.M.MEHTA TROPHY– RUN ON 15TH JANUARY, 2017:

 

After the race, Jockey Suraj Narredu (MAGICAL MEMORY) reported that passing 200 metres he had lost his whip as he was tightened for room.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “STAR OF HARMONY” (DRAWN-4) IN RACE NO 167 - THE G2 GOLD CUP - RUN ON 18TH FEBRUARY 2017 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that Jockey Suraj Narredu was questioned on 26th February, 2017 in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Jockey Suraj Narredu said that he was instructed to ride his own race. He said that his mount was inclined to run out throughout the race and passing the 600 metres the filly drifted outwards despite his efforts. Thereafter, coming into the straight, he had held on to his right reins and also used the whip from the left, however, it had no effect on the filly who kept trying to go out further and due to this he was unable to ride out his mount to complete advantage. He said that subsequently he had reliably learnt that after the race the filly had a sprain on her off fore and was treated for the same which may have been the reason for her behavior.

 

Trainer M.Narredu was questioned on 27th February, 2017. He stated that after the race, the filly had a sprain and was treated for the same which could be borne out from the Medication Log Book.

 

Having heard the submissions made and having viewed the video of the race and on scrutinizing the Medication Log Book, the statements made by the Trainer and Jockey was confirmed as the filly was subjected to an X-Ray on 19th February, 2017 and thereafter treated by the Club’s Vet and Dr.Prashant Wandhare. In view of the above, the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted and the enquiry was closed.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “HEADLINES” (DRAWN-7) IN RACE NO 168 - THE FEDORA PLATE (1000 METRES) - RUN ON 18TH FEBRUARY 2017 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that Trainer Altaf Hussain and Jockey Akshay Gaikwad were questioned in the above connection. The video of the race was viewed and when questioned, Tr.Altaf Hussain said that being a big field, he had instructed the Jockey to sit in 6th/7th position and thereafter coming into the straight to do his best. When asked about his observation of the race, Trainer Altaf Hussain said that Jockey Akshay Gaikwad has an awkward whipping action which he has been trying to correct and had also requested the Riding Instructor to correct his whipping action. He said that the filly has undergone a tie back surgery and she was still a maiden. He said that he intends to race the filly next over 1200 metres or 1400 metres and would put up a better rider.

 

Jockey Akshay Gaikwad confirmed the instructions and said that he had ridden out the horse and did his best. He added that in the process of using the whip, it almost slipped out of his hand, hence he was cautious thereafter.

 

Having heard the submissions made and viewing the video recording of the race, the Stipendiary Stewards were not entirely satisfied with the Jockey’s riding effort. However, keeping in mind the explanation of the Trainer that the filly had undergone a tie back surgery and she was still a maiden and that Jockey Akshay Gaikwad is of an average calibre, no action was initiated in the matter and the Trainer and Jockey have been informed that the enquiry was kept open and a close watch will be kept on the future running of the filly.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “GENEROUS LADY” IN RACE NO.151 – THE D.N.ZODGE PLATE (DIV.I) RUN ON 8TH OCTOBER, 2016 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 12th March, 2017 interviewed Trainer C.D.Katrak and Jockey D.S.Daman who was accompanied by his Spokesman, Mr.Adhirajsingh Jodha, in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club having heard Jockey D.S.Daman/his Spokesman, Mr.Adhirajsingh Jodha as also Trainer C.D.Katrak and having viewed the video recording of Race No.151 as also the blow up of the incident of the loss of whip, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey D.S.Daman and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on GENEROUS LADY.  In that, he had lost the whip at a crucial stage of the race in a very suspicious manner and there was a drift in the odds. Further, the Trainer had also expressed his dissatisfaction with the riding.

 

However, taking an overall view and keeping in mind that he had already been stopped from riding in races with effect from 13th January, 2017 till date, the Stewards of the Club held the view that he had already suffered his punishment for unsatisfactory riding and therefore decided not to impose any further punishment on this count. He was therefore free to ride from 13th March, 2017 onwards.

 

However, the Stewards of the Club noted with grave concern that the details of the phone call records finally provided by Jockey D.S.Daman pertaining to the period August, September and October, 2016 when compared to the Mobile phone call records for the months of November, and December, 2016, appear to have been tampered with as they were not on the letter head of the Service Provider nor did they carry the logo and hence it cannot be relied upon. Therefore, the Stewards of the Club decided that this enquiry pertaining to his Mobile phone bill records would continue and appropriate action would be initiated against him if he was found guilty in any way.

SUB :ENQUIRY INTO JOCKEY KAVRAJ SINGH RIDING AT UNSANCTIONED/UNRECOGNIZED RACE MEETINGS AT KUWAIT DURING THE PERIOD OF HIS SUSPENSION:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 23rd February, 2017, 26th February, 2017, 1st March, 2017 and 12th March, 2017 interviewed Jockey Kavraj Singh in the above connection. (Jockey Kavraj Singh was represented by his Spokesman, Mr.P.S.Chouhan on 12th March, 2017).

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 12th March, 2017 having heard Jockey Kavraj Singh/his Spokesman, Mr.P.S.Chouhan as also the Stipendiary Stewards, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Kavraj Singh and held him guilty of :-

 

1)            Gross misconduct, wherein he admitted before the Stewards that he rode in Kuwait during the period of his suspension;

2)            Giving misleading evidence before the Stewards.

 

Having deliberated on the matter at length and considering the gravity of the offence, the Stewards of the Club in accordance with Rule-101( i ) read in conjunction with Rule 46 ( c ) and also Rule-18 (vii) (b) read along with Rule-202 (vi) & (vii) of the Rules of Racing, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license with immediate effect up to and including 16th April, 2017 (not permitted to ride work/mock race).
