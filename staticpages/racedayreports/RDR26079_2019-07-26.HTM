RACE NO.1 (8) –  THE JAMSHED DALAL TROPHY : (About) 1600 Metres – 8 Runners

 

At the behest of the Stipendiary Stewards, a Stewards’ Enquiry was held in this race. The reason being, approaching the 400 metres ASTOUNDING BAY (Yash Narredu) was angled inwards due to which KENNEDY (App.H.Zeeshan) shifted in thereby tightening STAR COMRADE (Bhawani singh) causing the latter to be checked. Thereafter, instead of taking corrective measures, Jockey Yash Narredu continued to urge his mount causing it to further shift in thus tightening and subsequently taking the ground of KENNEDY. As a result of this, KENNEDY lost its footing and was unbalanced thereby dislodging its rider. As a consequence of this incident, SILVER KING (S.Zervan) was tightened along the rails and STAR COMRADE (Bhawani Singh) had to be held in order to avoid the fallen rider. Also, the rider less KENNEDY shifted out on to ALOHA (N.B.Kuldeep) who had to be checked.

 

After considering the submissions made by Jockey Yash Narredu, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to maintain the Order of Placing as declared by the Judge. 

 

The Stewards after the last race interviewed Jockey Yash Narredu for dangerous riding on ASTOUNDING BAY. Having heard Jockey Yash Narredu and viewing the video recording of the race, the Stewards  were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 7 Race Days w.e.f. 10th August, 2019 to 1st September, 2019 (both days inclusive) for dangerous riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).  

 

After the race, ASTOUNDING BAY was observed to have an abrasion at the left fore fetlock joint.

 

RACE NO.2 (9) –  THE CLASSIC TALK PLATE : (About) 1400 Metres – 8 Runners

 

After being stalled PEERLESS (Akshay) played up and dislodged the rider. The filly was taken out and examined by the Club’s VO who certified her fit to race.

 

After being stalled, BAIT AND SWITCH (C.S.Jodha) played up in the stall. BAIT AND SWITCH was taken out and examined by the Club’s VO who certified him unfit to race, as such, the gelding was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s order.

 

In view of above, Trainers M.K.Jadhav and S.K.Sunderji have been asked  to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

MAKATI (N.B.Kuldeep) jumped out tardily losing about a length at the start.

 

HER EMINENCE (App. K. Nazil) jumped out tardily and thereafter stopped galloping. Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to take remedial measures and show the filly in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards before next accepting with her to race.

 

Soon after the start, SILVER STORM (S.S.Baria) shifted out and went across MAKATI (N.B.Kuldeep) who was slow to begin.

 

The Club’s V.O.  reported that after being stalled, BAIT AND SWITCH played up and banged its head against the starting gate and started bleeding from the upper jaw. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that SASAKWA had injury in both eyes (corneal opacity and ulcer).

 

After the race, MAKATI and THEA’S FIRE were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3 (10) –  THE DR.S.R.CAPTAIN TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres –  8 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer Vinesh, JUNO’S GUEST was examined at 0900 hours on 24/07/2019 and found to have urticaria, as such the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the filly from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, JOPLIN (App. K. Nazil) jumped and shifted out and had to be steadied.

 

Soon after the start, RIQUEWIHR (P. Trevor, dr.5) shifted in and went across BESHIKTASH (C. S. Jodha, dr.4) who had to be steadied. Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 200 metres, JOPLIN (App.K.Nazil) was observed to have lost its footing and stumbled.

 

In the final stages of the race, BESHIKTASH (C. S. Jodha) leaned inwards and at the same time FLAMING MARTINI (S. Zervan) shifted out and due to the close proximity of the two runners, Jockey C. S. Jodha accidentally struck Jockey S. Zervan with the whip. Jockey C. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that RIQUEWIHR had right eye injury (corneal opacity and ulcer) and KING KHALIL had right eye injury (mild corneal ulcer).

 

After the race, BESHIKTASH and JOPLIN were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4 (11) –  THE ISMAIL KHAN PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – 16 Runners

 

SHIVALIK SKIES (Amyn Merchant) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, WE MAKE THE RULES (App. P. Vinod, dr.14) jumped inwards and made contact with STAR SILVER (App. A. S. Peter, dr.13), who in turn shifted in on to WHISPERING QUEEN (Altaf Syyed, dr.12). App.P.Vinod has been cautioned.

 

As the start was given, App. Raghuveer Singh (GRAND EYES, dr.2) was observed to have held the frame of the gate, due to which he was unbalanced causing his mount to whip around and in the process he was dislodged. Due to this incident NIGHTFALL (App. N. B. Kuldeep dr.3) had to be checked. App. Raghuveer Singh has been cautioned.

 

Passing 200 metres, AQUARIUS (App. K. Pranil) shifted out and went on to RETAINED ASSET (Santosh G.). App. K.Pranil has been cautioned.

 

DECCAN KING (S.A.Amit) trailed the field. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to ensure his ward runs with the field next time out.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that POWER OF THOR had right eye injury (corneal opacity and ulcer).

 

After the race, NIGHTFALL was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5 (12) –  THE HAWKE BAY PLATE (DIV.II) : (About) 1000 Metres – 12 Runners

 

As the start was given, BELENUS (S.Zervan) swayed her head and put her head in the adjoining stall and planted.  Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been fined Rs.1,000/- for not producing the registered Racing Colours in respect of his charge BELENUS (S. Zervan). The filly raced in emergency colours i.e. “ALL BLACK”.

 

Passing 200 metres, BLACK CHERRY (App. P. S. Kaviraj) shifted out thereby forcing CRACKLIN’ ROSIE (Amyn Merchant) outwards on to BITCOIN (App. Aniket Karande). App. P. S. Kaviraj has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, App. K. Nazil (SIR RAMON) reported that the saddle on his mount had slipped during the race as such he could not ride out his mount to its complete advantage. Trainer Hosidar Daji has been fined Rs.2,000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that I AM THE WAY had left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.6 (13) –  THE ALLEY CAT PLATE : (About) 1200 Metres – 14 Runners

 

Trainer Vinesh has been fined Rs.1,000/- for accepting with his charge JUNO’S GUEST in two races on the same day. As per the General Conditions published in the Prospectus, the filly was struck out from this race and accepted to run in THE DR.S.R.CAPTAIN TROPHY.

 

At the request of Jockey Yash Narredu, his mount TANJO was examined by the Club’s VO who certified the mare fit to race.

 

EPIPHANY (App.N.Nadeem) refused to be  stalled despite substantial efforts and in the process the mare fell backwards, as such, EPIPHANY was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s order. Trainer Nazak Chenoy has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

ARC SHINE (App.K.Nazil) played up as the start was effected resulting in her jumping out slow and losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

After the race, Trainer Nazak Chenoy lodged a verbal complaint in connection with the loading process of her charge EPIPHANY which led to the mare being withdrawn. The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the said complaint.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that MOZART had right eye injury (corneal opacity) and UNTITLED  had left eye injury (sore).

 

After the race, TANJO and FLOWER DUST were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7 (14) –  THE HAWKE BAY PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres – 12 Runners

 

Soon after the start, ADELE (App.M.Ayyar, dr.2) shifted out and at the same time THOMAS HARDY (Akshay, dr.5) shifted in thereby tightening and forcing ROYAL ACE (App.Niranjan S.Parmar, dr.4) inwards onto GOLDEN GUEST (P.Trevor, dr.3) causing it to jostle with ADELE (M.Ayyar dr.2). After the race, Trainer Nazak Chenoy lodged a verbal complaint that Jockey P.Trevor has been bullying apprentices and in this race he has deliberately shifted in and caused interference to her horse. The video recording was shown to Trainer Nazak Chenoy and it was explained to her that it was a culmination of things but Trainer Nazak Chenoy did not agree and persisted that her compliant be lodged. The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into this matter and a report will be submitted. 

 

Soon after the start, CABELLERO (S.A.Amit dr,8) shifted out under urgings and went on to BONAFIDE (App.Gagandeep Mann, dr.9) forcing it outwards on to IMPALA (App.Aniket Karande, dr.10). Jockey S.A.Amit has been cautioned.

 

Passing 800 metres, BONAFIDE (App.Gagandeep Mann) shifted in and went across CABELLERO (S.A.Amit) who had to be checked. After questioning, App.Gagandeep Mann has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Approaching 400 metres, HIGHLAND EMPRESS (S.Zervan) was observed to have dropped back and stopped galloping.

 

After the race, Jockey S. A. Amit (CABELLERO) reported that the saddle used on his mount had slipped during the race as such he was unable to ride out his mount to complete advantage. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been fined Rs.2,000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

Jockey Akshay (THOMAS HARDY) reported loss of whip 150 metres from the winning post. This being his first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2019, no action has been initiated.

 

The Club’s V.O. reported that HIGHLAND EMPRESS stopped galloping at about 400 metres before the winning post. The filly was on lateral recumbency and unable to get up. The filly did not respond to the treatment given, hence she was shifted to the Equine Hospital for further diagnosis and treatment. The Equine Hospital reported that on ultrasound examination, the filly was observed to have a fracture of L4 – L5, a complete fracture of transverse process of L4 & L5, rupture of lumbo sacral joint (L5 – S1). Hence the filly was euthanized on humane grounds at the Trainer’s request.
