RACE NO.1 (143) – THE S. PADMANABHAN CUP: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

On arrival at the starting gates, on a request from App. Aditya Waydande, EARTH was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was certified fit to Race.

 

At the start, ALPINE STAR (App. N. Bhosale, dr.1) jumped outwards thereby forcing FOURTH WING (Yash Narredu, dr.2) outwards onto BEE MAGICAL (Neeraj Rawal, dr.3) causing the latter to be steadied.

 

At the start, SON OF A GUN (K. Nazil, dr.6) jumped and carried EARTH (App. Aditya Waydande, dr.7) outwards causing the latter to be steadied.

 

Soon after the start, EARTH (App. Aditya Waydande) shifted in onto SON OF A GUN (K. Nazil) who further shifted in and went across ARDAKAN (Vivek G.) causing the latter to  check and change course. App. Aditya Waydande has been cautioned.

  

From the 300 metres, EARTH (App. Aditya Waydande) drifted out despite the riders efforts thereby carrying FOURTH WING (Yash Narredu), BEE MAGICAL (Neeraj Rawal) and BISHOP (Prashant P. Dhebe)  outwards. After the Race on questioning, App. Aditya Waydande stated that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight, despite his efforts. As such, Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

EARTH was trotted and found to be sound.

 

BISHOP was examined at the stables on 9th March 2026 and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (144) – THE J. P. VAZIFDAR TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

On arrival at the starting gates, on a request from App. N. Bhosale, THE ATHABASCA was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and was certified fit to race.

 

THE ATHABASCA (App. N. Bhosale, dr.5) lost about a length at the start.

 

At the start, SENECA (Vivek G., dr.7) was observed to have lost its footing and stumble. After the Race, Jockey Vivek G. reported that his mount was not galloping fluently, the gelding was subjected to veterinary examination and was found to be sound.

 

At the start, EMPEROR RODERIC (S. Mosin, dr.4) was observed to have lost its footing and stumble.

 

Soon after the start, EMPEROR RODERIC (S. Mosin) shifted out and went across THE ATHABASCA (App. N. Bhosale) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

THE ATHABASCA was observed to have trailed the field. After the race, on veterinary examination, the gelding was found to have broken a blood vessel, is not permitted to race for the ban period of four weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the Race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

EMPEROR RODERIC was observed to have lost its left fore shoe.

 

DON JULIO was observed to have lost its right hind shoe.

 

RACE NO.3 (145) – THE RAJPIPLA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 10

 

After being stalled, HAVOC (R. Ajinkya, dr.7) was fractious and thereafter lost about five lengths at the start. As such, Trainer P. S. Chouhan has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, MAGENTA (T. S. Jodha, dr.2) jumped inwards and went across AGE OF REASON (Neeraj Rawal, dr.1) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 1100 metres, ABSOLUTE SERENITY (S. Zervan) shifted in and went across LION HEART (Akshay Kumar) causing the latter to be steadied. Jockey S. Zervan has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, SPECTRA (Prashant P. Dhebe) shifted out and carried ABSOLUTE SERENITY (S. Zervan ) outwards. Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe has been cautioned.

 

After the Race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal (AGE OF REASON) reported that his mount was running erratic throughout the race. This being the first outing of the filly, no action has been initiated.

 

After the Race, the Sr. V.O. reported the following:

 

AEGEAN SEA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4 (146) – THE J. RUSTOMJI SOPARIVALA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 12

 

On arrival in the paddock, CROWN FLAME (Akshay Gaikwad) was observed to have sported a Tongue Strap, permission for the same was not sought, the tongue strap was removed before the  filly left the paddock and Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been fined Rs.1,000/- for his lapse.

 

GO TONTO (Bhawani Singh, dr.11) was reluctant to be stalled. As such, Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey Vivek G., rider of LUCKY DIAMOND and Trainer Narendra Lagad ,Trainer of LUCKY DIAMOND placed third by the judge, against Jockey S. G. Prasad, rider of QUEEN CAROLINE, placed second by the judge stating that “in the last 200 metres QUEEN CAROLINE has drifted out on to LUCKY DIAMOND whereby the Jockey had to check his horse on two occasions, whereby LUCKY DIAMOND lost a certain 2nd place as the  horse was full of running and the verdict between the  2nd & 3rd horse is Head“

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockey and the Trainer, in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the Video Recording of the Race from multiple angles and acting on their own observations of the Race, the Stewards decided to uphold the objection, termed it as accidental and revised the order of placing is as follows:.

 

STARIA                       -           FIRST                         SPORTING ALICE     -           FOURTH

LUCKY DIAMOND     -           SECOND                    SAINT SIZZLE            -           FIFITH

QUEEN CAROLINE  -           THIRD

 

In view of above, Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe (LIGHTING SPEAR) reported that his mount was shying away from the other runners throughout the race. This being first run of the filly, no action has been initiated.
