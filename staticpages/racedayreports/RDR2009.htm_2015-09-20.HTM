1. THE NEONA PLATE  - DIVISION III   (About) 1200 Metres.   Time: 1.00 P.M. Race no: 143

 

Trainer C. Rajendra has been fined Rs.3,000/- for late Declaration in respect of his charge BARKAT.

 

KICKBOXER (App. G. Amit dr.2) jumped out angling inwards onto BARKAT (P. Trevor).

 

Soon after the start RED FORT (App. Parbat Singh dr.8) was jostled and tightened between SPANISH SAGA (C. S. Jodha dr.7) and MAKE IT SHINE ( T. S. Jodha dr.9)

 

Approaching 200 metres Jockey R. Shelar (DEVOTED) was observed to have lost his whip this being his first offence, no action has been initiated.

                                                                                                           

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of MAKE IT SHINE. A report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, BARKAT was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race MAKE IT SHINE and LAS SALINAS were found to be sound.

 

 

2. THE ELOISE PLATE  - DIVISION II  (About) 1400 Metres.   Time: 1.30 P.M. Race no: 144

 

STALWART (D. K. Ashish) was reluctant to stalled. Trainer Ivor Fernandes has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

MAQSAD (N. S. Parmar dr.5) was observed to have jumped out awkwardly inwards going on to RESILIENT (Kishore Kadam dr.4)

 

Passing the 600 metres TRUE HEIR (App. Yash Narredu) was observed to have jostled for room with MAQSAD (N. S. Parmar) in an effort to make a run from the outside.

 

After the race MAQSAD was found to be sound, but having abrasion on its right nostril. TRUE HEIR and ROYAL WISH were found to be sound.

 

 

3. THE NEONA PLATE  - DIVISION I  (About) 1200 Metres.   Time: 2.00 P.M. Race no: 145

 

FORTUNA (Santosh G.) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

BIG BRO (App. N. Nadeem dr. 2) jumped out awkwardly outwards onto CHASE THE ACE (Satish Nayak dr. 3) and FEW DOLLARS MORE (Akshay dr. 6) jumped out awkwardly inwards onto BEAU ROYALE (J. Chinoy dr. 5) which resulted in DIVINE SPARK (R. Shelar dr. 4) being tightened for room forcing him to check his mount and drop back.

 

Soon after the start MY FREEDOM (Amyn Merchant dr.10) shifted out and made contact with HORSE POWER (App.Vishal dr.11)

 

Passing the 1000 metres FORTUNA (App. Santosh. G) was observed to have brushed the false rails.

 

At the winning post FORTUNA (App. Santosh G.) veered out sharply and went across MIDNIGHT RUN (App. Bhawani Singh) causing the later to clip its heels and dislodging the rider. App. Santosh G. has been suspended for two days i.e. 10th and 11th October 2015 for careless riding.

 

After the race MIDNIGHT RUN was found to be sound.

 

 

4. THE SECRET MEMORY PLATE  (About) 2400 Metres.   Time: 2.30 P.M. Race no: 146

 

Passing the 600 metres STARRY BROOK (App. Yash Narredu) was observed to have shifted in onto SIMONA (P. Trevor) forcing the later to check his mount. App. Yash Narredu has been fined Rs. 1000/-

 

After the race TOP CAT and SIMONA were found to be sound.

 

 

5. THE RAO SAHEB KEDARI GOLD TROPHY (About) 1400 Metres.   Time: 3.00 P.M. Race no: 147

 

ALDERLEY EDGE shifted in soon after start tightening the field on his inside. Jockey D. K. Ashish has been fined Rs. 3,000/-

 

Jockey Malcolm Kharadi’s reins were observed to have slipped about 250 metres from the post.

 

Passing the 200 Metres LOGANO (App.S.Joseph) shifted out and bumped CHA CHA CHA (Dashrath Singh). Apprentice S.Joseph has been cautioned.

 

In the last 50 metres VISTANA (App. Santosh G.) went on to ALDERLEY EDGE forcing the later to check and drop back. App. Santosh G. has been suspended for 16th October 2015 for careless riding.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (CHA CHA CHA) reported that as the race was fast he could not keep up with the pace.

 

After the race CHA CHA CHA was found to be sound, but having cut injury on its right fore cannon. LOGANO, WINGS OF GLORY and WOVEN DREAMS were found to be sound.

 

 

6. THE VILLOO C. POONAWALLA GOLD TROPHY  (About) 1000 Metres.   Time: 3.30 P.M. Race no: 148

 

Trainer C. Rajendra has been fined Rs.3,000/- for late Declaration in respect of his charge PRICELESSGIRL.

 

AME (N. S. Parmar) played up in the stall and hooked one of its fore leg in the adjoining stall. The gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race. Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

BUCKLE UP (C. S. Jodha) was restless and was not allowing its rider to settle in the stall.  The Starting Stall Certificate of the gelding has been revoked.

 

JORDANA (J. E. Pereira) was restless and was not allowing its rider to settle in the stall.  Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

AME (N. S. Parmar dr.5) jumped out awkwardly outwards and went across MAJESTIC (Suraj Narredu dr.7)

 

Jockey J. Chinoy (AZALEA) reported that his mount was hanging out throughout and had a very awkward action as such he could not ride out.

 

After the race Jockey Neeraj Rawal (ZAHAB) reported that his mount tried to run out in the straight.

 

GODSTOUCH (App. Yash Narredu) was observed to have hung out in the straight. Trainer advised remedial measures.

 

After the race PRICELESSGIRL was found to be sore on her right fore leg.

GODSTOUCH and AZALEA were found to be sound.

 

 

7. THE S A POONAWALLA MULTI-MILLION (GRADE 3) (About) 1600 Metres.   Time: 4.00 P.M. Race no: 149

 

Passing the 200 Metres JIGSAW(App.Yash Narredu) shifted in on to FEARLESS NADIA (D. K. Ashish). App. Yash Narredu has been cautioned.

 

Jockey Suraj Narredu has been fined Rs. 3,000/- for the excessive use of whip (Second offence) on his mount CONTINENTAL.

 

After the race JEFFERSON was found to be sore on left fore leg. CONTINENTAL and FEARLESS NADIA were found to be sound.

 

8. THE NEONA PLATE  - DIVISION II  (About) 1200 Metres.   Time: 4.30 P.M. Race no: 150

 

ROCK THE TURF (J. Chinoy) planted in the stall were by losing considerable ground at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start PURPLE PARADE(Bhawani Singh dr.11) shifted in and made contact with LOCARNO(App.S.Joseph).

 

SNOW BLAST (R.Shelar) jumped out awkwardly throwing its head up.

 

Approaching the 400 metres FLYING SHOW (D. K. Ashish) shifted in causing interference to MAKE WAY FOR ME (Neeraj Rawal). Jockey  D.K.Ashish has been cautioned.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh (CEASARINA) reported that he did not get a clear run at any stage of the race.

 

After the race SNOW BLAST and ROCK THE TURF were found to be sound.

 

 

9. THE ELOISE PLATE  - DIVISION I   (About) 1400 Metres.   Time: 5.10 P.M. Race no: 151

 

Trainer C. Rajendra has been fined Rs.3,000/- for late Declaration in respect of his charge NAME OF THE GAME.

 

GREY TYPHOON (Bhawani Singh) lost about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start TERRIFIC (App.D.M.Ayyar dr.5) shifted in and made contact with ALFONSO (Dashrath Singh dr.4)

 

ARMEL (App. N. Nadeem) shifted in soon after start tightening the field on his inside.  App. N. Nadeem has been fined Rs. 2,000/-

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of NAME OF THE GAME. A report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race ENSIGN was found to be sound, but having gum injury. NAME OF THE GAME  and SOFIA were found to be sound.

 

After the race SILLY DUDE was found to be sound. At the request of the trainer Altaf Hussain the horse was scoped at the equine hospital and was observed to have grade II EIPH.
