RACE NO.1 (172) – THE  BOUVET-LADUBAY CHAMPIONSHIP (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Mts, Runners–12

 

Trainer Nina M.Lalvani has been fined Rs.5000/- for declaring Jockey Paul Mulrennan on her charge DISTINGUISHED in a race confined to Indian Jockeys. 

 

Near 500 metres, DOUBLE DELITE (T.S.Jodha) was inconvenienced as FLUENT VICTORY (App.P.Trevor) went across the former. App.P.Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Jockey Akash Agarwal (LOYAL TYCOON) shifted in under use of the whip and in the process carried GAME OF POWER (Dashrath Singh) inwards and at the same time LADY BLACK STAR (Imran Chisty) shifted outwards and made contact with GAME OF POWER near 100 metres. Jockeys Akash Agarwal and Imran Chisty have been cautioned.

 

DISTINGUISHED (Malcolm Kharadi) lugged in and FLUENT VICTORY (App.P.Trevor) lugged out in the straight. Trainers Nina M. Lavani and Narendra Lagad have been asked to take remedial measures with their respective charge before next accepting to race them.

 

After the race, FIERY FORTUNE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, FLUENT VICTORY, DISTINGUISHED, LOYAL TYCOON & HISHAM were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2 (173) – THE  BOUVET-LADUBAY CHAMPIONSHIP (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Mts, Runners–13

 

At the start, SEA WONDER (T.Atul) and DEVIL IN DISGUISE (S.Sunil) jumped out awkwardly. THE REBEL (App.A.Sandesh) also jumped out awkwardly and made contact with DEVIL IN DISGUISE.

 

Near 1000 metres, HICKORY (Y.S.Srinath) was hampered and lost position as BLAU HIMMEL (C.Alford) lugged in. Further, the gelding lugged in more due to the presence of SILVER SCREEN (Akash Agarwal) on its outside. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures with his charge BLAU HIMMEL before ext accepting to race.

 

Soon on straightening for home, HICKORY (Y.S.Srinath) shifted out to progress in the race and in the process forced SILVER SCREEN (Akash Agarwal) to travel wide. Jockey Y.S.Srinath has been cautioned.

 

After the race, BLAU HIMMEL, TOP ACTION & CRESCENDO were found to be sound. CRESCENDO had an abrasion on the right hind bulb of heel.

 

RACE NO.3 (174) – THE UNITED SPIRITS CHALLENGE: (About) 1000 Metres, Runners – 9

 

OUR FORTUNE (A.M.Tograllu) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Karthik G. has been asked to give the mare more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to race.

 

Near 700 metres, HIGHLAND FLAME (S.K.Jadhav) became keen and caused BULLET (Dashrath Singh) to shift out, who in turn clipped the hind heels of BLUMARINE (Imran Chisty) and stumbled.

 

In the final 300 metres, BULLET (Dashrath Singh) lugged in and carried OCEAN WISH (R.Hughes) inwards. Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

OUR FORTUNE (A.M.Tograllu) lugged in and scrapped the rails in the final 100 metres. Trainer Karthik G. has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the mare.

 

After the race, BLUEMARINE was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind gaskin.

 

RACE NO.4 (175)  – THE  BLACK DOG TROPHY): (About) 1600 Metrrs, Runners - 17

 

KALINGA STAR (Dashrath Singh) was slow in the stride, losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, LAST TANGO (App.A.Sandesh, dr.7) and FETISH (S.Zervan, dr. 9) jumped out awkwardly. NORTHERN ROCK (S.P.Ranjane, dr.12) and SADDLERS RULE (App.P.Trevor, dr.13) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside and inside respectively and were involved in a jostle with each other.

 

An Objection was lodged by App.P.Trevor, the rider of SADDLERS RULE (placed second) against Jockey Dashrath Singh, the rider of KALINGA STAR (placed first). The reason being, in the final 200 metres, KALINGA STAR kept bumping him and taking him in and did not allow him to ride out or to use the whip freely.

 

The Stewards having heard the evidence of the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their observation of the incident, decided to uphold the Objection and revised the Order of Placing as under:

SADDLERS RULE        -           First;                             DIVINE MAJESTY                    - Third

KALINGA STAR                        -           Second                         OCEAN WARRIOR                   - Fourth

 

In the above connection, the Stewards interviewed Jockey Dashrath Singh for careless riding on his mount and having heard him and viewing the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and held him guilty of careless riding on his mount KALINGA STAR in the above race. In that, his mount shifted in under use of the whip in the last 200 metres and bumped SADDLERS RULE (App.P.Trevor) couple of times and he made no effort to correct the horse. However, keeping in mind his clean record in the recent past, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 20th February, 2011 (permitted to ride work/mock race). Further, he was warned for the future.

 

After the race, EXCELLTASTIC was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.5 (176)  – THE  McDOWELL SIGNATURE TROPHY (DIV.II):(About) 1400 Mts - Runners –13

 

DANCING DAME (C.Rajendra) and HARLAMOFF (B.Prakash) were reluctant to be stalled. HIGH SOCIETY (Imran Chisty) planted and took no practical part in the race. Trainers Imtiaz A.Sait, M.K.Jadhav and N.B.Chenoy have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the Declarations, the Owner of ENCHANTED requested for replacement of the declared Jockey, Towfeeq R Shaikh. As such, Jockey Towfeeq R.Shaikh was replaced with Jockey A.M.Tograllu.

 

Passing 400 metres, MOMENT TO REMEMBER (D.K.Ashish) shifted out due to which GAMBA (S.Sunil), who was following, shifted out and was steadied due to the presence of HARLAMOFF (B Prakash) making its run on its outside. Jockey D.K.Ashish has been cautioned for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

After the race, VIA CHASELLAS was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, DANCING DAME was found to be sound.

After the race, SEA SHOWER & TENDER ROMANCE were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7 (178) – THE ANTIQUITY RARE ‘FUSION OF THE FINEST’ CUP: (About) 1200 Metres  - Runners – 13
