RACE NO.1(238) –THE SAMUEL NATHAN PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 5 

 

Soon after the start, App. K. Nazil (GRANDE VITESSE) in an effort to settle his mount was unbalanced and lost its footing.

 

Approaching 600 metres, SIMON SAYS (Amyn Merchant) racing between BRABOURNE (S. Zervan) and TAKE FIVE (P. S. Chouhan) was tightened for room and had to be steadied.

 

Jockey S. Zervan (BRABOURNE) reported the loss of his whip, near the 400 metres. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into the incident and will submit a report shortly.

 

After the race, CARNIVAL was observed to have broken a blood vessel. Being a second time bleeder, the mare is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SIMON SAYS and CARNIVAL were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(239) – THE P.A.D’AVOINE TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 8

 

Trainer Nosher Cama has been fined 2,000/- for declaring incorrect shoeing on his charge BRONX wherein the gelding was declared to run with Aluminium, hind bare shoes but ran with Aluminium, both fore square toe shoes.

 

On the arrival at the start, BAKHTAWAR (Kiran Naidu) was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified him fit to race.

 

After the race, while pulling up, BRONX (P. S. Chouhan) stumbled and fell along with its rider due to which VITESSE (App. K. Nazil) who was on his haunches shied outwards thereby dislodging its rider.

 

After the race, ART O WAR, GRAND TENTON and BAKHTAWAR were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(240) –THE CURSETJEE DHUNJISHAW TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 6 

 

TURNING POINT (C. S. Jodha) jumped out slow losing about 3 lengths at the start. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to give the filly more gate practice and show her to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, FLAG FLYING (App. H. Zeeshan, dr. 4) was observed to have lost its footings and stumbled.

 

Passing the 1000 metres, ALLORA (Dashrath Singh) shifted in and went across ODESSA (S. Zervan) who had to be steadied. Jockey Dashrath Singh has been cautioned.

 

After the race, MOONSHINE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.4(241) –THE D.W.REID PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres–Runners - 9

 

Soon after the start, OLIVIA KASPEN (App. K. Nazil) shifted in and went across DEVOTED (App. S. A. Amit) and RICH N RARE (App. N. B. Kuldeep) due to which both the horses had to be steadied. App. K. Nazil has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 350 metres, BEYOND FORCES (App. H. Zeeshan) shifted out and carried OLIVIA KASPEN (App. K. Nazil) outwards due to which ALLEGRIA (S. Zervan) was inconvenienced. Thereafter, passing the 250 metres OLIVIA KASPEN (App. K. Nazil) shifted in thereby tightening ALLEGRIA (S. Zervan) who had to be checked. After questioning, App. K. Nazil has been cautioned.

 

After the race, BULLION EXPRESS and BOHEMIAN were observed to have broken a blood vessel and are not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. Both the horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, RICH N RARE, FINANCIER and DEVOTED were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(242) –THE  P.A.D’AVOINE TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 9

 

WE BREAK THE RULES (Dashrath Singh) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

PACIFIC DUNES (Santosh G.) jumped out slow, losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, WE BREAK THE RULES (Dashrath Singh, dr. 4) jumped outwards and went across PRICELESS ART (Neeraj Rawal) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, MAJESTICAL (Bhawani Singh) shifted out thereby inconveniencing RELENTLESS PURSUIT (C. S. Jodha).

 

From the 300 metres, RINCON (App. G. Amit) shifted out under pressure and as a result carried WE BREAK THE RULES (Dashrath Singh) and POLYNEICES (Kishore Kadam) outwards. After questioning, App. G. Amit has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey Kishore Kadam (POLYNEICES) reported that his mount was weakening in the final stages of the race and as such leaned inwards.

 

After the race, RINCON, WE BREAK THE RULES, PRICELESS ART and DIXIELAND were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(243) – THE V.P.KOREGAONKAR PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 10

 

At the start, ROYAL MEWS (App. K. Nazil, dr. 7) jumped inwards on to FRIBOURG (App. H. Zeeshan, dr. 6) and made contact.

 

Coming into the straight, Jockey S. G. Prasad (BEE QUIRKY) was observed to have pulled up his mount and passing the 100 metres, in an effort to dismount the horse, the Jockey fell. The Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the above incident and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

Approaching 300 metres GOLDEN HORDE (C. S. Jodha) shifted out and went across FROSTED (App. Raghuveer Singh). Thereafter, GOLDEN HORDE further shifted out and carried FRIBOURG (App. H. Zeeshan) outwards. After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha reported that his mount hung out in the straight. As such, Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of ARABIAN STORM (App. N. B. Kuldeep) and GOOD THING (D. A. Naik) in the above race and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, FROSTED and ROYAL CLASSIC were found to be sore by the left fore leg. Both the horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, BOUNTY QUEEN, ARABIAN STORM, FRIBOURG and BEE QUIRKY (examined on 24/03/2018) were trotted and found to be sound.
