RACE NO.7 (97) – THE STAR PRINCESS PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1000 Metres -  Runners - 10

 

At the best of the Stipendiary Stewards, a Stewards’ Enquiry was held in this race. The reason being, passing 200 metres MIDSUMMER KNIGHT (H.G.Rathod) forced his way between ROYAL GESTURE on the outside and RED TABASCO on the inside, where there was insufficient room, as a result RED TABASCO was squeezed out and thereafter MIDSUMMER KNIGHT further shifted in and caused severe interference to DOCTOR NO who was racing along the rails. Thereafter, ROYAL GESTURE (D.K.Ashish) shifted in under use of the whip and squeezed the horses on his inside.

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Steward/Racing Advisor  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to disqualify MIDSUMMER KNIGHT (placed Fourth) and to revise the order of placing of the race as under :-

 

RIVER DRAGON                      -           First;                 ROYAL GESTURE        -           Third;

BEEMER                                  -           Second;            SEA BUCANEER         -           Fourth

 

The Stewards after the last race, interviewed Jockey H.G.Rathod, the rider of MIDSUMMER KNIGHT for dangerous riding on his mount and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 16th January, 2011 to 17th February, 2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

The Stewards also interviewed Jockey D.K.Ashish, the rider of ROYAL GESTURE, for careless riding on his mount and not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by him, resolved to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 16th January, 2011 to 20th January, 2011 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, SEA BUCANEER was found to be sound.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ARSHAVIN” IN RACE NO. 90 – THE VICTORY MARCH PLATE RUN ON SUNDAY, 26TH DECEMBER 2010 :

The Stewards at their meeting held on Thursday, 30th December, 2010 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. They interviewed Trainer  Faisal A.Abbas and Jockey S.Sunil and having considered the submissions made by them and having viewed the video recording of Race No.90, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.Sunil in the subject case and held him guilty of not allowing his mount ARSHAVIN to run on its merits in the above race. In that, he waited for too long to make his move in the straight. Thereafter, when his horse shifted wide out under use of the whip, he stopped the use of whip and corrected the horse and despite the horse travelling 8 to 9 horses wide at that stage, and Jockey S.Sunil having all the chance to change his whip hand, he did not do so and resumed using the whip with his right hand thereby further aggravating the outward movement of the horse thus losing valuable ground at a crucial part of the race and lost the race by half a length. The Stewards noted that there was a considerable drift in the odds on ARSHAVIN.

 

Therefore, taking an overall view, the Stewards decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 16th January, 2011 to 27th March,  2011 (both days inclusive) (Not permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “STRATEGIC CHANGE” IN RACE NO. 85 – THE NAWABZADA RASHIDUZZAFAR KHAN TROPHY RUN ON SUNDAY, 26TH DECEMBER 2010 :

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer S Padmanabhan and Jockey Neeraj Rawal with regard to the caption subject matter.

 

The Jockey was instructed to go to the front and if someone chases then to sit well up and not to cut pace. When asked for the observation of the Jockey’s riding, Trainer Padmanabhan stated that soon after the start, STARTEGIC CHANGE was racing on the inside of STAR ALLIANCE and then the Jockey chose to drop out and sit 2nd. The Trainer was of the opinion that the running of the horse did not have an effect on the end result of the race.

 

Jockey Neeraj Rawal confirmed the Instructions. When asked about the reason for dropping out about 100 metres after the start, the Jockey stated that his mount was slow and he had brushed to move up in the field and had eased up once he realized that he could cut pace with STAR ALLIANCE. He added that he had followed the second part of the Instructions which were to be well up and he had been in touch with STAR ALLIANCE who was racing in front.

 

The matter was reported to the Stewards for necessary action.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 30th December, 2010 considered the above report. They interviewed Jockey Neeraj Rawal and having heard him and having viewed the video recording of Race No.85, decided that no action was called for in the matter.
