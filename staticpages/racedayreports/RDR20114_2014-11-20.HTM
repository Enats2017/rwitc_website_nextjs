RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT  OF dk b f “BULLSEYE”WHO WAS COMPULSORILY SAMPLED ON 6TH DECEMBER, 2013 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED TO TRAINER P.SHROFF FROM THE STUD FARM – MATTER REFERRED BACK TO THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB BY THE BOARD OF APPEAL – DE-NOVO ENQUIRY :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 28th July, 2014, 27th August, 2014, 17th September, 2014 and 28th October, 2014 considered the above matter.

 

At the outset, Trainer P.Shroff was informed that all the Stewards were not satisfied with the wording of his letter of apology. He was asked by Mr.Dixit to rephrase it in a manner that would indicate a proper apology and which would be accepted by all Stewards. The wordings of the letter of apology was given to Tr.P.Shroff to consider and submit it in this manner. 

 

Trainer P.Shroff had initially stated that there was nothing wrong with his letter of apology and that it should suffice. However, as this was unacceptable to all the Stewards, he said that he would give the new letter the following day. As the enquiry continued, Trainer P.Shroff, of his own accord, signed the letter in the room in the presence of all the Stewards and handed his new letter of apology to the Chairman.

 

The Stewards considered all the submissions made by Tr.P.Shroff and also permitted him to bring in witnesses. The Stewards after having enquired into the matter and having applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and to all papers, proceedings reports and records and also having permitted Tr.P.Shroff to make submissions in the subject matter, decided that he was in breach of the Medication Rules governing the finding of BOLDENONE (an anabolic steroid) in the urine sample of BULLSEYE and held him vicariously responsible for the following reasons:-

 

1)            BULLSEYE had left his yard in mid-April, 2013 for summering at Nanoli Stud as per the Left Charge form submitted by him. Though BULLSEYE went for sampling directly from Nanoli Stud on 6th December, 2013 it is a fact that she was under his care till 15th August, 2013 and thereafter was not subjected to any test of her urine by the R.W.I.T.C. Ltd. As an anabolic steroid could remain in the system of a race horse for up to 180 days, it cannot be conclusively established as to when and where the BOLDENONE was administered. Also, the reports submitted by Nanoli Stud and Agricultural Farms from two international labs on the segmental hair testing analysis carried out by them in respect of BULLSEYE cannot be relied upon since there was no chain of custody of the hair sample.

 

2)            Though BULLSEYE went for sampling directly from Nanoli Stud in December, 2013, all the charges of “conspiracy and sabotage” are yet to be proved and the Crime Branch investigation in respect of the F.I.R. filed by Tr.P.Shroff, is still in progress. It has been established in the enquiry that there is a close relationship between Tr.P.Shroff and the proprietor of Nanoli Stud and his horses often shuttle between Nanoli Stud and his yard. Also it was noted that Nanoli Stud had employed a farrier who was warned off by the RWITC Ltd. and he was one of the prime suspect of sabotage, as alleged by Tr.P.Shroff.

                                                                                                                         

3)            Tr.P.Shroff has stated that a private sample collected at the Stud Farm was sent by Nanoli Stud in November, 2013 which was clean and this strengthened his suspicion of sabotage, though the Rules of Racing do not take cognizance of the result of such private samples as there is no chain of custody under the control of the RWITC. Ltd.

 

4)            From the experience of past positives for the same drug of horses coming from Nanoli Stud, and in his own interest, Tr.P.Shroff ought to have had the sample of BULLSEYE tested by the Club’s accredited lab before she left Nanoli Stud and ensured her security before her sampling in Mumbai.

 

5)            The Stewards noted that as per the earlier decision of the Stewards which has been notified in the Club’s Racing Calendar, should the urine sample of any horse whenever tested, be reported positive for the presence of any anabolic steroid the Stewards would deal with such cases at their absolute discretion in the most stringent manner. The Stewards also noted that even as per the Calendar Notification dated 24th September, 2008 concerning “Sampling of Racing Stock at the Stud Farms” states “in case of a positive, the same penalties will be applicable to the Trainer as are published in the Racing Calendar for the breach of norms for Random Sampling of horses in Training” and hence the responsibility for the horse which had specifically gone for summering remained with Tr.P.Shroff. The Stewards further noted that at various meeting of the Turf Authorities of India starting from November, 11, 2013 and recommended unanimously by the Chairmen of the Turf Authorities of India and by the Veterinary Commission of India, and not objected to by the RWITC Ltd. Stewards at any stage a two year ban on a trainer is applicable on the finding of an anabolic steroid in the sample of any horse in training.

 

6)            The Stewards were seized of the fact that as per Calendar Notification “all horses coming from Stud Farm/s after a spelling/summering sting should be Compulsorily sampled on arrival at the Pune/Mumbai Race course” and that in case of horse being found positive for anabolic steroids or sex hormones, the Stewards would deal with such cases in the most stringent manner at their absolute discretion.

 

Taking into view the ongoing police investigation and that certain facts had surfaced which threw some doubts, and considering the police report filed in the bail application in court dated 22nd May, 2014 of Mr.Deepak Popat, the Stewards took everything into consideration and revised their decision. The police investigation is till going on and not concluded and the Stewards are awaiting the final report in the matter.

 

Notwithstanding all these facts, taking an overall view and taking into view the submissions made by Tr.P.Shroff, the Stewards took a very lenient view and decided as under :-

 

1)   To fine him a sum of Rs.1,00,000/-.

 

2)    For a period of three years starting from 29th October, 2014 he will not be allowed to take charge of any race horse from any Stud Farm except 2-year-olds from stud farms or his other horses leaving and returning from another racing centre.

 

Tr.P.Shroff is at liberty to continue with his investigation and should he present irrefutable fresh evidence in support of his claim of sabotage, the Stewards may reconsider the matter at their sole discretion.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT  OF dk b f “BULLSEYE” WHO WAS COMPULSORILY SAMPLED ON 6TH DECEMBER, 2013 FOR THE PRESENCE OF ANABOLIC STEROIDS AND SEX HORMONES SINCE THE FILLY HAD ARRIVED TO TRAINER P.SHROFF FROM THE STUD FARM – MATTER REFERRED BACK TO THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB BY THE BOARD OF APPEAL – DE-NOVO ENQUIRY :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meetings held on 28th July, 2014, 27th August, 2014, 17th September, 2014 and 28th October, 2014 considered the above matter.

 

The Stewards enquired into the matter and applied their minds to the entire facts and circumstances of the case and considered all papers, proceedings, reports and records including the documents submitted by Dr.Ravinder Reddy and heard Mr.K.N.Dhunjibhoy and Dr.Ravinder Reddy in the matter at length. Taking into account that there have been several cases in the past also of horses testing positive of Anabolic Steroid from Nanoli Stud Farm, and in order to safeguard the interest of the sport of racing and the reputation of the R.W.I.T.C., Ltd. as also the interest of M/s Nanoli Stud and Agricultural Farms, the Stewards felt that it would be prudent not to allow the to and fro movement of all racing horses between R.W.I.T.C., Ltd. and M/s Nanoli Stud and Agricultural Farms for a period of three years. However, in the absence of any specific Rule in the Rules of Racing of the Club or any norm published in the Racing Calendar under which the Stewards could charge a stud farm, they decided not to initiate any action against Nanoli Stud and Agricultural Farms in the subject case.

 

However, Mr.K.N.Dhunjibhoy was requested that in order to safeguard the interest of the sport of racing and the reputation of the R.W.I.T.C., Ltd. as also the interest of M/s Nanoli Stud and Agricultural Farms, he may voluntarily stop the to and fro movement of his horses between Nanoli Stud & Agricultural Farms to the R.W.I.T.C. Ltd. Mr.K.N.Dhunjibhoy stated that on his own he has not moved any of his horses to and fro between Nanoli Stud & Agricultural Farms to the R.W.I.T.C.,Ltd. since last few months, and will continue this till police investigation is concluded.

RE :  ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT  OF dk b g “ZODIACE” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.154 – THE S.A.POONAWALLA MULTI-MILLION (GR.2) RUN ON SUNDAY, 21ST SEPTEMBER, 2014 - - IN CHARGE OF TRAINER DALLAS TODYWALLA :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 20th November, 2014 interviewed Tr.Dallas Todywalla regarding the positive report dated 30th October, 2014  from LGC, New Market, UK, in respect of the sample of urine taken from his charge ZODIAC (Placed Third) in Race No.154 – The Poonawalla Multi-Million (Gr.2) run on 21st September, 2014 which read as under :-

 

 “THE CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS WAS PERFORMED USING DOCUMENTED LGC METHODS, WHICH IDENTIFIED THE PRESENCE OF BUTORPHANOL, XYLAZINE, PENTAZOCINE, FLUNIXIN AND BETAMETHASONE IN THE URINE SAMPLE”

 

The Sr.V.O. informed the Stewards that after the race ZODIAC had showed symptoms of distress in the sampling yard and was treated by the Veterinarians of the Equine Hospital with the following drugs as reported by the Equine Hospital :-

 

“BUTORPHANAOL, XYLAZINE, FLUNIXIN, BETAMETHASONE, CALCIUM, BOROGLUCONATE and RINGERS LACTATE”.   PENTAZONE was not part of medication of the emergency treatment given to ZODIAC

 

At this meeting, Trainer Dallas Todywalla was handed over a copy of the above report and was requested to inform the Sr. Veterinary Officer of the Club, in writing, within five days of opening the envelope that identifies the sample, i.e. latest by 25th November, 2014, whether he wished to send the split sample to The Hong Kong Jockey Club Lab or L C H, France, for Confirmatory analysis and also the name of the witnessing analyst, if any, failing which the Stewards shall treat the report of the primary laboratory as conclusive.

 

Tr.Dallas Todywalla was asked to conduct his own enquiries and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.V.O. of the Club, as and when he would be required to.
