RACE DAY REPORTS


FALSE RAILS WERE PLACED 9 METRES WIDE AFTER THE WINNING POST TILL 1000 METRES AND THEREAFTER 3 METRES WIDE UPTO THE WINNING POST.  THE STARTING GATES FOR ALL THE RACES WERE POSITIONED AS MEASURED BY THE RODOMETER, EXCEPT THE SECOND RACE (2400 METRES) WHERE THE STARTING GATE WAS POSITIONED AT 2416 METRES.

 

RACE NO.1(190) – THE  N.K.PUDUMJEE TROPHY (DIV.I): (About)  1200 Metres – 8 Runners – NO REPORT

 

RACE NO.2(191) – THE F.A. & K.A. IRANI TROPHY : (About) 2400 Metres – 4 Runners

 

When NEGOTIATOR (Neeraj Rawal) was brought into the Parade Ring, Jockey Neeraj Rawal observed that the saddle on his mount was not his. On enquiring, it was found that the saddle of MYLA ROSE, runner in the 3rd Race, was picked up by the employee of Trainer Dallas Todywalla and applied on NEGOTIATOR. Jockey Sandeep Jadhav and Trainer Dallas Todywalla have been fined Rs.2,000/- each.

 

RACE NO.3(192) – THE  INDICTMENT TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – 5 Runners

 

MOON SHADOW (Dashrath Singh) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Mansoor Shah has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey D.K.Ashish (FALCON) reported the loss of whip near 150 metres. This being the second occasion of him losing the whip during the current Pune Meeting, Jockey D.K.Ashish has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

An Enquiry has been initiated into the poor performance of FALCON and a report will be submitted later. 

 

After the race, MYLA ROSE was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind pastern. FALCON was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(193) – THE  GREY MAGIC TROPHY (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres – 14 Runners

 

SUNSHINE HEART (A.Sandesh) began awkwardly at the start.

 

SEACAUCUS (R.Shelar) lugged-in for a brief period in the straight. Trainer Damodaran D.Pillai has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

EARTH ORBIT (S.P.Ranjane) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

After the race, BETHLEHEM was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that EARTH ORBIT was examined at the stables in the morning on 12/10/2014 and found to be sore by the left fore leg. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

ADDITIONAL REPORT/S:

 

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “ESSENSE OF LOVE” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.162 – THE PHOENIX RESIDENCES TURF CHALLENGE (DIV.II) RUN ON 27TH SEPTEMBER, 2014 AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS RUNS DURING THE PUNE MEETING 2014 – TRAINER H.J.ANTIA

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 11th October, 2014 considered Note from the Stipendiary Stewards on the above subject and interviewed Trainer H.J.Antia.  The Stewards considered all the submissions made by Trainer H.J.Antia and also considered all papers, documents and proceedings as regards the captioned matter. The Stewards having deliberated the matter at length were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer H.J.Antia and held him guilty of contravening the Calendar Notification Re : Comparative Performance of Horses winning within a short span of time”. The Stewards noted that prior to Race No.162, ESSENCE OF LOVE raced four times during the current Pune Meeting and in the last three runs had finished more than 20 lengths behind the winner and thereafter showed sudden marked improvement to win Race No.162 on 27th September, 2014 in a short span of thirteen days. Also, Trainer H.J.Antia admitted before the Stewards that the horse had shown sudden marked improvement for which he had no explanation to offer, except stating that he had not fancied the horse and this win had surprised him. The Stewards also noted Trainer H.J.Antia’s past record. However, keeping in mind that the horse was at longer odds, the Stewards took a lenient view in the matter and decided :-

 

a)       To suspend his Trainer’s license for 26th October, 2014;

b)       To fine him a sum of Rs.25,000/-.
