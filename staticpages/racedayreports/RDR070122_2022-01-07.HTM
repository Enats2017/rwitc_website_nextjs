RACE NO.1(36) – THE LADY MOURA PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  10 -

 

SUFIYAH (Bhawani Singh, dr.7) jumped out slowly losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, POWER OF NEATH (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2) jumped outwards at the same time LAGERTHA (Mudassar Nazzar, dr.3) jumped inwards and they made contact thereafter, HILAD (App. A Prakash, dr.4) shifted in thereby inconveniencing LAGERTHA (Mudassar Nazar, dr.3).

 

Approaching the 900 metres, POWER OF NEATH (Neeraj Rawal) shifted out and went across FLOWER POWER (App. P. S. Kaviraj) who had to be checked. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

It was observed that passing the 700 metres, the reins of Jockey Mudassar Nazar (LAGERTHA) had broken and as such he lost control. After the race, on inspecting the equipment, it was found to be in serviceable condition. However, Jockey Mudassar Nazar has been asked to be more cautious with his riding gear.

 

Approaching the 400 Metres LAGERTHA (Mudassar Nazar) shifted in thereby inconveniencing WILD FIRE (App. Aniket Karande) in view of the incident mentioned above no action has been initiated other than noting the same.

 

From the 350 metres SUFIYAH (Bhawani Singh) was observed to be inclined outwards despite the riders efforts thereby carrying LA PEREGRINA  (M.Ayaar) outwards.  After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh reported that his mount SUFIYAH hung-out in the straight. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the improve performance of UNTITLED (D. R. Shubham) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, SFIYAH and POWER OF NEATH were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(37) – THE TASAWWUR PLATE  - DIVISION II: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  11

 

BRAVE EAGLE (App. Kirtish Bhagat, dr.2) was slow to begin at the start.

 

At the start, DAWNSTAR (Hanumant Singh, dr.7) jumped outwards and went across POWER OF THOR (P. S. Kaviraj, dr.8), who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, SHADOWS (S. A Amit) shifted out under urgings thereby inconveniencing DAWNSTAR (Hanumant Singh). Jockey S. A. Amit has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 1200 metres, BRAVE EAGLE (App. Kirtish Bhagat) shied and made contact with the rails due to which DAWNSTAR (Hanumant Singh) who was racing on his haunches had to check and drop back.

 

Approaching the 100 metres, SHADOWS (S. A Amit) weekend and dropped back due to which CHEZZA (S. Zervan) racing on its haunches had to be checked and thereafter S.Zervan (CHEZZA) was unable to ride out to complete advantage due to lack of room.

 

Passing the 100 metres, SUSSING (D.R.Shubham) leaned inwards thereby tightening MEMORABLE MOMENT (Vishal N. Bunde). Jockey D.R.Shubham has been cautioned.

 

App. P. S. Kaviraj (POWER OF THOR) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit he was also observed to have used the same in an improper manner this being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting he has been fined Rs.3000/- for excessive use of the whip and further fined Rs.2000/-improper use of whip on his mount POWER OF THOR.

 

After the race, Trainer Narendra Lagad reported that his Apprentice Jockey P. S. Kaviraj (POWER OF THOR) was not ready when the start was effected. The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking into it and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

An enquiry has been initiated in running and riding of SUSSING (D. R. Shubham) and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, SUSSING and DAWNSTAR were trotted and found to be sound.

 

After the race, BRAVE EAGLE was observed to have lost its left fore shoe and CHEZZA lost its right fore shoe.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF (DREAMS, DR.2) IN RACE NO.65 THE CONRAD PUNE CUP RUN ON 28THNOVEMBER 2021.

 

Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy vide her letter dated 17th December 2021, informed that her instructions were to jump and sit back, save the filly as much as possible, and once he turned for home to ride her out and do his best further, she also explained the reason for the change of tactics.

 

Jockey T. S. Jodha on being questioned on 20th December 2021 with regards his riding stated that his instruction were to take a good jump and sit behind try to come from the back and do his best, and if you are not in the fighting line not to punish the mare. To a specific question he said that if you are in a winning position only then to use the whip. To another question he confirmed that he was well aware of the rule that all riders have to do their best so as to obtain the best possible position in the race.

 

Race in question was viewed and it was impressed upon him that his handling in the straight was feeble; race no. 35 the mare’s previous race where the mare was ridden by App. P. Shinde was viewed and having watched both the races Jockey T. S. Jodha was informed that the Stipendiary Stewards would like to question him in the presence of the trainer.

 

On 24th December 2021 Trainer Nazzak B. Chenoy and T. S. Jodha were questioned into the captioned subject matter and the Trainer was informed that having gone through her letter the Stipendiary Stewards had taken objection to the riding efforts of Jockey T. S. Jodha especially in the straight. Thereafter the race in question was viewed and the trainer reiterated that she had asked him not to punish the mare unnecessarily. Jockey T. S. Jodha on being question in the presence of the trainer stated that the filly was not in form and even if he had ridden flat out she would have may be run a length or two better, to a specific question he concurred with the Stipendiary Stewards and agreed that it was his mistake and would be careful in the future.

 

Having heard the submissions made and viewing the race, the Stipendiary Stewards are not satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Jockey T.S. Jodha he has been fine Rs.5,000/- for failing to persevere with his mount.

*****

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF (BOTERO) (DR.4) IN RACE NO.54 THE GLASNOST PLATE RUN ON 21st NOVEMBER 2021.

 

As Jockey C. Umesh was unavailable due to his riding engagements he was contacted over video call on 29th December 2021 and on being questioned he said that his instructions were to take a good jump, try and go to the front, and coming to the straight to do your best.

 

The race in question was viewed and on being asked for his observations Trainer P. Shroff stated that he has followed the instructions and after the race the Jockey informed him that being the first run and also that he was weakening he had not pressed hard on him when asked for his observations Trainer P. Shroff stated that the Jockey had followed his instructions and had kept niggling and riding in the straight.

 

Having heard the submissions made and viewing the race, it was impressed on both the Trainer and Jockey that the Stipendiary Stewards take a strong objection to the Jockey’s riding efforts in the straight. However, considering that it was the first run of the gelding Jockey C. Umesh has been fined Rs.10,000/- for failing to persevere with his mount and Trainer P. Shroff has been fined Rs.10,000/- for tacit approval.
