RACE NO.1(55) – THE JAIDEV PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  10

 

At the start, FIDATO (Anthony Raj S., dr.4) jumped inwards onto MAJORELLA BLUE (T. S. Jodha, dr.3).

 

Passing the 1200 metres, due to a tightly pack field MOONSHOT (Prashant P. Dhebe) had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 800 metres, MAJORELLA BLUE (T. S. Jodha) leaned inwards onto TIME (P. Trevor) who had to be steadied. Jockey T. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 400 metres, TIME (P. Trevor) shifted out due to which MAJORELLA BLUE (T. S. Jodha) was tightened and had to be checked and thereafter carried LOVE WARRIOR (P. S. Chouhan) outwards and had to be steadied. After questioning, Jockey P. Trevor has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

App. M. S. Deora (SIM SIM) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his fourth offence during the current Pune Meeting 2022, the matter has been reported to the Stewards of the Club for such action as they may deem fit.

 

After the race, Jockey Antony Raj S. (FIDATO) reported that the saddle on his mount had shifted forward soon after the start. This being the third instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, a statutory penalty of Rs.10,000/- has been imposed on Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait.

 

After the race, LOVE WARRIOR was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(56) – THE SPEARHEAD PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners -  7

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor (DYF) reported that the saddle on his mount had slipped back during the race. This being the first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, a statutory penalty of Rs.2,000/- has been imposed on Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha.

 

After the race, LADIDA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine hospital reported that LADIDA had a left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.3(57) – THE SPARK OF GOLD PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  15

 

Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been fined Rs.1,000/- for accepting his charge COUNT THE WINS in this race whilst the gelding was on Veterinary Ban. The gelding was struck out at the Acceptance stage.

 

After being stalled, CAPRIFLA (Antony Raj S., dr.10) played up and casted himself in an awkward position resulting in the saddle got displaced and considering that the field was ready to dispatch the gelding was withdrawn before coming under Starter’s order. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

DALASAN (Amyn Merchant, dr.5) jumped out slowly and thereafter did not raise an effective gallop. In view of above and a similar behavior of the gelding in the past, Trainer S. Waheed has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Starter, before next accepting to race.

 

SONG SONG BLUE (S. A. Amit, dr.3) and ANGELS TRUMPET (N. B. Kuldeep, dr.7) jumped out slowly losing about 3 lengths and 2 lengths respectively at the start.

 

It was observed that soon after the start, JUICED (N. S. Parmar, dr.14) had travelled wide thereby carrying CUPIDO (K. Nazil) along. After the start, on questioning, Jockey N. S. Parmer stated that his mount has a tendency to get very keen whilst racing in close proximity with the other runners. As such, he had tried to keep him away in the initial part of the race. Having heard the explanation of Jockey N. S. Parmar and not being entirely satisfied with the same, as such he has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, CHARMING STAR (J. Chinoy) leaned inwards thereby tightening and inconveniencing ANGELS TRUMPET (N. B. Kuldeep) thereafter once again leaned in and made contact with ZACAPA (C. S. Jodha). Jockey J. Chinoy has been severely reprimanded.

 

Passing the 150 metres, ZACAPA (C. S. Jodha) shifted out and made contact with CHARMING STAR (J. Chinoy). Jockey C. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey N. B. Kuldeep (ANGELS TRUMPET) reported that the saddle on his mount had slipped back during the race. This being the first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, a statutory penalty of Rs.2,000/- has been imposed on Trainer Subhag Singh.

 

RACE NO.4(58) – THE IDAR GOLD TROPHY (GRADE 3): (About) 2400 Metres –Runners- 5

 

After the race, A STAR IS BORN & CAPRISCA were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(59) – THE SEPTEMBER HANDICAP: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners -  12

 

At the start, FRANKIE (C. Umesh, dr.1) jumped inwards brushing the front gates.

 

At the start, KIEFER (Antony Raj S., dr.4) jumped outwards thereby bumping and forcing LIAM (P. Trevor, dr.5) onto IT’S MY TIME (Suraj Narredu, dr.6) who was inclined inwards as a result of which IT’S MY TIME shifted out at the same time HOOVES OF THUNDER (Yash Narredu, dr.9) jumped inwards onto STUNNING VISUAL (S. A. Amit, dr.8) causing the latter to be steadied thereafter HOOVES OF THUNDER shifted out and went across FLASHING FAMOUS (S. G. Prasad, dr.10) causing the latter to check and change course.

 

Passing the 400 metres, KARYNA (Bhawani Singh) shifted in under urgings and went across GOLDEN KINGDOM (C. S. Jodha) who had to be steadied. As such Jockey Bhawani Singh has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha (GOLDEN KINGDOM) reported that the saddle on his mount had slipped back during the race. This being the second instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, a statutory penalty of Rs.5,000/- has been imposed on Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha.

 

After the race, LIAM was trotted and found to be sound but had injury on right fore fetlock and HOOVES OF THUNDER had a gum injury.

 

The Equine hospital reported that FRANKIE had a left eye injury.

 

RACE NO.6(60) – THE C. A. KUTS TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 12

 

CELLINI (P. Trevor, dr.5) was reluctant to stall. Trainer Bezan Chenoy has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BABY BAZOOKA (App. M. S. Deora, dr.6) took an awkward fly jump brushing the haunches of RAMBLER (S Zervan, dr.7) who inturn shifted out onto PEPPER (App. Haridas Gore, dr.8) at the same time SAN SALVATORE (K. Nazil, dr.9) jumped inwards and made contact with PEPPER.

 

Soon after the start, TOMORROWS DREAMS (Vishal N. Bunde) shifted in onto GINSBURG (C. S. Jodha) who had to be steadied. Jockey Vishal N. Bunde has been severely cautioned.

 

Soon after the start, SPRING GROVE (Prashant P. Dhebe) shifted out under urgings and went across EXCLUSIVE (T. S. Jodha) causing the latter to be checked.  Jockey Prashant P. Dhebe has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 350 metres, PEPPER (App. Haridas Gore) shifted in under urgings and went across THE AWAKENING (Yash Narredu). As such, App. Haridas Gore has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Passing the 300 metres, GINSBURG (C. S. Jodha) was observed to have weakened and thereafter shifted out under pressure due to which TOMORROWS DREAMS (Vishal N. Bunde) racing on its haunches had to check and change course as a result inconveniencing SAN SALVATORE (K. Nazil). After the race, on questioning, Jockey C. S. Jodha confirmed the same and the filly was subjected to Veterinary examination and found to be sound. However, Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race and to report in case he finds anything unusual.

 

After the race, Jockey K. Nazil (SAN SALVATORE) reported the loss of whip passing the 400 metres. This being the first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, TOMORROWS DREAMS was trotted and found to be sound and was observed to have lost its left hind shoe.

 

The Equine hospital reported that LORD VADER had a right eye injury (sore) and TOMORROWS DREAMS had a right eye injury.

 

RACE NO.7(61) – THE AZZURRO PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  14

 

At the start, TARZAN (N. B. Kuldeep, dr.5) jumped outwards and at the same time NORD (S. G. Prasad, dr.6) jumped inwards and they made contact.

 

At the start, WINSTAR (T. S. Jodha, dr.8) was slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, ZUKOR (App. M. S. Deora) shifted in under urgings and bumped A GOOD CHANCE (Amyn Merchant) who was inclined outwards and in the process ZUKOR and A GOOD CHANCE had lost their strides. After questioning both the Jockeys, App. M. S. Deora has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, ZUKOR, TURMERIC TOWER & SUITED ACES were trotted and found to be sound.
