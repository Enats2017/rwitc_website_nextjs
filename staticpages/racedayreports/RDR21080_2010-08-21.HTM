RACE NO 1 (78) - THE  ASPIRING STAR PLATE :  (About) 2000 Metres. Runners - 4

 

DRAMA QUEEN (App. P Trevor) reared up and fell on the rails in front of the Grand Stand. The filly was examined by the Club’s Sr.V.O. after reaching the start and was found to have bleeding injuries of left hind hock and from muzzle. As such, DRAMA QUEEN was certified unfit to race and hence was withdrawn on veterinary grounds, before coming under the Starter’s Order.

 

RACE NO 2  (79) - THE  HAWKE BAY PLATE : (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 8

 

THE REBEL (Neeraj Rawal) jumped out throwing its head up.

 

URBAN WARRIOR (S. Zervan) hung out throughout the race. Since the gelding had bled, no action was initiated in the matter.

 

Trainer Vinay Lagad was questioned regarding the improved performance of his charge FESTIVE LIGHT. He stated that she has jumped out well as compared to her previous starts where she has always jumped out slowly. He also said that she has bad joints and she has soundness problems and has just come right. On showing him the previous start of the horse in Race No 44, Tr.Vinay Lagad stated that the race was run on the monsoon track, she has preferred the going today as it was run on the Main Track which is harder. Tr.Vinay Lagad was told that he has contravened the Calendar Notification of running a horse in a short span of time and showing vast improvement. Since the horse was available at longer odds and there was a change of the track, the explanation tendered by Trainer Vinay Lagad has been noted and no action has been taken against him apart from severely cautioning him for the future.

 

After the race, URBAN WARRIOR was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated period of 4 weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, CHOCOLATE was observed to have an abrasion on the right hind bulb of heel

 

RACE NO 3 (80) – THE  GIRONDE PLATE (DIV.II): (About)  1000 Metres.  Runners - 9

 

SURMOUNT (Vikram Jodha) lugged in throughout the race. The Sr.V.O. was requested to examine the gelding and he reported that SURMOUNT had returned sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

Jockey Y S Srinath (AQUALINA) reported that his filly was lugging-in in the straight. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

ALEUTIAN ZONE (Ishwar Pardeshi) was observed to have shifted out sharply about 100mts after the jump and caused BANANA BOY (Malcolm Kharadi) to be checked. Further, the filly  shifted in at about 500 metres, causing GLORIA ( H G Rathod ) to be checked by her rider. Jockey Ishwar Pardeshi  has been fined Rs.3000/- and further has been severely cautioned for the future for failing to exercise control over his mount. 

 

Jockey R Shelar (CLAIM TO FAME) has been severely cautioned for shifting in at about 800 metres.

 

After the race, ALEUTIAN ZONE was observed to have an abrasion on the left hind bulb of heel and BANANA BOY had lost its left fore plate. AQUALINA was found to be sound

 

RACE NO 4 (81) – THE RAMBUS PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1400 Metres. Runners - 8

 

Jockey Imran Chisty (DEAR HENRY) reported that nearing the Winning Post, APPALINA (A M Tograllu) came in and slightly bumped him. Jockey A M Tograllu has been fined Rs. 2000/- for failing to exercise control over his mount. Further, he has been severely cautioned for the future,

 

After the race, SINE QUA NON & DEAR HENRY were found to be sound.

RACE NO 6  (83) - THE  AKKASAHEB MAHARAJ TROPHY :  (About) 1200 Metres.   Runners - 10

RACE NO 8 (85) - THE  SPECIALIST PLATE:   (About)  1600 Metres. Runners - 11

 

INDIAN SUMMER (Shrikant Kamble), planted and took no part in the race. Since the gelding had behaved in a similar manner in its last outing, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter in two Mock Races, before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey S Zervan (BORN DESTINY) reported that at about 200 metres he was steadied running in between GREEN SIGNAL (Imran Chisty) who hung out on his inside and TELL ME MORE (Ladjadj Stephane) who was hanging in on his outside inspite of both  jockey’s efforts to correct their respective mounts. Trainer Dallas Todywalla & Narendra Lagad have been asked to take remedial measures.

 

TELL ME MORE (Ladjadj Stephane) further kept hanging-in in the straight in spite of Jockeys efforts and in the final 50 metres he was observed to have changed his whip hand to his left as he stated that he felt it would help his horse to spurt as he saw the outside horse closing in on him. This resulted in the horse further shifting in and in anticipation, GREEN SIGNAL (Imran Chisty) was steadied by his rider and shifted in onto RIDING FANTASY (Y S Srinath) who was racing on his inside along the rails, who had to be checked. Since TELL ME MORE has behaved similarly in the past and has been banned, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to show the gelding in a Mock Race to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards.

 

After the race, OCEAN WARRIOR was found to be sound, having an abrasion on the left hind fetlock. TELL ME MORE & GREEN SIGNAL were found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING OF RACE NO.27 – THE CHAKORI TROPHY RUN ON 23RD JULY, 2010:

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 21st August, 2010 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. They interviewed Trainers Dallas Todywalla and Faisal A.Abbas and Jockeys Dashrath Singh, S.Sunil and S.Zervan who was represented by Trainer P.Shroff, and having heard the submissions made and viewing the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with explanation tendered by Trainers Dallas Todywalla and Faisal A.Abbas and Jockeys S.Sunil and Dashrath Singh. In that, the manner in which this race was run, led the Stewards to suspect a collusion wherein one Jockey rode in a manner so as to benefit another horse. However, in the absence of concrete evidence, the Stewards decided to give them the benefit of a grave doubt in the subject case and to severely caution them for the future.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE INCIDENT OF FALL OF JOCKEYS C.RAJENDRA (CANDY FLOSS) & J.PRADEEP (ORION STAR) IN RACE NO.71 – THE SAINT EMILLION PLATE RUN ON 15TH AUGUST, 2010 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that nearing 200 metres, CANDY FLOSS (C.Rajendra) had changed course and shifted in and whilst doing so, CANDY FLOSS’ hind quarters touched RIO DEL RAY’s shoulder whereby CANDY FLOSS shied and ducked in and bumped the right hind quarters of WILD IMAGINATION (Dashrath Singh). Due to the above, WILD IMAGINATION came in contact with MACHO UNO (S.Sunil) who was racing on the inside and because of the impact, shifted out and caused interference to CANDY FLOSS  who was checked and lost balance, dislodging the rider. ORION STAR (J.Pradeep) who was racing just behind, collided into the fallen horse and also fell. The Stipendiary Stewards felt that ENTICEMENT (C.S.Jodha) who was racing in front, had also shifted out and tightened the field on the outside, however, the fall of horses could not be attributed to him alone and therefore considered this incident as accidental.

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on Saturday, 21st August, 2010 considered the above report and decided that no action was called for in the matter.
