RACE NO.1(47) – THE  HAMBURG AMBASSADOR CUP: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 7

 

At the start, MEGGIDO (Kavraj Singh, dr.5) jumped awkwardly inward and went on to AZADEGAN (Akash Agarwal, dr. 4).

 

App.Bhawani Singh (WORD OF HONOUR, dr.6) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for shifting in before the marker and in the process going across the field on his inside.

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that at the request of Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait, J’ADORE  was scoped after the race at the Equine Hospital and reported to be having EIPH (Grade III). The filly is not to race for the stipulated period of four weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ASHWA YODHAA was found to be sound, but having an abrasion at both hind pasterns.  J’ADORE, WORD OF HONOUR and MEGGIDO were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(48) – THE LUFTHANSA SENATOR CUP: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 3

 

Jockey Kavraj Singh (ROYAL PASSION) reported that his mount leaned inwards in the straight. Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

THE BIG BULL (S.Zervan) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer P.Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

RACE NO.3(49)  – THE INDO-GERMAN CHRISTMAS FEST CUP: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners – 5

 

Jockey Kavraj Singh (CAESARS STAR) has been fined Rs.1,000/- for using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount.

 

RACE NO.4(50) – THE  HDFC-ERGO TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 6

 

Near 700 metres, Q’E’D (Neeraj Rawal) was inconvenienced while racing on the inside of SPRING LOADED (Dashrath Singh).

 

Passing 400 metres CARIBBEAN LEGEND (Darren Williams) was inconvenienced as JEFFERSON (P.Trevor) shifted ground inwards.

 

After the race, CARIBBEAN LEGEND was found to be sound, but having a cut injury at the right hind cannon.  IRELAND was found to be sound, but having a gum injury.

 

RACE NO.5(51) – THE HANNOVER MESSE TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  12

 

At the start, FLYINGANGEL (Darren Williams, dr.11) jumped awkwardly inwards and made contact with PRESIDENT (Kavraj Singh, dr.10), who in turn went on to FIREPROOF (Neeraj Rawal, dr. 9).

 

At the start, KHALEEFA (App.Yash Narredu, dr.6) jumped out awkwardly and then was steadied when tightened.

 

Approaching 400 metres, FLYINGANGEL (Darren Williams) shifted ground inwards and in anticipation, MARTINA (S.Zervan) was steadied. 

 

Approaching 200 metres, PRESIDENT (Kavraj Singh) shifted ground inwards, due to which MARTINA (S.Zervan) was again steadied.

App.Yash Narredu (KHALEEFA) reported that his mount lugged out in the straight. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey S.Zervan (MARTINA) was reminded of his obligation to ride out his mount till past the Post.

 

After the race, KHALEEFA, PRESIDENT, ALETA and SAPORELLI were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(52) – THE  JUNGHEINRICH INDIA TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 13

 

At the request of Trainer H.J.Antia, MOUNTAIN SONG was examined at 1150 hours on 05/12/2014 and was found to be sore by the left fore leg. As such, the mare was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race. 

 

WIND CRAFT (S.G.Prasad) was restless in the stall. Trainer H.J.Antia has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting stalls, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, YOU’RE THE BOSS (App.Akshay Gaikwad, dr.13) jumped out awkwardly and brushed the gates, due to which the Jockey lost his right stirrup iron which he was able to regain near 800 metres.

 

At the start, BLUE BAY (Kishore Kadam, dr.11) shifted in and made contact with SANSANI (A.Ashhad Asbar, dr.10). Jockey Kishore Kadam has been cautioned.

 

At the start, CAPILANO (App.Bhawani Singh, dr.12) shifted in and went across BLUE BAY (Kishore Kadam, dr.11) and SANSANI (A.Ashhad Asbar,dr.10). App.Bhavani Singh has been reprimanded.

 

Near 200 metres, SANSANI (A Ashhad Asbar) scrapped the rails.

 

BLUE BAY (Kishore Kadam) was held up for a clear run in the final few strides.

 

After the race, CAPILANO was observed to have a cut injury at the left hock and ANTARTICA ZONE had lost its right hind plate. YOU’RE THE BOSS, SANSANI and BLUE BAY were found to be sound.

At the start, DHANYAVAAD (Vikash Tamang. dr.9) and FABRIZIO (Malcolm Kharadi, dr 10) jumped awkwardly outwards. BEYOND HORIZONS (App.Bhawani Singh, dr.11) jumped awkwardly outwards and made contact with NIGELLA (App.S.J.Sunil, dr 12) wherein BEYOND HORIZONS was steadied and lost position.

RE :  CONFIRMATORY ANALYSIS REPORT IN RESPECT  OF b g “STROGANOFF” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.221 – THE GOEL GANGA GROUP SUPER SPRINT RUN ON SATURDAY, 18TH OCTOBER, 2014 -  IN CHARGE OF TRAINER FAISAL A.ABBAS :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 7th December, 2014 interviewed Trainer Faisal A.Abbas regarding the Confirmatory Analysis Report dated 5th December, 2014 from LCH, France, in respect of the sample of urine taken from STROGANOFF (Winner) in Race No.221 – The Goel Ganga Group Super Sprint run on Saturday, 18th October, 2014 which read as under :-

 

“…..It is hereby confirmed that the sample, which has been analysed as set out above, contains BOLDENONE”  

 

A copy of the above report was handed over to Tr.Faisal A.Abbas and he was asked to continue his own enquiries and to lead evidence, if any, to prove mitigating circumstances, within 15 days and to also attend the enquiry to be held by the Stipendiary Stewards and the Sr.Veterinary Officer of the Club, as and when he would be required to.

 

As per the Medication Rules,  the Stewards of the Club decided to disqualify STROGANOFF (Winner) in Race No.221 – The Goel Ganga Group Super Sprint run on Saturday, 18th October, 2014 and to revise the order of placing of the above race as under :-

FLANKER                             -               FIRST;                                 CAPTAIN ABDON   -             THIRD;

ZAMBIAN                              -               SECOND;                             COLD PLAY                         -             FOURTH

 

Further, considering STROGANOFF has been reported positive for an Anabolic Steroid, which is banned by the Government of India except for therapeutic use with prior permission from the Club’s Sr.Veterinary Officer, the Stewards of the Club decided to ban STROGANOFF from racing up to 7th June, 2015.
