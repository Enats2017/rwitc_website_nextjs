RAILS WERE PLACED AT THEIR ORIGINAL POSITION

 

RACE NO.1 (122) THE KUMAR R. DALAL GOLD TROPHY : (ABOUT) 1600 METRES- RUNNERS - 5

 

After being stalled ,ZAFFERANO (P.Trevor , dr 2) was fractious and thereafter lost about three lengths at the start.  As such, Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha  has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 400 meters, OPUS DEI (Akshay Kumar) drifted out thereby carrying ZAFFERANO (P.Trevor) outwards. Jockey Akshay Kumar has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey Yash Narredu (KINGS GAMBIT) reported loss of whip passing the 300 meters. This being his first instance in the current Mumbai Meeting 2025 – 26. As such, no action has been initiated however, he has been cautioned. 

 

After the race, the Sr V.O reported the following :

 

FORTUNATE SON was trotted and found to be sound.

 

ZAFFERANO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO. 2 (123) THE MYSTICAL TROPHY : (ABOUT) 2400 METRES - RUNNERS - 7

 

It was observed in the paddock that Jockey David Allan sported the wrong racing colors on his mount CACCINI. The same was bought to the attention of the Stipendiary Stewards, as Trainer Karthik Ganapathy could not furnish the correct  racing colors , Jockey David Allan raced in the Emergency Colors i,e. ALL BLACK. As such, Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been fined Rs 1,000/- for his lapse and the Clerk of the Scales has been cautioned.  

 

Soon after the start, ALLEZ L’ETOILE (A.Prakash) shifted in and went across LITTLE JOHN (P.Trevor) who was marginally slow to begin. Jockey A. Prakash has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 450 meters, HARRISON (Aditya Waydande) leaned in thereby tightening BLACK THUNDER (Prashant  P. Dhebe) causing the latter to be steadied. App Aditya Waydande has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 300 meters ALLEZ L’ETOILE (A. Prakash) drifted out under pressure thereby carrying DIEGO GARCIA (Suraj Narredu) outwards. After the race, on questioning Jockey A. Prakash stated that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight despite his efforts. As such, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

 

Jockey Suraj Narredu was observed to have used the whip in an improper manner on his mount DIEGO GARCIA. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2025-26. As such ,he has been fined Rs. 3,000/-

 

After the race, the Sr VO reported the following :

 

DIEGO GARCIA was observed to have a gum injury.

 

HARRISON was observed to have a gum injury.

 

LITTLE JOHN was trotted and was found to be sound but was observed to have injuries on both its hind pasterns.

 

CHICAGO CHIMES was trotted and found to be sore by left fore. As such, the gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

RE:MERCY PETITION LODGED BY JOCKEY A.SANDESH  IN RESPECT OF THE SUSPENSION IMPOSED ON HIM BY THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB FOR A PERIOD OF 12 DAYS I,E. W.E.F. 21ST JANUARY 2026 TO 29TH MARCH 2026 (BOTH DAYS INCLUSIVE) AND TO FINE A SUM OF RS. 1,00,000/- - RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “BEZALEL” (DRAWN - 8) IN RACE NO. 42 – THE SYNTHESIS TROPHY (DIV. II) (DIST. 1400 MTRS.) RUN ON 21ST DECEMBER 2025 :

 

The Stewards at their meeting held on 22nd February, 2026, having considered the submissions made by Jockey A. Sandesh, after due delebration, decided to revise the period of the suspension of his Jockeys License and accordingly his license shall now remain suspended upto Sunday, 8th March, 2026.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “FOXY” (DRAWN 04) IN RACE NO.112 - THE BYRAM N. JEEJEEBHOY TROPHY - DIST 1000 MTRS : RUN ON 12TH FEBRUARY 2026 :

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 22nd February 2026, having heard the submissions made by App S. Siddharth, Master Trainer P. Shroff and Trainer Behram Cama, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race in question, having deliberated the matter at length. The Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. S. Siddharth. In that, he was instructed to sit in 3rd, 4th position and do his best in the straight. However, considering that he had received an interference soon after the start, he made no efforts to improve his position and sit as instructed despite having ample opportunity to do so, thereby placing his mount at a disadvantage. Moreover Trainer Behram Cama and his Master Trainer P. Shroff also expressed their dissatisfaction with App. S. Siddharth. However, keeping in mind that he is an upcoming Apprentice, the Stewards decided by majority to suspend his Jockey’s License for two Race Days i,e. 7th and 8th March, 2026 for incompetent riding.
