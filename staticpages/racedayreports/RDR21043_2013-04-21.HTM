RACE NO.1(348)  – THE TRISTAR PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 8  

 

At the start, MONARCHOS (C.S.Jodha) jumped awkwardly.

 

RACE NO.2(349)  – THE  CURSETJEE DHUNJISHAW TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 3

 

BIZARRO (P.Trevor) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer C.D.Katrak has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

RACE NO.3(350) – THE MADRAS RACE CLUB TROPHY: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners – 4

 

HURRICANE WIND (A.Sandesh) stumbled at the start.

 

OKAVANGO (D.K.Ashish) was inclined to lug-in in the straight. Since the gelding was reported to be sore after the race, no action has been initiated in the matter.

 

After the race, OKAVANGO was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, MONTEZUMA was observed to have lost its left hind plate. MASEEHA was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(351) –THE  JIMMY BHARUCHA  TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 6

 

At the start, PRESENCE (A.Sandesh) brushed the Gates as the colt jumped out and shifted inwards and made contact with KNIGHT OF KE (Kishore Kadam).

 

PRESENCE (A.Sandesh) lugged-in in the straight. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the colt.  

 

RACE NO.5(352) – THE  SWEET DREAM PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners – 9

 

Approaching 800 metres, BY THE WAY (A.Ashhad Asbar) was tightened as KRYPTON (A.Sandesh) leaned inwards due the presence of NIGELLA (Akshay) on its outside.

 

SPEEDING (D.K.Ashish) lugged out in the straight.  Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

Jockey T.S.Jodha (VIVA ZAPATA) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit. This being the third offence with regard to use of the whip, the matter was reported to the Stewards.

 

The Stewards interviewed Jockey T.S.Jodha and having heard him and viewing the video recording of the race, held him guilty of using the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. Therefore, the Stewards decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2012/13, it will be granted to him, however, his license will stand suspended we.f. 18th July 2013 to 19 July 2013 (permitted to ride work). 

 

The Sr.V.O. reported that after the race MOON RAKER was observed to be returning sore from the right fore leg. First Aid treatment was given and the gelding was sent to the Equine Hospital by Horse Ambulance for further management. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, VIVA ZAPATA was found to be sore by the right fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s VO before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SPEEDING was observed to have a cut injury at the left hind pastern and had lost its left fore plate. DAUGHTEROFDESTINY and KRYPTON were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(353) – THE  DOLLAR PRINCESS PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 8

 

STAYING ALIVE (I.Pasha) jumped out throwing its head.

 

Passing 800 metres, KING JULIEN (A.Sandesh) was tightened while racing on the inside of SHEER ROMANCE (D.K.Ashish) who lugged in at that stage. SHEER ROMANCE continued to lug in during the race. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

While straightening for home, ZIDAAN (P.Trevor) shifted out to make his bid and forced QUID PRO QUO (Neeraj Rawal) outwards, who was tightened on to HAWAIIAN PRINCESS (C.Rajendra). Jockey P.Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 200 metres, CREAM OF THE CROP (N.S.Parmar) shifted out and hampered the running of HAWAIIAN PRINCESS (C.Rajendra).

 

Jockey N.S.Parmar (CREAM OF THE CROP) reported the loss of whip, a few strides away from the Post.

 

Afte the race, STAYING ALIVE, SHEER ROMANCE and CREAM OF THE CROP were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.7(354) – THE  RACING JOURNALISTS TROPHY: (About) 1800 Mts – Runners – 10

 

At the start, LAVENDER BROWN (A Sandesh) jumped awkwardly.

 

Soon after the start, LAVENDER BROWN (A.Sandesh) was hampered for a run while racing on the inside of ZAMBEZI (D.K.Ashish) who shifted in at that stage. Jockey D.K.Ashish has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 400 metres, ZAMBEZI (D.K.Ashish) stumbled when the filly clipped the heels of STAR GAZING (P.Trevor).

 

After the race, LAVENDER BROWN and STAR GAZING were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(355) – THE  K.H.IRANI PLATE:  (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 16

 

At the start, PLATED (C.S.Jodha) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Near 800 metres, CAPRI (A.Sandesh) shifted in and carried PLAN B (Abhishek Habbu) inwards, who caused interference to TAJ E SHAHI (T.S.Jodha), who in turn went on to JOSEPHINE (I.Pasha). Jockey A.Sandesh has been fined Rs. 3,000/-.

 

After the race, MINSTREL SON was found to be sound.
