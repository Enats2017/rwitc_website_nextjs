RACE NO.1(78) – THE INGLENOOK PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  7

 

Passing the 800 metres, BIRKIN BLOWER (P. Trevor) leaned inwards thereby inconveniencing DESERT FIRE (App. Mustakim Alam). Jockey P. Trevor has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 500 metres, ONE WISH (R. Ajinkya) shifted out at the same time MENILLY (Dashrath Singh) leaned inwards thereby tightening AEGON (App. P. Shinde) racing between them. After questioning all concerned, Jockeys R. Ajinkay and Dashrath Singh have been Fined Rs.2,000/-each.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, BIRKIN BLOWER (P. Trevor) weakened and dropped back due to which DESERT FIRE (App. Mustakim Alam) racing on his haunches had to check and change course.

 

Passing the winning post, AEGON (App. P. Shinde) collapsed and died on the way back even after immediate resuscitation  procedure and treatment.

 

After the race, BIRKIN BLOWER was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, JUDY BLUE EYES was trotted and found to be sound at the stables.

 

The Equine hospital reported that ONE WISH had right eye injury.

 

The Equine hospital reported that MENILLY had left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.2(79) – THE JEFFERSON PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres – Runners -  5

 

Jockey Akshay Kumar (MOUNT SINAI) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Pune Meeting 2022, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, KAMARIA had cut injury on left hind fetlock.

 

RACE NO.3(80) – THE SURAIYA & MASOOM MASTER TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  7

 

At the start, THE PIANIST (Dashrath Singh, dr.3) jumped inwards and went across CIPHER (T. S. Jodha, dr.2) who was marginally slow to begin and thereafter made contact with COGNOSCO (A. Sandesh, dr.1).

 

At the start, DOWSABEL (S. Saqlain, dr.6) jumped inwards and brushed the haunches of SILVER STEPS (App. P. Vinod, dr.5) thereafter SILVER STEPS shifted in at the same time SANDMAN (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.4)  leaned outwards and they made contact.

 

Passing 300 metres, CIPHER (T. S. Jodha) shifted out under urgings onto SANDMAN (Vishal N. Bunde) causing the later to be steadied. Jockey T. S. Jodha has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, Jockey Vishal N. Bunde (SANDMAN) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight, despite his efforts. Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh (THE PIANIST) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey C. S. Jodha (RON) reported that the Cross Nose Band on his mount broke immediately after the start. On examination, it was found that the said equipment was in a good condition. However, Trainer Himmat Singh has been asked to be more cautioned in the future.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Saqlain (DOWSABEL) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SANDMAN & THE PIANIST were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(81) – THE SERUM INSTITUTE OF INDIA TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres –Runners- 7

 

At the start, CELLINI (App. P. Shinde, dr.1) jumped outwards and at the same time IMPERMANENCE (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.2) jumped inwards and they made contact.

 

At the start, DILBAR (App. A. S. Peter, dr.5) jumped inwards and at the same time TOMORROWS DREAMS (N. B. Kuldeep, dr.3) jumped outwards due to which RODRIGO (A. Sandesh, dr.4) drawn between them was inconvenienced.

 

Approaching the 600 metres, RODRIGO (A. Sandesh) leaned outwards at the same time   ESPERANZA (S. Zervan) shifted in thereby tightening DILBAR (App. A. S. Peter) causing the later to check and drop back, due to which ENLIGHTENED (P. Trevor) racing onto its haunches had to be checked. After questioning all concerned, Jockeys A. Sandesh has been fined Rs.3,000/- and Jockey S. Zervan is being reported to the stewards for such action as they may deem fit.

 

After the race, Jockey N. B. Kuldeep (TOMORROWS DREAMS, dr.3) reported that at the start his mount jumped outwards brushing the front gates due to which he lost his left stirrups which he regained shortly.

 

After the race, ESPERANZA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ENLIGHTENED & IMPERMANENCE were trotted and found to be sound.

 

The Equine hospital reported that TOMORROWS DREAMS had right eye injury.

 

RACE NO.5(82) – THE VILLOO C. POONAWALLA MILLION: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  7

 

HAWK OF THE WIND (S. J. Sunil, dr.3) jumped out awkwardly thereby losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Passing 200 metres, HAWK OF THE WIND (S. J. Sunil) shifted out thereby inconveniencing SWEET EMOTION (N. S. Parmar). Jockey S. J. Sunil has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been fined Rs.500/- for mounting Jockey A. Sandesh late on his charge GOLD KITE in the paddock while all other the horses were leaving the paddock.

 

After the race, SWEET EMOTION was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

The Equine hospital reported that HOUSE OF LORDS had left eye injury (sore).

 

The Equine hospital reported that SWEET EMOTION had left eye injury (sore).

 

RACE NO.6(83) – THE S. A. POONAWALLA MILLION (GRADE 3): (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 7

 

At the start, GREAT GUNS (R. Ajinkya, dr.3) jumped outwards and went across DANGEROUS (C. S. Jodha, dr.4) who was slow to begin and made contact with KALAMITSI (Y. S. Srinath, dr.5).

 

Jockey P. S. Chouhan (EMPEROR RODERIC) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Pune Meeting 2022, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, Trainer Narendra Lagad expressed his dissatisfaction into the riding of Jockey J Chinoy (GOLDEN NEIL) as he did not follow his riding instructions. An enquiry has been initiated into the riding of GOLDEN NEIL.

 

Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha has been fined Rs.500/- for bringing his charge DANGEROUS late in the paddock and further he has been fined Rs.500/- for mounting Jockey C. S. Jodha late in the paddock while all other horses were leaving the paddock.

 

After the race, EMPEROR RODERIC had cut injury on right hind pastern.

 

After the race, DANGEROUS was trotted and found to be sound. Further the filly was scoped and found to be EIPH (Gr.1).

 

RACE NO.7(84) – RACING ON PLAY.RWITC.COM PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners -  10

 

MARINE GIRL (D. R. Shubham) bolted off with the rider on the way to the start and was apprehended near 200 metres. Since the filly had almost run the entire course, Trainer Altaf Hussain was called and with his due concern the filly was withdrawn before coming under Starter’s order. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, SIERRA DELA PLATA (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2) jumped outwards and went across MY PRINCESS (S. Saqlain, dr.3) causing the later to be steadied.

 

Soon after the start, HAGIBIS (App. A. S. Peter) shifted in and carried KAITLAN (A. Sandesh) inwards due to which PRINIA (P. Trevor) racing on its haunches had to be checked and change course thereafter HAGIBIS further shifted in under urgings thereby tightening and inconveniencing KAITLAN. After questioning App. A. S. Peter and not being satisfied with his explanation, his Jockey’s licence has been suspended for one race day i.e. on Sunday, 9th October 2022 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

Passing the 600 metres, STARRY SPIRIT (Antony Raj S.) was observed to have dropped for no apparent reason and thereafter ran detached from the field. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to ensure that his charge puts up a reasonable performance in the future.

 

After the race, App. A. S. Peter (HAGIBIS) reported the loss of whip passing the 150 metres. This being the first instance during the current Pune Meeting 2022, no action has been initiated.

 

After the race, PRINIA was trotted and found to be sound. Further the filly was scoped and found to have EIPH (Gr.1).

 

RACE NO.8(85) – THE GREAT SECRET PLATE: (About)  1600 Metres – Runners -  15

 

At the start, ALPHA GENE (Dashrath Singh, dr.3) jumped outwards and made contact with RED RIOT (Amyn Merchant, dr.4).

 

At the start, CAPRIFLA (Vishal N. Bunde, dr.13) jumped inwards and went across RAGNAR (Bhawani Singh, dr.12) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

At the start, WILLY WONKAA (S. J. Sunil, dr.15) jumped inwards onto JUICED (Prashant P. Dhebe, dr.14) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, RECIPROCITY (App. Mustakim Alam) was observed to have been a bit erratic as a result shifted out and had to be steadied. Trainer H. J. Antia has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, TWELFTH EARL (App. P. Vinod) shifted out and went onto CAPRIFLA (Vishal N. Bunde) forcing it outwards onto WILLY WONKAA (S. J. Sunil). App. P. Vinod has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 300 metres, ALPHA GENE (Dashrath Singh) reacted differently to the whip as a result shied outwards and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, WILLY WONKAA (S. J. Sunil) shifted out and carried TRUTH AND DARE (R. Ajinkya) outwards. Jockey S. J. Sunil has been cautioned.

 

Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been fined Rs.500/- for bringing his charge CAPRIFLA late in the paddock.

 

After the race, NORD was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, WINSTAR was found to be sore by left fore. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, WINSTAR had lost right fore shoe.

 

After the race, RUE ST HONORE’ was trotted and found to be sound.
