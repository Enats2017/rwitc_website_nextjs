ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

MUMBAI MEETING 2010/11, SEVENTEENTH DAY, SUNDAY, 16TH JANUARY, 2011.

 (PENETROMETER:  2.1; GOING: GOOD)

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED 5 METRES AWAY FROM THE ORIGINAL POINT FROM 1400 METRES UP TO THE WINNING POST. FOR THE RACES STARTING FROM THE BACK STRETCH RAILS WERE PLACED AT AN AVERAGE WIDTH OF ABOUT 2.5 METRES FROM 1300 METRES UP TO 1000 METRES.

 

RACE NO 1. (133) THE ATMOSPHERE PLATE  (About) 1200 Metres. Runners – 7

 

At the request of Jockey Y.S.Srinath, the saddle on his mount GLAMOROUS was adjusted at the start.

 

Jockey Suraj Narredu (GREY PHANTOM) has been fined Rs.2000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker and in the process went across the rightful ALEUTIAN ZONE (App P Trevor). 

 

Soon after the start, SWOOSH (C Rajendra) shifting in before the Red Marker and hampered the running of GLAMOROUS (YS Srinath) who in turn inconvenienced VICTORESS (Towfeeq R Shaikh) who had also shifted out at that stage, as such, Jockey C Rajendra has been fined Rs.3000/- for failing to exercise proper control over his mount.

 

App P Trevor (ALEUTIAN ZONE) reported that his mount lugged in during the race. Trainer Sangramsinh N Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ALEUTIAN ZONE was observed to have a cut injury at the left hock and ROB N ROB was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 2. (134) THE AHMED I. RAHIMTOOLA TROPHY (About) 1000 Metres. Runners- 5

 

Soon after the start, SPRAUNCY (Amyn Merchant) has been fined Rs.2000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker and hampered the running of FLYING KISS (Dashrath Singh) who in turn shifted in made contact with BLACK MAGIC WOMAN (David Allan).

 

RACE NO 3. (135) THE GREATER MUMBAI POLICE TROPHY (About) 2400 Metres. Runners – 4

 

CAMACHO (CD Hayes) jumped out awkwardly towards the outside.

 

After the race, MIA STELLA was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 4. (136) THE RANJIT V. BHAT MEMORIAL GOLD TROPHY (About) 1000 Metres. Runners – 5

 

In the final stages of the race, FOREVER YOUNG (Dashrath Singh) shifted out and in the process caused ARES (YS Srinath) to travel wide who in turn forced EL TROPICO (App P Trevor) to travel wide.

 

SCHNELL (SP Ranjane) scrapped the rails as the gelding straightened for home.

 

 

Trainer Bezan Chenoy and Jockey YS Srinath were questioned with regard to the running and riding of ARES. The Jockey was Instructed to ride a waiting race and make his move at about 300 metres. Further, Trainer Bezan Chenoy concurred that the Jockey should have made his move from the inside of FOREVER YOUNG near 400 metres rather than waiting behind FOREVER YOUNG. Jockey opined that if he had taken gap on the inside FOREVER YOUNG, and there was possibility that FOREVER YOUNG could have shifted in and close that gap so he chose to come from the outside. Considering that the FOREVER YOUNG was about 3 lengths ahead while Jockey YS Srinath waited with ARES and riding out and after switching to the outside of FOREVER YOUNG near 200 metres, ARES had not improved in the remaining part of the race, the explanation was noted.

 

Trainer SK Sunderji and App P Trevor were questioned with regard to the running EL TROPICO. The Apprentice was Instructed to follow the front runners, ride out from 400 metres, do his best in the straight. It was observed that the Apprentice had lost position between 800 metres and 700 metres and should have closer to FOREVER YOUNG.  Trainer opined that the Apprentice had lost position near 700 metres and had waited for too long in the straight to make his move. Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by the Apprentice, he has been reprimanded for handling of his mount.

 

After the race, EL TROPICO and ARES were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 5. (137) THE SAHAAN JUVENILE MULTI-MILLION (About) 1400 Metres. Runners - 9

 

CROWN RULE (C.D.Hayes) backed out abruptly while being stalled and fell. The colt was examined by the Club’s V.O. and was found to be sore by the right hind leg, as such, the colt was withdrawn from the race before coming under the Starter’s Order. Trainer P.Shroff has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and to show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter, at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, SOUND OF RIVER was observed to have lost its left hind plate.

 

RACE NO 6. (138) THE CONTINUAL PLATE  - DIVISION I (About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 11

 

ASTON MARTIN (Mlalcolm Kharadi) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer M.K.Jadhav has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

PREMIER CONNECTION  (Y.S.Srinath) lost about 2 lengths at the start.

 

Jockey B Prakash (TZIGANE) has been fined Rs.2000/- for shifting in before the Red Marker.

 

Approaching 200 metres, GAMBA (S Sunil) kept an erratic course and in the process hampered the running of VISIONAIRE (App P Trevor). Trainer SK Sunderji has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

 

In the final stages of the race, TELL ME MORE (Dashrath Singh) lugged in and caused TZIGANE (B Prakash) to shift in who was awkwardly placed behind the heels of ASTON MARTIN (Malcolm Kharadi) and was steadied. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race with the gelding.

 

Jockey Zameer Sayyed (CAPTAIN SMART) has been reprimanded for not improving his position in the early part of the race and not being vigorous in his riding effort.

 

After the race, TZIGANE was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 7. (139) THE CLUB MED MILLION (About) 1200 Metres. Runners - 9

 

In the final 200 metres, VIOLETTA (B Prakash) lugged in and shifted ground and travelling some distance further took the running of SCHOLAR (Dashrath Singh) who was steadied. Trainer P Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race with the mare.

 

After the race, VIOLETTA was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO 8. (140) THE RAMNIWAS RAMNARAIN RUIA GOLD CUP (GRADE 2) (About) 2000 Metres. Runners – 8       No Reports

 

RACE NO 9. (141) THE CONTINUAL PLATE  - DIVISION II  (About) 1400 Metres. Runners – 10

 

App A Sandesh (LEAP OF FAITH) reported that his mount lugged in during the race. Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ADALLIA was found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “RIVER GEM” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.103 – THE NEW YEAR TROPHY (DIV.II) (1200 METRES) RUN ON  2ND JANUARY, 2011 AS COMPARED TO ITS PREVIOUS RUN IN RACE NO.56 – THE UTTAM SINGH TROPHY (DIV.I) (1200 METRES) RUN ON 12TH DECEMBER, 2011 WHERE IT WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer S.K.Sunderji and Jockey D.K.Ashish in the above connection. Trainer Sunderji stated that he had given similar instructions to Jockey D.K.Ashish on both the occasions, i.e. to make the running and to do his best. Jockey D.K.Ashish confirmed the instructions and stated that he had done his best in both the races.

 

Trainer S.K.Sunderji attributed the poor performance of RIVER GEM in Race No.56 to the following reasons :-

 

1)         In Race No.56 the field was much stronger as compared to Race No.103;

2)         In Race No.103 the winner clocked 1 Minute 13.51 seconds as compared to Race No.56 where the winner clocked 1 Minute 11.94 seconds i.e. 1.53 seconds faster;

3)         In Race No56, it was the first run of the gelding in Mumbai and the horse has shown slight improvement thereafter;

4)         The common horse UPROAR finished behind RIVER GEM in both the races.

 

After viewing the video recording of both the races, the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted.

---------------------------

RE : COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “ADALLIA” (PLACED THIRD) IN RACE NO.114 – THE COLUMBUS PLATE (DIV.I) (1200 METRES) RUN ON 6TH JANUARY, 2011 AS COMPARED TO HER PREVIOUS RUN IN RACE No.80 – THE ALVARITA PLATE  (1200 METRES) RUN ON 23RD DECEMBER, 2010 WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that they had questioned Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait in the above connection.

 

Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait attributed the poor performance of ADALLIA in Race No.80 to the following reasons :-

 

1)         In Race No.80 it was the first run of the horse in Mumbai and she had the disadvantage of being drawn on the extreme outside i.e. Draw -14;

2)         In Race No.80 his instructions to Apprentice M.A.Roushan were to be in forward position. However, immediately after the race the Jockey reported that the horse did not show initial speed as such he was unable to follow the instructions;

3)         In Race No.114 he had declared a stronger rider i.e. Jockey Imran Chisty who managed to ride the filly in a much better fashion, followed the instructions and the mare managed to finish third;

4)         On both the occasions, ADALLIA was available at 20/1.

 

After viewing the video recording of both the races, the explanation tendered by the Trainer and Jockey was noted.
