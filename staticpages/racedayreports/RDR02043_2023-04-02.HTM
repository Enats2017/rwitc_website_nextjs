RACE NO.1(170) THE STAR FORTUNE MILLION: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES - RUNNERS - 13

 

STRAUSS (Neeraj Rawal, dr.12) played up as the start was effected thereby losing considerable ground at the start. Trainer K. S. V. Prasad Raju has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

It was observed that SWIFT (P. Trevor) was inclined outwards in the straight. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of SNOWFALL and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, DESERT FIRE was found to be sore by the right fore. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s Sr. V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ARCANA was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(171) THE MAHARAJA SIR HARISINGHJI TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1600 METRES - RUNNERS - 10

 

AHEAD OF MY TIME (A. Sandesh, dr.5) was reluctant to stall. Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 400 metres, AHEAD OF MY TIME (A. Sandesh) shifted out under urgings thereby tightening GIANT STAR (Vishal N. Bunde) who had to be steadied. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of AHEAD OF MY TIME and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, REGENCY SMILE was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(172) THE BOMBAY CLUB MULTI - MILLION: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES  - RUNNERS -  9

 

On arrival at the starting gates, at the request of Jockey P. Trevor, VERROCCHIO was examined and found to be sound, the colt came back sound after the race.

 

After the race, Jockey Bhawani Singh (THE GENERAL) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight. Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of DREAM ALLIANCE and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, BUBBLY BOY was found to have injury on right fore heel.

 

RACE NO.4(173) THE C. N. WADIA GOLD CUP (GRADE 2): (ABOUT) 2400 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

Passing the 2200 metres, BLAZING BAY (T. S. Jodha) veered out and had to be steadied.

  

RACE NO.5 (174) POONAWALLA STUD FARMS AUCTION SALE STAKES: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, SENORITA D (Yash Narredu) shied away thereby inconveniencing SERRANO (N. S. Parmar) who had to be steadied.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, PROPHECY (A. Sandesh) leaned outwards due to which GENEROSITY (P. Trevor) racing on its haunches had to be steadied thereafter approaching the 300 metres MISTY (P. S. Chouhan) shifted in under urgings thereby tightening and inconveniencing GENEROSITY (P. Trevor). Jockeys P. S. Chouhan and A. Sandesh have been cautioned.

 

Passing the 300 metres, SERRANO (N. S. Parmar) shifted out and went across PROPHECY (A. Sandesh) who had to be checked. After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar reported that his mount was running green and was inclined outwards.

 

After the race, Jockey P. S. Chouhan (MISTY) reported loss of whip passing 300 metres. This being his second instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2022/23, he has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, SERRANO and PROPHECY were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(175) THE ROYAL ARION CLUB MILLION: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES -  RUNNERS -  12

 

At the start, JET TYPHOON (P. Shinde, dr. 6) jumped outwards and made contact with MOJO (Imran Chisty, dr.7).

 

At the start, FIDATO (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.11) jumped outwards and made contact with HELA (Bhawani Singh, dr.12).

 

After the race, JET TYPHOON was observed to have broken a blood vessel (2nd time) and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s Sr. V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.7(176) THE RAPIER MILLION: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES - RUNNERS - 11

 

WILD SPIRIT (A. K. Aniket, dr.7) jumped out slowly losing about 4 lengths at the start. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at the morning starting trails, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, BETWEEN FRIENDS (App. Hardias Gore, dr.6) jumped inwards and went across SPECULATOR (Bhawani Singh, dr.5) who was marginally slow to begin and made contact with MALAKHI (A. Sandesh, dr.4).

 

Passing the 700 metres, DOC MARTIN (S. Zervan) leaned outwards at the same time BOMBER (Dashrath Singh) shifted in thereby tightening SHADOWS (Vishal N. Bunde) racing between them causing the latter to check and drop back due to which BETWEEN FRIENDS (App. Haridas Gore) racing on its haunches had to check and change course thereby inconveniencing SPECULATOR (Bhawani Singh). After questioning, Jockeys S. Zervan and Dashrath Singh have been fined Rs.2,000/- each.

 

Jockey A. Sandesh (MALAKHI) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2022/23, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, WILD SPIRIT was trotted and found to be sound.
