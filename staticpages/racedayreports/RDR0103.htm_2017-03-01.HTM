RACE NO.1(196) – THE PRICELESS PLATE: (About) 2000 Metres – Runners – 8                                             

 

UNCLE SCROOGE (Neeraj Rawal) jumped out and thereafter did not raise an effective gallop thus losing about 4 lengths at the start. On being questioned after the race, Jockey Neeraj Rawal reported that his mount had tried to stop galloping and had to be niggled to carry on. As such, Trainer M. K. Jadhav has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing 700 metres, Jockey C. S. Jodha (RELENTLESS PURSUIT) was observed to have forced a passage between CAESARS STAR (App. Parbat Singh) and GLORIOUS OPINION (Santosh G.), in the process inconveniencing GLORIOUS OPINION (Santosh G.). Jockey C. S. Jodha has been cautioned.

 

Passing 250 metres, EIGER’S TIGER (Dashrath Singh) shifted out and at the same time ELEGANT BEAUTY (J. Chinoy) shifted in, due to which SABIQ (A. Sandesh) who was racing on their haunches, had to be checked and changed course.

 

Jockey Santosh G. (GLORIOUS OPINION) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit. This being his first offence, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

After the race, RELENTLESS PURSUIT was trotted and found to be sound. SABIQ had a gum injury.

An enquiry has been initiated into the running and riding of LOGIC and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, RAIN DANCE was observed to have a cut injury at the right hind bulb of heel. CENTAURUS, AEOLUS MAXIMUS, LOGIC, BAY OF LOVE, ALLEGRIA and RIDGEWOOD STAR were found to be sound in trot.

RE : APPEAL FROM TRAINER FAISAL A.ABBAS IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS TRAINER’S LICENSE W.E.F. 15TH FEBRUARY, 2017 TO 16TH APRIL, 2017 AND 29 DAYS FROM THE FIRST RACE DAY OF THE PUNE MEETING 2017 AND A FINE OF RS.50,000/ - ENQUIRY INTO THE POSITIVE REPORT IN RESPECT OF b g “CAPTAIN” (WINNER) IN RACE NO.74 – THE MARRINSKY PLATE (DIV.II) RUN ON SATURDAY, 27TH AUGUST, 2016 – TRAINER FAISAL A.ABBAS WAS GRANTED A STAY ON THE PUNISHMENT UNTIL HIS APPEAL WAS HEARD :

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 27th February 2017 considered Appeal dated 10th February, 2017 from Trainer Faisal A. Abbas on the captioned subject matter.

 

The Board of Appeal studied all papers, documents, proceedings, grounds of appeal etc. in the captioned matter. The Board of Appeal also heard Trainer Faisal A. Abbas / his Spokesman, Dr. Prashant Wandhare and considered the submissions made by him/his spokesman. The Board of Appeal having deliberated the matter and having applied their minds to the same, held the view that there was no doubt about the vicarious liability on the part of the trainer and that he was careless in the stable management. However, majority of the members of the Board felt that there was some merit in the mitigating circumstances presented by the trainer and that the over all punishment was rather harsh. The Board of Appeal therefore decided, to partially allow the appeal and accordingly modified the punishment by reducing the period of suspension from 90 days to 45 days and increased the fine from Rs.50,000/- to Rs.1,00,000/-. His deposit was ordered to be refunded.

 

In view of the stay granted, Trainer Faisal A. Abbas’ punishment would now come into effect from 2nd March, 2017 to 15th April, 2017 (both days inclusive).

 

Further, Trainer Faisal A. Abbas was informed that all the disabilities, as communicated to him vide office letter dated 8th February, 2017 would be applicable to him during the period of his suspension, as stated above.
