RACE NO.1(297) –THE MILITARY PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 5 

 

LADY BE GOOD (S. Zervan) jumped out slow losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that at the request of the Trainer, LADY BE GOOD was scoped at the Equine Hospital after the race and found to be EIPH (Gr.III). The filly is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ADMO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(298) – THE  VIBRANT PLATE: (About) 1800 Metres – Runners – 6

 

At the start, COUNSELLOR (Dashrath Singh, dr. 6) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

At the start, SILVER EDGE (Deep Shanker, dr. 3) jumped outwards and made contact with HARRIER (P. S. Chouhan, dr. 4).

 

Approaching 1200 metres, GOLD BOND (S. Zervan) shifted out and brushed the haunches of SHANGRI LA (N. S. Parmar).

 

The Sr. V.O. reported that at the request of the Trainer, HARRIER was scoped at the Equine Hospital after the race and found to be EIPH (Gr.II).

 

After the race, SILVER EDGE and HARRIER were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(299) –THE BANARSIDAS MILLION: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners – 8

 

EL COMANDANTE (J. Chinoy) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Altaf Hussain has been asked to give the colt more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, RUN FOREST RUN (App. H. Zeeshan, dr. 5) jumped outwards and made contact with EL COMANDANTE (J. Chinoy, dr. 6).

 

Approaching the 100 metres, RUN FOREST RUN (App. H. Zeeshan) was observed to have shifted in under the use of the whip. After questioning, App. H. Zeeshan has been cautioned.

 

After the race, Jockey N. Tauseef Ahmed (BENEVOLENCE) reported that his mount was green and hesitant to raise a fluent gallop. Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, ANATEVKA was trotted and found to be sound. The filly had a swelling at the girth area.

 

GRAND ARCHITECT (Deep Shanker) was placed fifth and EL COMANDANTE (J. Chinoy) was placed sixth by the Judge, in this race.

 

RACE NO.4(300) –THE VISCOL PLATE : (About) 1200 Metres–Runners - 7

 

Passing the red marker, MANZANITA (App. Niranjan S. Parmar) shied outwards starting a chain reaction as a result inconveniencing OPTIMUM (App. H. Zeeshan), STAR ANISE (App. G. Amit) and STARR VERDICT (App. Vishal N. Bunde).

 

After the race, App. Niranjan S. Parmar (MANZANITA) reported that his mount kept shying away from the railing throughout the race and in the straight hung out despite his efforts. Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

After the race, MANZANITA and OPTIMUM were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5(301) –THE SHARA BELLE TROPHY: (About) 1000 Metres–Runners - 6

 

At the request of Trainer Faisal A. Abbas, DANCE EMPEROR was examined at 1815 hours on 21/04/2018 and was found to have fever. As such, the gelding was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on Veterinary grounds. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting with him to race.

 

At the start, ART O WAR (Raghuveer Singh, dr. 4) jumped inwards and made contact with FINE TUNE (App. H. Zeeshan, dr. 3).

 

Passing the 200 metres, FINE TUNE (App. H. Zeeshan) shifted out under the whip and carried WIND CRAFT (App. M. Ayyar) outwards. Thereafter, passing the 150 metres FINE TUNE further shifted out and made contact with WIND CRAFT (App. M. Ayyar) who in turn shifted out inconveniencing MALAVIKA (Vishal N. Bunde). 

 

An objection was lodged by App. M. Ayyar, the rider of WIND CRAFT (Placed Second) against FINE TUNE (App. H. Zeeshan, Placed First). The reason being, in the last 150 metres FINE TUNE bumped his mount and pushed him at about 100 metres and also just before the winning post despite which his mount was full of running towards the end and this had cost him a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by App. M. Ayyar and App. H. Zeeshan who were accompanied by their Trainers, Mr. Sangramsinh N. Joshi and Mr. Mansoor Shah respectively, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing of the above race as under:-

 

WIND CRAFT                      -               FIRST;                                  MALAVIKA                                 -               THIRD;

FINE TUNE                          -               SECOND;                             ARABIAN STORM                  -               FOURTH

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held after the last race, interviewed App. H. Zeeshan who was accompanied by his Master, Trainer Shazaan Shah, for careless riding on his mount FINE TUNE and having heard him/his Master and having viewed the video recording of the race, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. H. Zeeshan and decided to suspend his Apprentice Jockey’s license for 2 race days for careless riding on FINE TUNE.  It was noted that Mr. H. Zeeshan’s Apprentice Jockey’s license already stands suspended up to 4th August, 2018 for an earlier offence on HUDSON. Therefore, the Stewards decided that should he apply for an Apprentice Jockey’s license for the Season 2018/19, the same will be granted to him, however, his license shall stand further suspended w.e.f. 5th August, 2018 to 11th August, 2018 for careless riding on FINE TUNE (permitted to ride work/mock race). Further, he was warned to be more careful in the future.

 

After the race, MALAVIKA, ALMOST MAGICAL (examined on 23/04/2018) and ART O WAR (examined on 23/04/2018) were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(302) –THE LAURITA PLATE : (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 11

 

SIR SONG (S. A. Amit) jumped out slow losing about a length at the start.

 

At the start, ADVANCE TO CONTACT (S. G. Prasad, dr. 11) jumped out awkwardly inwards, at the same time PARADISE (Kunal Bunde, dr. 9) jumped outwards due to which STAR ACE (Anil Baandal, dr. 10) drawn between them was tightened for room and had to be steadied.

 

At the start, MASTER SERGEANT (App. Vishal N. Bunde, dr. 5) jumped inwards and made contact with AEOLUS MAXIMUS (Dashrath Singh, dr. 4).

 

Passing the 400 metres, STAR ACE (Anil Baandal) drifted out under pressure and raced in the middle of the course.

 

After the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh (AEOLUS MAXIMUS) reported that his mount hung out in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Zervan (GALTERO) reported that his mount was uncomfortable in the straight and was not striding out freely as such he was unable to ride the mount to its complete advantage.

 

After the race, GALTERO was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HIS MASTER’S VICE, AEOLUS MAXIMUS, SIR SONG and ADVANCE TO CONTACT were trotted and found to be sound. STAR ACE had lost its right fore shoe.

RE : (1) APPEAL FROM JOCKEY C. S. JODHA IN RESPECT OF ALLEGED ACCUSATIONS MADE AGAINST HIM BY THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB – ENQUIRY INTO THE DRIFT OF ODDS ON “AIRLIFT” IN RACE NO.153 – THE CIRCLE OF BLISS PLATE RUN ON 8th OCTOBER, 2017 AT THE PUNE RACE COURSE; AND (2)  APPEAL FROM JOCKEY C. S. JODHA IN RESPECT OF BEING SEVERELY CAUTIONED FOR THE FUTURE BY THE STEWARDS OF THE CLUB IN THE ENQUIRY INTO THE IN AND OUT RUNNING OF HORSES ON 28th AND 29th OCTOBER, 2017 UNDER THE CHARGE OF TRAINER FAISAL A. ABBAS:         

 

The Board of Appeal at its meeting held on 18th April, 2018 considered Appeals dated 2nd February, 2018 and 2nd March, 2018 from Jockey C. S. Jodha in the above connection.

 

The Board of Appeal having studied and considered all documents and papers that were submitted including Jockey C. S. Jodha’s Grounds of Appeal and heard the submissions made by him and his Spokesman, Mr. Akhtar Peerbhoy. The Board of Appeal felt that the Stewards of the Club had only made observations in the above cases which were conveyed to Jockey C. S. Jodha and there were no orders passed by them. The Chairman referred to the under mentioned Rules of Racing of the Club:

 

Rule 46 of the Rules of Racing of the Club states “An Appeal shall lie to the Board of Appeal from any decision or order of the Stewards of the Club not being a decision or an order on an Objection in a particular race taken immediately at the conclusion of such race”.

 

Rule 47(a) of the Rules of Racing of the Club states “Notwithstanding anything contained to the contrary in these Articles or Rules of Racing or in any Bye-law for the time being in force, only the person aggrieved by any decision or order of the Stewards of the Club shall be entitled to prefer an Appeal in writing from such decision or order to a body called “The Board of Appeal” hereinafter called the Board”.

 

In view of this, the Board of Appeal decided that since the appeals do not meet the criteria of Rule 46 and Rule 47(a) of the Rules of Racing of the Club, these appeals do not lie with the Board. The deposits paid by Jockey C. S. Jodha towards the appeals were ordered to be refunded.
