EASTERN MONARCH (Akshay Kumar, dr.7) was reluctant to be stalled. As such, Trainer Sanjay Kolse has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the colt to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

RAMBLER (C. Umesh, dr.1) was reluctant to be stalled.  As such, Trainer Shazaan Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

ASHWA BRAZIL (App. Bharat Singh, dr.5) lost about a length at the start.

 

At the start, CINDERELLA’S DREAM (Amyn Merchant, dr.2) jumped inwards onto RAMBLER (C. Umesh, dr.1).

 

Approaching the 700 metres, BEE MAGICAL (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in and went across CINDERELLA’S DREAM (Amyn Merchant) racing along the rails. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

After the Race, App. Bharat Singh (ASHWA BRAZIL) reported that his mount hung-in in the straight, despite his efforts, the gelding was subjected to veterinary examination and found to be sound. As such, Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.2 (69) – THE MULRAJ GOCULDAS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 7

 

After being stalled, IRISH GOLD (Akshay Kumar, dr.7) was fractious and reared up thereby delaying the start of the race. As such, Trainer Dallas Todywalla has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the horse to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, SON OF A GUN (A. Prakash, dr.3) jumped out awkwardly.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, MISS AMERICAN PIE (David Allan) shifted out under the use of whip thereby tightening and inconveniencing TURN AND BURN  (N. S. Parmar) racing on its outside causing the latter to check and drop back. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation, Jockey David Allan has been fined Rs.5,000/-. 

 

In the final stages of the Race, DREAM SELLER (App. Ramswarup) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing DIVINE STAR (App. Mustakim Alam) racing along the rails causing the latter to check and drop back. After the race on questioning, App. Ramswarup stated that his mount hung-in in the straight, despite his efforts. As such, Trainer P. S. Chouhan has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the Race, Jockey Vivek G. (MARKET KING) reported loss of whip in the final stages of the race. This being his second instance during the current Mumbai Meeting 2025/26, he has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, IRISH GOLD was trotted at the stables and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3 (70) – THE JAYARAMDAS PATEL GOLD TROPHY: (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

On arrival at the starting gates, the Tongue Strap used on BLACK THUNDER was observed to have come off, the same was checked and put back on. As such, Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been fined Rs.1,000/- for his lapse.

 

Soon after the start, BREAK POINT (Akshay Kumar, dr.3) jumped inwards and went across KIMIKO (App. Bharat Singh, dr.2) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 200 metres, KIMIKO (App. Bharat Singh) shifted out thereby carrying LOOKING LIKE A WOW (App. Ramswarup) outwards. App. Bharat Singh has been cautioned.

 

RACE NO.4 (71) – THE P. R. MEHTA TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1000 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

G VALENTINO (Akshay Kumar, dr.9) lost about two lengths at the start.

 

At the start, SHADAI (C. Umesh, dr.2) jumped inwards onto SEQUENTIAL (App. Mustakim Alam, dr.1).

 

At the start, LATIOS (Vivek G., dr.6) jumped inwards and made contact with TURBULENT (A. S. Peter, dr.5).

 

Soon after the start, LATIOS (Vivek G.) shifted in and went across TURBULENT (A. S. Peter). Jockey Vivek G. has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 900 metres, BLUE JET (Yash Narredu) shifted out onto GUNFIRE (Neeraj Rawal) at the same time HIGHLAND SPIRIT (Antony Raj S.) shifted in due to which LATIOS (Vivek G.) racing between them had to be checked. After questioning all concerned, Jockey Antony Raj S. has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

After the Race, Jockey Vivek G. (LATIOS) reported that his mount was shying away from the other runners and thereby finishing the race on the outer portion of the track. As such, Trainer Aman Altaf Hussain has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

After the Race, Trainer Narendra Lagad lodged a complaint to the Stipendiary Stewards that he was not satisfied with App. Musakim Alam’s riding on his charge SEQUENTIAL, the Stipendiary Stewards are looking into the same and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, the V.O. reported the following:

 

G VALENTINO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

LATIOS was observed to have a cut injury above its left hind hock and lost its left hind shoe.

RE: REPORTED TO THE STEWARDS FOR CARELESS RIDING ON “ESPERANZA”– RACE NO.61 – THE PLEASURE HUNT TROPHY - RUN ON SATURDAY, 10TH JANUARY 2026:

 

In connection with the captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 18th January 2026, having heard the submissions made by App. Ramswarup and his Master Trainer Adhirajsingh Jodha, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race in question and having deliberated in the matter at length. The Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by App. Ramswarup. In that, in the last 50 metres he had shifted out on his mount ESPERANZA, because of which Jockey R. Ajinkya (FONTANA) had to check his horse and change course, this cost Jockey  R.Ajinkya  a certain race. It was also noted that App. Ramswarup had made no visible efforts to correct his mount. Therefore, the Stewards held App. Ramswarup guilty of careless riding on his mount “ESPERANZA” in the above race and decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for two race days i.e. on 8th & 12th February 2026 (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “MADAM RICH” (DRAWN 5) IN RACE NO.110 – THE THREPTIN FILLIES’ AND MARES’ STAKES (GRADE 3) (DIST. 1800 MTRS.) RUN ON SATURDAY, 4TH OCTOBER 2025:

 

In connection with the above captioned subject matter, the Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 18th January 2026, having heard the submissions made by Jockey A Prakash, Trainer B. Prithviraj, as also the Stipendiary Stewards, having viewed the video recording of the race in question having deliberated in the matter at length. The Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey A Prakash. In that, in an effort to follow his instructions Jockey A Prakash hit the front but thereafter misjudged the pace and took an unusual lead thereby placing his mount at a disadvantage and ran him into the ground.

Keeping all the circumstances in mind including Trainer B.Prithviraj’s dissatisfaction with his riding, the Stewards held Jockey A Prakash guilty of Unsatisfactory Riding on his mount MADAM RICH in the above race and decided to suspend his Jockey’s licence for six race days i.e. w.e.f 8th February to 7th March 2026 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).
