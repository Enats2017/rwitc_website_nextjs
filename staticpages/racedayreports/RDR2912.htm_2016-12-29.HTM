Soon after the start, ADVANCE TO CONTACT (Bhawani Singh, dr.11) shifted in under urgings thereby inconveniencing GLORIOUS ANGEL (C. S. Jodha dr.10). After questioning, Jockey Bhawani Singh has been fined Rs. 2,000/-.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey C. S. Jodha, the rider of GLORIOUS ANGEL (Placed Second) against POWER SHADOW (Neeraj Rawal, Placed First). The reason being, POWER SHADOW shifted out at about 300 metres whereby he received a check. Thereafter, between the 150 and 100 metres, POWER SHADOW again shifted in due to which he had to check his mount and despite the two checks, his horse was moving fast and finished on, and he had lost a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards upheld the objection and revised the order of placing of the race as under :-

 

GLORIOUS ANGEL           -               FIRST;                   ADVANCE TO CONTACT                -               THIRD;

POWER SHADOW             -               SECOND;             ELSA                                      -               FOURTH

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held after the race interviewed Jockey Neeraj Rawal for careless riding on his mount POWER SHADOW. Having heard Jockey Neeraj Rawal and viewing the video of the race, the Stewards  were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by him and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license for 15th January, 2017 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race). 

 

Subsequently, Jockey Neeraj Rawal informed this office that he did not wish to appeal against the above decision and requested to advance his punishment date. In view of the same, his Jockey’s license now stands suspended for 12th January, 2017 for careless riding on his mount POWER SHADOW.

 

After the race, SEACRETS and POWER SHADOW were trotted and found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ETERNAL DANCER” (DRAWN 15) IN RACE NO.58 - THE DHUN P KOTHAVALA TROPHY (1000 METRES) – RUN ON THURSDAY, 15th DECEMBER, 2016 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that although ETERNAL DANCER had received interference at the start and Jockey Kishore Kadam had reported after the race that the filly had stumbled after entering the straight, they still decided to take on record the views of Trainer Rehanullah Khan and Jockey Kishore Kadam, in this matter as it looked more like an awkward stride and not a stumble.

 

The video of the race was viewed in the presence of the Trainer and Jockey and Jockey Kishore Kadam was asked to elaborate on the above incident, to which he explained that he was knocked out badly at the start due to which he was unable to get into a handy position. Thereafter, approaching the 400 metres the filly had stumbled whereby he collected the filly and had not put pressure as he was worried for the wellbeing of the filly and himself.  The Trainer concurred with the Jockey’s views.

 

Considering the Jockey’s apprehension about his and the filly’s wellbeing after the filly had taken an awkward stride in the straight and also keeping in mind the interference at the start, the enquiry was closed.

RE:WITHDRAWAL OF “ZAZOU” FROM RACE NO.60 –THE NAWABZADA RASHIDUZZAFAR KHAN TROPHY, RUN ON 18/12/2016 -TRAINER ALTAMAASH A.AHMED:

 

ZAZOU was accepted to race within 10 days of the mare having received the Extra Corporeal Shock Wave Therapy. The mare was withdrawn and Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed was fined Rs.10,000/- for having contravened the Calendar Notification.

 

Further, on a report from the Sr. Veterinary Officer stating that Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed had failed to record the treatment given to ZAZOU, an enquiry was initiated in the matter.

 

When questioned, Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed stated that he was aware of the Calendar Notification in this regard but had miscalculated the number of days and had accepted the mare on the 9th day of her having received the treatment. Further, although he had inadvertently missed recording the treatment given to the mare in the Medical Log Book, the same was recorded in his medical requisition slip.

 

Having heard the submissions made and scrutinizing the Medical Log Book and the Requisition Book, Trainer Altamaash A. Ahmed was fined a further sum of Rs.5,000/- for this lapse on his part.
