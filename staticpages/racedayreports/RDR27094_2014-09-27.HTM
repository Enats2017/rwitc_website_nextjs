RACE NO.1(157) – THE  DANCING GLANCES PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – 8 Runners

 

Trainer Imtiaz A.Sait has been fined Rs.1,000/- for presenting his charge ST BARTS in the Parade Ring with a Tongue Strap, an equipment for the use of which no prior permission was obtained. The gelding left the Parade Ring without a Tongue Strap.

 

ST BARTS (C.S.Jodha) stumbled at the start.

 

GRAND MARCH (I.Pasha) was inclined to lug-out while negotiating the bend and lugged-out further in the early part of the straight and went across ST BARTS (C.S.Jodha). Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, GRAND MARCH, SUN HOT and OCEAN STAR were found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(158) – THE WARSAW PACT PLATE : (About) 1400 Metres – 6 Runners

RACE NO.3(159) – THE  DANCING GLANCES PLATE (DIV.I): (About) 1200 Metres – 9 Runners

 

MUMBAI KING (App.Yash Narredu) lost 2 lengths at the start.

 

Near 1000 metres, POLAR ZONE (App.S.J.Sunil) drifted out and went on to SOMBRERO (P.Trevor), who in turn went on to WINDSOR QUEEN (C.S.Jodha) who travelled wide.

 

On questioning, Jockey S.G.Prasad (MOUNTAIN WIND) stated that his instructions were to be in 2nd/3rd/4th position. He said that his mount was tightened at the start between BURGUNDY ROSE (Darren Williams,) and SOMBRERO (P.Trevor), and therefore he could not follow his instructions. Thereafter, his mount did not respond to his urgings. The Club’s V.O. was requested to examine the horse and he reported that no abnormality was detected.

 

After the race, BURGUNDY ROSE was observed to have an abrasion at the left hind fetlock. MUMBAI KING was found to be sound.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ANCIENT TREASURE” IN RACE NO 139 RUN ON 14TH  SEPTEMBER 2014 – TRAINER MANSOOR SHAH & JOCKEY AMYN MERCHANT

 

Trainer Mansoor Shah and Jockey Amyn Merchant were questioned regarding the captioned subject matter.

 

Trainer Mansoor Shah stated that the Instructions to the Jockey were to take a good jump, settle wherever comfortable, come close with the field gradually since the colt is drawn on the outside and to do his best. He said that he had also informed the Jockey that the colt tends to run out as he had done in its run during the Mumbai Meeting. He added that he had given the colt a mock race during the Pune Meeting but the colt raced along the rails and therefore he had not drifted out. He said that he had declared Jockey Amyn Merchant as he felt that the Jockey would be able to control the colt better. To another question,  he said that he had not contemplated any change of equipment as the colt had run fairly in the Mock race.

 

After reviewing the race, the Trainer observed that the Jockey could not bring the colt in close to the field as he hung outwards and thereafter ABDERUS took him further out while straightening for home. He said that since the colt was hanging out, the Jockey had to control him and ride him. He opined that in the last 200 metres the jockey could have used the whip a little earlier but he had to control the horse as well and therefore was probably late in using the whip.

 

To another question, Trainer Mansoor Shah stated that he had given the colt a minor chance as he had no form coming into this race. His main concern was whether the horse would run straight as he had prepared the horse to run straight and had even given him a Mock race which was satisfactory. He said that he was satisfied with the riding effort of the Jockey keeping in mind that the horse has the habit of hanging out which was known to the Jockey and it may have played on his mind and hence he was more concerned in controlling the horse and riding out as well. 

 

Jockey Amyn Merchant confirmed the instructions and added that he was aware that the horse has the habit of hanging out and had behaved similarly during the Mumbai Meeting. He added that in the race, the horse had jumped well and he had settled him nicely and gradually moved in close on to the field but the horse started to hang out while negotiating the bend and he had to control his mount and ride out hence he was at his mount hands & heels. Also, while straightening for home, his mount was taken wide by ABDERUS and he had used the whip but the horse did not respond and between the 150 – 100 metres his mount had stumbled and he had to gather the horse again and ride out. It was put to the Jockey that he should have been more vigorous from 350 metres onwards to which he reiterated that he had done his best under the circumstances.

 

The matter was reported to the Stewards for action as deemed fit to take.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 27th September, 2014 interviewed Trainer Mansoor Shah and Jockey Amyn Merchant in the above connection and having viewed the video recording of Race No.139 as also Race No.298 run during the Mumbai Meeting 2013/14 and keeping in mind that ANCIENT TREASURE had hung out in the previous race also, decided to give them the benefit of a grave doubt and severely cautioned them for the future.

 

Further, in view of the horse’s repeated behavior of hanging out in a race, the Stewards decided to ban ANCIENT TREASURE from racing until shown to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards in three Mock Races.

Trainer Nosher Cama was questioned with regard to the captioned subject matter.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “ZARAR” IN RACE NO 138 RUN ON 14TH SEPTEMBER, 2014 – TRAINER MANSOOR SHAH :

Trainer Mansoor Shah was questioned with regard to the captioned subject matter.

 

At the outset, the Trainer admitted that the gelding had performed poorly. He said that the Instructions to the Jockey were to take a good jump, sit in a forward position, keep a straight course and ride out. The Trainer observed that the Apprentice had not ridden the horse soon after the start and the horse did not run freely. He said that and he did not have any specific reason for the poor performance but the weak handling by the Apprentice may be a reason for the poor showing. He added that when the gelding placed in its last start, that was the first time that the gelding had ever Placed. As for the future plans, the Trainer  opined that the gelding may not have early speed and he would race the gelding over an extended trip over 1200 metres or 1400 metres..

 

The explanation tendered by the Trainer was noted, however, Apprentice K.N.Bhati has been cautioned for not riding out his mount soon after the start to take up the position as instructed.

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “AFFIRMATION” IN RACE NO 139 RUN ON 14TH SEPTEMBER 2014 – TRAINER NAZAK CHENOY :

 

Trainer Nazak Chenoy was questioned with regard to the captioned subject matter.

 

The Trainer stated that Blinkers were used for the first time in the career of the gelding which had not suited him. She added that the gelding had worked well with the Blinkers during the morning Track work and also had jumped well out of the Gates during the trials.  The Trainer said that she had declared Jockey A.Sandesh and with a change of equipment, she had expected the gelding to perform well but he ran a disappointing race. She added that next day morning, the gelding had developed fungal infection in the chest area which may have contributed in the gelding being uncomfortable. She said that the gelding was still a maiden.

 

To another question, she stated that the gelding had been racing well over distances of 1200 and 1400 metres and therefore she had raced him in this 1200 metres race. She said that as compared to the previous race, there were only two changes, viz.. the gelding had Blinkers on and there was a change of Jockey to A.Sandesh from N.S.Parmar who has ridden him all the time. The Trainer informed that she would be discontinuing the use of Blinkers as a measure to improve the gelding in the next race. The explanation was noted.
