RACE NO.1(343) – THE GOODBYE PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 12 

 

GUNGA DIN (J.Chinoy) played up in the stall. The gelding was taken out and examined by the Club’s V.O. who certified him fit to race. Also, it was observed that the Sheep Skin Nose Band on GUNGA DIN was broken. Since it was not a declared equipment, the same was removed. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching 150 metres, RIPOSTE (App.Jorawar Singh) shifted in and went across BIG BRO (App.G.Amit) who had to be checked.

 

After the race, SPEED MAGIC was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HOPE SPRINGS was observed to have broken a blood vessel. Being a second time bleeder, the colt is not to race for the stipulated ban period of eight weeks and has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HOPE SPRINGS, GUNGA DIN and ARMEL were trotted and found to be sound. GUNGA DIN had a gum injury.

ADDITIONAL REPORT:

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO 1) THE COMPLAINT RECEIVED FROM APPRENTICE JAYKUMAR AGAINST JOCKEY S.ZERVAN FOR HITTING HIM ON THE HEAD IN THE JOCKEYS’ ROOM AFTER THE RUNNING OF THE EIGHT RACE OF THE DAY ON SATURDAY, 23RD APRIL, 2016 AND 2) JOCKEY S.ZERVAN’S RUDE BEHAVIOUR TOWARDS AN OFFICIAL OF THE CLUB :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Saturday, 23rd April, 2016 and thereafter on Sunday, 24th April, 2016 interviewed Jockey S.Zervan as also Apprentice Jaykumar who was accompanied by his Master, Tr.M.Narredu, in the above connection.

 

The Stewards of the Club heard Jockey S.Zervan as also Apprentice Jaykumar who was accompanied by his Master, Tr.M.Narredu. They  also heard Mr.Kuleshwar Singh, Assistant Stipendiary Steward of the Club and viewed the video recording of Race No.341. Having heard all concerned and having deliberated the matter, the Stewards of the Club were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey S.Zervan and held him guilty of gross misconduct. He was informed that the Stewards appreciate that the safety of the Jockeys and Horses in a race is of prime importance and cases of interference will certainly be dealt with severely. However, if a Jockey is aggrieved for any reason, he has no right to take law into his own hand but has to report the incident to the Stipendiary Stewards who will look in the matter and take appropriate action. However, keeping in mind that Jockey S.Zervan had admitted his mistake and apologized for the same and assured the Stewards that he would not repeat this behavior, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided that should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2016/17, it will be granted to him, however, his license shall stand suspended w.e.f. 28th July, 2016 to 7th August, 2016 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race), Further, he was required to give a written apology to Mr.Kuleshwar Singh for his rude behavior and he was informed that a repetition of a similar offence will entail severe disciplinary action.

---------------------

RE : APPEAL FROM TRAINER VINESH IN RESPECT OF THE FINE OF RS.20,000/- IMPOSED ON HIM BY THE STIPENDIARY STEWARDS – ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPARATIVE PERFORMANCE OF “SHIVALIK ROSE” IN RACE NO.306 RUN ON 9TH APRIL, 2016 WHICH THE FILLY WON AS COMPARED TO HER RUN IN RACE NO.274 RUN ON 26TH MARCH, 2016 WHERE SHE WAS UNPLACED :

 

The Stewards  of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 24th April, 2016 considered appeal dated 19th April, 2016 from Trainer Vinesh in the above connection. The Stewards of the Club considered all papers and proceedings before them including Trainer Vinesh’s grounds of appeal and having heard him and his Spokesman, Mr.Peter Deubet,  decided to partially allow the appeal and to reduced the fine to Rs.15,000/-.

-------------------

RE:ENQUIRY INTO THE COMPLAINT RECEIVED FROM JOCKEY S. S. RATHORE FOR HITTING HIM WITH AN IRON ROD AFTER MORNING TRACK WORK ON 24TH MARCH, 2016 WHICH HE ADMITTED BEFORE THE STIPENDIARY STEWARDS :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 17th April, 2016 interviewed Jockeys T.Mahesh and S.S.Rathore in the above connection. The Stewards of the Club heard Jockey T.Mahesh as also Jockey S.S.Rathore who was accompanied by his Witness, Jockey Dharmender and having deliberated the matter, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey T.Mahesh and held him guilty of gross misconduct on the premises of the race course. However, keeping in mind that he had admitted his guilt and tendered a written apology for the same, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license with immediate effect i.e. from 18th April, 2016 to 24th April, 2016. Thereafter, should he apply for a Jockey’s license for the Season 2016/17, it will be granted to him, but his license shall stand suspended with effect from 28th July, 2016 to 20th August, 2016 (both days inclusive). Further, he was warned that on a repetition of a similar offence, very severe action will be initiated against him. Jockey T.Mahesh was told that during the period of his suspension he is not permitted to ride work/mock race, he is not permitted to attend racing, on course or off course or to reside on the premises of the racecourse.
