RACE NO.3 (36) – THE ALBERTINI PLATE:  (ABOUT) 2000 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

On arrival at the starting gates, OLD FASHIONED (P. ADDITIONAL REPORTS:

 

RE: MORNING TRACKWORK HELD ON 9TH AUGUST 2026:

 

GOLDEN DEGREE was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not permitted to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. As such, the gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RE:   MERCY PETETION LODGED BY JOCKEY S.G.PRASAD IN RESPECT OF SUSPENSION OF HIS JOCKEY’S LICENCE FOR 6 RACE DAYS DURING THE PUNE MEETING 2026 - UNSATISFACTORY RIDING ON HIS MOUNT SHIRSA IN RACE NO. 136 – THE J.V. SHUKLA – ASHWAMITRA PLATE (DIST. 1400 METRES) (DRAWN 6) RUN ON 26TH FEBRUARY 2026 AT THE MUMBAI RACE COURSE

 

The Board of Appeal after hearing the submissions made by Jockey S.G.Prasad and his spokesperson Mr. S. Kapoor, decided to convert the remaining two race days of his punishment into a suspended sentence and the suspended sentence would come into force should he be found guilty of unsatisfactory riding at any time up to the end of the Pune Meeting 2027.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “ENCINO” (DRAWN 03) RUN IN RACE NO. 31. THE YOHAN .Z.POONAWALLA TURF CLUB TROPHY GRADE III DIST. 1400 MTRS ON SATURDAY, 8TH AUGUST 2026.

 

In this connection, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey A.Sandesh . In that, it was noted that he had ridden an over confident race which may have cost him the race. Therefore, keeping the above in mind , it has been decided to suspend his Jockeys licence for two race days i.e. on 6th and 12th September 2026 (permitted to ride work/mock race) for riding an overconfident race. Further, Jockey A. Sandesh been severely cautioned for his unprofessional behavior/statement.

 

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RUNNING AND RIDING OF “DIEGO GARCIA” (DRAWN 2) RUN IN RACE NO.137 - THE AZIZ H. AHMEDBHOY TROPHY (DIST. 2400 MTRS) RUN ON 7TH MARCH 2026:

 

In this connection the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Yash Narredu. In that, it was noted that he had failed to preserve his mount in the early part of the race and the same was contrary to its established running pattern thereby placing his mount at a disadvantage. Therefore, it was decided to fine Jockey Yash Narredu a sum of Rs.10,000/- for riding an ill-judged race.
