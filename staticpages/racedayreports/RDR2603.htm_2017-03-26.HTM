RACE NO.1(243) – THE JAYANT M.SHAH PLATE: (About) 2400 Metres – Runners – 8                                             

 

At the request of Jockey Dashrath Singh, the saddle on his mount KITTY HAWK was readjusted.

 

OTHELLO (J. Chinoy) was restless in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. RED FORT (Nirmal Jodha) jumped out slow, losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainers Vishal Gaikwad and Magansingh P. Jodha have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Soon after the start, KITTY HAWK (Dashrath Singh) shifted in and went across KOAL (App S. A. Amit) and CARE FREE (C. S. Jodha) who had to be steadied. Jockey Dashrath Singh has been fined Rs.1,000/-.

 

Jockey S. J. Sunil (DANSE DEBONAIRE) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his third offence, his license has been suspended w.e.f. 15th April, 2017 to 16th April, 2017 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).

 

After the race, VENTURA, KITTY HAWK and CARE FREE were trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(244) – THE CURSETJEE DHUNJISHAW TROPHY (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 5                                            

                                                                                                                                                      

After the race, BENEZEER was found to be sore by the left fore leg. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.3(245) – THE MANYATTA PLATE: (About) 1600 Metres – Runners - 9

 

Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been fined Rs.1,000/- for accepting with his charge FROSTED, whilst the filly was under Starter’s ban. The filly was struck out at the acceptance stage.

 

DEM SAO (Bhawani Singh) refused to be stalled inspite of substantial efforts, as such, the mare was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order. Since DEM SAO had behaved in a similar manner in her previous start, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the mare to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

HURACAN (P. Trevor) was restless in the stall and not allowing the rider to settle. Trainer Subhag Singh has been asked to give the gelding more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start ROYCE (N. S. Parmar, dr.10) jumped outwards and had to be steadied.

 

Approaching 1200 metres, in an attempt to settle his mount, Jockey A. Sandesh (STAR ACE) shifted out and went across BLACK JAGUAR (Shrikant Kamble) who had to be steadied and thereafter continued outwards and made contact with OSCILLATION (App. S. A. Amit) forcing him outwards.

 

After the race, Jockey P. Trevor (HURACAN) reported that upon passing 400 metres, in an attempt to change his whip hand, his reins had slipped out of his hands. The same was borne out from the CCTV footage of the race and Jockey P. Trevor has been asked to be more careful in the future.  

 

After the race, Jockey N. S. Parmar (ROYCE) reported that his mount hung out in the initial stages of the race. Trainer A. M. Jadhav has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

An objection was lodged by App. S. A. Amit, the rider of OSCILLATION (Placed Third) against STAR ACE (A. Sandesh, Placed Second). The reason being, near the first bend, STAR ACE (A. Sandesh) who was racing along the rails, shifted out and bumped his horse severely whereby he lost considerable ground and thereafter, from the last position his horse covered ground in the straight and in his opinion the above incident had cost him the race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned, in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to over-rule the objection.

 

RACE NO.4(246) – THE D.W.REID PLATE: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners - 8 

 

At the start, MAGNUM OPUS (N. B. Kuldeep, dr. 3) jumped outwards on to MAGICAL MEMORY (A. Sandesh, dr.4).

 

Soon after the start, ARAKAWAH (Shrikant Kamble) shifted in and went across MAGICAL MEMORY (A. Sandesh) who had to be steadied.

 

The saddle on HOUSE OF COMMONS (P. Trevor) was observed to have slipped during the race. Trainer Nirad Karanjawala has been fined Rs.2,000/- for faulty saddling of his charge.

 

Jockey Santosh G. (AN JOLIE) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his second offence, he has been fined Rs.5,000/-.

 

After the race, MAGICAL MEMORY was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ANVILL STAR and MAGNUM OPUS were found to be sore by the right fore leg. Both the horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.5(247)–THE MEMORIES OF YOU PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners - 10

 

At the start, WILD FIRE (Dashrath Singh, dr.6) jumped outwards on to STAR ANISE (C. S. Jodha, dr.7) and thereafter shifted in and went across DANCE OF FIRE (Bhawani Singh, dr.5) who was slow to begin.

 

Soon after the start, BROOKLYN BRIDGE (Akash Agarwal, dr.8) shifted in due to which STAR ANISE (C. S. Jodha, dr.7) also shifted in thereby tightening DANCE OF FIRE (Bhawani Singh) for room, forcing Jockey Bhawani Singh to check his mount and drop back.

 

Passing 150 metres, WILD FIRE (Dashrath Singh) weakened and dropped back on to ARC SHINE (Amyn Merchant) who had to be checked and change course thereby inconveniencing BROOKLYN BRIDGE (Akash Agarwal).

 

After the race Jockey Bhawani Singh (DANCE OF FIRE) reported that his mount was inclined to run out from the 800 metres despite his efforts. Since the filly had behaved in a similar manner in the past, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, Jockey Kishore Kadam (MACGYVER) reported that his mount hung out in the straight. Trainer B. Prakash has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the gelding.

 

After the race, GAZINO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(248) – THE TATA HOUSING TURF CHAMPIONSHIP (GR.3) :(About) 1800 Metres – Runners - 3

 

COLOMBIANA (A. Sandesh) jumped out slow, losing about 3 lengths at the start.

 

RACE NO.7(249) –THE KARL UMRIGAR TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners – 12

 

Soon after the start, POINT THE STAR (Bhawani Singh, dr.3) shifted out on to GANDALF (Shrikant Kamble, dr.4) who had to be steadied.

 

Soon after the start, LA MAGNIFIQUE (Neeraj Rawal, dr.6) shifted in and went across the field thereby inconveniencing MAGICAL SCRIPT (Kishore Kadam) who had to be steadied. Jockey Neeraj Rawal has been cautioned.

 

Approaching 1100 metres, FORTITUDE (N. S. Parmar) shifted in inconveniencing POINT THE STAR (Bhawani Singh).

 

Approaching 1000 metres, SOMEONE SOMEWHERE (J. Chinoy) shifted in thereby inconveniencing FORTITUDE (N. S. Parmar).

 

Passing 900 metres, SANDALPHON (S. Sunil) shifted in thereby tightening SOMEONE SOMEWHERE (J. Chinoy) along the rails.

 

After the race, Jockey S. Sunil (SANDALPHON) reported that his mount was running green throughout the race.

 

After the race, SANDALPHON was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.8(250) –THE SION F.NESSIM PLATE: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners – 12

Since the filly had behaved in a similar manner in her previous starts, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures and to show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter in a Mock Race, before next accepting to race.

 

DIBABA (S. J. Sunil) jumped out slow, losing about 3 lengths at the start.

 

At the start, DOUBLE NINE (App. Vishal N. Bunde, dr.7) jumped outwards on to ROYAL SAPPHIRE (Bhawani Singh, dr.8) who had to be checked.

 

After the race, FOREVER YOURS and FIREBOLT were found to be sore by the left fore leg and right fore leg respectively. Both the horses have to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.
