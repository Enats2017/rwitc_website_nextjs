KIMIKO (C.Umesh, dr.4) lost about three lengths at the Start, Trainer Karthik Ganapathy has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the Mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to Race.

 

After the Race the V.O reported the following:

 

GOLDEN DANCER was examined and was found to have an injury on its Left Hind Fetlock

 

 RACE NO.2 (100) – THE MATHRADAS GOCULDAS TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 6

 

At the Start, MARKET KING (Vivek G. dr.2) jumped outwards onto SUN OF A GUN (A.Prakash dr.3)

 

After the Race, an objection was lodged by Jockey P. Trevor rider of IRISH GOLD, placed third by the judge against Jockey Neeraj Rawal rider of ULTIMO, placed 1st by the judge and Jockey Vivek G, rider of MARKET KING placed 2nd by the judge stating that “ in the last 50 meters ULTIMO came out and MARKET KING has come in and I had to break my horse’s stride and momentum which cost me a certain Race.” The Stewards after having heard the submissions made by all three Jockeys, having viewed the replay of the Race from all angles, in conjunction with the Stipendiary Stewards and acting on their own observations of the Race decided to Over-Rule the objection and the placing remain unchanged as declared by the Judge.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards after questioning all concerned decided to fine Jockey P. Trevor Rs 10,000/-and Jockey Vivek G.  Rs 5,000/-   

 

After the Race, the V.O reported the following:

 

CHRISTOPHANY was trotted and was found to be Sore by Right Fore. As such, the mare has to be passed Fit by the Club’s V.O before next accepting to Race.

 

RACE NO.3 (101) – THE NADIR & YAWAR RASHID TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1400 METRES – RUNNERS - 8

 

Trainer Behram Cama has been fined Rs.3,000/- for putting in a late declaration in respect of his charge AXLROD.

 

At the start, SENECA (P. Trevor, dr.4) jumped inwards onto ASHWA BRAZIL (Bharat Singh, dr.3) at the same time AXLROD (T. S. Jodha dr.1) jumped outwards onto WINGS OF FURY (Neeraj Rawal dr.2) due to the incident WINGS OF FURY had to check and drop back.

 

In the final stages of the race, BISHOP (Vivek G.) shifted out under the use of whip thereby carrying AMAZING RULER (Amyn Merchant) outwards, After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation, Jockey Vivek G. has been fined Rs 5,000/-

 

After the Race, the Jockey’s reported the following:

 

Jockey T. S. Jodha (AXLROD) reported that the saddle used on his mount slipped passing the 800 metre marker. As such, Trainer Behram Cama has been fined 2,000/- for his lapse.

 

Jockey Amyn Merchant (AMAZING RULER) reported that the saddle used on his mount slipped soon after the start . As such, Trainer Faisal A Abbas been fined Rs 2,000/- for his lapse

 

After the Race, WINGS OF FURY was trotted and found to be sound

 

 

RACE NO.4 (102) – THE R. M. & RUKMANI GOCULDAS MILLION: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS – 12

 

ASHWA ANKARA (K. Nazil dr.10) was reluctant to be stalled .Trainer Sangramsinh N. Joshi has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the Mare to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials before next accepting to Race.

 

At the start, AGEISJUSTANUMBER (Mustakim Alam dr.9) jumped inwards onto HOUSE OF LORDS (Bharat Singh dr.8) who further shifted in and went across MILA (T. S. Jodha dr.7).

 

Soon after the start, SILVER STRIKE (C. Umesh) shifted in and went across PURE (N. S. Parmar) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 900 Metres AGEISJUSTANUMBER (Mustakim Alam) shifted in thereby tightening and inconveniencing MILA (T. S. Jodha) racing on its haunches causing the latter to check and drop back. After questioning and not being satisfied with the explanation, Jockey Mustakim Alam has been fined Rs. 3000/-.

 

After the Race the V.O reported the following:

 

STARSEED was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.5 (103) – THE N. M. IRANI TROPHY: (ABOUT) 1200 METRES – RUNNERS - 9

 

Soon after the start, ORGANISED CHAOS (Nirmal Jodha) leaned in onto ACE IN THE FIELD (Akshay Kumar) who further shifted in onto JANHAVI (Mustakim Alam)  who made contact with SPARKS FLY(A. Prakash) After questioning and not being satisfied ,Jockey Nirmal Jodha has been fined Rs 2,000/-

 

Approaching the 400 Metres PALOMA (Yash Narredu) leaned out onto ORGANISED CHAOS (Nirmal Jodha) who further shifted out thereby carrying STORM MAJESTY (P. Trevor) outwards. After the Race on questioning, Jockey Yash Narredu (PALOMA) reported that his mount was running erratic throughout the race. As such, Trainer M. Narredu has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to Race.

 

The Equine Hospital reported the following:

 

ROGUE was examined at the Stables and was found to be sound
