RACE NO.2(48) –  THE SEA RULER PLATE : (About) 1200 Metres – 8 Runners

 

At the request of Trainer Altamaash A.Ahmed, ANTARCTICA was examined at 1800 hours on 23/08/2019 and found to be sore by the right fore leg, as such, the filly was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn on veterinary grounds. The filly has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

STEPPENWOLF (Yash Narredu) was reluctant to be stalled. Trainer Nosher Cama has been asked to give the colt more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Passing the 100 metres, BARACK (App.P.S.Kaviraj) shifted out under pressure and carried NIGHT HUNT (Akshay) outwards, who in turn went across PLEIADES (App.S.A.Amit) who had to be steadied. After questioning, App. P. S. Kaviraj has been reprimanded.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that PLEIADES had left eye injury (sore).

 

After the race, STEPPENWOLF was observed to have an abrasion at the right hind fetlock joint. NIGHT HUNT was found to be sound in the trot.

 

RACE NO.3(49) –  THE MAYOR’S TROPHY : (About) 1800 Metres –  8 Runners

 

Soon after the start, TURACO (A. Sandesh, dr.5) shifted in and went across ARROWHEAD (N. S. Parmar, dr.3) causing the latter to be checked and change course. Jockey A. Sandesh has been cautioned.

 

Passing the 600 Metres, ARROWHEAD (N. S. Parmar) was observed to have weakened and dropped back and thereafter maintained an erratic course thereby tightening and inconveniencing TURACO (A.Sandesh) and ELATION (P.Trevor) causing both horses to be checked. On being questioned after the race, Jockey N.S.Parmar stated that his mount was erratic despite his efforts. Trainer P.Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

   

After the race, Jockey Dashrath Singh (BLACK CHERRY) reported that his mount was inclined outwards in the straight. Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to take remedial measures before next accepting to race the filly.

 

Jockey S. Zervan (SILVER KING) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount . This being his first offence during the current Pune Meeting, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

The Equine Hospital reported that POLARIS had left eye injury (sore).

 

After the race, BLACK CHERRY was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.4(50) –  THE SECRET ROMEO PLATE : (About) 1200 Metres – 13 Runners

 

Soon after the start, CRAY CRAY (Akshay, dr.9) shifted in under urgings on to ARABIAN MUKTAR (Amyn Merchant, dr.5) who in turn shifted in thereby tightening SAMURAI (App.K.Pranil, dr.4) who in turn shifted in and made contact with ISINIT (App. A. S. Peter, dr.3). Due to this incident, SAMURAI (App. K. Pranil) had to be checked. Thereafter, ETERNAL DANCER (N. S. After questioning all concerned, Jockey Akshay and Jockey N. S. Parmar have been suspended for 14th September, 2019 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

Parmar) thereby starting a chain reaction, due to which CRAY CRAY (Akshay) and WABI SABI (Santosh G.) were inconvenienced and OUTSTANDING (App. Raghuveer Singh) had to be checked. After questioning, Jockey J. Chinoy has been suspended for 14th September, 2019 for careless riding (permitted to ride work/mock race).

RE: ENQUIRY INTO THE RIDING OF JOCKEY DASHRATH SINGH ON “MAJESTIC WARRIOR” IN RACE NO.224 – THE AHMED I.RAHIMTOOLA TROPHY (DIV.I) RUN ON 6TH APRIL, 2019 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that immediately after the race Trainer Narendra Lagad had lodged a verbal complaint that he was unhappy with the riding of Jockey Dashrath Singh on MAJESTIC WARRIOR.

 

Due to the off-season and non availability of Jockey Dashrath Singh, the enquiry could not be completed earlier. Thereafter, the enquiry had to be postponed twice since Jockey Dashrath Singh was riding at Kolkotta.

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad and Jockey Dashrath Singh were subsequently questioned on 31st July, 2019 in the above connection.

 

Trainer Narendra Lagad confirmed that immediately after the race he had lodged a verbal complaint that he was not satisfied with the riding of Jockey Dashrath Singh on MAJESTIC WARRIOR in Race No.224.  He said that this was just the second run of the horse in Race No.224 and subsequently having watched the race again he had observed that the horse was hanging out in the straight and Jockey Dashrath Singh had explained to him that the horse had reacted to the whip and hung out.  He said that subsequently he has started using Blinkers on the horse during track work and the horse showed improvement and considering this, he would like to give Jockey Dashrath Singh the benefit of the doubt in this case.

 

Jockey Dashrath Singh said that coming into the straight he used the whip 2 or 3 times from the right and the horse started shifting out under pressure, therefore, he immediately put his whip away, collected the horse, brought him on an even keel and got into him but when he again used the whip, the horse shied away not giving him sufficient time to react. Jockey Dashrath Singh reiterated that he had done his best.

 

Having heard the submissions made by the Trainer and Jockey and having viewed the video recording of the race, it was pointed to Jockey Dashrath Singh that whilst he himself admitted that the horse shifted out after the first 2/3 cracks of the whip on the right, as a corrective measure, he should have immediately changed his whip hand. Not being satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Dashrath Singh, he has been fined Rs.5,000/- for failing to change his whip hand despite his mount shifting out.
