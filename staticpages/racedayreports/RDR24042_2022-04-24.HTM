ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

MUMBAI MEETING 2021/22, SPECIAL RACE DAY, SUNDAY, 24TH APRIL, 2022.

 

(PENETROMETER: 3.8 (LONGCHAMP); GOING: GOOD)

 

RACE DAY REPORTS

 

RAILS WERE PLACED AT THE ORIGINAL POINT.

 

On a request from Trainer Zadmal Singh, permission was granted to Trainer Subhag Singh to saddle his charge TARZAN in the 3rd race, at his own (Trainer Zadmal Singh’s) risk and responsibility.

 

On a request from Trainer Dallas Todywalla, permission was granted to Trainer Nirad Karanjawala to saddle his charge BEEMER in the 6th race, at his own (Trainer Dallas Todywalla’s) risk and responsibility.

 

RACE NO.1(168) – THE TRAINERS TROPHY  - DIVISION II: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  11

 

In the process of backing in M’SARRAT (App. Haridas Gore, dr.9) kicked the front gate due to which the hind shoe came off as such the filly was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s order. In view of above, Trainer Rehanullah Khan has been asked to show his charge in a mock race to the satisfaction of the Starter, before next accepting to race. Thereafter the Sr. V.O. reported that on examination filly found to have cut injury on right hind fetlock and above hock joint, the filly has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, DOWSAEL (A. Prakash, dr.2) jumped inwards and went across CUPIDO (H. Zeeshan, dr.1), who was marginally slow to begin.

 

After the race, POWER OF BLESSINGS (Dashrath Singh) was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, ZACAPA was observed to have lost left fore shoe.

 

After the race, CUPIDO was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(169) – THE RACING OFFICIALS TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  12

 

At the start, HILMA KLINT (App. A. K. Aniket, dr.4) jumped outwards onto DALI SWIRL (App. A. Prakash, dr.5).

 

At the start, MAJORELLA BLUE (T. S. Jodha, dr.10) jumped inwards onto LAGERTHA (App. A. S. Peter, dr.9).

 

Soon after the start, MOUNT SINAI (K. Nazil) shifted in onto LAGERTHA (App. A. S. Peter). Jockey K. Nazil has been cautioned.

 

Approaching the 1000 metres, LORD AND MASTER (P. Trevor) leaned inwards on to POWER OF INFINITY (Dashrath Singh) who inturn shifted in thereby tightening RHYTHM OF NATURE (C. S. Jodha) in the process LAGERTHA (App. A. S. Peter) was also inconvenienced. After the race, Jockey P. Trevor reported that his mount LORD AND MASTER hung in throughout the race despite his efforts. Trainer P. Shroff has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

Approaching the 400 metres, DALI SWIRL (App. A. Prakash) shifted out forcing FAST RAIN (N. S. Parmar), MAJESTIC WARRIOR (App. Mustakim Alam) & LORD AND MASTER (P. Trevor) outwards. App. A. Prakash has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

After the race, LAGERTHA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, HILMA KLINT was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

After the race, LORD AND MASTER was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.3(170) – THE JAYANT M. SHAH AND CHAMPAK M. SHAH TROPHY: (About) 1800 Metres – Runners -  8

 

It was observed that App Kirtish Bhagat (CANDESCENT STAR) persisted with the use of whip from the right despite his mount  drifting out in the straight as such he has been fined Rs.2,000/- for improper use of whip. It was also was observed that App. Kirtish Bhagat had used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his third offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, his Jockey’s licence will stand suspended for first two Race Days of Pune Meeting 2022 (Dates for which will be intimated in due course) (permitted to ride work/mock race)

 

After the race, AIRA was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The mare has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

RACE NO.4(171) – THE HINDU TROPHY: (About) 1400 Metres –Runners- 6

 

MIGHTY THUNDER (Akash Agarwal, dr.2) jumped out tardily losing about 2 lengths at the start.

 

In the final stages of the race ESPERANZA (H. Zeeshan) leaned inwards and had to be steadied. After questioning, Jockey H. Zeeshan has been cautioned for persisting to use the whip from the left despite his mount shifting in.

 

RACE NO.5(172) – THE TIMES OF INDIA TROPHY: (About) 1200 Metres – Runners -  11

 

At the start, MONTGOMERY (N. S. Parmar, dr.6) jumped inwards the same time MY NAME IS TRINITY (App. A. S. Peter, dr.5) jumped outwards and they made contact.

 

At the start, MOJO (K. Nazil, dr.3) jumped inwards and went across SEIRRA DELA PLATA (Neeraj Rawal, dr.2) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

MOZELLE (T. S. Jodha, dr.7) shifted in and went across the field before passing the red marker. Jockey T. S. Jodha has been severely reprimanded.

 

After the race, Jockey P Trevor (SWIFT) reported that his mount was running green.

 

After the race, App. A. S. Peter (MY NAME IS TRINITY) reported that his mount was running green.

 

After the race, SWIFT was trotted and found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.6(173) – THE GOODBYE PLATE: (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 12

 

Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been fined Rs.1,000/- for coming late in the paddock to give instructions to App. A. K. Aniket and Jockey Raghuveer Singh as such delaying the start.

 

On the arrival at the start at the request of Jockey P Trevor his mount SILVER STEPS was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. and found to be sore from right fore and was certified unfit to race as such the gelding was withdrawn before coming under starter’s order. The gelding has to be passed fit by Club’s V.O., before next accepting to race.

 

On the arrival at the start, at the request of Jockey Neeraj Rawal his mount VERDANDI was examined by the Club’s Sr. V.O. who certified her fit to race.

 

At the start, WAFY (Raghuveer Singh, dr.4) jumped outwards throwing its head and made contact with BAKU (T. S. Jodha, dr.5). Trainer Imtiaz A. Sait has been asked to take remedial measures, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, VERDANDI (Neeraj Rawal, dr.6) jumped inwards and made contact with BAKU (T. S.  Jodha, dr.5) thereby unbalancing itself.

 

At the start, BEEMER (App. Kirtish Bhagat, dr.10) jumped inwards onto DAGGER’S STRIKE (M. Ayyar, dr.9) who was marginally slow to begin.

 

Approaching the 300 metres, BAKU (T. S. Jodha) leaned outwards onto SPIRITUAL ROCK (Dashrath Singh) and had to be steadied.

 

App. A. K. Aniket (CAMPAIGN) was observed to have used the whip in excess of the prescribed limit on his mount. This being his first offence during the current Mumbai Meeting 2021/22, he has been fined Rs.3,000/-.

 

RACE NO.7(174) – THE TRAINERS TROPHY  - DIVISION I: (About) 1000 Metres – Runners -  12

 

Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been fined Rs.1,000/- for bringing his charges SKY HAWK, CIPHER & SCOTTISH SCHOLAR late in the paddock as such delaying the start.

 

BETWEEN FRIENDS (C. S. Jodha, dr.7) was reluctant to stall at the start. In view of above, Trainer S. S. Shah has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the gelding to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

DAULAT MAI (Akash Agarwal, dr.12) planted and lost considerable grounds at the start. In view of above, Trainer Narendra Lagad has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show the filly to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

At the start, CIPHER (A. Sandesh, dr.3) jumped inwards and made contact with AHREN (App. Haridas Gore, dr.2).

 

Approaching the 200 metres, HISTORIC (H. Zeeshan) shifted out under the whip and carried BETWEEN FRIENDS (C. S. Jodha) outwards. Jockey H. Zeeshan has been fined Rs.2,000/-.

 

The Stipendiary Stewards will be looking in running and riding of BETWEEN FRIENDS and a report will be submitted shortly.

 

After the race, BETWEEN FRIENDS & CIPHER were trotted and found to be sound.
