RACE NO.1(157) – THE KOKU – KIKA TROPHY (DIV.I): (About) 1400 Metres – Runners - 12

 

After being stalled, ABU AL BUKHOOSH (A. J. Nilesh) played up and flipped over and got cast underneath the stall. The other horses were unloaded and the Starting Gate was moved and the gelding was taken out and withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order. In view of this behavior, Trainer Faisal A. Abbas has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. The Sr. V.O. reported that ABU AL BUKHOOSH had sustained bruise injuries on the poll and above the right eye. The gelding was sent to the Saddling Enclosure for further management by the Equine Hospital Vets. The Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

SIR DESMOND played up in the stall and put his left hind leg on the side bar which got stuck. The leg was relieved from the side bar and the gelding was taken out and found to have sustained bruise injury on the left hind leg, as such, SIR DESMOND was certified unfit to race and was withdrawn before coming under the Starter’s Order. In view of this behavior, Trainer S. K. Sunderji has been asked to give his charge more gate practice and show him to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race. Further, Trainer is required to obtain a fitness certificate for the gelding from the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

 

CELESTIAL LIGHT (C. S. Jodha) was reluctant to be stalled and GREY ONE (Vikram Jodha) jumped out slow, losing about 5 lengths at the start. Trainers Imtiaz A. Sait and Subhag Singh have been asked to give their respective charge more gate practice and show them to the satisfaction of the Starter at morning starting trials, before next accepting to race.

 

Trainer C. D. Katrak has been fined Rs.3,000/- for having declared Apprentice Jockey Arman Ali Jafri on his charge GREGORIAN CHANTS, while the Apprentice did not hold a valid license under the R.W.I.T.C. Ltd. Rules of Racing. He subsequently declared Jockey Amyn Merchant.

Approaching 1200 metres, MAKINO (Neeraj Rawal) shifted in and went across VIKING (Dashrath Singh) who had to be steadied.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE LOSS OF WHIP BY JOCKEY R. AJINKYA ON “BLACK JAGUAR” IN RACE NO.126 – THE PLEASURE HUNT PLATE (DIV.II)  - RUN ON 19th JANUARY, 2017 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that Jockey R. Ajinkya (BLACK JAGUAR) was observed to have lost his whip approaching the 400 metres,

 

The Stipendiary Stewards stated that having questioned Jockey R. Ajinkya and having viewed the blow up of the incident of whip fall, they were not certain whether the loss of whip was intentional or accidental and this being his  second offence during the current Mumbai Meeting, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as they deem fit.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 12th February, 2017 having considered the above report, decided to give Jockey R. Ajinkya the benefit of the doubt.

 

However, this being Jockey R. Ajinkya’s second offence, he was fined Rs.2,000/- by the Stipendiary Stewards.

*******************

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE LOSS OF WHIP BY JOCKEY AKSHAY GAIKWAD ON “SUPER BOLT” IN RACE NO.126 – THE PLEASURE HUNT PLATE (DIV.II) - RUN ON 19th JANUARY, 2017 :

 

The Stipendiary Stewards reported that Jockey Akshay Gaikwad (SUPER BOLT) was observed to have lost his whip approaching the 300 metres, After the race, Trainer Mansoor Shah had expressed his dissatisfaction regarding the manner in which the Jockey had lost his whip.

 

Having questioned Jockey Akshay Gaikwad and having viewed the blow up of the incident of whip fall, the Stipendiary Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Akshay Gaikwad as the loss of the whip was at a crucial part of the race which could have a bearing on the end result. As such, the matter was reported to the Stewards for such action as they deem fit.

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 12th February, 2017 considered the above report and having heard Trainer Mansoor Shah and Jockey Akshay Gaikwad and having viewed the video of Race No.126 as also the incident of the whip fall, were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Akshay Gaikwad. However, keeping in mind that he had recently completed riding ten winners and was permitted the use of the whip in races, the Stewards decided to give him the benefit of grave doubt and cautioned him to be more careful in the future. Further, he was advised to practice the use of the whip under the guidance of the Riding Instructor. The Stewards were also not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Trainer Mansoor Shah and decided to caution him for changing his statement in the enquiry.

RE : REQUEST FROM TRAINER NAZAK CHENOY TO REMOVE THE 2400 METRES MOCK RACE BAN IMPOSED ON “LUCAS”:

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Sunday, 12th February, 2017 considered letter dated 11th February, 2017 from Trainer Nazak Chenoy on the captioned subject matter and in view of the assurance given by her that LUCAS will only be racing over shorter distances for the foreseeable future which would not involve him being required to gallop past the Track Entrance/Exit, they decided to remove the Mock Race ban imposed on the horse.

 

However, in future if Trainer Nazak Chenoy decides to race the horse in a race above 2000 metres, she would have to show the horse to the satisfaction of the Stipendiary Stewards in a Mock Race before accepting with the horse.
