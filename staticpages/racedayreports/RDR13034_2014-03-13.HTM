RACE NO.1(256) – THE ROYAL DEEDS PLATE:(About)1600 Metres– Runners - 9

Jockey S.G.Prasad (LIGHT UP MY DAY) reported the loss of whip near 300 metres. This being the second occasion of Jockey S.G.Prasad losing the whip during the current Mumbai meeting, he has been fined Rs.1,000/-.

 

After the race, PRINCE ARIUS  was observed to have broken a blood vessel and is not to race for the stipulated ban period of four weeks. The gelding has to be passed fit by the Club’s V.O. before next accepting to race.

After the race, PRINCE ARIUS was found to be sound.

 

RACE NO.2(257) –THE  SIMPLY GORGEOUS PLATE (DIV.II): (About) 1000 Metres –Runners – 12

Jockey Deep Shanker (CUT DIAMOND) reported that his mount ran green during the race.

 

An objection was lodged by Jockey N.S.Parmar, rider of  NATURE BOY (Placed Fourth) against WIND CRAFT (Prabhu Naidu, Placed First). The reason being, in the last furlong, his horse was full of running and there was enough gap on the inside of WIND CRAFT for him to go through, however, WIND CRAFT came in because of which he lost a certain race.

 

After considering the submissions made by the Jockeys concerned in conjunction with that of the Stipendiary Stewards  and relying on their judgment, the Stewards decided to overrule the objection.

 

Due to the interference to NATURE BOY (N.S.Parmar), the gelding while regaining its balance, shifted out and hampered BASIC LIFE (S.P.Ranjane) who in turn went out on to NEW STAR (D.K.Ashish).   

 

After the race, NATURE BOY was found to be sound, but having a bruise on the left hock. BE COOL was found to be sound.

RE : ENQUIRY INTO THE POOR PERFORMANCE OF “RUBENS” IN RACE NO.209 – THE NUCLEUS TROPHY (DIV.I) RUN ON SUNDAY, 16TH FEBRUARY, 2014 :

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on Thursday, 13th November, 2014 considered the evidence obtained by the Stipendiary Stewards in the above enquiry. They interviewed Jockey Dashrath Singh as also Trainer Narendra Lagad and having heard them and having viewed the video recording of Race No.209 as also two other races of the horse during the current Mumbai Meeting i.e. Race No.163 and Race No.243, the Stewards were not satisfied with the explanation tendered by Jockey Dashrath Singh and held him guilty of unsatisfactory riding on RUBENS. The Stewards noted that he was told by the Trainer that RUBENS performs well only when he is in a forward position and he was specifically instructed to make every effort to try and hit the front or to be in 2nd position. Even conceding that he received slight interference at the start, however, thereafter he did not use the whip or make sufficient effort to sit well up as instructed and on the contrary dropped back further due to which the horse performed poorly. Trainer Narendra Lagad also concurred with the Stewards that Jockey Dashrath Singh should have used the whip and tried to sit well up as instructed. The Stewards noted that Jockey Dashrath Singh was riding a fancied runner and such riding from a Jockey of his calibre was not accepted. However, taking an overall view and keeping in mind Jockey  Dashrath Singh’s recent clean record at this centre, the Stewards took a lenient view and decided to suspend his Jockey’s license w.e.f. 30th March, 2014 to 12th April, 2014 (both days inclusive) (permitted to ride work/mock race).
