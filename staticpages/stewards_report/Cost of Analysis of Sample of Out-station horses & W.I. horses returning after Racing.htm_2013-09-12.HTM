ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD,

 

NOTICE

To: All concerned.

 

RE: COST OF ANALYSIS OF SAMPLES IN RESPECT OF OUT-STATION HORSES & WESTERN INDIA HORSES RETURNING AFTER RACING AT OTHER CENTRES

 

The Committee of the Club at its meeting held on 27th August 2013 has decided that the cost of analysis of the urine samples drawn from out-station horses and Western India based horse/s returning after racing at other Turf Clubs will be borne by the respective Owner/s of that horse/s.

 

This notice will come into force with immediate effect.

 

 

Sd/-

Sr. Stipendiary Steward,

ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD  

Pune: September 9, 2013.
