ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD.

 

NOTICE

To: All Trainers.

 

RE: 2 YEAR OLD HORSES – PERMITTED TO BE BROUGHT IN TO CLUB’S STABLES

 

As per the Prospectus for the Pune Meeting 2013, races for 2 year old horses are scheduled from 29th September 2013 onwards.

 

In view of the above, Trainers are hereby informed that they are permitted to swap/ exchange the 2 year old horses that are accommodated outside the Club’s stables with the older horses in their charge.

 

This movement of horses will be permitted w.e.f 16thth September 2013.
