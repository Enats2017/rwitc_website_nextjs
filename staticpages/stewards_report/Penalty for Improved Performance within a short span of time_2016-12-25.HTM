ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

 

R E V S I E D    N O T I C E

 

To: Trainers/ Jockeys.

 

RE: PENALTY FOR IMPROVED PERFORMANCE WITHIN A SHORT SPAN OF TIME

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 25th December 2016 have decided that in the absence of any obvious reason like a severe interference or accident or similar incident, a marked improvement in the performance of a horse, within 10 days of its earlier run, will result in a mandatory minimum fine of Rs.10,000/-.

 

This is not in derogation to any other punishment which may be imposed on a Trainer/ Jockey after due enquiry.   

 

 

Sd/-

Assistant Secretary,

ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

Mumbai: December 30, 2016.
