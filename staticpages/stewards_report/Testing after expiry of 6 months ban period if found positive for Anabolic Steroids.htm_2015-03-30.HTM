ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

 

N O T I C E

To: All Trainers

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 29th March 2015 have decided that when a horse banned from racing for a period of 6 months if found positive for Anabolic Steroid/s, then such horse/s shall be subjected to testing for Anabolic Steroids before acceptance and shall not be accepted to race till such time that the sample comes negative for Anabolic Steroids. The testing charges shall be borne by the Owner concerned.

 

 

 

Sd/-

Sr. Stipendiary Steward,

ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

Mumbai: March 30, 2015.
