ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

 

N O T I C E

To: Jockeys

 

RE: REVISION OF FINES FOR OFFENCES RELATING TO THE USE OF WHIP IN EXCESS

 

The Stewards of the Club at their meeting held on 12th July 2016 have decided to increase the fines with regard to the Use of Whip in excess as under, which will be effective from the commencement of the Pune Meeting 2016:

 

1st Offence – Rs.3,000/- (increased from Rs.1,000/-)

2nd Offence – Rs.5,000/- (increased from Rs.3,000/-)

 

All other norms with regard to the notice pertaining to the Use of Whip remain the same.

 

 

Sd/-

Assistant Secretary,

ROYAL WESTERN INDIA TURF CLUB, LTD

Pune: July 14, 2016.
